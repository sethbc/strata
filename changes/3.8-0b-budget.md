### Changed
- The delay holds up to 5.4 s, and a division up to that length plays unhalved.
- A bypassed delay frees CPU only; its line no longer takes memory the voices need.
- A MIDI map, arc ring or grid fader on the delay time now spans 10 ms to 5.4 s, so the same position plays a longer time than before; scenes and PSETs keep their times.
- Karplus's lowest pitch is 11.7 Hz: a lower note plays 11.7 Hz.
- Restart norns after updating: a new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataDelay.sc`.
