### Fixed
- Under sustained overload, a setting or a re-pitch sent to an ii port just before a note no longer takes the room the queue holds for a line still waiting, so that line is no longer held back an extra step (#719).

### Changed
- Each ii device now says in its registration whether its commands address ports (the ER-301 and the TELEXo do), and the ii queue reads it there instead of from a list of its own; a device that does not say is refused (#717).
