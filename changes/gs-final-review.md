### Fixed

- Holding a loop on the grid's patterns page, a further key of the line now moves the loop's other end even after a variation switch, an order's next letter, a chain link or a recall replaced the pattern; before, it turned that step on in the new pattern.
- On a 16×16 with side 1 on one pane and side 2 on the other, a voice's keys on the two panes no longer pair up: holding a step on one side and pressing a key on the other makes no loop and sets no level, and each key toggles its own step.
- In move, randomize (RN, ALT+K2) and the Euclidean fill give each step they turn on a random value A, as a tap does, so the new hits move the voice.
