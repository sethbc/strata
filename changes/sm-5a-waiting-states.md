### Changed
- With quantize actions on beat or bar, a grid key whose action waits for the next beat or bar blinks between 15 and 2 until it acts: the play key (PL, and NV on the 8×8), a voice's FZ key, both keys of a scene whose recall waits (from the grid or from SCENES' K2 and K3) and the position cell of a waiting jump or two-key loop. Before, only a waiting bookmark key blinked, and a second press could take an action back with nothing on the grid to show it was waiting.
- SEQ's hint reads `K2:cancel` while a pattern start waits for its beat or bar, since K2 then takes the start back.
- On the BUFFER page with ALT held, arc ring 1 is drawn at 8 while the selected voice's scan is held, whether the ring or a grid's HD key holds it.
- The grid manual says that a hold (a step held for its editor, a recorder key held to clear, CL and RN, NV held for the chooser) lights nothing more until it acts.
