### Changed
- **Development: the 3.7 design.** No code changes. `docs/design/3.7-design.md` is the design for rhythm, approved on 2026-11-15: a buffer cut into up to 64 slices, by its transients or into equal parts, played by steps, grid keys and MIDI notes; scan modes (bounce, one shot) and a scan as a speed; a tempo, a key and a normalize gain that follow each file, with voices that stay on the beat by stretch or repitch; trig conditions with fill, pattern directions and a chance for the next scene; a delay on the beat with ping-pong; an envelope per voice and four random sequences as modulation sources. The facts it was built from, each with its source, are under docs/design/3.7-facts/.
