### Changed
- Each voice's filter and sends now run once per voice, in one mix stage after its grains, instead of in every grain synth. The sound is the same (measured), except that a filter, send, amp or crossmod change or an accent during a source switch's crossfade or cut now also reaches the old buffer or input tail, and a voice filter on hears a per-synth fade (the attack, a release, a switch or a slot fade) a little differently.
- Each grain stream takes about 16 KB less real-time memory.
