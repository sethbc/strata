### Added
- Grid keys for the gesture recorders (a press records, plays and stops; a half-second hold clears), the macro faders and banks (the 256's page 1 rows 10-13 and bank keys, a macro fader page key on the 128s' faders page) and the morph (a morph fader on every scenes page but the 64's, and the 256's A and B rows).
- The 256's page 1 row 9 columns 5-11 mute voices 1-7 on a tap and solo them on a half-second hold; every layout's voice keys light a running voice that is silenced at 6.
- A step key held 0.3 s opens the step editor for its voice and step; its release closes it.

### Changed
- Grid step keys toggle on release, not on press, on every layout, so a held step can be edited without toggling it first. A page change, a half switch on the 64 or a pattern replaced while a step is held ends it: its release toggles nothing.
- Every grid freeze key waits for the next beat or bar while `quantize_actions` is on.
