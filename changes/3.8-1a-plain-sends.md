### Changed
- Sends are plain sends. Every voice plays dry at full level with a dry level of its own, the delay and reverb mixes are now their levels, and a new `delay to reverb` sets how much of the echoes the reverb takes. A default start sounds as before. A PSET saved before 3.8 keeps its delay and reverb values, now read as levels: 3.7's default reverb of 30% plays a little quieter (about 3 dB), and a delay mix adds its echoes over the dry sound instead of crossfading.
