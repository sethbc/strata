### Fixed
- A buffer voice's own note, played from MIDI, MPE or a sequencer step, now plays its exact pitch instead of a rate rounded to 0.01 (note 62 played about 4 cents flat, and low notes up to about 32 cents flat), so it is in tune with the extra notes of its chord. The buffer rate's range, display and feel under the encoders, arc and faders are unchanged.
