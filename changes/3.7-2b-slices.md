### Added
- Slices: a buffer's audio cut into up to 64 slices, in equal parts or at its transients (a sensitivity picks how many), kept with the file's settings.
- A buffer voice's `slices` setting (off, one, overlap): MIDI note 36 and up play its slices at the voice's own pitch, one at a time or each in a note of its own; in one shot a slice plays to its end.
- A step's `slice` on SEQ (a buffer voice's, 1-64): the step plays that slice; on an overlap voice each step's slice rings on past the next step.
