### Added
- Development only: the engine has an envelopes area with two new commands, `setFastEnv` and `stopFastEnv`: up to sixteen envelope nodes, one per matrix slot, each fired by a voice's hits (its start, a retrigger, an extra note) and writing a fast destination's offset with an attack, decay, sustain, hold, release, curve and loop. Nothing calls them yet (3.7's envelopes in Lua do), so nothing sounds different. A new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataEnvelopes.sc`: restart norns (SYSTEM > RESTART) after updating.
