### Added
- FX rows sit under dim section labels (master, filter, delay, reverb) that take no cursor stop, with a scroll marker at the right edge.
- K3 on FX bypasses or restores the effect of the row under the cursor (filter, delay or reverb), each with its own last mix.

### Changed
- FX shows filter mix, delay feedback and mix, and reverb mix and damping in percent.
- The FX hint row names the effect (`K3:delay bypass`) and reads `E3:value` on the master rows.
