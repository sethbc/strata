### Added
- Scan modes: a buffer voice's new `scan mode` row on VOICE (PARAMS > voice extras) picks what its scan does at the loop's end: `wrap` (round again, as before), `bounce` (back and forth) or `one shot` (once, then silent until the voice's next hit). Changing the mode restarts the scan; the grid's position page shows where it plays.
- Voices that follow a file's tempo: the `follow` row (`off`, `stretch`, `repitch`) makes a buffer voice reading a file with a tempo scan at the clock's tempo over the file's, keeping its pitch (stretch) or moving it with the speed (repitch, between a quarter and four times), and puts it back on the beat once a bar. Scenes store both rows and switch them at the end of a transition. With `buffer scan` 1, `scan mode` one shot and `follow` stretch a sliced loop stays on the beat at any tempo.

### Changed
- A buffer voice's scan is a speed, where 1 is the audio's own rate (it was loop lengths per second); VOICE shows it as `1.00x`.

### Fixed
- The playhead (on BUFFER and the grid) at `buffer position` 1 sits at the loop's last start, where the voice plays, not at its start (#726).
