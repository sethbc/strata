### Changed
- An LFO's destination is its own routing in the modulation matrix, beside R1-R16: the LFO page looks and works as before, and the destination is saved with the matrix's routings (with each PSET in `<pset>.matrix.data`, in scenes and between sessions) instead of as a parameter. A PSET saved by 3.7 loads with its LFOs aimed at nothing (every other LFO setting loads as saved), so aim them again and save the PSET; a MIDI mapping of an LFO's destination made in 3.7 no longer moves anything and should be removed in PARAMETERS > MAP.
- An LFO and the matrix routings on the same setting add up before the setting's range limits them, and a voice started under an LFO starts at the value the LFO has it at. When an LFO stops moving a setting, the setting returns to its value at the LFO's next update (1/48 s) rather than at once.
- A scene whose LFO has another destination dips that LFO's depth to 0 at the middle of a transition and switches the destination there (3.7 switched it at the transition's end), and the morph switches it at its crossing.
- An LFO on a buffer or input voice's main parameter (its buffer position or grain size) is added in the engine, as a matrix routing to it is.
