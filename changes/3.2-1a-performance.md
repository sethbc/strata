### Added
- A performance setting on the SETUP page: auto, standard or high, and on high a buffer length of 16, 60, 120 or 300 s. Auto picks high on a Pi 4 or Compute Module 4 norns, so longer buffers (60 s by default) arrive there with no setup; the engine steps the length down when memory is short and SETUP shows it. The setting is saved at once, applies at the next start, and is never part of a PSET or scene (`docs/user/technical.md`, Performance tiers).

### Changed
- The engine's `bufferState` poll now sends `slot * 1000 + seconds` (it was `slot * 100 + seconds`), so a slot can report up to 300 s.
- The engine writes `performance_active.data` beside the scenes file at every start, and the script reads it before adding its params.
