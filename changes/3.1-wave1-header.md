### Added
- The header shows eight page dots (the current page bright), a play triangle while the patterns play and `REC` while a buffer records, beside `[M]` and the scene.
- A changed value can show in the header for 1.5 s in place of the dots and indicators.

### Changed
- The header no longer shows master density (it is on the FX page's first row and MAIN's arc ring 1). `[M]` moves left to make room.
- The BUFFER page's messages (saved, busy, empty, load failed) show on the bottom row of whatever page is up.
