### Added
- **A `note cap` per voice** (VOICE's new notes section, 1-8, default 8; kept in PSETs, never in scenes): how many notes the voice will play at once, its own and up to seven more from the note pool all voices share. Nothing plays chords yet: MIDI and the sequencer will, once they play through the new note allocator, which picks each note's slot, steals the oldest note when the pool or the cap is full (never a locked voice's for another voice, never one younger than 50 ms), cuts a synth voice's extra notes when it freezes and sends at most 16 expression messages a step.

### Changed
- **A frozen synth voice's quantize measures from the pitch it had when it froze**: changing its freq while it is frozen no longer moves the scale its grains snap to; unfreezing goes back to the freq's note.
