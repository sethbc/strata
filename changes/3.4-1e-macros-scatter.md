### Added
- 16 macros in four banks (PARAMETERS > macros): each moves up to eight targets at once, each between two ends of its own along the target's scale; a target is any setting an LFO can move, or an LFO's rate or depth, or a matrix routing's depth. Macros map to controllers through PARAMETERS > MAP, and an LFO can move a macro's targets around their values (the destinations `macro 1` ... `macro 16`). The targets are saved with each PSET (beside it, `<pset>.macros.data`), in scenes and between sessions; a recall, a fade and the morph set a macro without moving its targets.
- Scatter (PARAMETERS > scatter): slow, smooth random movement of every unlocked voice's grain pitch and position (and, when switched on, size, filter cutoff and level), with a depth and a rate; scenes store its eight settings.
