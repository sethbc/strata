### Added
- MIDI out per voice: a voice's steps and played notes go to a MIDI port, plain on its channel or MPE in the port's lower zone (each played note's bend, pressure and slide with it). Set on the IO page or in PARAMETERS > out midi; off by default.
- Eight CC out slots, each sending a modulation source as a CC on a port and channel.
- Program change in and out for scenes: a program change 0 to 15 recalls scene 1 to 16, and a scene recall sends its program change. Both off by default.
