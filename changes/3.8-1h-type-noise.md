### Added
- A new voice type, **noise**: colored noise and crackling dust through a bank of four resonances at the voice's pitch, from a breathy wash to a ringing whistle. Choose it on a voice's VOICE `type` row; its `q` (the width of the resonances) is MAIN's E3, and `color`, `grit`, `dust` and `bank` shape the rest. It plays about as loud as the Pulse voice, and a limiter keeps its narrowest, most accented settings under full scale.
- Restart norns after updating: a new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataTypeNoise.sc`.
