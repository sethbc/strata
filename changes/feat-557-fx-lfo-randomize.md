### Added
- ALT+K2 on FX randomizes the section under the cursor (master, filter, delay or reverb) within musical ranges, keeping the filter mix and the bypasses; again within 5 seconds it undoes.
- ALT+K2 on LFO randomizes the selected LFO's shape and rate, keeping its destination, depth, offset and on/off.

### Changed
- ALT+K2 on LFO now resets the setting only when held for half a second (a quick press randomizes).
