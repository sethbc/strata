### Added
- **A step can move a voice and leave it there** ([Moving a voice](docs/user/sequencer.md#moving-a-voice)): each step can hold two values, A and B (1 to 15), and each voice two move targets, **seq move A** (`auto`, the default: position on a buffer voice, pitch on any other; or position, pitch, size, cutoff) and **seq move B** (`off`, the default, or one of the four), in PARAMETERS > the voice's extras. A value moves its target in fourteen equal moves (position across the voice's loop, size and cutoff across their ranges; pitch follows in a later step of this work) and stays until a later step sets that target again, the patterns stop, the setting is turned by hand, the pattern is replaced, a target, the step mode, the source or the type is changed, the voice wraps with no step set, or its length is cut past the step. A step lock on the same setting wins for its step, and the value comes back after it. Values work in every step mode, and a step's level stays its strength in all of them; in move, a step turned on is given a random value A. The row a value holds shows `[S]` on VOICE and MAIN while it stays. Scenes keep both targets; voice presets leave them alone.
