### Added
- Tunings: Strata plays one tuning, which every MIDI key, quantized pitch, step degree and chord follows. Built in: 12 equal (the default, exactly 3.7's pitches), the five just tunings of norns' intonation library, pythagorean, quarter-comma meantone, 19, 24 and 31 equal, and Partch's 43; and your own Scala (`.scl`) files in `data/strata/tunings/`. A PSET saves the tuning; one saved before 3.8 loads 12 equal.
- A custom scale: the last scale option, any set of the twelve notes, held as the new `scale mask` param (a scene global beside the scale and root).
- The scale and tuning editor: SETUP's new `scale & tuning` row opens it, to set the root, the scale, each of its twelve notes (a MIDI note toggles its note while it is open) and the tuning.
- MIDI out in MPE mode sends a tuned note's distance from its nearest key in its bend.

### Changed
- Quantized grains land on the scale tone nearest their own pitch, as quantized MIDI notes and turns already did: a pitch between two notes (a jittered grain, a fractional pitch shift) may land a tone away from where 3.7 put it, and one exactly between two tones goes down. The chord interval set of a scale whose notes musicutil lists out of order (In Sen Pou) now takes its notes in order.
