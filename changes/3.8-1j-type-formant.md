### Added
- A new voice type, formant (`vox` in MAIN's list): a singing, vowel-like voice whose two formants move between an "ee" and an "ah" (`vowel`), with their width (`bw`) and a scale on both (`formant`, its main parameter, also each note's slide). Pick it on any voice's VOICE `type` row. A new engine file: restart norns (SYSTEM > RESTART) after updating, since maiden's `;restart` does not load it.
