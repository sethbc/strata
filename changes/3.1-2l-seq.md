### Added
- SEQ page: all seven patterns at once, each step drawn at its level (empty steps as outlines), with a step cursor. E2 moves the step, E3 sets its level (0-15), K3 turns it on or off, K2 plays and stops the patterns. ALT+E2 moves to another voice's row, ALT+E3 changes the tempo, ALT+K2 clears and ALT+K3 randomizes the voice's pattern. While the patterns play, the playing column is brighter; the header shows the tempo. The arc's button plays and stops the patterns on this page.
