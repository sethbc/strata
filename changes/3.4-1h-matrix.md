### Added
- A modulation matrix of 16 routings from any modulation source to any LFO destination (PARAMETERS > matrix: each routing's depth, -100 to 100 %, and on/off): a routing moves its destination by its source's value times its depth, around the value you set, and adds up with the destination's LFOs before the range limits it. A source is an LFO, a macro, a gesture recorder or the input level; a destination is any setting an LFO can move, never a macro. Macros can move a routing's depth. The routings are saved with each PSET (beside it, `<pset>.matrix.data`), in scenes and between sessions; a scene fades the depths, and a routing that changes its source or destination fades to 0 and switches halfway.
