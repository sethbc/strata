### Added
- An XY morph between four scenes, with a pad on the 16×16 grid: `morph y` (PARAMETERS > morph) is a second axis and `morph C` and `morph D` two more scenes, so the morph blends scenes A, B, C and D at the corners of a square, each by how near the position is to its corner, and the settings that cannot blend follow the nearest corner. With `morph y` at 0 % it is the morph between A and B as before. On MOD the morph section gains `C`, `D` and `y`; on the 16×16 grid, page 3's rows 11-15, columns 12-16 are a 5×5 pad that sets both axes with one press.
