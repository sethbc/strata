### Added
- MAIN's voice list shows how many notes a voice plays at once, from two (`1+ reso 3`).
- SETUP's `note off` row (the MIDI note off setting), `mpe & zones` row (K3 opens an editor for the MPE zones' member channels, the bend range and each voice's split zone and range, with a learn: ALT+K3 on a voice, then play its lowest and highest notes) and `note pool` row (how many extra notes the voices share, 0-16, kept in `notes.data`; `may xrun` on the standard tier above 4).
