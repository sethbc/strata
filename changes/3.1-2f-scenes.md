### Added
- The SCENES page: eight scene slots in two columns (filled box when populated, current scene bright, a mark at the sequencer position) and the transition time, sequencer on/off, sequence length and scene duration as fields. K2 recalls with the transition, K3 at once, ALT+K3 saves; saving over a populated slot asks for a second ALT+K3 within 2 seconds, and moving the cursor cancels the question. The arc button recalls the cursor slot.
