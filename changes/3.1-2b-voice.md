### Added
- The VOICE page: every parameter of the selected voice in four sections (synth, grain, crossmod, mix), with each value's own text and unit and crossmod sources by voice name (`3 fold`). E2 moves through the rows and on into the next section, E3 edits (ALT+E3 finer), K2/K3 jump to the previous/next section, ALT+K2 resets the row to its default and ALT+K3 locks the voice. The section and its position show at the top right (`grain 2/4`).
- With an arc connected, VOICE marks the rows the four rings edit; the arc's button starts and stops the selected voice there.
- When a voice's source or the selected voice changes, the VOICE cursor stays on its parameter if the new rows still have it.
