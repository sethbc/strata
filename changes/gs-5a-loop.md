### Added
- **Loop part of a line from the grid** ([Performing](docs/user/sequencer.md#performing)): on the patterns page's side 1, hold a step and press another key of its line, and the voice plays between the two keys, in its own direction, for as long as you hold the first one. Under the loop the voice's place in its pattern moves on, so when you let go it plays the step it would have reached, in step with the other voices; conditions count the voice's own passes. While a voice loops, its set steps outside the loop dim. A page change or unplugging the grid ends the loop at the next step; a variation switch, a chain link, a recall or a paste leaves it. A loop is never saved.

### Changed
- **Setting a step's level on the grid moved to side 2**: on the patterns page's side 1 a second key of a held step's line now makes a loop instead of setting the held step's level; set levels with side 2's hits line (line 1), the step view or the screen's step editor.
