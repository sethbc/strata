### Added
- SEQ's edit view (ALT+K2 held on SEQ): a voice's length, division, swing, gate, gate length and release, a Euclidean fill and a clear row; the step's level, probability, ratchets, gate, note, position, pitch and size step locks and bookmark; and the chain and quantize settings, each edited with E2 and E3.
- The step editor: the same edit view for any voice and step, opened by the grids over any page, with arc rings for the step's locks, probability, ratchets, note, bookmark and level.
- SEQ's pattern rows draw the steps past a voice's length as dots, mark a gated voice with `G` and light each voice's own playing step.

### Changed
- On SEQ, ALT+K2 held past half a second opens the edit view (it canceled before); a quick press still randomizes the pattern.
