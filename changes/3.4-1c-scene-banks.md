### Added
- 16 scenes in two banks, with names. The SCENES page shows the bank of the slot under the cursor (ALT+E2 jumps a bank), each slot with its name; ALT+K2 names a slot through norns' text entry (up to 12 characters, on an empty slot too, kept when a scene is saved into it). The header shows `S1`-`S16`, the scene sequence length goes up to 16, arc ring 1 on SCENES runs over the 16 slots and four fields, and the grids' scene pages hold scenes 9-16 and lengths 9-16 (16×16: columns 9-16 of rows 1-3, row 6 columns 3-10; 16×8: columns 9-16 of rows 1-4; 8×16: rows 9-16 of columns 1-3, column 5 rows 9-16).

### Changed
- The scenes file is format 6. Scenes saved by an older Strata load into slots 1-8 with 9-16 empty, and their default names (`scene 1` and so on) are dropped; the first save keeps the older file as `scenes.data.v5` (or the format it was).
- The header's page dots start 4 px further left (x 36), ready for a ninth page.
