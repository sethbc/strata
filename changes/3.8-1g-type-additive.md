### Added
- An additive voice type (`add` in MAIN's list): a bank of 16 sine partials over the pitch, with a tilt from bright to almost a sine (MAIN's E3), a stretch that bends the series out of tune, a balance of the odd and even partials, and one slow wander of the brightness. Partials that would pass 21.6 kHz are silent. Pick it on a voice's VOICE `type` row.
- Restart norns after updating (SYSTEM > RESTART): a new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataTypeAdditive.sc`.
