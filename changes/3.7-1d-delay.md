### Added
- The delay on the beat: with FX's new `sync` row on (`delay sync` in PARAMS > fx), the delay's time follows the tempo in a division of the beat, from 1/32 to one bar (`delay division`, 1/8D at first), picked with E3 on the `time` row, arc ring 3 or the 256's delay fader. A division that does not fit in the delay's 2.7 seconds plays at half its length (or a quarter, ...), and the `time` row shows it (`1 bar>1/2`). The delay time in seconds is kept and comes back when sync is off. Scenes store sync and the division, which switch at the end of a transition.
- Ping-pong (FX's `ping` row, `delay ping-pong`): turned up, the delay's repeats alternate between left and right. Scenes store it and crossfade it. Off by default, so the delay sounds as before.

### Changed
- The delay's time reaches 2.7 seconds (it stopped at 2), at no more memory. An LFO or a matrix routing on the delay time now moves further than before, since its range is wider.
