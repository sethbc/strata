### Added
- A saw voice type: three slowly beating saws on each side through the Pulse's resonant lowpass, for a wide pad. Any voice can choose it on its VOICE page's `type` row; its width (MAIN's E3), cutoff and res are the Pulse's own, so a Pulse voice switched to saw keeps its filter, and `detune` sets how far the saws spread.
- Restart norns after updating (SYSTEM > RESTART): a new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataTypeSaw.sc`, which SuperCollider compiles only at a restart.
