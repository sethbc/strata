### Added
- Six LFOs (three before), with three new shapes, smooth random, sample-and-hold and drunk, a start phase, and LFO-to-LFO modulation: each LFO's rate and depth can follow a lower-numbered LFO.
- The LFO page shows three LFOs at a time around the selected one, marks an LFO that runs in the engine with `~`, a rate capped at 10 Hz with `max` and a routed LFO with `R`; the arc's depth rings follow the three shown.

### Changed
- LFOs on a voice's grain pitch, position, size, voice filter cutoff and level run in the engine, smoothly and at up to 50 Hz; the others run in the script at up to 10 Hz, updating 48 times a second whatever the tempo.
- The LFOs are Strata's own instead of norns' LFO library: their parameters have new names, and PSETs and scenes saved with 3.3 load their LFO settings (a random LFO becomes sample-and-hold at twice the rate); the library's min, max, baseline and reset settings are gone.
- The free rate is set in Hz (0.003-50 Hz) instead of as a period; ALT+K2 on the LFO page randomizes the shape, rate or clocked rate and phase.
