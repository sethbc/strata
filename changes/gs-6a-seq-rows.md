### Added
- **SEQ's edit view reaches what the grid does** ([SEQ](docs/user/pages/seq.md#the-edit-view)): new voice rows after `mode`: `floor` (what a closed gate lets through, `0.25`), `move A` and `move B` (the two move targets; `cutoff` turns on a voice filter that is off, as on the grid), a length for each lane (`len A`, `len B`, `len chance`, `len repeats`, `len gate`: 1 to 16, or `-` while the lane follows the step played), and `letter` (the voice's pattern A to D; while the patterns play a turn waits for the next bar and the row shows `A>B`, and an order that is on shows after the letter, `B AABA`). The step section gains `val A` and `val B` after `level` (1 to 15, `-` for none). `mode` now turns as the grid's mode keys do: turning to `move` gives the set steps without a value A a random one.
