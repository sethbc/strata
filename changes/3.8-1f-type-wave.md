### Added
- A new voice type, **wave**: a wavetable voice that glides through eight tables, from a pure sine to a bright buzz. `morph` (its main parameter) picks the place between them, `morphRate` and `morphDepth` let it wander on its own, and `detune` spreads three slightly detuned copies into a slow chorus. Choose it on a voice's VOICE `type` row; each note of a held chord plays its own wave and follows the voice's morph.
- **Restart norns after updating** (SYSTEM > RESTART): a new engine file, `lib/engine/StrataTypeWave.sc`, and two changed ones, which SuperCollider compiles only at a restart.
