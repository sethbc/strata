### Added
- Input history: an input-sourced voice can play from up to 16 s back in the live input (60 s on the high performance setting) while the input keeps recording. It is the history row in the voice's input section on VOICE, a scene field that fades, and an LFO destination (`v<i> hist`).
- Input-sourced voices follow pitchbend and frequency cross-modulation, as buffer voices do.

### Changed
- Synth and input voices transpose by grain rate instead of a pitch shifter: an exact transposition with no smearing. Pitch now steps grain by grain instead of gliding, and a buffer voice's pitch also sets how fast its grains read the sample.
- Synth and input voices' grains start a few milliseconds in the past (further back at high pitch; in triggered mode grains above pitch 2 are shortened instead), and position spread is now a random backward offset of up to half a second instead of a smeared copy.
- The engine has new files under `lib/engine/` (`StrataVariation.sc`, `StrataLoop.sc`, `StrataFreeze.sc`, `StrataMix.sc`): restart norns after updating.
