### Added
- The arc can draw a ring from 12 o'clock, clockwise for positive values and counter-clockwise for negative ones, for params whose range runs through zero (pages adopt it in later 3.1 changes). At exactly zero one dim LED stays lit.
- Pages can give the arc a second set of rings while K1 (ALT) is held, and their own action for the arc button (MAIN's stays start/freeze).

### Changed
- Arc turns too small for a whole step (LFO depth and clocked rate) are forgotten when the page changes, so they never step a param on another page.
