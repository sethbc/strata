### Added
- **The grid's perform side** ([Grid Page 6: Voices](docs/user/grid/pages/voices.md#side-2-the-perform-side)): the voices page's lit key pressed again shows a second side, a line a voice, on every grid size: mute and solo, the four patterns A to D (a tap waits for the next bar and blinks while it waits; tap it again to switch at once), OR (hold it and tap letters to set the voice's order, which starts at the next bar; tap it alone to turn the order off or on), LP (the loop's latch: arm it, latch a held loop, or end a latched one), R1 to R4 (a roll while held, at 1 to 4 hits a step), the step mode keys pulse, move and accent (every voice's mode seen at once), and FL (fill while held, on any line). The 8×8 shows the mutes, letters, OR and LP on its left half and the rolls, the mode keys and FL on its right, so it now has a fill key.
