### Added
- Reverse grains: each voice's reverse param is the chance that a grain plays backward.
- Pitch, pan and size jitter per voice: each grain's pitch (up to 12 semitones either way), stereo place and length (up to half or double, never past 0.5 s) vary at random.
- Interval sets per voice (octaves, fifths, the scale's chord, or five custom intervals of -24 to +24 semitones), each grain adding one at random, with a probability that a grain takes one at all.
- Scale quantize: a global scale (off or any norns scale) and root, and per voice quantize, which lands every grain's pitch on the scale, steps a synth voice's freq through it on E3 and the arc, and snaps the voice's MIDI notes. A buffer voice quantizes around the rate it plays (a MIDI note transposes it without leaving the scale). All of it is in the VOICE page's new variation section and in scenes; randomize and mutations leave it alone.
- The engine has a new file, `lib/engine/StrataScale.sc`: restart norns after updating.
