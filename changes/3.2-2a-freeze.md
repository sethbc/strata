### Added
- Freeze per voice: a voice's grains keep playing what they were playing (a synth voice its last 4 s, an input voice the 4 s up to its history, a buffer voice its position: the scan stops), while pitch, spread and the grain settings still move. It is the freeze row in VOICE's mix section, a key on the 256 (page 4 column 12, or row 16 columns 1-7 while column 8 is held), 128h (page 4 column 12) and 128v (sources page row 14), and can be mapped to a MIDI CC. MAIN shows `[F]` after `[L]` and `[T]`. A source change unfreezes; scenes save freeze and its mode; randomize and mutations never change them.
- Spectral freeze on the high performance setting: the freeze mode row offers `spectral`, which holds one sound spectrum and keeps granulating it. On standard, freeze always holds.

### Changed
- MAIN's status row draws `[L]` and `[T]` a few pixels further left, to make room for `[F]`.
