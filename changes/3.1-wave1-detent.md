### Added
- Fine adjustment: ALT+E3 moves a continuous value a tenth as far per detent (the MAIN parameter and the FX rows). Values in whole steps (sources, envelopes, MIDI settings) still step whole values. ALT+E2 and the arc are never fine.
- Center detents: a value whose range runs through 0 (pan, buffer scan, crossmod amounts) stops at 0 when a turn reaches or crosses it, even on a fast turn, and stays there for the next 3 encoder detents or 24 arc ticks.
