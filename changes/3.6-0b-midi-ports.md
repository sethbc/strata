### Changed
- One module now owns the MIDI ports, their listeners and hot-plug. MIDI notes play as before.
- MIDI hot-plug handlers a mod installed before Strata now keep running while Strata runs, and are put back when it stops (before, Strata replaced them).
- A MIDI device moved to another port in SYSTEM > DEVICES now prints the same `midi connected` and `midi disconnected` lines as a plug and an unplug, once the menu closes.
