### Added
- A MOD page, the sixth of nine (after LFO): the macros (the shown bank, each with its first target and value; K3 shows a macro's eight targets and their two ends, E3 sets them, ALT+K3 learns the next setting you touch), the morph (scenes A and B and the position), scatter (on, depth, rate and the settings it moves), the four gesture recorders (K3 records, plays and stops; ALT+K3 overdubs; ALT+K2 held clears), the six LFOs at a glance (K3 shows one on the LFO page) and the modulation matrix's 16 routings (`R3 L2>v3 pitch~  +40%`; K3 shows a routing's source, destination, depth and on/off, ALT+K3 on its destination learns). K2 jumps to the next section; ALT+E2 on a macro shows the next bank; a quick ALT+K2 randomizes scatter's depth and rate or every complete routing's depth, with its undo. The header has nine page dots.
- MOD's arc rings: the shown bank's four macros, or the depths of four routings around the cursor in the matrix; with K1 held the morph, scatter's depth and rate and morph scene B; in a macro's targets the row's two ends, the macro and the row; in a routing its depth, source, destination and the routing shown. The arc's button on MOD turns a routing on or off, shows the next bank, presses a recorder or turns scatter on or off.
- SCENES' arc rings 3 and 4: the morph position and morph scene B (20 ticks a scene).
- MAIN shows `~` after the selected voice's name while the engine moves it: an LFO running in the engine on one of its settings, scatter or a routing of the matrix.
