### Fixed

- External routing choices recall the first active PSET entry in files with LF or CRLF line endings.
- Older presets that omit a voice's quantize switch restore its saved pitch.
- A silent PSET recall starts or stops mutations at the saved interval.
- A silent PSET recall sets the internal clock to the saved tempo before restoring clocked controls.
- An empty gesture recorder uses its recalled quantization for the next recording.
