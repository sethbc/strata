### Added
- **The engine plays up to eight notes on a voice.** Each voice's own note and up to seven extra notes from a pool all voices share: a synth voice's extra note is a voice synth of its own, a buffer or input voice's a grain stream of its own, each at its own pitch with its own bend, gain and expression, and every voice-wide setting reaches them all. The own note can rest silent (parked) while other notes hold, a voice's notes follow its stop, a source switch, a buffer load and a freeze, and the pool never holds more notes than it is set to.
