### Added
- Arc rings on the VOICE page: the four rings edit the cursor's group of four rows; choices and numbers step one value per 20 ticks, and rings past the last row are dark.
- Arc rings on the SEQ page: the step cursor, the step's level (the grid follows), the tempo and the voice row.
- Arc rings on the SCENES page: the scene cursor (moving it cancels a pending overwrite question) and the transition time.
- MAIN's ALT rings: with K1 held, the rings set the selected voice's grain pitch, grain spread, level and pan (dark for Sub).

### Changed
- MAIN's arc ring 3 is grain pitch for an input-sourced voice, whose ring 2 already is grain size.
