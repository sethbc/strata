### Added
- Each voice's pattern has its own length (1-16 steps), division (1/32 to a bar per step) and swing (50-75 %), so voices can run polyrhythms from one start; a division reset puts every voice back at step 1 at the next bar.
- Steps can hold a probability, ratchets (up to four hits, never closer than 20 ms), step locks of the buffer position, grain pitch and grain size, a note in scale degrees (a chord is stored; voices play its first note), a bookmark to jump to and a gate length; scenes keep them with the patterns.
- Gated voices: with a voice's seq gate on, it is heard only while a step's gate is open, with its own gate length and release.
- Euclidean fill: spreads a number of hits evenly over a voice's length, with a rotation.
- Pattern chaining: every few bars, the next saved scene of a range gives its patterns, and nothing else.
- A setting for following the clock's transport (SETUP's transport row, on by default): with MIDI clock or Link, a start plays every voice from step 1 and a stop stops the patterns.
- When the clock restarts or jumps while the patterns play and they do not follow it (the internal clock's reset, a new clock source), every voice keeps its place in one count; and a moment when the norns is busy never makes the patterns rush through the steps they missed.
