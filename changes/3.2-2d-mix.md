### Added
- Each voice has its own filter (lowpass, highpass or bandpass, with cutoff, resonance and mix) after its grains, and its own delay and reverb sends, in a new "filter & sends" section on VOICE. With both sends at 100% (the default) the effects sound exactly as before; a voice with its sends at 0 plays dry at full level, whatever the global delay and reverb mixes. The filter cutoff and both sends are LFO destinations (`v<i> vfilt`, `v<i> dsend`, `v<i> rsend`) and scene fields.
- Delay and reverb bypass (`delay bypass`, `reverb bypass` in PARAMS > fx, stored in PSETs, not in scenes): a bypassed effect stops running, which gives back its CPU and memory, and comes back empty when the bypass is lifted. A mix of 0 still only silences the effect.
- A richer reverb on the high performance setting: a feedback delay network with a decay close to the standard reverb's.

### Changed
- On the FX page, K3 on a delay or reverb row toggles that effect's bypass (its section row shows `byp`) and keeps its mix; on the filter rows K3 still turns the filter's mix off and back on.

### Fixed
- The FX page's layout checks select their rows by param id, so a row added to the page can no longer move a check onto the wrong row (#114).
