### Added
- Overdub with feedback and loop recording, both playable while they record: grains steer around the record head.
- A record source per recording: input, pre-master, post-master or one voice.
- Arm a recording by the source's level, with a 10 ms pre-roll so the take keeps its attack.
- Recordings that start on the clock (synced takes keep the slot playing until just before they start).

### Changed
- Recordings fade in and out over 5 ms, so a take no longer starts or stops with a click.
