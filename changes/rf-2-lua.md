### Added
- Reverb freeze, on the high performance setting: `reverb freeze` in PARAMETERS > fx, the reverb's `freeze` row and ALT+K3 on any reverb row on the FX page, and RF on the grid's FX page hold the reverb's tail as a wash you can play over. Frozen, the tail keeps its level and its top within 1 dB over a minute and takes in no new sound. FX's dot in the header stands tall, the reverb's section row shows `frz` and its `size` row `held` (a new size waits for the release; `damp` still shades the wash). A bypass ends it, a bypassed reverb cannot be frozen, and it is never saved in a PSET or a scene; it can be mapped to MIDI. On the standard setting the freeze row shows `--`, RF is dark and a set goes back to off (#819).
