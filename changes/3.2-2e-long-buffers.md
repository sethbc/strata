### Added
- Buffers up to 300 s on the high tier: recording, loading, clearing and saving work at the tier's buffer length, the record duration goes up to it, and the BUFFER page shows lengths of a minute and more as minutes and seconds (`1:05`).

### Changed
- Long files are checked without stalling the engine: a file longer than 16 s is checked in the background before it loads (load, record or clear the buffer meanwhile and the later action wins), and saving a long buffer no longer holds up other commands while it writes.
