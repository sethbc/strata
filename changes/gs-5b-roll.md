### Added
- **A held roll** ([Performing](docs/user/sequencer.md#performing)): while a roll is held, the voice plays its last played hit again 1, 2, 3 or 4 times a step, never closer than 20 ms, with its notes and the values it holds; a rolled hit swells at the hit's level in every mode, `move` included, and outside targets hear each one. The voice's own steps are silent underneath, but its place in the pattern moves on, so letting go lands it where it would have been. A page change or unplugging the grid ends a roll at its next hit, a stop of the patterns ends it at once, and a variation switch or a chain link leaves it. The grid's roll keys come with the perform side.
