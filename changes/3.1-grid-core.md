### Added
- Grid: hold a step key and press another key of the same voice to set the held step's level (its share of 15 along the voice's steps), on every grid size.

- Grid scene saves (row 3 of the scene page on the 16×16 and 16×8) need a half-second hold; the key lights while held, and a shorter press saves nothing. Unplugging a grid mid-hold saves nothing.
