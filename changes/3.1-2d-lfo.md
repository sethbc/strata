### Added
- LFO page: a line under the selected LFO's row shows all its settings (destination, shape, mode, rate, depth, offset, on/off). E2 moves along it and E3 changes the setting under the cursor; the line scrolls, with `<` and `>` when settings lie off either end, and the hint row names the setting.
- LFO page: ALT+E3 on the destination jumps a group (none, the global destinations, then voice 1 to voice 7), ALT+K2 resets the setting under the cursor, and the arc's button turns the selected LFO on or off.

### Changed
- LFOs start at 25 % depth, so turning one on is audible straight away. Scenes keep the depth they stored, and scene files without LFO settings still recall silent LFOs.
- LFO page keys: K2 and K3 select the previous and next LFO (E2 selected it before), ALT+K3 turns it on or off (K3 before), and E3 edits the setting under the cursor instead of always the depth. The destination steps both ways and stops at the ends instead of wrapping.

### Fixed
- An LFO keeps its offset when it is turned on or off, from the LFO page, PARAMETERS or a scene recall; norns' LFO library used to set it back to 0 on every on/off. Changing the baseline still resets it. Fixes part of #103.
