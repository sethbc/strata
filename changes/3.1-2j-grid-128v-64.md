### Added
- 8×16 grid: four pages (patterns, faders, scenes, sources), stepped by row 8 of column 8, whose brightness shows the page.
- 8×16 grid faders page: four upright faders for the selected voice (row 16 minimum, row 1 maximum), a fader page selector in column 5, and voice select in column 8.
- 8×16 grid scenes page: instant recall, recall with a transition, save on a half-second hold, the scene sequencer on/off and its length.
- 8×16 grid sources page: each voice's source (synth, buffers 1-8, input), and record and clear keys for buffers 1-8.
- 8×8 grid: hold row 8 column 1 for half a second to switch between steps 1-8 and 9-16; the shown half's playhead lights, and column 1 blinks while the other half plays.

### Changed
- 8×8 grid: row 8 column 1 plays or stops when you let go of it; columns 2-8 toggle voices 1-7, so voice 7 is reachable.
