### Added
- **seq gate floor** (PARAMETERS > the voice's extras, 0-100%, default 0%): how much of a gated voice a closed gate lets through, so a gated voice can play quietly between its steps and at its whole level while a step's gate is open. At 0% a closed gate is silent, as before. Scenes fade it and PSETs store it; a voice preset leaves it as it is.
