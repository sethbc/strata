### Added
- **Each lane of a pattern can loop at its own length** ([Lanes](docs/user/sequencer.md#lanes)): beside a voice's hits, its value A, value B, chance, repeats and gate are lanes, whose cells are the steps' own settings. A lane given a length of 1 to 16 keeps its own place, which starts at cell 1 when the voice starts, moves on by one at every step the voice takes (played, silent or skipped) and wraps at its length, always forward; with no length a lane is read at the step played, as before. So a 5-step value lane over 16 hits shifts against the pattern every bar. Conditions still count the hits' passes. Scenes keep the lengths (a scene saved before them follows everywhere), the chain carries them, and the undo of a chain start puts them back. Setting the lengths from the grid and SEQ comes in a later step of this work.
