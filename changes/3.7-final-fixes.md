### Added
- On an overlap voice a pattern's slice takes the voice's own note first, as on one; while that slice rings on to its end the next step's slice plays in a note of its own, without restarting it, and one-shot slices tapped one after another from MIDI ring over each other in every `midi note off` mode.
- A recording synced to the clock keeps a normalized buffer at its level while it waits for its start.
