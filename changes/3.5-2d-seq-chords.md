### Added
- A sequencer step's notes sound together as a chord: its first note is the voice's own, the rest extra notes from the note pool. A chord ends wherever a step's note ends (the voice's next played step, a stop, a replaced pattern, a shorter length). A chord takes only free room: past the voice's note cap or the pool it keeps its first notes at every step, and a step never ends, replaces or re-pitches a note MIDI holds (a MIDI note may take a sequenced one's place).
