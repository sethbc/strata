### Added
- An envelope per voice as a modulation source (EN1 to EN7 on MOD): attack, decay, sustain, release, curve and loop, fired by the voice's steps, notes and start, held for a gated step's length; routed through the matrix to a voice's pitch, position, size, cutoff or level it runs in the engine, exactly and at once, and to any other setting Strata follows it (marked `~` on MOD when it is too fast to follow exactly).
