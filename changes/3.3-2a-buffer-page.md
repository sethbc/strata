### Added
- BUFFER page: the record settings as fields (source, duration or beats, level, feedback, loop, threshold, sync), four at a time, with the selected voice's bookmark, a long file's start and the scrub friction.
- BUFFER page: the buffer under the cursor's waveform, with each voice's playhead and grain spread, the record head of an overdub or loop recording, and the selected voice's bookmarks.
- BUFFER page: slot rows show `ARM`, `REC`, `DUB` or `LOOP` while recording, and the header shows `ARM` while a recording waits to start.
- BUFFER page: K2 held loads a random file, ALT+K2 held fills every empty buffer, ALT+K3 held saves the loop region (trim).

### Changed
- BUFFER page: ALT+K2 (clear) and ALT+K3 (save) act on release instead of press; three buffer rows show at a time, under the waveform.
