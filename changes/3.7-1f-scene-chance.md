### Added
- A chance for the scene sequencer's next scene: `next`, `prev` and `rand` (0-100% each, defaults 100%, 0%, 0%, which play in order) pick the scene after each recall, and what they leave holds the scene for another duration. The pick is made once, so the grids' next-scene light shows the scene that will play. PSETs store the chances; scenes do not.

### Changed
- SCENES' fields are rows of two that scroll with the cursor, with a scroll bar at the right edge: seven fields, the four of before and the three chances.
- The scene sequencer keeps the next scene it picked while that scene can still play, even at the default chances: lengthening the sequence during a scene no longer plays the newly added slot next (on scene 4 of 4, lengthening to 6 plays scene 1 next, where 3.6 played scene 5), and a scene saved into a skipped empty slot during a scene waits until the next round (with scenes 1 and 3, saving 2 while 1 plays still plays 3 next). The grid's next-scene light shows the scene that will play.
