### Added
- **A step's value can move a voice's pitch** ([Moving a voice](docs/user/sequencer.md#moving-a-voice)): with a move target on `pitch` (the default on a synth or input voice), a value of 1 to 15 moves the voice from seven degrees below its own pitch to seven above (8 is its own), on the tuning and scale a step's note uses: a synth voice's frequency, a buffer voice's rate, an input voice's grain pitch. A step's note and chord count their degrees from the pitch value the voice stands on, kept within fourteen degrees either way. A step's pitch lock adds to it on a synth or buffer voice and wins for its step on an input voice. While a held MIDI key plays the voice's own note, a pitch value gives way, until the next step that sets one.
