### Added
- crow: choosing a gate source for an output sets the jack at once, 0 V unless that voice sounds, so a gate moved off a sounding voice never stays high.
- A step's note on an external instrument ends just before the next step (30 ms early, or half a very short note), so crow's gates and the ER-301's and TELEXo's triggers retrigger on consecutive steps.
- ii: when Strata stops leading the bus it sends at most fifteen lines at once, inside crow's own sixteen; a gate it could not lower then, or that was high when crow's cable came out, is lowered the next time Strata leads the bus.
- The ER-301 and the TELEXo play only while Strata leads the ii bus, as Just Friends and W/ do; a turn of `txo attack` or `txo decay` sends only that setting.
- MIDI out: a synth moved to another port in SYSTEM > DEVICES while its notes sound gets all notes off on its new port, and the device that takes its old port gets nothing; nothing is sent to a port a remote grid, a remote arc or `midi grid` holds, and the port rows show a port's owner beside its number.
- A MIDI grid through midigrid takes its device's port while attached, so its pads play no voice through the MIDI input; `rescan` no longer reconnects a midigrid that is attached.
- Crow's retrigger action, OSC's and Teletype's open a gated voice's own gate length, as a played note does.
- A 14-bit fader box moves its macro across each step of its high byte without a jump; IO's `osc` title shows a new sender at once; remote's arc line reads `4 rings (waiting)` while a monome arc is followed instead; W/ tape's loop rows read `K3:start` and `K3:end`.
- Loading a PSET while the tape loop runs keeps norns' input and tape out of the loop.
