### Changed
- **Each voice has a step mode** (SEQ's `mode` row, or PARAMETERS > the voice's extras > **seq gate**), which says what its steps do to it: **pulse**, the new default (while the patterns play and the voice has a step inside its length, it is heard only while a step's gate is open; a voice with no step sounds as before, and stopping the patterns lets every pulse voice sound again), **gate** (3.8's seq gate on: gated with the patterns stopped too), **accent** (3.8's seq gate off: a step only swells a voice that keeps sounding) and **move** (a step adds no swell and plucks no string; MIDI out and the other targets get it at full velocity). Scenes and PSETs saved before keep their accent or gate and play as they did; the chain never carries the mode. SEQ's pattern view marks a voice `P`, `G` or `M` where the `G` stood (accent has no mark), and a pulse voice's notes on MIDI out and the other targets last its gate length, as a gated voice's do.
