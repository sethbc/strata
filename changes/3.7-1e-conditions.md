### Added
- Trig conditions: a step can play on the A-th of every B passes (B up to 8), on the first pass only, after the voice's last conditioned step played (`pre`) or the voice above's did (`nei`), or while fill is on, each with its opposite (`!`). SEQ's edit view has a `cond` row in its step section.
- Fill: held on the 256's row 8 column 9 and the 128h's row 8 column 4, latched on SEQ's new `fill` row (K3), through the `fill` param, crow's `fill` trigger action or OSC's `/strata/fill`. SEQ shows `fill` beside the selected voice's row while it is on. It is never saved on.
- Pattern directions: each voice plays forward, reverse, ping-pong or random (`seq direction`), with a chance of a step up or down instead (`seq misstep`). Both are scene fields, and a chain link carries them.
- A chance for the chain's next scene: `chain next`, `chain previous` and `chain random` (100, 0 and 0 by default: in order, as before); what they leave over keeps the scene playing. They are rows in SEQ's chain section.

### Changed
- The 128h's row 8 column 4 is now the fill key; columns 1-3 still play and stop the patterns.
