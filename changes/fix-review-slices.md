### Fixed

- SLICE keeps the selected marker when a nudge reaches its limit. It shows just the playable cuts after a shorter load and retains the others for a longer reload.
- New cuts leave at least 10 ms at the end of the audio. Selecting Equal before the waveform arrives cuts the file as soon as its length is known.
- Changing slice mode clears any partial turn of arc ring 3 before the count or sensitivity changes.
