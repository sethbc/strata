### Added
- Development only, until the script uses them in a later 3.7 change: the engine's buffer grains can bounce or play once through (scan modes), play a region of their own per note (`noteRegion`), follow a tempo ratio on their read rate, keep a gain per slot (`setSlotGain`, set back to 1 whenever a slot is loaded, cleared or recorded into), take a scan phase from the script (`syncScan`) and a sample's root note. Nothing sounds different until the script sends them. No engine file is renamed; as with every engine change, restart norns (SYSTEM > RESTART) after updating.

### Changed
- A buffer voice's scan is a speed: at 1 it moves through the audio at its own rate, whatever the loop's length (it was loop lengths a second). A non-zero scan saved before 3.7 plays at another speed.
