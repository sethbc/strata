### Added
- Four gesture recorders: each records your hand edits of continuous settings (encoders, arc rings, grid faders) and loops them, free or rounded to whole beats or bars and started on the beat or bar, with overdub, up to 1024 moves or 10 minutes. The loops are saved with each PSET and between sessions, and scenes store whether each one plays (PARAMETERS > gestures).
- Beat- and bar-quantized scene recalls, freezes, bookmark recalls, position jumps and pattern start: with PARAMETERS > sequencer > quantize actions on beat or bar (off by default) they wait for the next beat or bar of the clock, and the header says so. A step's bookmark lock never waits.
