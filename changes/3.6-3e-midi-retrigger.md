### Fixed
- MIDI out sends a note off before it starts a note again that already sounds, so every port and channel has had as many note offs as note ons once nothing sounds (#709).
- On an MPE port whose member channels are all taken, a step note sharing a channel with a played note no longer resets that note's bend, and when a played note on a shared channel ends, the played notes still on it send their own bend, pressure and slide again.
