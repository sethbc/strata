### Added
- A morph between two scenes: the `morph` param (PARAMETERS > morph) blends every value that differs between the scenes `morph A` and `morph B` name, and switches voices, sources and the other settings that cannot blend as it passes the middle (at 52 % on the way up, 48 % on the way down). It writes params, so the blend can be saved as a new scene; a PSET restores the morph's position without applying it.
