### Added
- Slice keys on the grids' position rows: on every layout, the row of a voice set to play slices, whose buffer has slices, shows 16 of them in place of positions. A press plays its slice (at the beat or bar under quantize actions) and letting go ends it, except in one shot with a moving scan; a playing slice lights 15, a held key 11, a slice 9.

### Changed
- A slice row's bookmark keys pick banks: while a voice's row shows slices, its strip's four bookmark keys pick the bank the row shows (slices 1-16, 17-32, 33-48, 49-64), per grid, and recall no bookmark; bookmarks are then recalled from BUFFER and from steps.
