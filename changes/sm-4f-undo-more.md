### Changed
- The one undo holds three more things, each undone by a quick ALT+K2 within 5 seconds where it was made (#922, #927):
  - a start with the chain on: on SEQ it brings back all seven patterns it replaced, their steps and each voice's length, division, direction and misstep;
  - a random sequence's `new`: on MOD, on the sequence's row or in its settings, it brings back its sixteen values;
  - a matrix learn that rewired a routing: in MOD's matrix it brings back the routing and the value of the setting the learn took.
