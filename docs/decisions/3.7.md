# 3.7 decisions (rhythm)

The other releases: [README](README.md).

The design's decisions, its calls and its "Cost if wrong" list are in `docs/design/3.7-design.md` (its "Amended 2026-11-16" block rules where the body differs). What follows was decided while it was built, as rulings while the packages were built, reviewed and landed (the plan is removed at the release; git history keeps it). Every device behavior below is built from the documents (the fact files under docs/design/3.7-facts/) and is unverified on the device until the 4.0 hardware check (#6).

The grain reader (package 1a, `lib/engine/StrataPositions.sc`):
- **A one shot goes silent only once `bufPos` + the phase is strictly past 1** (1a's review), or below 0: at exactly 1, the span's last start, it sounds, so a one shot at `bufPos` 1 with the scan at 0 sounds while it is held and a backward one shot from 1 opens at full level, with no fade-in (`oneShotLevel`, `docs/notes/engine/positions.md`). The design says so (§2.1, "past the end"; §3.5, "in one shot with the scan at 0, the note sounds while it is held"), against the plan's and 1a's brief's "at or past 1", which silenced the encoder's end stop, a bookmark at 1.000 and an LFO clamped at the top. If wrong: one comparison.

The onsets pass (`lib/engine/StrataOnsets.sc`, package 1b):
- **Each hop's energy is summed in pairs with `copySeries`, not taken from `integral`** (unnumbered, 1b's build and review): design §3.3 and its fact file (`docs/design/3.7-facts/buffers.md` §1) give `squared`, `integral`, then `copySeries` at the hop ends, but sclang's `Signal.integral` returns one Float, the whole Signal's sum, not a running sum (`prSignalIntegral`, `lang/LangPrimSource/PyrSignalPrim.cpp`, the same in 3.13.0 and 3.14.1), so there are no ends to take. `hopEnergies` squares a chunk and adds its even and odd elements eight times over, so each of a chunk's 64 sums is one hop's: C primitives only, three Signals a level, none per sample (#131), and no running total to round. The check compares it with each hop's own `copyRange(...).squared.integral` on noise. If wrong: none in what it finds; a faster primitive would change only `hopEnergies`.
- **The mean before a hop counts as at least half `onsetFloor`, and a strength is the rise in level** (unnumbered, 1b's review, the controller's ruling): the mean of the four hops before a hop is floored at half the floor so the divide after silence is finite (a hop above the floor there is a candidate, as the 2x rule would make it anyway); a candidate's strength is log2 of its ratio less 1 (the 2x threshold), then scaled so the strongest is 1. With the plain ratio, one hit after near-silence (a ratio of about 3.6e5) made every candidate inside a texture print `0.000`, so 2b's sensitivity would have been all or one; design §3.3 says "its strength is the rise". Detection is unchanged. If wrong: a strength scale that spreads the candidates too evenly, changed in `onsetChunkStrengths` alone.

The delay on the beat (package 1d, `lib/delay_sync.lua`):
- **A scene's apply ends with an open hook, `scene_applied`** (unnumbered, 1d's review, the controller's ruling): `S.lib.scenes.apply_entries` runs every `S.register_scene_applied({name, fn})` hook once, right after it clears `S.scene_recall_in_progress`, each contained. The delay holds its `setDelay` while a scene applies and its hook sends one with all four values, so a recall's send is never a mix of old and new values and never deferred (`docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`): an instant recall's and a fade's landing go at once; a morph's waits as every morph write does. No other seam marks the end of an apply: `scene_recalled` runs at an instant recall and a fade's first step only, and a field's own `set` cannot know it is the last. If wrong: a three-line hook removed, and the delay's recall send deferred.

The checks (Wave 1's landing):
- **A new engine area's time goes in CI's area-times comment** (unnumbered, the controller's ruling, in place of plan ruling 5): 1b's `onsets` and 1c's `envelopes` add their times there, and since #731 (1c) the structure test sums every line and checks the stated total and the "about N minutes" the docs quote, so a true table is worth more than one move at the release. If wrong: one comment line.

A file's settings (package 2d, `lib/samples.lua`):
- **A slot's binding names the audio that plays; a replace take drops the slot's own record when it ends** (unnumbered, 2d's build and two reviews, the controller's rulings): a replace take unbinds its slot when the engine stops playing the old audio (`take_started` in `lib/record.lua`, once): at the send for a take with no delay, whose slot the engine empties at once (`prepareTake`, a threshold's wait included); for a synced take at the slot's first 0 s report (the engine empties it `bufferFadeWait + recordPrepMargin`, about 0.21 s, before the start) or at its "recording" report; and a canceled take's 0 s report unbinds a slot still bound. So through a synced take's arm wait (up to a bar or more at a slow tempo) the old audio plays on with its settings, its key and its slices, and a dub into a slot a canceled take emptied never inherits the old file's settings. Plan-lua §2 put the unbind at the take's send (`recording_started`); the first review's ruling moved it to the "recording" report, which left a canceled threshold take's emptied slot bound to the old file (the second review's N1). The record goes in `confirm_buffer_audio` when the new take settles (or when it settles with no audio): a replace take canceled before the engine prepared its slot puts back the take it replaced, which then still has its settings. If wrong: the three unbind sites.
- **An unbind resends `scaleNote` and `rootNote` too** (unnumbered, 2d's build): the brief names the resend at a bind; at a clear or a replace take's start the running voices of the slot go back to 60 and the root's reference at once, so the engine's root note never lags Lua's. If wrong: one call.
- **No gain is sent while the slot records** (unnumbered, 2d's build): the peaks reader rereads a playable recording's peaks every 2 s, and a normalized file's gain sent then would boost the take while it is made, against design §4.4 ("a take is heard at its own level while it is made"; a recording of Strata's output into a boosted slot cannot feed back through the boost). The take's end rereads its peaks and sends it. If wrong: one condition in `send_gain`.

Slices (package 2b, `lib/slices.lua`, `lib/notes.lua`):
- **An overlap step's slice that does not play to its end is released at the voice's next played step** (unnumbered, 2b's build): ruling 32 has an overlap voice's step slices ring past the next step to their end, which only one shot with a moving scan has. In wrap, bounce or with the scan at 0 a slice has no end and no key to lift, so each step would add a looping slice until the cap and the pool filled. Such a slice is released at the voice's next played step and wherever the step's overrides end; a one-shot one rings on, ended by its time. If wrong: one call in `apply_step`.
- **In release mode a voice's last key leaves it running while a note of it plays to its end** (unnumbered, 2b's build): design §3.5 has a one-shot slice play to its end "whatever the key does", but release mode stops a voice at its last key (`midi_note_off_for`), which would cut it. The voice runs on and the slice ends by itself (`S.lib.notes.plays_to_end`); when its last such note ends at its time (the own note's included, 2b's review), the voice, its keys all up, stops at the next LFO step, as release mode would have stopped it. Only the held-back lift marks it, and a key down, the voice's stop and its start clear the mark (2b's second review: a mark set at every MIDI note's end stopped a voice a pattern step or K3 restarted within a step). If wrong: one condition in `end_key` and one step hook.
- **A MIDI note outside the slices is no note of that voice** (unnumbered, 2b's build): it holds no key there and releases nothing, so in release mode its lift never stops the voice, and the latch's "next chord replaces" never ends the slices that sound. A `started` for a note that plays nothing would reach the targets' hook, which would start an external note no off ends, so the note hooks gain a field of their own, `heard(voice, note, velocity)` (the controller's ruling on 2b's review), which `lib/midi_in.lua` runs for every note a voice receives, before the slice filter, and which starts nothing; the slice editor's key row listens through it. If wrong: one hook field.
- **Each slice hit restarts its scan; any other send of the own region goes only on a change; a change of the option clears it** (unnumbered, the controller's rulings on 2b's build and review, design §2 and §3.5): a played slice and a one-voice step's slice send the region every time (`set_own_region(voice, region, true)`), the same slice again included, so its scan restarts in every mode with its playhead; a sync, a recall, the option's clear and a region's end send only a change, since the engine restarts the own scan at every `regionLength`. A voice's own region set under `one` ends when `v<i>_slices` changes, so a voice turned off never keeps reading its last slice. If wrong: one flag, one action.
- **A slice note from MIDI parks** (`park = true`, unnumbered, 2b's build): the brief's MIDI note names no `park`; without it a released own slice under a held overlap slice would sound on, unheld. If wrong: one field.

Scan and follow (package 2a, `lib/scan.lua`, `lib/playheads.lua`):
- **A scan mode change restarts through `S.lib.positions.restart_scan`** (unnumbered, 2a's build): the engine's mode change never restarts its scan, so 2a sends `t_scanReset` with the mode (Wave 2's facts) through positions, which pairs it with the playhead's reset as a jump does. `lib/positions.lua` had no door for a restart alone (a jump moves `buf_pos` and, at 1, the loop), so 2a adds the function there, a few lines in 2e's file. If wrong: one function moved.
- **A voice follows only while its file has a tempo** (unnumbered, 2a's build): `S.lib.scan.following(v)` needs a buffer voice, `follow` not off and a tempo, and the bar sync and the playhead's beats read it; with `follow` on and no tempo the ratio is 1 (the brief) and nothing is synced, since there is no beat to stay on (design §2.4: "no sync ... to a voice that does not follow"). If wrong: one condition in `following`.

The envelopes (package 2f, `lib/envelopes.lua`):
- **`owns` takes a matrix slot, and the matrix tells which of its routings play** (unnumbered, 2f's build): the brief's `M.owns(routing)` is `owns(k)`, routing k as the matrix names its routings, and `lib/matrix.lua` exports `playing(k)` (its own test of a routing that plays: on, both parts known, a depth other than 0, `v<i>_main` resolved) so the envelopes never copy it. A route's settings, as the engine has or will have them, are the send budget's answer (`expected_value` of `env route <k>`: the settings table, or `false` after a stop), as the matrix's fast channels are, so nothing is sent twice and a stop goes once. If wrong: a function's argument, and one export.
- **Loop turned on starts a stopped envelope in Lua's model at once, and turned off finishes the cycle** (unnumbered, 2f's build and review): the engine's node starts its loop as soon as it is set to loop with no run in progress (`docs/notes/engine/envelopes.md`), so turning `env loop` on records a hit from 0 with no hold when the voice has had none or its last has ended; a running envelope runs on and then loops. Turning it off mid-loop moves the model's hit to the start of the cycle it is in (2f's review), since the engine's `EnvGen` runs that cycle to its end. The design says only that a looping envelope runs "whether or not its voice plays". If wrong: the model's phase against the engine's for a looping envelope routed both ways, until its next hit.

The slice editor (package 2c, `lib/ui/slice_editor.lua`):
- **The editor's hints are in the other modal editors' colon style, each whole on the hint row** (unnumbered, 2c's build and review; one style in every view since #736): the plan's `E2 slice E3 nudge K2 back K3 add` is 143 px of norns' font, wider than the 128 px hint row, which would cut it to `... K3 ad`. As SEQ's step editor and the zones editor write theirs, K2 and K3 first, every key a view takes named: markers `K2:back K3:add E2:sel E3:move` (125 px), with ALT `K3:delete E2:rows E3:fine` (104); rows `K2:back E2:slices E3:edit` (100), with ALT `E2:row E3:edit` (57). The markers view names E2 by shortening E3's word (X3's review): with `E3:nudge` no E2 word fits (`E2:slice` 135 px, `E2:sel` 129, `E2:slc` 128 to the edge), and `move` says what a nudge does. If wrong: four strings.
- **Keys are named as norns names notes: 60 is C3, so the key row runs C-2 to G8** (unnumbered, 2c's build): the plan says "C-1 to G9" (60 as C4), but norns' `musicutil.note_num_to_name` and the zones editor (3.5) name 60 C3; one script naming one note two ways would mislead. The range, 0-127, is the plan's. If wrong: one function.
- **From unset, the first turn up of the `key` row sets note 60** (unnumbered, 2c's review): the note that plays rate 1 (`NOTES.rate_root_note`), so a file is a few detents from its key instead of 61 from C-2; a turn down from unset does nothing. If wrong: one line.
- **BUFFER's `slices` field shows `tr <n>` for transients** (unnumbered, 2c's review): the plan's `tran <n>` is 32 px at 64, which cuts the 23 px `slices` label to `slic` in the field's 49 px; `tr 64` (22 px) and `eq 64` (24 px) leave it whole. The editor's own `mode` row still says `transients`. If wrong: one string, or a shorter label.
- **In `off`, the `amount` row sets `count` and makes nothing** (unnumbered, 2c's build): the plan says the amount is set "then `S.lib.slices.make`", but in `off` there are no slices to make, and a make could drop hand edits while the flash says nothing of it. The count is kept for when `equal` is picked. If wrong: one branch.
- **A mode change also flashes `edits dropped` when the record was edited** (unnumbered, 2c's build): the plan names the flash for the amount, but a mode change makes the slices again too, and drops the edits the same way. If wrong: one flash.
- **The rows view's ALT hint is `E2:row E3:edit`** (unnumbered, 2c's build; colon style since #736, `E2:row` as the zones editor's ALT hint): the plan gives no ALT hint for the rows view; ALT+E2 picks a row there and ALT+E3 edits as E3 does, and ALT+K2 does nothing (as in the zones editor). If wrong: one string.
- **From no tempo, the first turn up of the `tempo` row sets the clock's tempo** (unnumbered, 2c's build): the plan gives the row's steps (0.1, below 20 it clears) but not where it starts; at 0.1 a detent, starting at 20 would take a thousand detents to reach 120. A turn down from none does nothing. If wrong: one line.
- **The editor reads the slices and the file's settings at each use, and does nothing without them** (unnumbered, 2c's build): 2c was built beside 2b and 2d, so `S.lib.ui_slice_editor.ready()` is false on a build without their modules: BUFFER's field shows `--`, `open` refuses and the editor's layout states draw BUFFER as it is. Its listener on `S.lib.samples.on_bind` is registered at the first open, so a test's double plugged before init receives it. With 2b and 2d landed both are always true and the guard is spent. If wrong: a few `ready()` checks to delete.

The grids' slice rows (package 2e, `lib/positions.lua`):
- **A bank with no slices is not picked, and a held slice key ends with its page** (unnumbered, 2e's build): the brief has keys 1-4 set the bank and dark keys for a bank that holds none; a dark key that picked an empty bank would show an empty row that the bank's own reset rule (back to 1 when the slices no longer reach it) undoes at the next read, so such a press does nothing. A slice key still held when its grid shows another page or leaves gets its note off at the next playhead tick, as every held position key ends there, so a wrap slice never sounds on with no key to end it (review focus 2). If wrong: one condition in `strip_key`, one loop in `end_stale_port`.
- **The slice rows' tests use doubles set as fields after startup, not `before_init` replacements** (unnumbered, 2e's build): `h.slice_doubles` (`test/helpers.lua`) sets the consumed functions on `S.lib.slices`, `S.lib.scan` and `S.lib.samples` (making each table while its module is absent) once `finish_startup` has run, so once 2b, 2a and 2d land their start steps run unharmed and the doubles still stand in for exactly the functions 2e calls. If wrong: the doubles move into `before_init`.
- **A one-shot tap released before its quantized play keeps the play and sends its note off right after it** (unnumbered, 2e's build): the key's entry is marked `released` and the scheduled play sends `S.lib.slices.note_off` once it has played, which a one-shot slice note (`hold_to_end`) ignores; so a scan that leaves one shot before the beat never leaves a wrap note with no release. If wrong: one flag and one call in `press_slice_key`.
- **A waiting slice key's header flash is `v<v> sl <n>`** (unnumbered, 2e's build): the brief names no label; `v7 sl 64 at bar` is 15 characters, inside the header's 74 px flash room, where `slice` would be cut. If wrong: one string.
- **The bind listener goes in at the first bank pick** (unnumbered, 2e's build): `S.lib.samples` is absent at include time on 2e's branch, and no pick exists to forget before one; 2d's `on_bind` takes a listener at any time. `bank(port, v)` reads 1 whenever the slices fall short, so the LEDs and keys are right before any bind. If wrong: the call moves to include time (samples is included before positions).
- **Only the 64 ends a slice key's release under another voice, named by its layout record** (unnumbered, 2e's review): the review asked for the fallback on every layout that selects a grid voice, but the 128h's rows and the 128v's columns are each one voice's, and two of them can hold the same cell, so there a release under its own voice that found none would end another row's slice while its key is down. The 64's record says `rows_show_grid_voice = true`, and only there does a release that finds no key under the grid voice end that cell's key under the voice it was pressed for. If wrong: one field, read in `grid_key`'s release.

Wave 2's landing (batch 5):
- **A slice hit restarts its scan once, through the region's resend, relying on a retrigger after every hit** (unnumbered, the landing): `S.lib.notes.set_own_region(v, region, true)` resends even the same region in wrap and bounce (the engine restarts the own scan at every `regionLength`, and the playhead resets with it); on a one-shot voice it sends only a changed region and resets no playhead, since the retrigger that follows every hit (`play_own`, a quiet chord's caller, the step's `voices` output: true of every caller today) restarts the engine's scan (`positionsHit`) and the playhead (`lib/playheads.lua`'s `voice_hit` hook). A one-shot hit that changes the region restarts the scan twice back to back (the `regionLength` send and the retrigger's `t_scanReset`: within one control block one trigger, else about 1.3 ms apart; it drives only the scan's `Phasor` and `Sweep`, so it is inaudible) and the playhead once. If wrong: a future caller that hits without retriggering leaves a one-shot re-hit unrestarted.

SEQ's fill mark (fix package X3, `lib/pages/seq.lua`):
- **The pattern view's fill mark is a lowercase `f`, at x 108** (unnumbered, #735 and X3's review): 1e's `fill` (10 px from x 106) ended where a gated voice's `G` begins and read `fillG`; the strip between step 16's cursor (to x 104) and the `G` (from x 116) is 11 px, so no word fits with a space before the `G`. `f` (4 px) keeps three blank columns after the cursor and a space's width (4 px) before the `G`. Lowercase because the row's marks right of the cells are per-voice flags (`G`, `+`, `-`), where a capital `F` reads as MAIN's `[F]`, this voice frozen, while fill is global; `f` is the case of the `fill` row, the `fill` condition and its param. Weighed: `fl` or `fi` (6 px from x 106, one blank column after the cursor, cramped and no clearer), a symbol (`*`, `>`, `»`: fit, but say nothing of fill), and the word `fill` in SEQ's header (clear and global, but against ruling 9 and a change to `lib/ui/header.lua`). If wrong: one string and its x.

The 64's position key (fix package X6, `lib/positions.lua`):
- **The 64's position key ends under the voice that pressed it, as its slice keys do** (unnumbered, the fix of #733, a bug from before 3.7): a held position key on the 64 is released under the voice that pressed it, read through the same `rows_show_grid_voice` field, so a grid-voice change while it is held (row 7, or a press on another grid) leaves no recorded cell and no half of a loop; the other layouts keep their own voice's release (a 128h test guards it). If wrong: the field read in the position key's release.
