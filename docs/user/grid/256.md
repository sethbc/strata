# 16×16 Grid Layout (256 buttons)

The full layout: patterns, parameter faders, scenes, buffers and FX. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

**Rows 1-7: Pattern Sequencer** (pages 1 and 2)
- Each row represents one voice
- 16 steps per pattern
- Tap a step key to turn its step on or off: since 3.4 the step changes when you let go, not when you press. Hold a step and press another key on its row to set its level; hold a step for 0.3 s to open the step editor for it, and let go to close it (see [Monome Grid](../grid.md))
- Current step highlighted during playback

**Row 8: Transport & Page Controls** (every page)
- Columns 1-4: Play/stop pattern sequencer
- Columns 5-8: Tempo adjustment (-10, -1, +1, +10 BPM; sets norns' clock tempo). A tempo key lights while you hold it, and the screen's header shows the new tempo (for example `tempo 130`) for a moment. Under a MIDI or crow clock, which set the tempo themselves, it shows the tempo the clock runs
- Columns 13-16: Page selection (1-4; page 2 = parameters, page 3 = scenes, page 4 = buffers / FX)
- Column 12: Page 5 (positions): bright on page 5, dim otherwise, as the other page keys. See Page 5 below

**Row 9: Gesture Recorders, Mute and Macro Banks** (Page 1)
- Columns 1-4: gesture recorders 1-4. A tap (let go within half a second) starts recording (from the first thing you move), plays what was recorded, stops it and plays it again, as MOD's K3 does, acting as you let go; hold a key for half a second to clear its recorder (holding never plays, records or stops another recorder's recording first, and letting go after the clear does nothing). Lit 15 while recording, 10 playing, 5 stopped, 2 empty
- Columns 5-11: mute keys for voices 1-7. Tap to mute or unmute the voice; hold for half a second to solo or unsolo it. A muted voice keeps running and stepping, silent. Lit 2 while muted, 15 while soloed (and heard), 8 otherwise
- Columns 13-16: macro banks 1-4. Press to show that bank's four macros on rows 10-13, on MOD and on every other grid. Lit 15 for the bank shown, 6 for a bank whose macros have targets, 2 for an empty one
- Column 12 does nothing

**Rows 10-13: Macro Faders** (Page 1)
- The four macros of the bank shown (row 10 = the bank's first macro ... row 13 its fourth: macros 1-4 on bank 1, 13-16 on bank 4), faders as the parameter faders below: column 1 = 0, column 16 = 1, lit up to the value. They follow a macro moved on MOD, the arc, MIDI or a gesture recorder

**Rows 9-12: Parameter Faders** (Page 2)
- Each row is a fader for one parameter of the voice selected in row 14
- Press a column to set the value: column 1 = minimum, column 16 = maximum, following each parameter's curve (frequency and cutoff faders are exponential, so low frequencies get as many columns as high ones)
- Faders whose range runs through zero (pan, buffer scan and the cross-modulation amounts) have a column that sets exactly zero (column 9 for their symmetric ranges); each side of it spreads its half of the range over its own columns
- The row lights up to the current value (dim bar, bright value position)
- The grain envelope fader steps through its five shapes (column 1 = hann, column 16 = trap); the grain mode fader is continuous on columns 1-8 and triggered on columns 9-16
- Faders run through the voice's synthesis parameters (amp and pan last), then grain size, density, pitch, spread, envelope and mode, then source / amp mod / freq mod for cross-modulation slots 1-3
- For a voice whose source is a buffer, the first faders are buffer position, rate, scan, level and pan instead of the synthesis parameters; for an input-sourced voice the faders start with grain size (the grain parameters, then level and pan). The sub voice has no pan, so it has no pan fader

**Row 13: Fader Page Selection** (Page 2)
- Each fader page shows the next four parameters (rows 9-12)
- Columns 1-4, 1-5 or 1-6 select the page, depending on how many parameters the voice has (Folder and Sub have 5 pages, the other voices 6; a buffer- or input-sourced voice has 5, except an input-sourced Sub, which has 4); the selected page is bright

**Row 14: Voice Selection & Status**
- Columns 1-7: Select voice for parameter editing
- Columns 9-15: Toggle voices on/off (lit 12 running, 6 running but muted or silenced by a solo, 2 stopped)

**Row 15: Pattern Operations** (pages 1 and 2)
- Columns 1-7: Clear pattern for each voice
- Columns 9-15: Randomize pattern for each voice
- Hold a key for half a second to clear or randomize: the keys are lit dim, and the one you hold lights up until it acts. Letting go sooner changes nothing, so a stray press never wipes or scrambles a pattern

**Row 16: Voice Lock & Pattern Copy/Paste**
- Columns 1-7: Lock/unlock each voice (lit while locked)
- Column 8: hold it to make columns 1-7 freeze or unfreeze each voice instead; while it is held they show which voices are frozen (bright) and which are not (dim). With PARAMETERS > sequencer > quantize actions on beat or bar, a freeze waits for the next beat or bar
- Columns 9-15: Copy and paste patterns (column 9 = voice 1 ... column 15 = voice 7). Press a voice's key to copy its pattern: the key lights up. Then press another voice's key to paste the copied pattern onto it, replacing that voice's steps and levels. The copy stays, so you can paste it onto several voices; pressing the copied voice's own key changes nothing
- Column 16: Clear the copy (lit while a pattern is copied). Clear it before copying another voice
- The copy is what the pattern held when you copied it: editing that voice later does not change what gets pasted. Each grid keeps its own copy, and unplugging the grid forgets it
- A paste ends any level setting on the voice it lands on: if you were holding one of its steps, the next key you press on that voice turns its own step on or off instead of setting the held step's level
- Row 16 works the same on every page (1-5)

**Scene Page (Page 3)**
- Columns 1-16 are scenes 1-16 on rows 1-3 (columns 1-8 the first bank, 9-16 the second)
- **Row 1**: Scene recall (instant) - Press to immediately load scenes 1-16
- **Row 2**: Scene recall (with transition) - Press to smoothly crossfade into scenes 1-16
- **Row 3**: Scene save - Hold for half a second to capture the current state to scenes 1-16. The key lights up while you hold it; letting go sooner saves nothing, so a stray press never overwrites a scene
- **Row 4, Columns 1-4**: Gesture recorders 1-4, as on page 1's row 9
- **Row 5, Column 1**: Toggle scene sequencer on/off
- **Row 5, Columns 3-10**: Set scene sequence length 1-8
- **Row 6, Columns 3-10**: Set scene sequence length 9-16
- **Row 7**: The morph fader (PARAMETERS > morph): column 1 is scene A, column 16 scene B, and the columns between blend the two. It follows a morph moved on the screen, the arc, MIDI or a gesture recorder
- **Row 9, Columns 1-16**: Choose the morph's scene A (lit 15 for the scene chosen, 6 for a saved scene, 2 for an empty one)
- **Row 10, Columns 1-16**: Choose scene B, lit as row 9
- **Rows 11-15, Columns 12-16**: The XY morph's pad ([Modulation](../modulation.md#morph)), a 5×5 square of positions between the morph's four scenes: A at the bottom left (column 12, row 15), B at the bottom right (column 16, row 15), C at the top left (column 12, row 11), D at the top right (column 16, row 11). A press moves the morph there on both axes at once, in quarters: the column sets `morph` (column 12 is 0 %, 16 is 100 %), the row `morph y` (row 15 is 0 %, row 11 is 100 %), so the bottom row is the plain morph between A and B. A gesture recorder records the press. The key nearest the position is lit 15, a corner 8 when its scene is saved and 2 when it is empty, the other keys 4; it follows a morph moved on the screen, MIDI or a gesture recorder, and a corner's scene picked on MOD. Rows 11-15, columns 1-11 do nothing
- **Row 8**: Play/stop, tempo and the page keys (column 12 for page 5, columns 13-16 for pages 1-4), as on every page
- **Row 16**: As on every page (see Row 16 above)

Scene LED feedback:
- Populated scenes glow (brightness 6-8)
- Current scene is bright (brightness 15); the scene a crossfade goes to blinks, one change per crossfade step, until it lands
- Empty scenes are dim (brightness 2)
- While the scene sequencer runs, the scene it will recall next glows at brightness 10 in row 1 (the current scene stays at 15). Empty slots are skipped, so it is the next slot that holds a scene; after the last scene of the sequence that is the first one

**Buffers / FX Page (Page 4)**
- **Row 1, Columns 1-8**: Record into buffer 1-8 with the record settings, as the [BUFFER](../pages/buffer.md) page's K3 does (the source, duration or beats, level, feedback, loop, threshold and sync); press again while it records to stop it and keep what was captured, or while it is armed (waiting for its threshold or a synced start) to cancel it, recording nothing. Lit 15 while recording, 11 while armed and waiting to start, 8 when the buffer holds audio, 2 when empty
- **Row 2, Columns 1-8**: Clear buffer 1-8 (lit 4)
- **Rows 3-7**: Faders for filter frequency, filter resonance, delay time, delay feedback and delay mix. Press a column to set the value (column 1 = minimum, column 16 = maximum; frequency and delay time are exponential); the row lights up to the current value and follows changes made on the screen, arc or by a scene
- **Row 8**: Play/stop, tempo and page selection, as on page 1
- **Rows 9-15**: One row per voice (row 9 = voice 1 ... row 15 = voice 7) choosing its source: column 1 = synth, columns 2-9 = buffer 1-8, column 10 = input. The selected source is lit 15, the others 4, empty buffers 2. Column 12 freezes or unfreezes the voice (bright while frozen, dim otherwise; see [VOICE](../pages/voice.md)), at the next beat or bar while quantize actions is on. Columns 11 and 13-16 do nothing
- **Row 16**: Lock/unlock each voice, as on page 1. Holding column 8 turns columns 1-7 into freeze keys, as there
- The LEDs follow buffer and source changes made anywhere (screen, PARAMETERS, scenes, a recording finishing)
- The 16×8 and 8×16 have a sources page with the same record and clear keys (see their pages); the [8×8](64.md) has none

**Page 5: Positions** (row 8, column 12)
- Rows 1-7: one row per voice, 16 positions across its buffer. Press to jump there (outside the voice's loop the loop goes back to the whole buffer first); hold one key and press another on the row to set the loop from the start of the earlier key to the end of the later one, in either order. Only voices reading a buffer respond. What a press does and what the cells show: [Positions](../grid.md#positions)
- Rows 9-15: voice 1-7 bookmark strip (row 8 + voice). Columns 1-4 recall bookmarks 1-4; hold column 6 (store) and press 1-4 to store the playhead; column 7 holds the scan while pressed; column 8 restores the whole loop. Columns 9-16 are dark. The strip's lights: [Positions](../grid.md#positions)
- Rows 8 and 16 work as on every page
