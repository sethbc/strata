# 16×16 Grid Layout (256 buttons)

Two panes, each showing its own page, with a strip each. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

- **Rows 1-7: the top pane**, a page of seven lines (since 3.8 every page is the same on every size; see [Monome Grid](../grid.md) for the pages)
- **Row 8: the top pane's strip**: column 1 play/stop, column 2 fill (held), columns 3-9 the top pane's pages 1-7, columns 10-16 voices 1-7 on/off
- **Rows 9-15: the bottom pane**, another page, or the same page again
- **Row 16: the bottom pane's strip**: column 1 tempo down by 1 BPM, column 2 tempo up by 1 BPM (held, either repeats after half a second, ten times a second), columns 3-9 the bottom pane's pages 1-7, columns 10-16 mute voices 1-7 (15 muted, 4 not)

A 16×16 starts on patterns over [voices](pages/voices.md). A page key moves only its own pane: row 8's the top one, row 16's the bottom one. Changing one pane's page ends what that pane's held keys were doing and leaves the other pane's and the strips' alone (a held fill stays on, a held tempo key keeps repeating). The same page on both panes is allowed: on patterns, a voice's keys in both panes are one voice's, so on side 1 a second key in either loops the voice from the step held in the other ([the loop](pages/patterns.md#side-1-every-voice)), and on side 2's hits line it sets that step's level.

The pages: [Patterns](pages/patterns.md) (on either pane, or on both, a side each: a press on side 1 picks the voice side 2 shows, on either pane), [Faders](pages/faders.md), [Scenes](pages/scenes.md), [Sources](pages/sources.md), [Positions](pages/positions.md) (on either pane, or on both, a side each), [Voices](pages/voices.md) and [FX](pages/fx.md) (on both panes, one set: the grid's).

What the 16×16 gave up in 3.8: one play key, not four; tempo by 1, not by 1 and 10, on row 16; the fixed pairs of halves (patterns with the macros, the faders, the scenes with the XY pad, the buffers with the FX faders) give way to any two pages.
