# 8×8 Grid Layout (64 buttons)

Pattern sequencing, one half of the 16 steps at a time. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

**Rows 1-7: Pattern Sequencer**
- One row per voice, showing steps 1-8 or steps 9-16 (hold row 8 column 1 to switch)
- Each voice plays its own length, 1-16 steps (seq length, see [Sequencer](../sequencer.md)), whichever half is shown: the half you don't see keeps playing. Steps past a voice's length don't play: a set one lights dimly (level 1) and an empty one stays dark, so for a voice of 8 steps or fewer the steps 9-16 half shows only those dim marks
- The playhead lights in the shown half
- Tap a key to turn its step on or off: since 3.4 the step changes when you let go, not when you press. Hold a step for 0.3 s to open the step editor for it, and let go to close it (see [Monome Grid](../grid.md)); switching the half while you hold it closes the editor and leaves the step as it was
- Hold a step and press another key in the same row to set the step's level (column 8 = 15, column 4 = 8)

**Row 8: Controls**
- Column 1: Tap to play/stop (it acts when you let go). Hold for half a second to cycle steps 1-8, steps 9-16 and the position view (see below). Lit bright while the shown half plays; while the other half plays it blinks with the beat (on for one beat, off for the next). When stopped it shows which half is up: dim for steps 1-8, brighter for steps 9-16
- Columns 2-8: Toggle voices 1-7 on/off (lit 12 running, 6 running but muted or silenced by a solo, 2 stopped)

Each 8×8 grid has its own half; a grid plugged in shows steps 1-8.

**Position view** (the third view of row 8 column 1's hold; the key shows 15 while playing and 6 stopped)
- Rows 1-2: the selected voice's buffer as 16 positions (row 1 the first half, row 2 the second). Press one to jump there (outside the voice's loop the loop goes back to the whole buffer first). Hold one and press another to set the loop from the start of the earlier position to the end of the later one, in either order (the two may be on different rows). What a press does and what the positions show: [Positions](../grid.md#positions)
- Row 3: the bookmark strip. Columns 1-4 recall a bookmark; hold column 6 (store) and press one to store it (while store is held you can pick another voice on row 7: a bookmark press stores for the voice now picked). Column 7 holds the scan; column 8 puts the loop back to the whole buffer. The strip's lights: [Positions](../grid.md#positions)
- Rows 4-6: dark
- Row 7, columns 1-7: pick the voice (bright selected, medium a buffer voice, dim other)
- Row 8: columns 2-8 still toggle voices

**What the 8×8 leaves to the screen**: it has no faders, no scene page and no source, record or clear keys. Choose each voice's source and record, load and clear buffers on the [BUFFER](../pages/buffer.md) page (or VOICE, or PARAMETERS), and freeze a voice on [VOICE](../pages/voice.md). Save and recall scenes on [SCENES](../pages/scenes.md). Lock voices and edit their parameters on [MAIN](../pages/main.md) and VOICE. The gesture recorders, the macros and the morph are on the screen, the arc and MIDI. The position view shows a voice only while it reads a buffer, so set a voice's source on the screen first.
