# 8×8 Grid Layout (64 buttons)

Half of a pane at a time, and a small strip. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

- **Rows 1-7: the left or the right half of the pane**: columns 1-8 or 9-16 of a page of seven lines (since 3.8 every page is the same on every size; see [Monome Grid](../grid.md)). On patterns that is steps 1-8 or 9-16 of each voice; the half you don't see keeps playing, and a step's level runs over the eight keys shown (key 8 = 15, key 4 = 8). A fader of sixteen keys is drawn across the half's eight
- **Row 8: the strip**: column 1 is NV, columns 2-8 voices 1-7 on/off (lit 12 running, 6 running but muted or silenced by a solo, 2 stopped)

**NV**: tap it to play or stop the patterns (it acts when you let go; lit 15 while playing, 4 stopped). Hold it half a second to show the **chooser** on rows 1-4: column c is page c, row 1 its left half, row 2 its right half, rows 3 and 4 the halves of its second side, where it has one. Press a key to show that page, side and half at once; the chooser stays until you let go of NV, which then plays nothing. The shown choice lights 15, the other choices 4; a page that is not there yet, and the second side of a page that has none, stay dark and do nothing, as do column 8 and rows 5-7. Opening the chooser ends what the pane's held keys were doing, as a page change does.

Each 8×8 grid has its own page and half; a grid plugged in shows the left half of patterns.

The pages: [Patterns](pages/patterns.md), [Faders](pages/faders.md) (fader pages 1-8 and types 1-8 on the left half, pages 9-10, the macro banks and types 9-12 on the right), [Scenes](pages/scenes.md), [Sources](pages/sources.md) (the voices and the input on the left half, the buffers on the right) and [Positions](pages/positions.md) (cells 1-8 or 9-16; on its second side the marks on the left half, the slots on the right); the other two arrive in the rest of 3.8.

What the 8×8 changed in 3.8: NV's hold shows the chooser in place of cycling the halves and the position view, and the 8×8 gains every page, a half at a time.
