# 8×16 Grid Layout (128 buttons vertical)

One pane turned upright: voices 1-7 down columns 1-7, the strip down column 8. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

A monome 128 reports itself as 16×8: set its port's rotation on [SETUP](../pages/setup.md) to 90 or 270 to play it upright with this layout.

- **Columns 1-7: the pane**, a page of seven lines, each line running down its column: on patterns, column v is voice v and row s its step s (since 3.8 every page is the same on every size; see [Monome Grid](../grid.md)). A fader on a page runs up its column: row 16 the minimum, row 1 the maximum
- **Column 8: the strip**, the 16×8's row 8 from top to bottom: row 1 play/stop, row 2 fill (held), rows 3-9 pages 1-7 (the lit key pressed again shows the page's second side, where it has one), rows 10-16 voices 1-7 on/off

The pages: [Patterns](pages/patterns.md), [Scenes](pages/scenes.md); the other five arrive in the rest of 3.8, and their page keys are dark until then. Each 8×16 grid shows its own page. The positions page and its strip: [Positions](../grid.md#positions).

What the 8×16 changed in 3.8: the key at (8,8) no longer steps through the pages: every page has its own key on column 8.
