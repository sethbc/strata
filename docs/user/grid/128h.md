# 16×8 Grid Layout (128 buttons horizontal)

One pane on rows 1-7 and its strip on row 8. Back to [Monome Grid](../grid.md).

A monome 128 lying flat uses this layout (its port's rotation on [SETUP](../pages/setup.md) at 0 or 180); stood upright (90 or 270) it uses the [8×16 layout](128v.md).

- **Rows 1-7: the pane**, a page of seven lines (since 3.8 every page is the same on every size; see [Monome Grid](../grid.md) for the pages)
- **Row 8: the strip**, the same as the 16×16's row 8: column 1 play/stop, column 2 fill (held: on while you hold it, off when you let go; a latched fill stays on; lit 15 while fill is on, however it was turned on, 4 otherwise; a page change leaves it held, unplugging the grid lets go; see [Sequencer](../sequencer.md#fill)), columns 3-9 pages 1-7 (the shown page bright; the lit key pressed again shows the page's second side, where it has one), columns 10-16 voices 1-7 on/off (lit 12 running, 6 running but muted or silenced by a solo, 2 stopped)

The pages: [Patterns](pages/patterns.md); the other six arrive in the rest of 3.8, and their page keys are dark until then. Each 16×8 grid shows its own page. The positions page and its strip: [Positions](../grid.md#positions).

What the 16×8 gave up in 3.8: two of its three play keys and the next-voice key; in their place it gains every page the 16×16 has.
