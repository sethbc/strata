# SEQ

The pattern sequencer on the screen. Back to [Screen Controls](../controls.md).

A placeholder in this build: the page shows only its name and the hint `E1:page`, and ignores the encoders, keys and arc. In 3.1 it shows every voice's 16-step pattern, with a step cursor for setting step levels, play/stop and the tempo, so patterns can be made without a grid. Until then patterns are edited on a grid ([Monome Grid](../grid.md)).
