# SCENES

The eight scenes and the scene sequencer on the screen. Back to [Screen Controls](../controls.md).

Until then scenes are recalled and saved on a grid's scene page, and the sequencer settings and saving and loading scenes on disk are in PARAMETERS ([Scenes and Automation](../scenes.md)).
