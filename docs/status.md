## Running the checks

- **Lua:** `lua test/run.lua`, `luajit test/run.lua` and `lua test/sync_check.lua`, from the repo root. They need only Lua 5.3 (norns' version) and LuaJIT.
- **Engine:** `test/run_engine_check.sh [path/to/norns]` compiles the engine against a norns checkout's `sc/core` and runs `test/engine_check.scd` on a silent local scsynth with norns' 8192 KB real-time pool.
  - It needs SuperCollider and a norns checkout (`git clone https://github.com/monome/norns ~/src/norns`, or set `$NORNS_DIR`; CI uses commit `227aa0442181e3eab3fc189ee6b5342648ce7300`).
  - It uses `$SCLANG` if set, else `sclang` on `PATH`, else the macOS app.
  - It stops after `$ENGINE_CHECK_TIMEOUT` seconds (1200 by default, which a full run of about 16 minutes on 3.14.1 or 3.13 (about 930 s of areas at 3.3.0) fits with about 4 minutes to spare, #570; CI passes `ENGINE_CHECK_TIMEOUT=570` to keep each shard below its 600 s limit; the check's own deadline is 30 s less) and at once on a compile error. Stopping sclang also stops its scsynth (descendants found with POSIX `ps`, so neither `pgrep` nor `timeout` is needed), a scsynth that outlives a crashed sclang is stopped through the PID the check writes to its work dir (#152), and a signal to the script (Ctrl-C, a caller's limit) stops sclang, its scsynth and the work folder and exits 130 (#331).
  - Each run boots scsynth on the UDP port in `$STRATA_SC_PORT` (57110 by default). Runs on different ports can overlap, for example one per worktree (`STRATA_SC_PORT=57141 test/run_engine_check.sh`); avoid 57120 and the few ports above it, which sclang takes for itself when several run. Two runs on the same port collide, so on a shared machine give each its own port or wrap the runs in a lock, for example `flock /tmp/strata-engine-check.lock test/run_engine_check.sh`.
- **Soak** (on demand, not in CI or a full run): `STRATA_ENGINE_CHECK_AREAS=soak test/run_engine_check.sh` runs rounds of a 60 s load, save and every-frame check of the saved file on a high engine of its own, to stress sclang's allocation as a long session would (#131). `STRATA_SOAK_ROUNDS` (40) and `STRATA_SOAK_SECONDS` (60; 120 or 300) size it; give a long soak a longer `ENGINE_CHECK_TIMEOUT` (the check's own deadline follows, 30 s under it). Run it with SuperCollider 3.13, norns' version, to be meaningful.
- **Engine check on SuperCollider 3.13:** `test/run_engine_check_313.sh [-n runs] [-j parallel] [-o log_dir]` runs the check in Docker on CI's SuperCollider (norns' 3.13), one container per run, so many can run at once; it builds its image (`test/docker/sc313/Dockerfile`) on first use and reports how many runs passed, failed or crashed (#131). Details in `docs/notes/lua/test-harness/engine-check-runner.md`.
- **Page renders** (on demand, not in CI): `lua test/render_pages.lua <dir>` then `python3 test/render_pages.py <dir> --font ~/src/norns/resources/norns.ttf` (needs Pillow) draws every page state as a PNG. The layout tests catch overlaps on one baseline and text off-screen; the renders are the check for text over a line or box and for strings crowding on nearby baselines.
- **Engine check on Linux:** install `supercollider-language`, `supercollider-server` and `jackd2`. scsynth needs a JACK server, and a dummy driver needs no sound card. Start one in the background, then run the check headless:

  ```sh
  nohup jackd --no-realtime -d dummy -r 48000 -p 1024 >/tmp/jackd.log 2>&1 &
  SCLANG=/usr/bin/sclang QT_QPA_PLATFORM=offscreen test/run_engine_check.sh ~/src/norns
  ```

  If scsynth does not boot, check that JACK is running (`jack_lsp` fails when it is not) and start `jackd` again. Run JACK at 48 kHz, like norns. The check fails at once, within seconds, with `scsynth did not boot` when the server cannot start, and with `port <n> already has a server` when another run holds the port (#471, #472).

## What 3.3 changed

CHANGELOG.md has every change. In short:
- **Sound:** 3.2's by default, apart from 5 ms record fades, a buffer voice restarted in its release starting its scan from its position, and, with the voice filter on, a grain synth's fades running before the filter (each voice's filter and sends now run in one mix stage per voice). The review fixes make the master brighter, steadier and somewhat louder (the tape drift's pitch shifter, #306), let a filter cutoff that jumps up glide at 100 octaves a second (#305), take out what lies under 15 Hz in FM and Ring (#309) and keep triggered grains 4 ms apart (#532).
- **Recording:** overdub and loop recordings that play while they record (grains steer around the record head), record sources (input, pre-master, post-master, a voice), takes armed by level with a 10 ms pre-roll, and takes synced to the beat or bar.
- **Files, waveforms and positions:** random loads and filling empty buffers, a long file's start point, trim saves of the loop region, peaks files and waveforms, playheads, position jumps, two-key loops, four bookmarks per voice, a scan hold, arc scrub with friction.
- **UI:** BUFFER's record fields, waveform and heads, its keys on release and new arc rings; a position page on every grid, a rotation per grid port; the arc's rings at half their old speed (#552).
- **Data:** `scenes.data` and `buffers.data` written at once through a checked `.part`, read by a parser (never run), with copies of unreadable and older files. Scenes are format 5 (the Pulse res turned the right way, #314), `buffers.data` version 2, and every PSET holds a hidden format line.
- **Code:** the record, waveform and positions areas (three new engine files: one norns restart), the per-voice mix stage, a copy ring per voice for a frozen input, the real-time floors of 3 MB standard and 3.5 MB high, the state invariants checked after every unit test.
- **Tests:** 1189 Lua tests.

- **Candidate:** a seconds-based scan mode. `bufScan` moves in valid-region lengths per second, so a 300 s slot scans 300 s per second at scan 1; loop regions are the answer until then.
- [#6](https://github.com/sethbc/strata/issues/6) **Hardware check on norns:** held until 4.0; it gates no 3.x release.
- **Limits to keep in mind:**
  - Lua's 200-local limit applies to each function: the locals probe in `test/run.lua` measures the free locals at the end of each file's main chunk and of each module's constructor function, and requires 30 in each (`strata.lua`'s main chunk uses 5). Group new constants into tables, and split a module that runs short.
  - LFOs top out at 10 Hz, the norns `lfo` library's ceiling. Anything faster belongs in the engine.
  - scsynth's real-time pool on norns is 8192 KB. 3.3.0's worst cases leave 4.84-5.06 MB free on standard and, with spectral freeze on every voice and the FDN reverb, 4.19 MB on high at the tightest, synth -> buffer -> synth (606 KB above the 3.5 MB floor, about 494 KB counting the spectral writers' late allocations), and 4.91-4.94 MB at the all-buffer one; `\strataGrainBuf` is 67.4 KB of its 88 KB limit and each mix stage 34.5 KB of 40 (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`). A new UGen with a delay line or table in the voice or grain SynthDefs needs that budget re-measured.
  - sclang's garbage collector fails under millions of small allocations (#131, closed as a platform limit of norns' SuperCollider 3.13). The rules that work around it are in AGENTS.md and `docs/notes/engine/files.md`, the device check is #6 section 6.
