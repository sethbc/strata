## Limits to keep in mind

- Lua's 200-local limit applies to each function: the locals probe in `test/unit/structure_test.lua` (which `test/run.lua` runs) measures the free locals at the end of each file's main chunk and of each module's constructor function, and requires 30 in each (`strata.lua`'s main chunk uses 5). Group new constants into tables, and split a module that runs short.
- An LFO whose home routing (its own destination, the matrix's slot 16 + n) is not fast plays at most 10 Hz (`LFO.lua_max_hz`), and the matrix sums it with the R routings, at most 12 sends a 48 Hz step in all (576 a second); one homed on a fast destination runs in the engine, up to 50 Hz. A modulation writer's sends share 150 messages per 100 ms (`ENGINE_BUDGET`), counting exactly the messages that reach the engine; a recall's or a morph crossing's discrete switches (voice starts and stops, source switches) go at once outside the count (ruling 93: about 60 messages a voice start, 417 for a crossing that starts all seven, and a crossing at most once per 0.3 s, ruling 94). Notes add their own: at most 16 `noteExpression` a 48 Hz step (768 a second), counted in the budget's window and never deferred. The budget is a number the hardware soak (#6) is to confirm.
- scsynth's real-time pool on norns is 8192 KB. With six engine LFOs, scatter on every voice and destination and sixteen looping envelope nodes running, every voice on the costliest voice def (Karplus) and every 3.8 node running (the seven output rings' writers, shimmer in both effects), the tightest case, high synth -> buffer -> synth with spectral freeze on every voice and the FDN reverb, leaves 4350 KB free (766 above the 3.5 MB floor; 4240, 656 above it, at 3.8.0), and 2080 KB with the note pool full (16 extra notes; 800 above the full-pool floor of 1280 KB; 1970 at 3.8.0), since 3.9's reverb freeze moved the FDN's lines out of the pool (#819), as the memory shard measured them on SuperCollider 3.13 in Docker (`test/run_engine_check_313.sh`; 3.8.0's figures were CI's run 44461789361; `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`); the standard tier's cases leave more (4900 KB at synth -> buffer -> synth, against 3072). The memory area also runs a third pool case (every pool slot's voice synth live and cutting: a type switch under a full held chord: 3210 KB on standard and 2720 on high, 2590 at 3.8.0) and the voice-source cases (every voice reading another's output ring: 5730 KB free on standard and 5150 on high, 3360 and 2780 with the pool full), in the same run. The pool cases read 80 to 90 KB lower when other check areas run before memory in one sclang; the figures are memory-first, as CI's shard runs it. In that run on 3.13: `\strataGrainBuf` is 91.8 KB of its 94 KB limit (90.4 to 92.7 between runs), `\strataGrainRing` 73.3, an envelope node 5.9 KB (sixteen at most), each mix stage 39.6 KB of 42, each output ring's writer 5.1 KB of 8, and every voice def is under one 52 KB limit: Karplus, the costliest, is 48.9 KB (48.1 on 3.14.1, `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`; its lowest pitch 11.7 Hz at 48 kHz); the additive type, the largest of 3.8's five new ones, is 48.0 KB at sixteen partials, the others 16.9 to 32.0 KB; and the costliest note is a buffer note (`\strataGrainBuf`). The delay's line is two buffers (ordinary RAM, 2 MB) and holds 5.4 s; `\strataDelay` takes 24.4 KB of the pool (its limit 30), `\strataReverb` 124.7 (130) and `\strataReverbFDN` 103.6 (110; 228.4 at 3.8.0, whose lines were `DelayL`'s own pool memory: since 3.9 they are sixteen buffers, 288 KB of ordinary RAM that `highMemoryBytes` counts, `docs/notes/engine/reverb.md`). The engine counts its fixed buffers once (`fixedBufferBytes`: the delay's line, the output rings, the window bank, the wavetables and shimmer's rings). A new UGen with a delay line or table in the voice or grain SynthDefs, a node per voice or a larger pool needs that budget re-measured against the floors (3072 KB standard, 3584 KB high, 1280 KB with the pool full), the high synth -> buffer -> synth case first.
- The largest scenes file the rework can write (sixteen scenes of full macros, routings and step attributes, chords included, every lane length set and every voice's four letters full) is 19474 tables and about 3.3 MB (3,278,194 bytes on Lua, 3,271,986 on LuaJIT, as `test/unit/matrix_test.lua` prints): 3.8.0's 6898, plus the `lanes` block's eight tables a scene (16 × 8), plus the `variations` block's 778 a scene (16 × 778). The data file reader's caps were tripled for it (`lib/data_files.lua`): `max_tables` 98304, 5.05 times the file, and `max_bytes` 12 MB, 3.8 times it. A file over a cap is refused whole. Reading it on the standard tier (CM3) is unmeasured until #6.
- sclang's garbage collector fails under millions of small allocations (#131, closed as a platform limit of norns' SuperCollider 3.13), and when a live frame points at freed memory ([#211](https://github.com/sethbc/strata/issues/211), fixed in 3.5; it also failed 3.14.1, and crashed #671's single-sclang runs): sclang 3.13 and 3.14.1 free a function's frame as it returns while a method frame that a kept closure holds keeps its caller, so the engine calls such methods from functions through `areaCall` (`docs/notes/engine/structure.md`, Must not break and Areas: the area hooks, `strataAreas` and the listed calls, which a structure test checks). Measured on 2026-11-13, the waveform area: the fix passed 96 of 96 and 48 of 48 runs on 3.13 and 24 of 24 on 3.14.1, where the code before failed 7 of 48 (3.13, 210910d1, a scratch runner) and 5 of 31 (3.14.1); every area in one sclang on 3.14.1, 6 of 6 against 2 of 6. Since #689 the structure test holds the rule for every function in the engine (the commands, the handlers and each function literal sclang compiles as one). Unsure: norns' own classes and the class library, which the rule cannot reach. If the collector's count-error lines, or sclang ending on signal 11 or 8 or hanging after them, are seen again in any shard, it is a regression of the rule, not noise to rerun: run the area under an instrumented sclang (structure.md, Areas, says how) and route the call it names through `areaCall`. The other rules that work around the collector are in AGENTS.md and `docs/notes/engine/files.md`; the device check is #6 section 6, and the 3.4 command rates' soak is #6's too.
- The ii bus has one leader, the rig's `bus leader` setting (off, strata leads, teletype leads, kept in `io.data`): Strata sends ii messages only while it leads through a connected crow, and a Teletype always leads, so a bus with both is the player's to set as `teletype leads` (`docs/user/io/ii.md`). The queue sends four lines each 48 Hz step and holds 48 (a note's off is never dropped); above the bus's rate (about 190 lines a second) the ports take turns, a note sent or dropped whole (its pitch with its trigger) and a chord to one port thinned to one note a step, so every voice's ports are played but each gets only some of its notes (#710, `docs/notes/lua/io/ii.md`). Leaving the lead sends at most 14 offs and 15 lines at once (`II.cleanup_sends`, `II.leave_lines`, inside crow's queue of 16, which no document lets us measure; #6 measures it): offs past the fourteenth are owed and lowered at the next lead, a release line past the fifteenth is dropped, and at a script's end the owed offs are lost, so those gates stay high until their port next plays (build ruling 47).
- Offs to outside targets are timed by the targets core's one 48 Hz step hook, so an off lands up to 1/48 s after its note's end; a step note ends 0.03 s before its gate (`TARGETS.step_gap_seconds`, build ruling 46), so at very fast ratchets its low can fall in the same tick as the next high.
- AGENTS.md has 7,995 words of its 8,000 after 3.9's reverb freeze (5 left; 7,927 after the rework, 7,926 after its first part): 7,195 after 3.8's package 0a moved the module table and other detail to the notes, then the packages' rows and rules, and a fix package's move of eight rules' detail to their notes files; 3.9's first part added no rule and rewrote only lines it made false, each no longer, but `setDelayFreeze`'s row, one word longer, and the Docker runner's line, which names t1039's `[-s version]`; the rework added no rule and rewrote the step locks' line within its words, and the stage's gate line names `gateFloor`, one word more; the reverb freeze added its command row, its guarded-command entry, its notes entry and one rule, shortening other lines to fit. A release that adds rows or rules makes room first, as #696 did for 3.6 and each of 3.7's and 3.8's package 0a did.
- The engine check's 27 areas take 1889 s in all at CI's speed (the workflow's area times, about 31 minutes; 1788 s before 3.9's reverb freeze, whose `reverb` area added 101 s, and 1768 s before 3.9's first part, whose checks, walks and sweep added 18 s), its slowest shard, `buffers,loops,types`, about 363 s.
