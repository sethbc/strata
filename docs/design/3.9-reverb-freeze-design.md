# 3.9: the high tier's reverb freeze

Status: written on 2026-11-25 from a conversation with the maintainer, whose answers are under Decided, after a throwaway measurement of the candidate freezes (Measured, below). A builder's read of the draft the same day found nothing it could not plan from; what it found in the repository is in the text (Constraints, sections 4, 5 and 7) and its two open points are calls 11 and 12. Not yet approved. It closes #819, the last open part of 3.9. The implementation plan comes next, written with `superpowers:writing-plans`, once this document is approved.

## Intent

3.8 made the effects "a mixer a player can hold": plain sends, a delay that freezes into a loop to play over, shimmer. The reverb's half of that was cut. A player on the high tier should be able to freeze the reverb's tail into a wash that stays, play dry and delayed sound over it for as long as they like, shade it, and let it go.

3.8's package 3a built the freeze its design described and measured it: frozen, the tail lost 2.4 to 3.2 dB above 6 kHz in six seconds and stepped by up to 1.24 dB at the freeze, so it was cut by the plan's own condition (#819). The cause was found then: an interpolating delay line whose time has ever moved never lands on a whole sample again, and the interpolation darkens a loop at every pass. This design builds the freeze that the finding asks for.

## Decided (the maintainer)

Carried from 3.8's design (§7.5, decision 15) and its plan's rulings, and not reopened:

1. The freeze is the high tier's alone. The standard tier's reverb (`FreeVerb2`) has no way in; there the freeze is not offered.
2. It is a toggle like the delay's: a binary param, in no PSET and no scene, no LFO destination and no macro target.
3. A bypass ends the freeze first, and a freeze set while the reverb is bypassed is refused, as the delay's.
4. Frozen, the reverb's input is closed after the diffusers, which also shuts shimmer's loop; the size is held until the release.
5. The pages reach the freeze only through `S.lib.fx_hold` (`offered`, `frozen`, `freezable`, `toggle`), never through the param's presence.

Decided on 2026-11-25:

6. **The bar is a minute.** The level and the band above 6 kHz stay within 1 dB after 60 s frozen, and the level's step at the freeze stays within 1 dB. 3.8's rule measured about seven seconds, which a tail that fades over a minute can pass.
7. **Measure before designing.** A standalone measurement of the candidates came first (Measured), so nothing is cut mid-build.
8. **The lines move into buffers** (Approaches, 1).
9. **Damp shades the frozen tail.** While frozen, `damp` stays live on the reverb's output: turning it darkens or brightens the held wash, and an LFO or a scene on it keeps working. The loop is untouched, so nothing is lost; at the release the loop takes the damp it has then.

Every other choice here is the design's own, listed under Calls made in this design.

## Goals

1. On the high tier, `reverb_freeze` holds the reverb's tail: after 60 s its level and its band above 6 kHz are within 1 dB of what they were just after the freeze.
2. The level's step at the freeze is within 1 dB, measured against an unfrozen twin on the same input.
3. A reverb that is not frozen is 3.8.0's, sample for sample.
4. The real-time pool gains room: the high tier's tightest worst case keeps more above its floor than before this work.
5. FX's `freeze` row, ALT+K3 on a reverb row, the grid's RF key and the header's frozen mark work for the reverb as they do for the delay, through the seams 3.8 left.

## Non-goals

- A reverb freeze on the standard tier.
- A reverb clear. A bypass empties the reverb.
- A freeze saved in a PSET or a scene, or reached by an LFO, a macro, the matrix or the sequencer.
- New arc rings, and new crow, OSC or ii actions. The param can be mapped to MIDI from PARAMS, as the delay's.
- Sound entering a frozen reverb (an accumulating freeze), and shimmer inside the frozen loop. A loop at a gain of 1 that takes input grows without bound.
- Any change to the standard tier's reverb, the diffusers, the line lengths, the matrix or the decay law.

## Anti-goals

- An unfrozen reverb that differs from 3.8.0's by one sample.
- A freeze that jumps at the freeze or fades inside a minute, shipped with a note. If the engine's own measurement misses the bar, the build stops and reports to the maintainer; it neither ships the freeze failing nor cuts it silently.
- Less room in the real-time pool than before.
- A reverb that is bypassed and frozen, or a freeze that comes back unasked at a lift or a PSET load.
- A freeze offered where the engine has none (the standard tier).

## Constraints

- **The real-time pool is 8192 KB on every norns.** `\strataReverbFDN` takes 229 KB of it today, about 144 KB of that its eight `DelayL` lines (each line's memory is the next power of two above its longest delay plus a block: 2048, 2048, 4096, 4096, 4096, 4096, 8192 and 8192 samples). The high tier's tightest case keeps 656 KB above its 3584 KB floor, 690 KB with the note pool full (`docs/status.md`).
- **One audio-rate `LocalIn` a SynthDef**; no `LocalBuf`; never size a delay line from a control (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`).
- **Bypass is explicit**: only `setBypass` frees or creates the reverb node, and a node a lift creates starts empty.
- **A bufnum left at its default is buffer 0**, so every `Synth(` of a def that takes buffers passes them (the delay's line and the shimmer rings already follow this, a source test reading every site).
- **SuperCollider 3.13** is norns' and CI's version; 3.14.1 is checked too. Both run in Docker (`test/run_engine_check_313.sh`).
- **`AGENTS.md` has 73 words of room** under its 8,000-word test (7,927 today). The command's row, the notes file's name and the freeze's rule do not fit as they are: the package that adds them shortens other lines of that file by as much, moving detail to the notes they point at.
- **The check server has scsynth's default number of buffers** and an 8192 KB pool (`test/engine_check.scd`). A node of the new def takes sixteen line buffers and two shimmer rings, and a check-only copy of today's def 229 KB of the pool, so the minute-long batch cannot hold every setting with its twin side by side: its cases are chosen to fit.
- **The hardware check is held to 4.0** (#6): CPU and memory on a Pi 4 are measured there, not here.

## Measured

A standalone copy of the FDN's core (no shimmer), rendered offline with `scsynth -N` on SuperCollider 3.13.0 and 3.14.1, with six cores in one def on the same input and the same drift: today's core never frozen (the twin), 3.8's freeze as built, and four candidates. 24 settings (size 0.2, 0.5, 0.8 and 1; damp 0, 0.5 and 1; pink noise above 100 Hz and white noise), six seeds each, frozen at 12 s (so the network has reached its level, size 1 included) and read until 60 s after it. The figures are the range over every setting and seed and agree between the two versions to 0.02 dB. The scripts, the results and the logs are kept beside the session's ledger (`.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-25-reverb-freeze-design/spike/`: `gen.scd`, `run.sh`, `analyze.py`).

| Core | Level's step at the freeze, against the twin | Level after 60 s | Band above 6 kHz after 60 s | Never frozen, against today's core |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 3.8's freeze as built | up to +3.9 dB | down to -7.0 dB | -1.5 to -20.2 dB | 0 |
| a whole-sample second read of every line, in the pool (`DelayN` beside `DelayL`) | -0.29 to +0.68 dB | -0.01 to +0.11 dB | +0.01 to +0.09 dB | 0, the size still and moving |
| the same with the lines in buffers (`BufDelayL` and `BufDelayN`) | the same figures | the same | the same | 0, the size still and moving |
| one line each on an audio-rate time, rounded while frozen | -0.26 to +0.67 dB | -0.28 to +0.13 dB | -0.67 to +0.09 dB | up to 0.0016 at a peak of 0.2 while the size moves |
| the second read on the two drifting lines only, audio-rate times on the other six | -0.28 to +0.67 dB | -0.18 to +0.11 dB | -0.36 to +0.09 dB | up to 0.0013 while the size moves |

What else it showed:

- The first row reproduces #819's failure, so the harness measures what 3a measured.
- The pool and buffer variants print the same figure in every case: `BufDelayL` and `BufDelayN` play what `DelayL` and `DelayN` play.
- An unfrozen reverb's own 0.9 s windows differ by -0.53 to +0.61 dB from one to the next on steady noise, which is why the step is read against a twin in the same window. Against the window before the freeze the second read stepped by -0.36 to +0.90 dB.
- Released while its input runs, a frozen core is back at the twin's level within 0.04 dB three seconds later.
- The band above 6 kHz has a step of its own at the freeze, against the window before it: -0.72 to +1.05 dB, the +1.05 in one setting (white noise, size 1, damp 1) and at most +0.91 dB in the others. The bar is on the level, so this passes; the build looks at it (Left to the builder).

What it did not cover: shimmer running, the engine's real def and its commands, a release with the input stopped, a size or damp change while frozen, the pool measured on a running server (the 144 KB is the line sizes' sum), and CPU. Its buffer core also differs from section 2's def in two places: the taps' damping was held while frozen, where the design keeps it live (the figures carry over, since damp never moved), and the drift was held by a plain `Gate`, so the release's 0.04 dB was read with the drift jumping back rather than gliding. Both are first measured by the engine package.

## Approaches

1. **The lines in buffers (chosen).** The FDN's eight lines move from `DelayL`'s own memory into buffers, as the delay's line did in 3.8's package 0b. Each line is read twice: interpolated, as today, and at whole samples for the frozen loop. About 144 KB returns to the real-time pool, for about 288 KB of ordinary RAM (two buffers a line). It costs more code than the next: the buffers are allocated, freed, zeroed at a bypass, passed at every reverb node and counted in the engine's memory arithmetic.
2. **A second read in the pool.** A `DelayN` beside each `DelayL`; only the def changes. The same sound, measured. It takes about 144 KB more of the pool: the tightest high case would keep about 512 KB above its floor in place of 656.
3. **Audio-rate times, no memory** (and its 48 KB hybrid). Dropped: it is no longer the same reverb when nothing is frozen, and the frozen tail keeps losing its top (0.67 dB in the first minute).

The maintainer picked 1: both hold the tail equally, and the pool is the one resource that cannot grow.

## Design

### 1. The lines

- Two mono buffers a line, sixteen in all, of the frames the lines take today: `fdnLineFrames`, 2048, 2048, 4096, 4096, 4096, 4096, 8192 and 8192 (powers of two, as `BufDelayL` reads them). One of each pair is the interpolated read's, the other the whole-sample read's: each `BufDelay` UGen writes its own input into its own buffer, so no two UGens write one buffer.
- They are the effects area's, allocated once with its state on the high tier (the performance area, order 10, has set the tier before the effects area, order 60, allocates) and freed with it after every stop. Their bufnums never change.
- They are ordinary RAM, 73728 frames (288 KB), and the high tier's memory arithmetic counts them (`highMemoryBytes`, `docs/notes/engine/performance.md`).
- Every `Synth(\strataReverbFDN` passes them, the engine check's own nodes included (on lines of their own, never the engine's); a source test reads every site, as it does for the delay's line and the shimmer rings.
- The bundle that frees a bypassed reverb zeroes all sixteen and sets the kept `frozen` to 0, as `emptyDelayLine` does for the delay (#889): a lifted reverb starts empty and unfrozen.

### 2. The def

`\strataReverbFDN` gains `frozen=0` and its line buffers. `hold` is a `Slew` of `frozen.clip(0, 1)` over 20 ms, reaching 0 and 1 exactly, as the delay's. With `hold`:

- **The input closes after the diffusers** (each diffuser chain's output times `1 - hold`). Shimmer's loop enters before them, so it is shut too.
- **Each line is read twice** on the same control-rate time, the length less the `LocalIn` block as today: `BufDelayL` (interpolated) and `BufDelayN` (whole samples), each fed the line's input.
- **The loop** reads the interpolated line crossfaded to the whole-sample one by `hold`, through the loop's `OnePole` at the damping times `1 - hold`, and every line's gain is `.max(hold)`: frozen, the loop is whole-sample reads, no damping, the Householder matrix and a gain of exactly 1.
- **The output taps** read the interpolated line through a `OnePole` of their own at the live damping, frozen or not. So the output keeps the interpolation and the damping the loop drops (the step's cause in 3.8), and `damp` shades a frozen tail without touching the loop.
- **The size is held**: a `Gate` before its lag, closed while `frozen` is above 0, so a size sent meanwhile waits and a release glides to it. The two drifting lines' drift is held the same way and glides back at a release rather than jumping (3a's `fdnHeldDrift`, in #819's first comment).
- **At `frozen` 0 it is today's def, bit for bit**: every product is times an exact 1 or plus an exact 0, the loop's `OnePole` and the tap's compute the same thing, and `BufDelayL` plays what `DelayL` plays.

Nothing rounds a time: `BufDelayN` reads whole samples whatever its time's fraction, and the taps keep the fraction.

`\strataReverb`, the standard tier's, gains an inert `frozen=0` so both reverb defs keep the same controls for the stub's map and the defaults test (3.8's plan ruling 25).

### 3. The command and the kept value

`engine.setReverbFreeze(frozen)`, `"i"`: 0 releases, 1 freezes; another value is ignored with one console line (`fastModIndicesValid`), so it joins the guarded commands.

- On the high tier it keeps the value with the reverb's other controls and sets the node; `startReverb` passes the kept value.
- With no node (a bypass has freed it) it keeps 0 and sets nothing; the bypass's free sets the kept value to 0 with the lines it empties. A reverb node is never born frozen.
- On the standard tier it keeps 0 and sets nothing.

The reverb's bypass and its freeze meet as the delay's do (`docs/notes/engine/delay.md`, Freeze by bypass, rows 1 to 5), with the same split between Lua's guards and the engine's.

### 4. Lua

`lib/fx_hold.lua` gains the reverb:

- **The param**: `reverb_freeze`, a binary toggle, default 0, name `reverb freeze`, save off, in the `hold` record after the delay's two (the fx group grows by one). It exists on both tiers, so the param list does not depend on the tier. Its sender calls `engine.setReverbFreeze(value)`.
- **`offered("reverb")`** is true on the high tier only (`S.performance.tier`). `frozen`, `freezable`, `toggle`, `release` and `any_frozen` follow from it unchanged.
- **Refused where it cannot act**: a set to 1 while the reverb is bypassed, or on the standard tier, puts the param back to 0 through `set_binary_param`, so a mapped controller hears the 0, and sends no freeze.
- **A bypass ends it first**: the bypass params' action already calls `release` for both effects.
- **The invariant `fx hold`** covers both effects: neither is ever bypassed and frozen. The PSET read hook releases a reverb a silent read left bypassed and frozen, as it does the delay.
- **A PSET load** leaves a reverb freeze as it is unless it bypasses the reverb; no scene holds it; a page randomize never changes it.

The pages' logic stands, since they read `offered`; FX gains one mark (`frozen_mark = "reverb"` on the `size` row, as the delay's `time` row has):

- **FX**: the reverb's `freeze` row shows `on` or `off` on the high tier and turns with E3; ALT+K3 on a reverb row freezes or releases; the hint is `K3:freeze` or `K3:unfreeze`; the section label's row shows `frz`. While the reverb is frozen its `size` row shows `held` after the label, as the delay's `time` row does. `damp` shows nothing: it is live. On the standard tier the row still shows `--`.
- **The grid's FX page**: RF (line 7, key 7) is 15 frozen and 4 not on the high tier, dark on the standard tier.
- **The header's frozen mark** already reads `any_frozen`.

### 5. Checks

The engine check gains a `reverb` area (its file, its name in `areaOrder`, its notes file), run on SuperCollider 3.13 and 3.14.1. Its cases run as seeded nodes side by side in one batch, so the minute is spent once:

- **Unfrozen is 3.8.0's**: against a check-only copy of the def before this work, the largest difference is 0, with the size still and moving, at shimmer 0 and 0.5.
- **The step**: frozen on steady noise once the network has reached its level, the level in the second after the freeze is within 1 dB of an unfrozen twin's in the same window, with shimmer at 0 and at 1.
- **The hold**: 60 s on, the level and the band above 6 kHz are within 1 dB of what they were just after the freeze. Across sizes 0.2 to 1, damp 0 to 1, pink noise above 100 Hz and white noise, and with shimmer at 1.
- **The input is closed**: sound sent to a frozen reverb does not enter it.
- **The release**: with the input stopped, the tail decays as an unfrozen tail does; with the input running, the level is back at the twin's.
- **Size and damp while frozen**: a size change waits for the release; raising `damp` lowers the output's top and lowering it again brings it back.
- **The drift** is held while frozen and glides back at a release without a step, checked on the helper alone with a known signal in place of the noise.
- **The commands** on a high and on a standard engine, the fresh defaults, a freeze kept through a bypass's fade and dropped at its free, a lifted reverb empty and unfrozen, the lines zeroed.

Every existing node of the FDN gains lines of its own, on check engines that allocate none (the standard tier's): the effects area's RT60 and pool probes, the memory area's twenty nodes (one set of lines between them, as they share one set of shimmer rings) and the shimmer area's case tables; the source test that reads every `Synth(` site for the shimmer rings (`test/unit/shimmer_test.lua`) reads the lines too. The performance area's memory formula and the figures in `docs/notes/engine/performance.md` move by the lines' 288 KB.

The memory area measures the def again and sets `fdnDefLimitKB` from what it reads (about 85 KB is expected, from 229), and measures the worst cases again; the tightest high case must keep more above its floor than the 656 KB it keeps today. The effects area keeps its checks of the FDN (its RT60 against `FreeVerb2`'s, its node after a lift).

On the Lua side, `test/unit/fx_hold_test.lua` gains the reverb's machine (the delay's table, less the clear, plus the tier), and the pages' tests the reverb's row, key and marks on both tiers; the test that pins `offered("reverb")` false on every tier becomes false on standard and true on high. Three tests plant a stand-in `reverb_freeze` param to reach the pages' reverb paths (`pages_fx_test.lua`, `grid_pages_fx_test.lua`, `ui_header_test.lua`); with the real param they use it on a high tier instead.

### 7. Work packages

Two, in order, each with its own review:

1. **The engine**: the lines in buffers, the def, the command, the `reverb` area, the memory and performance figures, the engine notes and `AGENTS.md`'s command row. ## Left to the builder

- Whether the FDN, its lines and its freeze move to a file of their own (`lib/engine/StrataReverb.sc`, as the delay has `StrataDelay.sc`) or stay in `StrataEffects.sc`.
- How the def takes its sixteen bufnums (an array control, or the first of a consecutive run), and the names of the new constants.
- The drift's return rate at a release (3a used 0.002 s/s).
- The band above 6 kHz at the freeze: measure its step on the real def and report it. If it passes 1.5 dB in any case, try a gain that rises with the closing input so the energy stays (`balanced` in #819's `fdn-step4.scd`), keeping the level's step inside the bar.
- Which CI shard takes the `reverb` area, keeping the shards even.
- The check's exact cases and seeds, so long as they cover section 5.

## Cost if wrong

- **The engine's def misses the bar where the spike passed** (shimmer, the real graph): the first package stops at its measurement, before anything else is built, and the maintainer decides. Nothing ships failing.
- **`BufDelayL` differs from `DelayL` somewhere the spike did not look**: the identity check fails in the first package; the fallback is approach 2 (the second read in the pool), which changes only the def and costs 144 KB of the pool.
- **Sixteen buffers are clumsy to pass and keep**: one array control and one alloc; the delay's line and the shimmer rings are the pattern.
- **Live damp on a frozen tail confuses** (the level moves a little as it turns): one `Gate` on the taps' damping holds it, and FX marks the row `held`.
- **The param existing on the standard tier misleads**: it is refused there and FX shows `--`; removing it there is a tier test in `lib/fx_hold.lua`.
- **CPU on a Pi 4** is unmeasured until #6: the def gains eight whole-sample reads and eight one-pole filters, on one node.

## Calls made in this design

Not asked of the maintainer; each can be overruled before the plan.

1. `reverb_freeze` exists on both tiers and is refused on the standard tier, the way a freeze is refused while bypassed. 3.8's design said the param "does nothing" there; refusing keeps a mapped controller's light honest.
2. FX marks the reverb's `size` row `held` while frozen, as the delay's `time` row; `damp` is unmarked.
3. Two buffers a line rather than two UGens writing one buffer: 144 KB more of ordinary RAM, and no reliance on two writers staying in step.
4. The line buffers are allocated on the high tier only.
5. The freeze's ramp is the delay's 20 ms.
6. A new check area and notes file, `reverb`, rather than more cases in `effects`: the minute-long batch gets its own place in a shard.
7. The step is measured against an unfrozen twin in the same window, not against the window before the freeze (3.8's review of 3a asked for this).
8. The checks freeze once the network has reached its level, and include white noise, size 0.2 and damp 1, which 3.8's seven cases did not.
9. Two packages, the engine first, and no cut written into the plan: a miss stops the build and goes to the maintainer.
10. The standard tier's def gains an inert `frozen` control (3.8's plan ruling 25, kept): the engine stub maps the reverb's controls to the last reverb def it reads, the standard one, and the kept defaults are read from it.
11. The level's 1 dB step binds with shimmer running too: the engine package's first measurement includes a shimmer twin (the builder's read asked).
12. The drift's hold and glide have a check of their own, on the helper with a known signal (the builder's read asked).
