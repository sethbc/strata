# Strata 3.6: integration

Date: 2026-11-14
Status: **for review.** The brainstorm of 2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14 decided the twenty points under "Decided in the brainstorm"; the body is written to them. Nothing here is built yet; the implementation plan comes after approval.
Builds on: Strata 3.5 (`docs/design/3.5-design.md`, shipped as 3.5.0), 3.4's interfaces for this release (`docs/design/3.4-design.md` §14, "Interfaces for 3.5 and 3.6") and `ROADMAP.md` (its 3.6 section and Cross-release decisions 4 to 7). Where this document and the roadmap differ, this document rules for 3.6, and "The roadmap against the facts" below says where and why.

Citations are `file:line` against `main` at 545b1e18 (3.5.0 and #680). Paths marked **(new)** do not exist yet. Every fact about a device, a firmware or a library comes from a document or a source file, never from a device: the four fact files beside this design, under docs/design/3.6-facts/, hold each one with its source and list what could not be verified (47 items across the three hardware files). `docs/design/3.6-facts/code.md` holds the merged code's interfaces this design names.

## Decided in the brainstorm (2026-11-13 and 2026-11-14)

All by the maintainer, each asked with its alternatives. "Not as recommended" marks the four where the recommendation was another option.

1. **Documents only.** 3.6 is built against the documented protocols and test stubs, and ships marked unverified on devices until the 4.0 hardware check (#6). No bench probes, no device check per integration. The alternatives were a few bench probes before the design, and checking each integration on its device.
2. **OXI E16: macros plus an on-device ring script** (not as recommended, which was macros only). Sixteen relative encoders control the sixteen macros, and Strata ships a Lua script for the E16 that draws the rings and labels from SysEx (§8.1).
3. **iii: a remote grid and a remote arc.** The device script is a thin remote over USB MIDI, so every existing grid layout and arc ring works unchanged. The alternatives were small surfaces of their own, and leaving iii for later (§9).
4. **Strata's own players**, one module per target behind one interface, with nb as one more target. The alternative was to reach Just Friends, crow and MIDI through nb's players (§3).
5. **A tenth page** (not as recommended, which was sections on VOICE, SETUP and FX). Everything external is on one new page (§11).
6. **No voice level followers in 3.6** (not as recommended). They wait for 3.8, so 3.6 has no engine work.
7. **Fader boxes: macros with pickup.** Fader n controls macro n once it crosses the macro's value; 14-bit where the device sends it (§8.2).
8. **One release, in waves.** Everything ships as 3.6.0.
9. **Program change out is in**, as a setting that is off by default. Just Friends' geode mode, W/ tape driven from scenes and reading a 16n over ii stay out.
10. **The page is named IO.**
11. **Targets play pattern steps and played notes.** A voice's external targets play its steps and the notes played into it from MIDI or MPE; MIDI out skips notes that came in on the same port.
12. **MIDI out has an MPE mode too** (not as recommended, which was plain MIDI only).
13. **TouchOSC layouts for a tablet and a phone**, generated from one description.
14. **Strata is its own nb host.** It reads the players that installed nb voice mods register and calls their documented functions; no nb code is copied into the repository. The alternatives were vendoring nb outside lib/, and leaving nb out.
15. **Rig settings are per device, outside PSETs**: the ii bus leader, the controller ports, the iii and MIDI-grid settings and OSC feedback. Loading a PSET or a scene never changes them (§2.3).
16. **Teletype: a fixed command set** on `CROW.C1` to `CROW.C3` (§6.4), not four assignable actions.
17. **A plain step plays the targets** at the voice's own pitch; a step with notes plays its chord.
18. **The tape loop sits after the master, with its settings in PSETs**, not in scenes (§12).
19. **A Launchpad through the midigrid mod.** Strata loads no midigrid code; one IO setting attaches the grid the mod provides (§9.5).
20. **MPE out: one voice per port**, in the port's lower zone. **Outputs that follow an engine-run LFO send Lua's model of it**, and IO marks them approximate (§5.3).

## The roadmap against the facts

Where the fact gathering changed what the roadmap sketched.

1. **The ii bus has one leader at a time.** Teletype is a leader only and has no follower address, so "Strata's ii output goes through Teletype" cannot be built. monome calls crow and Teletype both leading one bus unsupported, and crow's handling of a lost arbitration is a TODO (`docs/design/3.6-facts/crow-ii.md` §4). So the bus is a three-way rig setting: off, Strata leads, Teletype leads (§6.1).
2. **Teletype reaches Strata only through `CROW.C1` to `C4`.** Its queries (`CROW.Q0` to `Q3`) run in crow's interrupt path, cannot be answered from norns, and an open crow issue reports crashes when queries meet USB traffic (crow-ii.md §3). Strata installs no query handler.
3. **The Faderpunk and the Disting NT are USB MIDI devices for Strata.** The Faderpunk leads the bus by default, its follower mode is unimplemented and crow has no descriptor for it; crow has none for the Disting NT either, which takes MIDI and MPE over USB (`docs/design/3.6-facts/controllers.md` §4, §6).
4. **The E16's manual documents no host feedback.** Rings and labels are reachable only through a Lua script on the E16 that listens for SysEx (controllers.md §1). Decision 2 ships that script.
5. **midigrid keeps a Launchpad's top-row buttons** for switching between two 8×8 halves and never calls `grid.add` (controllers.md §5). The roadmap's use of the side buttons is dropped.
6. **norns already takes `/param/<id> <value>` over OSC** for every param, in real units, whatever a script does, and sends nothing back (`docs/design/3.6-facts/iii-osc-nb-softcut.md` §2). Strata adds feedback and a few action addresses, not a param address scheme.
7. **iii MIDI moves three bytes at a time** and its SysEx is undocumented (iii-osc-nb-softcut.md §1b). The remote protocol uses notes and CCs only.
8. **A tenth page changes no grid key.** No grid layout selects the screen page (`docs/design/3.6-facts/code.md` §2). Ten page dots need `HEADER.dots_x` moved from 36 to 32.
9. **The transport still only follows.** Strata's play key never starts norns' clock (3.4's Question 26), and 3.6 does not reopen it.

## Intent

3.6 connects Strata to the rest of a rig. A voice can also play Just Friends, W/, a TELEXo, an ER-301, an nb voice or a MIDI synth. crow's jacks carry Strata's modulation out and control voltages in. A sixteen-encoder controller, a fader box, a grid or arc in iii mode, a Launchpad and a phone or tablet running TouchOSC all become control surfaces. A Teletype can drive scenes and voices. A tape loop of the output runs on softcut.

The registries 3.4 built for this are in place: step outputs, modulation sources, the macros. Most integrations are one registration and a section of the new page.

Nothing in 3.6 can be tried on a device before the 4.0 hardware check. The design answers that with three rules: every message a module sends is checked in a test against the form its document gives; every device module does nothing until its setting is turned on; and everything ships labeled unverified until #6 confirms it.

## Goals

1. A voice's pattern steps and played notes also play external targets, with chords, gate lengths and note offs that never hang.
2. crow's two inputs are modulation sources or triggers, and its four outputs follow modulation sources, a voice's pitch or gate, or the clock.
3. Just Friends, W/ synth, ER-301 and TELEXo are targets over ii; W/ tape is controlled from the IO page; a Teletype controls Strata with three calls.
4. MIDI out: notes per voice on a port and channel, plain or MPE; eight CC out slots; program change in and out for scenes.
5. The E16 and a fader box control the sixteen macros; a grid or arc in iii mode and a Launchpad work as the grids and arc they are.
6. OSC feedback and two TouchOSC layouts.
7. One stereo tape loop on softcut.
8. One page, IO, holds all of it, and every setting on it is reachable from the norns alone.
9. With every 3.6 setting at its default, Strata sends nothing to any device and plays exactly as 3.5 does.

## Non-goals

- Voice level followers, and anything else in the engine: 3.8.
- Just Friends' geode mode; W/ tape driven from scenes; reading a 16n over ii; W/ delay.
- The Disting NT and the Faderpunk over i2c. Both are reached over USB MIDI.
- Answering Teletype's queries.
- Strata leading the transport.
- A TouchOSC control for every param (norns takes them all in already).
- Saving the tape loop to a file.
- Strata as an nb voice.
- A second crow (norns supports one).

## Anti-goals

- **A locked bus.** Strata must never send an ii message unless the rig setting says Strata leads. A PSET, a scene, a controller or OSC must not be able to change that setting.
- **Hung notes.** Every note sent to a target is ended by a stop of its voice, a stop of the patterns, a change of its route, an unplug that is followed by a replug, and the script's cleanup.
- **Loops.** A note, a program change, a CC or an OSC value that Strata received must not be sent back where it came from.
- **Changing 3.5.** With defaults, no device message, no new clock work beyond one hook, and no change to a scene or a PSET's meaning.
- **Hidden state.** What each route, jack and controller does shows on IO, with whether its device is present.
- **A claim of device support that reads as tested.** Every device page says which document it was built from and that it is unverified until 4.0.

## Constraints

- Everything in AGENTS.md applies: named constants, `send_param_to_engine`, the registries, `request_redraw()`, bounds checks, a cleanup step for every clock and handler, handlers on vports only, the data files rule, the locals probe, US English.
- **No engine change.** No `addCommand`, SynthDef or poll changes; the engine check runs only at the release, as a regression gate.
- **crow** (crow-ii.md §1, §2): one crow; a send to an absent crow is a silent no-op; `norns.crow.dev` is its presence; every script load and every reconnect runs `crow.reset()`, which clears input modes, output actions and `ii.self` handlers; a function assigned through the `crow` proxy uses one of 45 event keys per script session and keys are never reused; crow's ii leader queue holds 16 messages and drops the rest; matron reads crow at 115200 baud and `load()`s every event line as Lua.
- **norns' own crow clock.** With the clock source set to crow, input 1 is the system's; a "crow out" clock output is the system's too (crow-ii.md §1).
- **MIDI on norns.** 16 vports; a vport has one `event` handler; `midi.add` and `midi.remove` are single global callbacks that Strata's `lib/midi_in.lua` overwrites today (`lib/midi_in.lua:548-549`); PARAMS > MAP handles every CC on every vport, absolute or accumulated, with no pickup (code.md §7).
- **OSC on norns.** Port 10111; the script's `osc.event` runs first and the built-in `/param` and `/remote` handling always runs after it; no feedback (iii-osc-nb-softcut.md §2).
- **softcut** is reset at every script change, buffers cleared; it does not restore the mixer levels a script set (iii-osc-nb-softcut.md §4).
- **iii** is at v1.1.2; v1.1.0 (2026-03-22) renamed most of its API; a grid from 2022 on or the 2025 arc runs it; its MIDI buffers are 64 bytes (iii-osc-nb-softcut.md §1).
- **The tests' file scans** read every `lib/**.lua`: one include per module from `strata.lua` with a `lib/` path, the constructor shape, 30 free locals, every global read defined by a stub (code.md §14). No third-party code goes under lib/.
- **AGENTS.md has 146 words left** of its 8000 (code.md §14). §13 says how 3.6 fits.
- **`SCENE_DATA_VERSION` stays 7 and `PSET_FORMAT` stays 2.** 3.6 adds no scene field. Every new param is PSET-only and defaults to off, so a 3.5 PSET loads to 3.5's behavior.

## Approaches

### Who plays the external notes

1. **Strata's own targets (chosen).** A small interface (`note_on`, `note_off`, optional `express`) that each target module implements; one core module feeds it from the sequencer and from played notes, schedules note offs and guarantees the stops. Strata controls chords, budgets and settings. More code, all of it Lua and testable against stubs.
2. **Through nb.** Strata would build only an nb host and leave Just Friends, crow and MIDI to nb's player mods. Least code. Rejected: nb has no W/ or TELEXo player, Strata could not bound the ii traffic those mods send into crow's 16-message queue, and each mod is a separate install.
3. **One step output per device, no shared core.** The roadmap's sketch. Rejected once decision 11 added played notes: every target would repeat the note-off scheduling and the stop rules, and the fact gathering found five places where notes end without a step output hearing of it (code.md §5).

### Where the notes come from

Two feeds, because the two kinds of note live in different places in the merged code:
- **Steps** come from a step output. A plain step never passes through `S.lib.notes` (it is a `retrigger`), and only the event knows the gate length.
- **Played notes** come from note hooks on `S.lib.notes`, because latch, steals, parks, a freeze and a voice stop end notes with no message from the keyboard (`lib/notes.lua:437`, `:453`, `:338`, `:761`, `:751`).

The alternative, one feed from `S.lib.notes` for both, would lose plain steps and gate lengths. The other alternative, feeding played notes straight from `midi_note_on`, would hang a target's note whenever Strata's own note was stolen or parked.

### How crow is spoken to

1. **Text out, named events in (chosen).** Commands go through the `crow` proxy or as text; every handler crow needs is sent as a line of crow-side Lua that calls `tell` with a fixed event name, and norns receives it in `norns.crow.events`. No function is ever assigned through the proxy, so the 45-key limit never applies, and a reconnect resends the same lines.
2. **Proxy function assignments**, as norns' crow study shows. Rejected: each reconnect and each mode change would use up keys for the rest of the session.
3. **A script uploaded to crow.** Rejected: it needs a clock-driven upload at every start, and a second program to keep in step with Strata.

### What a device in iii mode is

1. **A remote grid or arc (chosen, decision 3).** The device sends keys and turns and draws what Strata sends. Strata keeps a frame buffer per device and sends only the LEDs that changed, under a budget.
2. **A surface of its own.** Less traffic, a new layout to design per device. Rejected by decision 3.

### Where rig settings live

1. **A data file per device (chosen, decision 15)**, read at start, written at each change, edited on IO as rows that are not params.
2. **Params.** Rejected: a PSET made on another rig could turn ii output on under a Teletype.

## Design

### 0. Restructure first (Wave 0)

Five packages that change no behavior and give the later ones their seams.

**0.1 Hooks in the merged modules.**
- **Note hooks.** `S.register_note_hook({name, started, ended, moved, expressed})` (`lib/state.lua`, registry `note_hooks`, open, read at each use; every field but `name` an optional function). `lib/notes.lua` calls them, each through `S.lib.ui_frame.contained_call`:
  - `started(voice, slot, note)` from `new_record` (`lib/notes.lua:148`), with `note = {owner, key, pitch, velocity}`;
  - `moved(voice, slot, note)` when a held note is re-pitched in place (branch 1, `:400-411`, and `play_own` on the same owner and key);
  - `ended(voice, slot, note)` from the local `forget` (`:155`) and when an own note is parked (`:478`); a parked note that plays again is a new `started`;
  - `expressed(voice, slot, note)` from `mark_due` (`:162`).
  A hook never yields and never calls `S.lib.notes.play`.
- **`S.lib.expression.raw(voice, owner, key) -> {bend, pressure, slide} | nil`** (`lib/expression.lua`): the note's bend as a ratio, pressure 0 to 1 or nil, slide 0 to 1 absolute or nil, from the records at `:166-177`.
- **`event.pitches`** on every step event (`lib/sequencer.lua`, `hit_event` `:698`): a list of absolute pitches as fractional MIDI notes, the step's chord when it has notes and the voice's own pitch when it has none, after the step's pitch lock. It is a copy; outputs must not change the event.
- **`S.register_scene_recalled(fn(scene_num))`** (registry `scene_recalled`, open): run when `S.current_scene` becomes the target, at a fade's first step (`lib/scenes.lua:790`) and at an instant recall (`:866`).
- **A virtual grid and arc.** `S.lib.grid_core.attach_virtual(port, device)` and `detach_virtual(port)` for ports above `S.MAX_GRID_PORTS` (constants `GRID_PORTS.remote` 5 and `GRID_PORTS.midigrid` 6); the loops that reconcile, rotate and count ports 1 to 4 leave those alone, the "grid keys" invariant covers them, and `grid_redraw()` and the cleanup already walk `pairs(S.grids)`. `S.lib.arc_core.follow_virtual(device)` and `unfollow_virtual()`: a virtual arc is followed only while no monome arc is connected, and a monome arc that arrives takes over. A device object provides what code.md §10 lists.
- **Param group records.** `lib/params_setup.lua` gains one record per 3.6 group (§13), each calling its module's `add_params()` when the module is loaded, as 3.4's loop does (`:641-644`).
- **Registries for the page and the targets** (`lib/state.lua`): `S.register_io_section` (§11) and `S.register_note_target` (§3), both open.
- **Orders and constants** (`lib/constants.lua`): the start and cleanup orders of §13 and `GRID_PORTS`.

**0.2 MIDI ports.** `lib/io/ports.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_ports`, owns `midi.add`, `midi.remove` and every vport's `event`:
- `listen(port, name, fn(data))` and `unlisten(port, name)`: several listeners on one port, called in registration order, each contained;
- `out(port) -> vport`: for sending;
- `on_change(name, fn(port, device | nil))`: a device arrived at or left a port;
- `device(port) -> name | nil`.
`lib/midi_in.lua` becomes its first user (its handler through `listen`, its hot-plug through `on_change`), with no change in what it does. From here on no other module sets `midi.add`, `midi.remove` or a vport's `event`.

**0.3 The IO page's shell.** `lib/pages/io.lua` **(new)** with the section registry and no sections; `"IO"` appended to `UI_PAGES` (`lib/constants.lua:76`), so no existing page index moves; `HEADER.dots_x` 32; `lib/io/rig.lua` **(new)**, the rig settings (§2.3); the tests and docs code.md §1 lists for a tenth page.

**0.4 Stubs.** `test/stub_crow.lua`, `test/stub_osc.lua`, `test/stub_softcut.lua` (with the `audio.level_*` calls), `test/stub_nb.lua` (the `note_players` global) and `test/stub_iii.lua` **(all new)**, each written from the norns, crow, nb or iii source the fact files cite, and each recording what the script sends. The crow stub builds the same text the real proxy builds and counts function assignments, so a test can assert there are none.

**0.5 Fact checks.** A docs-only package settles from source what documents can settle and the design leaves open: the nb host's duties (how a mod registers a player, what `nb:init`, `add_param` and `add_player_params` do that a host must repeat, and what a player needs at cleanup), the E16's 14-bit pairing, iii's LED range and its USB name, the `.tosc` schema from a generator library's source, and TouchOSC's default ports. It writes the answers into the fact files, files an issue for anything that contradicts this design, and names what stays unverified for #6.

| Bound | Value | Where |
|---|---|---|

| ii messages | 12 per 48 Hz step (`II.sends_per_step`), first in first out; at most 48 waiting (`II.queue_max`), then the newest is dropped and counted | §6.2 |

### 2. The shared pieces

**2.1 Presence.** A device module has two states that IO shows on its section's title line: its setting (off, or what it is set to) and its device (`[--]` absent). A module whose device is absent keeps its settings, sends nothing and costs nothing; when the device arrives it sets the device up again from its settings.

**2.2 Nothing by default.** Every route, input, output, slot and controller defaults to off, and the tape to off. A fresh Strata, or a 3.5 PSET, sends no MIDI, crow, ii or OSC message. A test asserts it: the whole-system run of §14 with defaults, every stub's sent list empty.

**2.3 Rig settings** (`lib/io/rig.lua`, `S.lib.io_rig`). A table of named settings in `io.data` in the script's data folder, through `S.lib.data_files` (written at each change, read at start, an unreadable file set aside as the other data files are):

| Key | Values | Default |
|---|---|---|
| `ii_bus` | `off`, `strata`, `teletype` | `off` |
| `e16_port`, `faders_port`, `remote_grid_port`, `remote_arc_port` | 0 (off), 1 to 16 | 0 |
| `e16_channel` | 1 to 16 | 16 |
| `e16_cc` | first of 16 CCs, 0 to 104 | 102 |
| `e16_encoding` | `rel1` (1 and 127), `rel2` (63 and 65) | `rel1` |
| `faders_channel` | 1 to 16 | 1 |
| `faders_cc` | first of 16 CCs, 0 to 80 | 32 |
| `faders_hires` | `off`, `on` (14 bits, the low 7 on CC + 32) | `off` |
| `midi_grid` | `off`, `on` | `off` |
| `osc_feedback` | `off`, `on` | `off` |
| `osc_port` | 1 to 65535 | 9000 |

`get(key)`, `set(key, value)` (validated, written, listeners told), `on_change(key, fn)`. Rig settings are never params, scene fields or PSET data, and nothing but a row of the IO page calls `set`. An invariant holds every value in its range.

### 3. Targets

`lib/io/targets.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_targets`.

**3.1 The interface.** A target registers with `S.register_note_target`:

```lua
{name = "jf",                       -- one of midi, crow, jf, wsyn, er301, txo, nb
 routed = function(voice) end,      -- true when this voice plays the target now
 note_on = function(voice, id, pitch, velocity, info) end,
 note_off = function(voice, id) end,
 express = function(voice, id) end, -- optional
 stop_all = function() end}
```

`id` is unique among the voice's sounding notes: `"s" .. k` for a step's k-th note, `"m" .. key` for a played note. `pitch` is a fractional MIDI note, `velocity` 0 to 1, `info = {source = "step" | "midi", owner, key}`. A target that plays one note at a time (crow, ER-301, TELEXo) plays the newest and returns to none when the last ends. Every call is contained: a target that raises prints once and is skipped until the patterns next start or its setting changes.

**3.2 The core** keeps, per voice, the notes it has sent on: `sounding[voice][id] = {pitch, targets, off_beat}`. A note off goes to exactly the targets its note on went to, whatever the routes are by then.

- **Steps.** One step output, `targets`, registered from a start step so it plays after `voices`. Its `available(voice)` is true when any target is routed for the voice. For each hit and each `k, pitch` in `event.pitches`: if `"s" .. k` is sounding it is ended first, then every routed target gets `note_on`, and the note's off is set for `event.beat + event.gate_beats`. Its `stop` ends every step note.
- **Played notes.** A note hook. `started` with owner `midi` sends `note_on` to the routed targets (`"m" .. key`); `moved` ends and restarts it; `ended` sends the off; `expressed` calls `express` on the targets that have one. Notes of owner `seq` are ignored here: the step output carries them, with their gate lengths.
- **Mute.** A voice silenced by a mute or a solo (`S.lib.voice_mix.silenced(voice)`) starts no target note; sounding ones end at their time.
- **The port a note came from.** `info` lets MIDI out skip a played note whose input port is its own output port (§4.1).

**3.3 Note offs.** One list of pending offs, checked from `S.lib.lfos.on_step` against `clock.get_beats()`. A new note on for an id cancels that id's pending off, so an off never lands after the note that replaced it. No coroutine per note.

**3.4 The stops.** Every sounding note of a voice is ended when the voice stops (the `voice_stop` registry), when a route of the voice changes (the route params' actions), and when the voice is muted with `TARGETS.mute_cuts` (true). Every note is ended when the patterns stop (step notes), at a rig or device change that removes a target, and at cleanup, before the MIDI handlers go (`CLEANUP_ORDER.io_targets` 142). When a MIDI output device returns after an unplug, MIDI out sends all notes off (CC 123) on each channel it had notes on.

**3.5 Pitch to volts.** One function for every target that takes volts: `(pitch - 60) / 12`, so MIDI note 60 is 0 V, the convention norns scripts use for Just Friends. Each target clamps to its device's range.

### 4. MIDI out

`lib/io/midi_out.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_midi_out`. Group `strata_out_midi`.

**4.1 Plain mode.** Per voice: `v<i>_out_port` (0 off, 1 to 16; default 0), `v<i>_out_channel` (1 to 16; default i), `v<i>_out_mode` (`plain`, `mpe`; default `plain`). A note on is the pitch rounded to the nearest note, clamped 0 to 127, at velocity `max(1, round(velocity * 127))`. Two sounding ids on the same port, channel and note are counted, and the note off goes when the last ends. A played note is not sent to the port it arrived on. Sends go through `S.lib.io_ports.out(port)`.

**4.2 MPE mode** (decisions 12 and 20). `v<i>_out_members` (1 to 15; default 15) and one global `midi_out_bend_range` (1 to 96 semitones; default 48). An MPE voice takes its port's lower zone: master channel 1, member channels 2 to `members + 1`; `v<i>_out_channel` is not used. A second voice set to `mpe` on a port that has one is refused by the mode param's filter, with a flash.
- **Setup**, sent when the mode turns on, the port changes or its device arrives: the MPE configuration message (RPN 6 on channel 1, value `members`), then RPN 0 on channel 2 with the bend range and RPN 0 on channel 1 with 2. Turning the mode off sends RPN 6 with 0.
- **Allocation.** A note takes the free member channel released longest ago; with none free, the channel with the fewest notes, oldest first.
- **Expression.** For a played note, from `S.lib.expression.raw`: bend as 14 bits over the bend range, pressure as channel pressure, slide as CC 74. A note's first values are sent just before its note on, in that order, at once. Later changes go through `express`, at most `MIDI_OUT.express_per_step` messages a 48 Hz step, each note's newest values, round robin. A step note carries no expression: its channel gets a centered bend before its note on.
- Plain and MPE voices can share a port, on different channels; the filter refuses a plain channel inside an MPE zone on its port.

**4.3 CC out.** Eight slots, each `cc_out<k>_source` (off, or a modulation source's key, the list built at init from `S.lib.mod_sources.registered()`), `cc_out<k>_port`, `cc_out<k>_channel`, `cc_out<k>_number`. From the LFO step hook, a slot sends `round(S.lib.mod_sources.fraction(key) * 127)` when it changed, at most `MIDI_OUT.cc_per_step` a step.

**4.4 Program change.** `midi_pc_in` (`off`, `on`; default `on`): a program change 0 to 15 on the input port recalls scene 1 to 16 through `S.lib.quantize.schedule("scene", ...)`, as a scene key does; an empty scene is ignored. The handler sits before the MPE branch of `midi_event` (`lib/midi_in.lua:338-340`). `midi_pc_out` (`off`, `on`; default `off`), `midi_pc_out_port`, `midi_pc_out_channel`: a scene recall sends its program change from the `scene_recalled` hook, except a recall that a program change on that same port caused.

### 5. crow

`lib/io/crow.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_crow`. Group `strata_crow`.

**5.1 Speaking to crow.**
- Presence is `norns.crow.dev ~= nil`. The module sets `norns.crow.add` and `norns.crow.remove` directly (norns restores its own at script clear); its `add` runs `crow.reset()` as the default does, then `M.setup()`, which sends every line the current settings need.
- **No function is assigned through the proxy.** Handlers are lines of crow-side Lua, for example `input[1].stream = function(v) tell('strata_in', 1, v) end`, and norns receives them as `norns.crow.events.strata_in`. The event names are `strata_in` (input, volts), `strata_change` (input, state) and `strata_tt1` to `strata_tt3` (§6.4). A test asserts the stub saw no function assignment.
- **One budget** for every line: `CROW.lines_per_window` per `CROW.window_seconds`, built on `lib/engine_sync.lua`'s pattern (a window counter, a pending table where the newest value per key wins, a flush clock that ends at its own wake). `M.send_now(line)` counts and sends; `M.send_value(key, line)` waits when the window is full. ii messages are lines too and count here.
- At cleanup: outputs to 0 V, input modes to `none`, and the handlers removed.

**5.2 Inputs.** Per input n (1, 2): `crow_in<n>_mode` (`off`, `cv`, `trigger`), `crow_in<n>_range` (`-5..5`, `0..5`, `0..10` V), `crow_in<n>_action` (`retrigger`, `next scene`, `freeze`, `record`, `patterns`), `crow_in<n>_voice` (0 the selected voice, 1 to 7).
- `cv`: mode `stream` at `CROW.stream_seconds`. The input registers a modulation source, `crow1` and `crow2`, names `CR1` and `CR2`, section `crow`, its range the param's, so MOD lists it and the matrix routes it with no matrix change.
- `trigger`: mode `change` at 1 V rising with 0.1 V hysteresis. The action runs on each rise: `retrigger` accents the voice (`engine.retrigger` on a running voice, never a new node); `next scene` recalls the next saved scene through the quantize scheduler; `freeze` toggles the voice's freeze param; `record` starts or stops recording on the selected buffer slot as BUFFER's key does; `patterns` starts or stops the patterns.
- While norns' clock source is crow, input 1 is left alone and IO shows `in 1  clock (norns)`.

**5.3 Outputs.** Per output n (1 to 4): `crow_out<n>_source` and `crow_out<n>_range` (`0..5`, `0..10`, `-5..5` V). The source is one of:
- `off`;
- a modulation source's key: from the LFO step hook, when the value moved by more than 1/200 of the range, `output[n].volts` through `send_value`, with a crow-side slew of 0.02 s. A source that is an engine-run LFO is Lua's model of it (decision 20): exact in phase for a clocked LFO, drifting for a free one, different draws for the random shapes; IO shows `~` after such an output;
- `v1 pitch` to `v7 pitch`, `v1 gate` to `v7 gate`: the module is the target `crow`, routed for a voice when any output names it. Pitch is §3.5's volts, no slew; a gate is 5 V while the voice has a sounding target note;
- `clock`: a pulse (`pulse(0.01, 5)`) every `crow_out<n>_div` beats (1/16 to 4), from one norns clock per output, one line a pulse.
An output that norns' own "crow out" clock uses shows `clock (norns)` and is left alone.

### 6. The ii bus

`lib/io/ii.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_ii`. Group `strata_out_ii` (the four devices' params, each added by its module).

**6.1 The leader.** `S.lib.io_rig.get("ii_bus")`:
- `off` (default): Strata sends no ii message and installs no ii handler.
- `strata`: crow leads when Strata sends. The targets below work. No Teletype handler is installed.
- `teletype`: Strata sends no ii message at all; the three calls of §6.4 are installed.
`M.leads()` is true only for `strata` with a crow present. Every ii send in the script goes through `M.send`, which returns false and sends nothing unless `leads()`. A test drives every 3.6 action under `off` and `teletype` and asserts the crow stub saw no line starting `ii.` other than the `ii.self` handlers.

**6.2 The queue.** `M.send(device, command, ...)` builds `ii.<device>.<command>(<args>)` and queues it; the LFO step hook sends at most `II.sends_per_step` a step through `send_now`, in order. Past `II.queue_max` waiting, the newest is dropped and counted, and IO's ii section shows the count. So a chord to several devices is spread over a few steps and never overruns crow's 16.

**6.3 The devices.** Each registers a target, and `M.on_ready(fn)` (crow arrived, or the bus became `strata`) and `M.on_release(fn)` (the bus is leaving `strata`, or cleanup) for its setup.

| Target | Module | Per voice | Note on | Note off | Ready, release |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `jf` | `lib/io/jf.lua` | `v<i>_out_jf` (off, on) | `jf.play_note(volts, velocity * jf_level)` | none (Just Friends shapes its own notes) | `jf.mode(1)`; `jf.mode(0)` |
| `wsyn` | `lib/io/wsyn.lua` | `v<i>_out_wsyn` (off, on) | `wsyn.play_note(volts, velocity * wsyn_level)` | none | `wsyn.ar_mode(1)`, `wsyn.voices(4)`; none |
| `er301` | `lib/io/er301.lua` | `v<i>_out_er301` (0 off, port 1 to 100) | `er301.cv(port, volts)`, `er301.tr(port, 1)` | `er301.tr(port, 0)` | none |
| `txo` | `lib/io/txo.lua` | `v<i>_out_txo` (0 off, output 1 to 32), `v<i>_out_txo_cv` (`pitch`, `envelope`) | `pitch`: `txo.cv(n, volts)`, `txo.tr(n, 1)`. `envelope`: `txo.cv(n, velocity * 5)`, `txo.env(n, 1)`, `txo.tr(n, 1)` | `txo.tr(n, 0)`, and `txo.env(n, 0)` | `envelope`: `txo.env_act(n, 1)`, `txo.env_att(n, ms)`, `txo.env_dec(n, ms)`; `txo.env_act(n, 0)` |

Globals: `jf_level` and `wsyn_level` (0 to 10 V; default 5), `txo_attack` and `txo_decay` (1 to 10000 ms; defaults 10 and 500). Just Friends and W/ allocate their own voices (six and four) and steal their oldest. Every command name and argument order above is crow's descriptor's (crow-ii.md §2), and each module's test asserts the exact line.

`lib/io/wtape.lua` **(new)**: W/ tape from IO rows, each an action that sends one command: record on and off (`wtape.record`), play and stop (`wtape.play`), reverse, loop start and loop end at the current time, loop on and off. Strata cannot read W/'s state, so each row shows what was last sent.

**6.4 Teletype** (decision 16). With the bus set to `teletype`, `setup()` sends three handlers: `ii.self.call1 = function(a) tell('strata_tt1', a) end`, and the same for `call2` (two arguments) and `call3` (three). Arguments are whole numbers as Teletype sends them.

| Teletype | Does |
|---|---|
| `CROW.C1 n` | Recalls scene n (1 to 16), through the quantize scheduler |
| `CROW.C2 v x` | Voice v (1 to 7; 0 the selected): x 0 stops it, 1 starts it, 2 toggles it |
| `CROW.C3 k v x` | k 1: retrigger voice v at velocity x (0 to 127). k 2: freeze voice v (x 0 off, 1 on, 2 toggle). k 3: mute voice v (the same). k 4: macro v (1 to 16) to x / 16383. k 5: the patterns (x 0 stop, 1 play, 2 toggle) |

Anything out of range is ignored with one console line. `CROW.C4` and the queries get no handler.

### 7. nb

`lib/io/nb.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_nb`. Group `strata_out_nb`. Strata is an nb host of its own (decision 14): it reads the global `note_players` table that installed voice mods fill, and loads no nb file.
- Per voice: `v<i>_out_nb`, an option of `none` and the players' names at init, with a hidden text param holding the chosen name so a PSET survives a changed list, as nb's own selector does.
- A chosen player is counted up (`count_up`) and a deselected one down, so its mod activates and deactivates it; the players' own params are added once at init (`add_params`).
- Note on: `player:note_on(round(pitch), velocity)`; note off: `player:note_off(note)`; at a voice stop and at cleanup `player:stop_all()`.
- With no mod installed the list is `none` and the module does nothing.
Package 0.5 confirms the host's duties from nb's source before this is built; where nb's host does more than this list, the package's findings rule.

### 8. Controllers

`lib/io/controllers.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_controllers`. Both controllers map onto all sixteen macros, whatever bank is shown. Their ports are rig settings.

**8.1 The E16** (decision 2).
- **In.** A CC from `e16_cc` to `e16_cc + 15` on `e16_channel` is a relative turn of macro 1 to 16: `rel1` reads 1 to 63 as up and 65 to 127 as down by `128 - value`; `rel2` reads above and below 64. Each tick calls `delta_with_detent("macro_" .. m, d, {source = "midi"})` (`lib/ui/detent.lua:80`), so macro learn and the touch hooks see it as a turn.
- **Out, plain.** When a macro's value changes by anything, its CC is echoed as 0 to 127, at most 16 a 1/15 s tick, in case the E16's firmware follows incoming CCs.
- **Out, rings.** One SysEx frame a tick when any macro changed: `F0 7D 53 54 01` then 16 values of two 7-bit bytes (14 bits, high first) then `F7`. Labels, sent at connect and when a macro's first target changes: `F0 7D 53 54 02 <index> <4 characters> F7`, the label `S.lib.macros.target_label` cut to four, or `M1` to `M16`. The page title `STRATA`: command `03`.
- **The script.** `extras/e16/strata.lua` **(new)**, loaded into an E16 scene with OXI's app: `controller.onSysex` checks the four header bytes and calls `leds.update(index, value, color)`, `slots.update(index, text)` and `page.setTitle`. It is not loaded by norns and is tested with the E16's globals stubbed.
- `docs/user/io/controllers.md` **(new)** gives the E16 setup: sixteen controls set to the relative type, the CCs and channel, the USB port.

**8.2 Fader boxes** (decision 7). A CC from `faders_cc` to `faders_cc + 15` on `faders_channel` is fader 1 to 16, 0 to 1 from 7 bits, or from 14 with `faders_hires` (the low byte on CC + 32, applied when it arrives). **Pickup:** a fader moves its macro only once it is picked up, which happens when its value comes within `CONTROLLERS.pickup_window` (2/127) of the macro's or crosses it. Any change of the macro that the fader did not make (a scene recall, a morph, an LFO, another controller) drops the pickup. IO shows the sixteen faders' states as a strip of marks. The 16n's and the Faderpunk's defaults (CC 32 to 47) are the rig defaults.

**8.3 PARAMS > MAP.** norns' MAP also acts on these CCs if the user mapped them, and would move a macro twice. Strata cannot stop MAP. The controllers section shows `map clash` when `norns.pmap` holds an entry for a CC, channel and device a controller uses, and the user page says to clear it.

**8.4 OXI One and Disting NT** need no code. `docs/user/io/devices.md` **(new)** gives each a setup: the OXI One as a sequencer over channel-per-voice MIDI with norns' MIDI clock and program changes for scenes; the Disting NT as a MIDI out target, plain or MPE.

### 9. Remote grids and arcs

`lib/io/remote.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_remote`, and two device scripts, `iii/strata_grid.lua` and `iii/strata_arc.lua` **(new)**, uploaded with monome's `diii` and never loaded by norns.

**9.1 The protocol** uses notes and CCs only, on the device's own port. Version 1.

| Direction | Message | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| Device to Strata | CC 0, 1, 2 on channel 1 | Hello: columns, rows (0 and 4 for an arc: its rings), protocol version. Sent at start and when asked |
| Strata to device | CC 2 = 0 on channel 1 | Send the hello |
| Device to Strata | Note on or off, channel 1 or 2 | Grid key: index `(y - 1) * 16 + (x - 1)`; channel `index // 128 + 1`, note `index % 128`; velocity 127 down, 0 up |
| Strata to device | Note on, channel 1 or 2 | Grid LED: the same index; velocity the level, 0 to 15 |
| Device to Strata | CC 16 to 19 on channel 3 | Arc ring 1 to 4 turned: value `64 + d`, d from -63 to 63 (a larger turn is split) |
| Device to Strata | Note 0 on channel 3 | The arc's key |
| Strata to device | Note on, channel 3 or 4 | Arc LED: index `(ring - 1) * 64 + (led - 1)`, split as the grid's; velocity the level |
| Strata to device | CC 3 = 0 on channel 1 | Frame end: show what was sent |
| Strata to device | CC 4 = level on channel 1 | Set every LED to a level |

**9.2 Attaching.** With `remote_grid_port` set and a device on that port, Strata asks for the hello; when it arrives with a grid's size, a virtual grid of that size is attached at `GRID_PORTS.remote`, and the layout is picked from its size as for any grid. The same for `remote_arc_port` and `follow_virtual`. The device leaving detaches it. A hello with another protocol version attaches nothing and IO says so.

**9.3 Drawing.** The virtual grid is a frame buffer: `led`, `all` and `segment` write to it, and `refresh` marks it due. A 30 Hz clock sends the differences from the last frame sent, at most `REMOTE.leds_per_tick` messages, then the frame end; what did not fit goes next tick, so a full 16×16 redraw lands within four ticks. The virtual arc's `segment` uses norns' own arithmetic for an arc's ring (`~/src/norns/lua/core/arc.lua`), so rings look as they do on a monome arc.

**9.4 The device scripts.** Each checks `device_version()` and shows a fixed pattern on a firmware older than 1.1.0. The grid script sends its hello, forwards `event_grid` as notes, and on `event_midi` sets LEDs with `grid_led` and refreshes at a frame end. The arc script does the same with `event_arc`, `event_arc_key`, `arc_led` and `arc_refresh`. They are tested with iii's functions stubbed, in a loop with `lib/io/remote.lua`: a key on the stubbed device reaches `grid_key`, and an LED Strata sets reaches the stub's `grid_led`.

**9.5 A MIDI grid through midigrid** (decision 19). With the rig setting `midi_grid` on, at start and at IO's `rescan` action Strata calls `grid.connect()` with no port. If what comes back is not one of norns' own grid ports and has `cols` and `rows`, it is attached at `GRID_PORTS.midigrid` and plays the layout of its size. With the mod off, or no supported device, nothing is attached. Strata loads no midigrid file.

### 10. OSC

`lib/io/osc.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_osc`.

**10.1 In.** norns' own `/param/<id> <value>` covers every param. Strata's `osc.event` adds what is not a param:

| Address | Does |
|---|---|
| `/strata/scene <n>` | Recalls scene n, through the quantize scheduler |
| `/strata/voice/<n>/run <0, 1 or 2>` | Stops, starts or toggles voice n |
| `/strata/voice/<n>/retrigger <velocity 0 to 1>` | Accents voice n |
| `/strata/patterns <0, 1 or 2>` | Stops, starts or toggles the patterns |
| `/strata/watch <param id>` | Adds a param to the feedback list |
| `/strata/hello` | Sends every watched value now |

It also remembers the last sender's host.

**10.2 Feedback.** With the rig setting `osc_feedback` on, a 15 Hz clock sends `/param/<id> <value>` for each watched param whose value changed, to the last sender's host at `osc_port`, at most `OSC.sends_per_tick` a tick, and `/strata/scene`, `/strata/voice/<n>/running` and `/strata/patterns` when they change. The watch list starts as the layouts' params. A value that arrived by OSC within the last 0.25 s is not sent back to its sender.

**10.3 The layouts.** `tools/touchosc/` **(new)**: one description (the sixteen macros; per voice run, mute and level; the sixteen scenes; the patterns) and a generator, run on demand like the page renders, that writes `extras/touchosc/strata-tablet.tosc` and `strata-phone.tosc`, both committed. A unit test checks every address in the description against the params and §10.1's table.

### 11. The IO page

`lib/pages/io.lua` **(new)**, a row page on `S.lib.ui_field_list` like SETUP, in sections.

```
IO                          ..........
 voice 3
> midi         port 2  ch 3
  mode         plain
  jf           on
  txo          out 3  pitch
 crow                          [--]
  in 1         cv  -5..5
E2 row  E3 value  K2 section  K3 do
```

- **Sections**, in this order, each registered by its module with `S.register_io_section({name, title, rows = function() end, status = function() end})`: `voice` (the selected voice's routes), `midi` (CC out, program change), `crow`, `ii`, `controllers`, `remote`, `osc`, `tape`. The page shows a section only when its module is loaded.
- **Rows** are `{label, kind, ...}`: `param` (a param id, edited with `enc_param_delta` or `set_binary_param`), `rig` (a rig key with its values), `action` (K3 runs it), `status` (text only) and `detail` (K3 opens the row's own list, through `S.lib.ui_modal`, for a CC slot's four fields).
- **Keys.** E2 moves the row, E3 changes it, K2 jumps to the next section's first row, K3 acts. The voice shown is the selected voice; ALT+E2 selects another through `S.lib.ui_keys.select_voice_by`.
- A section's title line shows its `status`: `[--]` with no device, the bus leader, a drop count, `map clash`.
- **Arc** (`lib/arc/io.lua` **(new)**): rings 1 to 4 are the tape's level, speed, feedback and wear.
- **Header.** Ten dots from x 32. No new indicator.
- **Layout states** for the layout tests and the renders: each section at the top of the list, with and without its device, and the detail list.

The grids gain nothing: a route is a setting, not a performance control.

### 12. The tape loop

`lib/io/tape.lua` **(new)**, `S.lib.io_tape`. Group `strata_tape` (decision 18).
- Params, PSET-only: `tape` (`off`, `on`), `tape_rec` (`off`, `on`; default on), `tape_length` (0.25 to 60 s; default 4), `tape_rate` (-2 to 2; default 1), `tape_feedback` (0 to 1; default 0.5), `tape_wear` (0 to 1; default 0.2), `tape_level` (0 to 1; default 0.7).
- With `tape` on: softcut voices 1 and 2 on buffers 1 and 2, panned left and right, loop 0 to the length with a 0.02 s fade; `audio.level_eng_cut(1)` sends Strata's output in; `rec_level` 1 while `tape_rec` is on; `pre_level` is the feedback; wear lowers the pre filter's cutoff from 16000 Hz to 800 Hz, so each pass comes back duller; the rate has a 0.2 s slew.
- The loop plays into norns' mix beside Strata's output, after Strata's limiter. The user page says so.
- With `tape` off, and at cleanup: the voices disabled and `audio.level_eng_cut` put back to the system's `cut_input_eng` value.
- The loop does not survive a script change (softcut clears its buffers).

**Files.** Every 3.6 module is `lib/io/<name>.lua` with the table `S.lib.io_<name>`: `ports`, `rig`, `targets`, `midi_out`, `crow`, `ii`, `jf`, `wsyn`, `wtape`, `er301`, `txo`, `nb`, `controllers`, `remote`, `osc`, `tape`. **Invariants** (`S.register_invariant`): the rig's values; targets (every sounding note names targets that are registered); MIDI out (an MPE zone has one voice; channel counts are not negative); remote (a virtual device is attached exactly while its port's hello is held).

### 14. Tests and docs

**Review focus**: the inputs this design implies and no single module's tests exercise.

1. **A device that is not there, or leaves mid-note.** Every module with its device absent sends nothing and raises nothing; a MIDI device that returns gets all notes off; crow returning gets every setting again.
2. **A note with no end.** A route changed while notes sound, a voice stopped under held keys, a latched chord replaced, the patterns stopped mid-gate, a PSET loaded while playing, cleanup: after each, every stub shows as many offs as ons.
3. **A loop.** A played note to its own port; a program change in that recalls a scene with program change out on the same port; OSC feedback answering the message that caused it; a controller's echo read back as a turn.
4. **A flood.** Sixteen held MPE notes all moving, a chord of eight to four ii devices at 300 BPM with ratchets, six LFOs on crow's outputs, a full grid redraw to a remote grid every frame: each bound of §1 holds, the newest values win, nothing is starved, and notes are never the thing delayed.
5. **The bus.** Under `off` and `teletype`, no action anywhere produces an ii command.
6. **crow's reset.** After a reconnect, and after `norns.crow.add` runs twice, crow holds exactly the current settings' lines and no function was assigned.
7. **A wrong setup.** A CC on the controller's channel outside its sixteen; a remote hello with another version or a size that is no layout; a Teletype call out of range; a fader box set to 7 bits with `faders_hires` on.

**A whole-system test** (`test/unit/io_system_test.lua` **(new)**): every 3.6 source on at once against the stubs for a simulated minute, asserting §1's bounds and focus 2's balance; and the same minute with defaults, asserting nothing was sent.

**Each device module's test** asserts the exact text or bytes it sends against the form its document gives, citing the fact file's line.

**Docs.** `docs/user/pages/io.md`; under docs/user/io/ one page each for MIDI out, crow, the ii devices and Teletype, controllers, devices (the OXI One, the Disting NT and the table of every supported device with the document it was built from, its version and "unverified until 4.0"), remote grids and arcs (with the `diii` upload), OSC and the tape; the notes files; `docs/user/controls.md` and README.md for the tenth page. Issue #6 gains a 3.6 section: one check per device, and each bound of §1.

## Cost if wrong

- **Documents only (decision 1).** The largest risk in the release: a document that is wrong, or a firmware that moved, makes a module do nothing or the wrong thing on a device, and nobody sees it before 4.0. What it costs: fixes after the hardware check, per device. What limits it: every module is off by default, so a wrong module harms nobody who has not turned it on; and each test pins the documented form, so a fix is one line and its test.
- **The ii bus setting.** If "Strata leads" is turned on with a Teletype on the bus, the bus can lock until a power cycle. The setting is off by default, per device, and IO's row says what it means.
- **Teletype's calls are inferred from crow's source**, not tried (crow-ii.md, Not verified 4 and 5). If forwarding does not work, §6.4 does nothing; the remedy is a small script uploaded to crow.
- **The E16 ring script** depends on OXI's scripting staying as its manual 1.0.1a describes. If it moves, rings and labels stop and the macros still work.
- **iii's API** changed in March 2026. The scripts pin 1.1.0 or later; another rename breaks them until they are edited, and Strata itself is not affected.
- **The nb host.** If nb's host does something Strata's does not, a voice mod may not sound or not release. 0e reads the source first; the fallback is to vendor nb outside lib/.
- **Every bound of §1 is a guess.** Too high floods a device; too low makes outputs steppy or LEDs slow. Each is one constant.
- **The tenth page** makes MAIN to SETUP one detent longer on E1 and moves the header's dots; if it reads as clutter, its sections can move to SETUP and VOICE without changing a module.
- **Lua's model of an engine LFO** on an output differs from what the voice hears for random shapes. If that matters, the remedy is to run such an LFO in Lua while it is routed out.
- **Pickup** can feel dead: a fader does nothing until it crosses the value. The alternative is a jump, which the maintainer declined.
- **The tape after the limiter** can clip norns' output when the loop and Strata both run hot. The level param is the control.

## Calls made in this design

Mine, not the maintainer's; any can be reversed.

- The hardware fact files are committed beside the design, as its evidence.
- All 3.6 modules live under `lib/io/`, with one AGENTS.md row.
- The ii bus defaults to off, not to Strata leading.
- Program change in defaults to on (the roadmap's "a setting turns it off").
- A muted voice starts no target notes.
- One pitch-to-volts rule for every target, MIDI note 60 at 0 V.
- Step notes reach targets from a step output and played notes from note hooks; sequencer notes are ignored by the hook feed.
- crow is spoken to in text with named events, never proxy function assignments.
- Note offs ride the 48 Hz step, with no clock per note.
- CC out has eight slots; the MPE bend range is one global param.
- W/ synth is put in AR mode and Just Friends in synthesis mode when their targets are ready, and Just Friends is put back at release.
- The remote protocol's channels and numbers; its version 1.
- OSC feedback goes to the last sender's host at a set port, and echoes of a value just received are held back 0.25 s.
- The tape's params and their ranges; four arc rings for it.
- No grid key or LED for anything in 3.6.
- Wave 0 includes a fact-check package.
