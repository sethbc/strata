2. **Bookmarks in scenes crossfade.** The registry's discrete keys must be whole numbers (`register_voice_params` requires a whole `{min, max}` holding the default, `lib/state.lua`, and `validate_scene` rejects a non-integer discrete extra), so bookmarks at 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75 cannot be discrete as the first draft said. *Recommend:* let them crossfade (no registry change; a bookmark changes nothing until it is recalled, and crossfading fits 3.4's morph). Alternative: a `held` set in the registry, keys switched at a transition's end without being whole, owned by 0b (`lib/state.lua`, `lib/scenes.lua`). Either way PSETs store them and VOICE shows them.
3. **A playable recording into a slot at another sample rate** (a 44.1 kHz file on norns' 48 kHz server: its grains play it at `bufRateScale`, while the record synth writes at the server rate). *Recommend:* such a recording becomes a replace take (the slot fades, empties, records at the server rate), with BUFFER flashing `buf <n>: replaced (rate)`; once a take is in it, the slot is at the server rate and overdubs play. Alternative: reset the slot's rate to the server's and overdub in place, which plays the old audio about 1.5 semitones sharp. A third option, writing at the slot's rate (the head moving `bufRateScale` frames per sample), is rejected: `BufWr` does not interpolate, so it would drop or repeat frames.
4. **When a synced replace take prepares its slot.** Preparing at the command silences the slot up to a bar plus 0.25 s before the take (4.25 s at 60 BPM). *Recommend:* the engine schedules the preparation (0 frames, the fade, the zeroing) at `delay - (bufferFadeWait + recordPrepMargin)` (`recordPrepMargin` 0.15 s, which covers zeroing a 300 s slot on a Pi 4), guarded by `slotChanges`, so the slot goes silent about 0.2 s before the take. An empty slot is prepared the same way. Alternative: prepare at once (simplest, a long silence before a bar-synced take).
5. **Two kinds of recording.** A *replace take* (feedback 0, loop off) keeps 3.2's behavior exactly: the slot fades, empties and reads 0 s until the take ends. Every other recording is a *playable recording*: the slot keeps playing while it records, and grains steer around the record head (§1.5). *Recommend* as written; the alternative (every recording fades the slot first) makes overdub deaf.
6. **Where an overdub or loop recording writes.** From frame 0 over the record length, and the slot's valid length becomes the longer of what it held and the record length, so a playable recording never shortens a slot. *Recommend* as written. Alternative: record into the selected voice's loop region (more mlr-like, but the region belongs to a voice, not the slot).
7. **One record command.** `recordBuffer` grows to eight arguments instead of a separate "record settings" command, so the engine keeps no record settings of its own. *Recommend* as written.
8. **Clock sync by a delay.** Lua works out the seconds to the next beat or bar and sends them with the record command; the engine prepares the slot (fade, zeroing) during the wait and starts the record synth with a timed bundle. *Recommend* as written. The alternative (Lua sends the command on the beat) starts replace takes on a playing slot up to about 0.17 s late.
9. **A bar is four beats** (`RECORD_SYNC.beats_per_bar`). *Recommend* the constant; the alternative is norns' `link_quantum` param, which only Link users set.
10. **5 ms record fades.** Every recording ramps its record and keep levels over 5 ms at its start and end, so no recording leaves a step in the audio. Replace takes gain this too (3.2's takes start and stop abruptly). *Recommend* as written; CHANGELOG says so.
11. **Threshold pre-roll of 10 ms.** A recording armed by level starts 10 ms before the crossing (a fixed `DelayN`), so it keeps the attack. *Recommend* as written.
12. **Bookmarks are positions only**, four per voice, absolute in the slot (0-1 of its valid region), defaulting to 0, 0.25, 0.5 and 0.75. Recalling one outside the voice's loop resets the loop to the whole slot. *Recommend* as written. Alternative: a bookmark stores position and loop (deterministic recall, but twelve hidden params per voice and nothing to edit on the screen).
13. **Record settings are global params** (PSETs, not scenes), as `record_duration` and `record_level` already are. *Recommend* as written.
14. **Peaks files live in Strata's data folder** (`~/dust/data/strata/buf<n>.peaks`), not beside the scratch takes as the roadmap says, so the audio file browser never lists them and the engine's stale-`.part` cleanup already covers the folder. *Recommend* as written.
15. **Grid access to the position page.** 256: page 5 on row 8 column 12 (free today). 128h: the sources page key (row 8 column 8) pressed again. 128v: the page key cycles through five pages. 64: row 8 column 1's hold cycles steps 1-8, steps 9-16 and positions. *Recommend* as written.
16. **The 128v shows all seven voices**, one per column with 16 positions down it, where the roadmap said one voice at a time. *Recommend* all seven (the 8×16 has the room); the 64 keeps one voice at a time.
17. **A position key outside the voice's loop** resets the loop to the whole slot and jumps there (mlr). *Recommend* as written. Alternative: keep the loop and clamp the jump into it.
18. **BUFFER's arc rings change**: ring 1 scrubs the position, rings 2-3 are the loop start and length, ring 4 the scan; ALT gives the rate, the friction and the record feedback. The record duration and level leave the rings (they stay screen fields). *Recommend* as written.
19. **ALT stops the scan only once ring 1 turns** with ALT held, until K1 comes up, so ALT+K2 and ALT+K3 never stop it. *Recommend* as written.
20. **New BUFFER holds act on release, without a clock.** K2 held 0.5 s loads a random file, ALT+K2 held fills every empty slot, ALT+K3 held saves the loop region (trim). ALT+K2 (clear) and ALT+K3 (save) then act on release instead of press. *Recommend* as written.
21. **Trim only saves.** It writes the loop region to a new file and leaves the slot as it is. *Recommend* as written. Alternative: also load the trimmed file back into the slot (sam's trim).
22. **The random folder** is the folder of the slot's current file, else the folder last chosen in the file selector, else `_path.audio`; not recursive. *Recommend* as written.
23. **Long-file start is per slot**, edited on BUFFER's `start` field, which reloads the slot's file from the new start 0.3 s after the last detent and is remembered in `buffers.data`. *Recommend* as written.
24. **The engine restarts the scan at every grain start** (a new grain synth, and a re-gated one, which 3.2 leaves mid-scan), so Lua's playhead estimate can reset exactly. A voice restarted during its release now starts its scan from its position instead of carrying on. *Recommend* as written.
25. **Post-master is Strata's own output**: norns' monitor, softcut and TAPE are mixed outside SuperCollider and are not in it. *Recommend* as written (documented).

**Questions for the 2026-11-08 amendment. All decided 2026-11-08, each as recommended** (the body is written to the recommendations). The decision itself (one stage per voice, the same sound) is the maintainer's; these are the choices §0c makes to carry it out.

- A1. **The voice-level gain moves with the filter.** **Decided 2026-11-08: as recommended (the gain after the filter).** The filter does not commute with a gain that moves: measured, putting a grain synth's level before the voice filter changes an accent by -36 dB of the output's peak at the default cutoff and by -10 dB at 200 Hz, res 0.9 (§0c.1). *Recommend:* the stage applies the voice-level gain (amp, the amplitude crossmod, the accent) after its filter, as the grain synth does today, so every steady state is exactly 3.2's. Alternative: keep the whole level in the grain synth (simpler, about 3.5 KB more per grain synth, and the sound changes whenever the filter is on).
- A2. **Two paths in the stage, one per source kind.** **Decided 2026-11-08: as recommended (two paths).** A synth-source grain synth carries no voice-level gain and no mirror (its voice synth does both), a buffer or input one carries both. *Recommend* two input buses and two filter banks in the stage (about 7 KB more per voice), so a switch between the synth source and another stays exact in its 2 s crossfade. Alternative: one path, whose gain and mirror follow the new source at a switch: the fading tail of the old one steps by up to its voice's amp for 2 s.
- A3. **Per-synth fades go before the filter.** **Decided 2026-11-08: as recommended (accepted).** The ASR, the kill fade and the slot and ring fades belong to each grain synth, so they now act before the voice filter (§0c.4, exception 1): -54 to -58 dB of the peak at the default cutoff while a fade runs, down to -18 dB at 200 Hz, res 0.9; exact when the filter is bypassed (mix 0, the default) and once the fade ends. *Recommend* accepting it: the only alternative is a filter per grain synth, which is what the decision removes.
- A4. **A voice's filter, sends and level reach its fading synths.** **Decided 2026-11-08: as recommended (one set of values).** Today a grain synth that a switch replaced keeps the values it had: only the live synth hears `setGrainParam`, `setVoiceControl` and `retrigger` (`StrataVoices.sc`). *Recommend:* one stage, one set of values: a filter or send change, an amp move, a crossmod routing or depth change and every accent during a switch's 2 s crossfade or 50 ms cut now reach the buffer or input tail too (§0c.4, exception 2). The changelog says so.

## Intent

3.3 makes the eight buffer slots something to play, not only to fill.
- **Recording** grows from "record the input into a slot" to overdub with feedback, a choice of what to record (the input, Strata's output before or after the master stage, or one voice), arming by level, takes that start on the clock and last a number of beats, and continuous loop recording that stays playable while it records.
- **Files**: a random file from a folder, filling the empty slots from one, a start point for files longer than a slot, and saving only a loop region.
- **Seeing the audio**: a 64-point waveform per slot on the BUFFER page and the grid, with the loop and a playhead per voice.
- **Playing positions**: grid rows that jump and set loops (mlr and glut), arc scrubbing with friction (angl and rangl), and four bookmarks per voice that 3.4's step locks will recall.

Everything defaults to 3.2's sound and behavior. Old scenes, PSETs and `buffers.data` load unchanged.

## Goals

1. Overdub with a feedback (keep) level, and loop recording that wraps until stopped, both playable while they record, without clicks at the record head.
2. A record source per recording: input, pre-master (after the filter, delay and reverb, before drift, limiter and saturation), post-master (what you hear), or one voice's output.
3. Arming by level, engine-side, with a 10 ms pre-roll.
4. Takes that start on the next beat or bar and last a length in beats.
5. A random file into a slot, the empty slots filled from a folder, a start point for long files, and saving a loop region.
6. A 64-point peak overview per slot, written by the engine to a file, read by Lua without blocking, and kept current during playable recordings.
7. Playheads estimated in Lua, exact at every reset, drawn with their spread.
8. Position rows on all four grid layouts, arc scrub with friction, and four bookmarks per voice behind a named interface for 3.4.
9. Three engine commands with changed signatures (`loadBuffer`, `recordBuffer`, `saveBuffer`), `stopRecord` extended, no new command, and one new poll.

## Non-goals

- 3.4's step locks, beat-quantized jumps and gesture recorders. 3.3 only provides what they call (§5.4).
- Recording into a voice's loop region, or anywhere but from frame 0 (Question 6).
- Editing audio (cut, paste, reverse a region, normalize). Trim saves a region; it does not crop the slot.
- Recursive folder scans, or a sample library browser.
- MIDI note mapping of bookmarks or positions (3.6's controller work).
- Positions for synth voices. Bookmarks, jumps and scrubbing apply to buffer voices; an input voice's place in its history stays the `history` param.
- softcut as a source or as a recorder (roadmap non-goal).

## Anti-goals

- Breaking a CM3. Everything in 3.3 runs on the standard tier, where the pool keeps over 1.6 MB above its 3 MB floor (§7).
- Old scenes or PSETs that sound different. Every new scene field (the bookmarks) defaults to values that change nothing until recalled; every new param defaults to 3.2's behavior (feedback 0, loop off, threshold off, sync off, source input, friction 100%, load start 0).
- New engine commands where an argument or a grain param will do (Goal 9).
- A replace take that behaves differently from 3.2's, beyond its 5 ms fades (Question 10).
- Changing what a grid row means on a layout that already has it. 3.3 adds a page or a view on each layout and lights an armed recording on the existing record keys; no existing key changes its action.
- Hiding state. Recording state (`ARM`, `REC`, `DUB`, `LOOP`), the record source, the bookmarks and the friction all show on BUFFER with their units.
- Stacking. A slot has at most one record synth; a new recording, load or clear frees the old one first.

## Constraints

**Platform** (as in 3.2, plus):
- **scsynth's real-time pool is 8192 KB.** Record synths, the pre-master tap and the per-grain head guard draw from it (§7). Buffers, including the eight small peak buffers, do not.
- **No `LocalBuf`** in any SynthDef (#130): the peak buffers are server buffers passed as controls. **Every SoundFile `readData` reads into a fresh array** (#131): the peak scan rides on `fileDecodes`, which already does.
- **matron polls carry one float.** The record state goes on a new poll encoded like `bufferLoadFailed`; the waveform goes through a file (roadmap Constraints).
- **norns' output bus** is hardware bus 0-1 (`CroneAudioContext.sc`: `out_b = Bus.new('audio', 0, 2)`). A synth after the master in `context.xg` reads what the master wrote this cycle with `In.ar`. norns mixes its monitor, softcut and TAPE outside SuperCollider, so they are not on it (Question 25).
- **`In.ar` reads a bus not yet written this cycle as silence**, so every reader of a tap runs after its writer (§1.3).
- **norns' clock lives in matron.** The engine never sees beats; Lua converts beats to seconds (`clock.get_beats`, `clock.get_beat_sec`) and sends a delay (§1.6).
- **`audio.file_info(path)`** gives a file's channels, frames and rate in Lua (`lua/core/audio.lua`), so the BUFFER page knows a file's length before loading it. `util.scandir` lists a folder through `ls` (one short subprocess).
- **`RecordBuf` loops over the whole buffer** and has no loop end, so a loop shorter than a slot needs a `Phasor` driving `BufRd`/`BufWr` (§1.4), as 3.2's ring writers do.

**Project:** everything in AGENTS.md, in particular fade-before-zeroing, `slotChanges`, `afterSlotFade`, replace-never-stack, `sendToSynth`, the file-write invariant, the velocity invariant, `send_param_to_engine`, the scene rules (`validate_scene`, migration defaults, discrete keys), the registries, and "the `voices` table is the only source of values" (§5.1 states the one transient exception). 3.2's structure: areas (`strataArea<Name>`, `areaState`), no edits to `lib/Engine_Strata.sc`, one owner per file per wave.

## Approaches

### Keeping a slot playable while it records

- **Playable recordings write in place and grains avoid the record head (chosen).** The slot keeps its audio and its voices; the record synth reads the old sample, scales it by the feedback and adds the new one. A grain whose read could meet the head during its life starts just behind it instead (§1.5), so no grain ever straddles the seam between old and new audio.
- **Fade the slot for every recording, as 3.2 does (rejected).** Simple and already safe, but overdub would be deaf and a loop recording silent until stopped.
- **Record into a ring and copy into the slot (rejected).** A second buffer per slot (49 MB on standard, 922 MB at 300 s), and the slot would lag the recording.

### How a record source reaches the record synth

- **Every record synth runs in one tap group at the tail of `context.xg`, after the master, and reads one stereo bus (chosen).** The bus is the input bus, a per-voice tap bus that the voice's mix stage writes (§0c), a pre-master bus that one persistent tap synth fills with the master's own `masterSum`, or norns' output bus. One placement rule serves every source, and every reader comes after its writer.
- **A record def per source, each placed beside what it records (rejected).** Four node-order rules, and a source switch mid-session would mean moving synths.
- **`\strataMaster` writes its pre-drift sum to a bus (rejected).** Exact, but it edits 2d's master and its 3.0 comparison checks. Calling the same `masterSum` method in a tap synth gives the same signal.

### Clock-synced starts

- **Lua sends the delay to the beat; the engine prepares during it and starts with a timed bundle (chosen).** The server starts the synth on the sample the bundle names, after the slot's fade and zeroing.
- **Lua sends the command at `clock.sync` (rejected).** Simple, but a replace take on a playing slot waits for the 60 ms fade and the zeroing (50-100 ms for a 300 s slot on a Pi 4) after the beat.
- **The engine follows the clock (rejected).** It cannot see norns' clock.

### The waveform's peaks

- **Loads: during the decode check, which already reads every frame. Recordings: the record synth keeps a peak per bin in a 64-frame server buffer, which sclang reads with one `/b_getn` (chosen).** No extra pass over the audio, and a loop recording's waveform stays current.
- **sclang reads the slot back (rejected).** 768,000 floats per channel over OSC for 16 s, 14 million for 300 s.
- **From the scratch take after it is written (rejected).** Late, and a loop recording has no take until it stops.

### Playheads

- **Lua integrates each voice's scan, with exact resets (chosen).** The engine restarts the scan at every grain start (Question 24), so Lua's estimate starts from the same place. Free, and drift-free between resets except for message latency.
- **A playhead poll (rejected).** One float per message: seven voices at 15 frames a second is 105 messages a second through matron.

### Arc friction

- **The glide is an engine-side scan offset (`scrubRate`) that Lua decays (chosen).** The engine integrates it per sample, so a fling glides smoothly.
- **Lua moves `buf_pos` at 15 Hz (rejected).** Each step jumps the grains' starts: a fling of one loop a second steps 7% of the loop every 67 ms.

### Where a voice's filter and sends run (amendment, 2026-11-08)

- **One persistent stage per voice with two paths, the voice-level gain after its filter (chosen; Questions A1, A2).** Every steady state is 3.2's to float rounding, a switch between the synth source and another keeps its crossfade, and each grain synth (each 3.5 note) is about 16 KB smaller.
- **In every grain synth, as 3.2 (rejected by the decision).** Exact, but 12-13 KB of filter and sends in each of up to 21 grain synths, and in each 3.5 note's.
- **One stage, the whole level left in the grain synths (rejected).** Simplest, but the filter then sees each accent, crossmod swing and amp move: measured -36 dB of the peak at the default cutoff (§0c.1).
- **One stage with one path (rejected).** About 7 KB less per voice, but the gain and the mirror can follow only one source kind, so the old tail of a synth-to-buffer switch steps in level for 2 s.
- **A stage per grain synth class, created and freed with the synths (rejected).** A new lifecycle (placement, `sendToSynth`, the fading and cutting sets) for what two persistent paths give.

### Bookmarks

- **Four positions per voice as voice params (chosen; Questions 2 and 12).** PSETs and scenes store them, VOICE edits them, and 3.4's locks recall one by number.
- **Position and loop per bookmark (rejected for now).** Twelve hidden params per voice and nothing editable on the screen.
- **A bookmarks data file (rejected).** PSETs would not carry them.

## Design

### 0. Package 0: the surface first

As 3.2 did with the engine split, 3.3 starts with packages that fix every interface the later packages build against, so Wave 1's engine and Lua packages can run in parallel. 0a and 0b change no behavior; 0c (amended 2026-11-08) moves the voice filter and sends into one stage per voice and keeps the sound, by measurement (§0c). 0a lands first (the memory floors are its first step), 0c follows it, 0b runs beside both.

**0a, the engine surface** (and the Lua call sites that must change with it, since the engine stub checks signatures):
- The three changed commands (§6) with their new signatures, the new arguments accepted and ignored: `loadBuffer` reads from the start, `saveBuffer` writes the whole slot, `recordBuffer` records the input as a replace take. Lua's three call sites pass the neutral values (start 0; region 0, 1; feedback 0, source 0, threshold 0, loop 0, delay 0).
- The `recordState` poll registered and never sent.
- Recording moves out of `lib/engine/StrataBuffers.sc` into a new area, `lib/engine/StrataRecord.sc` **(new)**, order 130: `\strataRecord`, `recordSlot`, `stopRecordSlot`, `stopRecording`, `slotRecording`, `cancelSlotRecording` and the `recordBuffer` and `stopRecord` commands, verbatim, with their state: the allocation of `recordSynths`, `recordFrameBuses` and `stopReplies` moves from `allocBufferState` into the record area's `allocState` (the variables stay in `lib/Engine_Strata.sc`). That `allocState` also allocates `numBuffers` two-channel control buses for the record heads (`areaState[\record][\headBuses]`: the head frame and the record length D, set to -1 and 0) and `numVoices` stereo voice tap buses (`\voiceTaps`). Order 130 is 3.3's; 3.4's `StrataModulation.sc` moves to 160.
- Two more areas with neutral contents: `lib/engine/StrataWaveform.sc` **(new)**, order 140 (it allocates the eight 64-frame peak buffers, `areaState[\waveform][\peakBuffers]`, and defines `writeSlotPeaks(index, frames, action)` as "call action now"), and `lib/engine/StrataPositions.sc` **(new)**, order 150 (it defines `slotScanPhase`, below, as today's scan).
- Both grain SynthDefs declare the 3.3 controls with neutral defaults: `recHeadBus` (the slot's head bus; the ring def ignores it), `scrubRate=0`, `scanHold=0` and `t_scanReset=0`. (The voice's tap, `tapOut`, is written by the voice's mix stage, §0c; 0a only allocates the tap buses.)
- `slotGrainReader` calls `this.slotScanPhase(bufScan, freeze, scrubRate, scanHold, t_scanReset)` (StrataPositions.sc; neutral: today's `Phasor.ar(0, this.scanRate(bufScan, freeze) * SampleDur.ir, 0, 1)`) and passes `recHeadBus` to `slotGrainPos`, which gains a last argument `recHead` and calls `this.slotHeadGuard(frame, lowest, highest, rate, durFrames, recHead)` (StrataRecord.sc; neutral: `frame`) on the frame it has chosen, before dividing by `validFrames`. The guard runs at audio rate (the start is built from `TRand.ar` and the scan's `Phasor.ar`) and sees `slotGrainPos`'s own lowest and highest starts (§1.5). 0a owns `lib/engine/StrataLoop.sc` for this one change.
- `bufferSlotPairs` and `slotLengthPairs` (`lib/engine/StrataLifecycle.sc`, 0a's until it merges, then 0c's) carry the slot's `recHeadBus`, so no grain synth ever reads a default head bus. ### 0c. The per-voice mix stage (decided 2026-11-08)

One persistent synth per voice, `\strataVoiceMix` **(new, `lib/engine/StrataMix.sc`)**, filters, levels and sends what all of the voice's grain synths play. Grain synths keep only what is theirs. Built in Wave 0 as package 0c, after 0a.

#### 0c.1 What moves, and why the level moves with the filter

Today `grainBody` (`lib/engine/StrataGrainDefs.sc`) runs: two `GrainBuf`s → the pan wander (`Balance2`) and `panBalance` → `voiceFilter` → the level → `voiceOutput` (dry, delay send, reverb send, mirror). The level is two kinds of gain:
- **E, the synth's own fades:** the ASR (`openedGate`; 0.1 s attack, `grainRelease` 2 s release, or a switch's forced linear release over 2 s), `killFade` (50 ms), the slot fade (`read[\level]`, `hasAudio` lagged 50 ms) and `ringLevel` (lagged 50 ms). E is 1 while a synth plays steadily.
- **V, the voice-level gain:** `(amp × (1 + modSig) × (1 + accent × accentAmt)).clip(0, 2)`, with `modSig` the amplitude crossmod times `modLevel` and `accent` the 5 ms / 0.4 s accent envelope. Buffer and input grain synths take it with `modLevel` and `accentAmt` 1; the synth source sets `amp` 1, `modLevel` 0 and `accentAmt` 0, so V is 1 there (its voice synth applies amp, crossmod and accent before the ring).

The filter is linear, so it can be shared, but it does not commute with a gain that moves within its memory: `F(x) × g(t)` is not `F(x × g(t))`. Measured (an offline render on SuperCollider 3.14.1: the engine's `multimodeFilter` with a gain after it and before it, pink noise and a 110 Hz saw, 6 s; the peak of the difference over the peak of the output, the larger of the two sources):

| Voice filter (mix 1) | constant gain | accent (perc 5 ms, at 3 Hz) | crossmod (a `\strataMod`-like follower) | amp steps (0.5 ↔ 0.7, 10 Hz) | 50 ms fades | ASR (0.1 s attack, 2 s release) |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| lowpass 5 kHz, res 0.5 (the defaults) | -138 dB | -36 dB | -56 dB | -38 dB | -58 dB | -54 dB |
| lowpass 1 kHz, res 0.5 | -138 dB | -21 dB | -43 dB | -24 dB | -44 dB | -40 dB |
| lowpass 200 Hz, res 0.9 | -134 dB | -10 dB | -21 dB | -14 dB | -18 dB | -18 dB |
| bandpass 1 kHz, res 0.9 | -127 dB | -15 dB | -32 dB | -15 dB | -28 dB | -30 dB |
| highpass 200 Hz, res 0.5 | -138 dB | -16 dB | -32 dB | -19 dB | -30 dB | -31 dB |
| bypassed (mix 0, the default) | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical | identical |

So the order must stay where a gain is a performance gesture (accents, crossmod, amp moves: always), and may change only where it is a fade (while one runs):
- **Stays in each grain synth:** the grains, their rate (pitch, bend, frequency crossmod by `rateLevel`), the pan, E, and one `Out.ar(out, sig × E)`.
- **Moves to the voice's stage:** the voice filter, V, the sends (`voiceOutput`), the mirror and 3.3's voice tap.

The grain SynthDefs lose `amp`, `velocity`, `accentAmt`, `modLevel`, `modAmpAmt1`-`3`, `mirrorOut`, `mirrorLevel`, `filterType`, `filterFreq`, `filterRes`, `filterMix`, `delaySend`, `reverbSend` and the named `delaySendOut`, `reverbSendOut` and `fxMixIn`; they keep `modBus1`-`3` and `modFreqAmt1`-`3` (the rate), `rateLevel`, `pan`, `t_trig` (triggered grains) and everything else.

#### 0c.2 Where it sits

Each voice has two stereo audio buses, its **synth path** and its **other path** (`areaState[\mix][\synthPaths]`, `[\otherPaths]`), and a group, its **mix group** (`[\mixGroups]`). A grain synth's `out` is its source kind's path: the synth source writes the synth path, a buffer or input source the other path.

```
voiceGroup:   strataInput, voice synths, ring writers                  (as 3.2)
processGroup: synth-source grain synths (head)                          -> synth paths
              mixGroups[v]: buffer and input grain synths of v (head)   -> other path of v
                            strataVoiceMix v (tail)                     -> mainBus, the send buses, granBuses[v], voiceTaps[v]
              strataMod v, just after mixGroups[v]
-> strataFilter x3 -> strataDelay -> strataReverb -> strataMaster -> tapGroup (3.3)
```

`\strataVoiceMix`, per block:

```
s = guard(In.ar(synthIn, 2))          o = guard(In.ar(otherIn, 2))     guard = zeroBadValues per channel (§0c.3)
fs = voiceFilter(s, ...)              fo = voiceFilter(o, ...)     one set of lagged cutoff, res, type weights and mix; two filter banks
fs, fo: CheckBadValues on the raw outputs -> SendReply, then guard   (§0c.3)
V  = voiceLevel(amp, modSig, accent)  = (amp x (1 + modSig) x (1 + accent)).clip(0, 2)
other = fo x V                        sig = fs + other
voiceOutput(sig, out, delaySend, reverbSend)   dry, delay send, reverb send exactly as 3.2's (sends and fxMix lagged fxMixLag)
Out.ar(mirrorOut, other)              the mirror: the other path only, filtered and after the level
Out.ar(tapOut, sig)                   3.3's voice tap: the whole output, before the sends
```

- **The mirror** carries today the filtered signal after the level (`voiceFilter`, then the level, then `voiceOutput`'s `sig × mirrorLevel`), from buffer and input grain synths only (`mirrorLevel` 1; the synth source 0). The stage keeps exactly that: `fo × V` is the other path's share, and the synth path never mirrors, so `\strataMod` still follows the voice synth plus the buffer and input grains, never one twice.
- **Two paths** (Question A2) because a synth↔buffer or synth↔input switch keeps both kinds for its 2 s crossfade, and only the other kind takes V and the mirror.
- **Node order:** synth-source grain synths stay at the head of `processGroup` (they read their ring, written in `voiceGroup`); buffer and input grain synths start at the head of their voice's mix group, so the stage, at its tail, reads both paths in the same block; the mix group sits just before the voice's mod synth, so the mirror reaches `\strataMod` in the same block, and the stage and the grains read the other voices' crossmod at the place today's freshly placed mirroring grain synth reads them (one block earlier or later, never both). Ring writers still record only the voice synths (the stage writes `granBuses` later, in `processGroup`).
- **Placement:** the mix area's `start` (order 120, after the voices area's groups) creates the seven mix groups at the tail of `processGroup`, in voice order, and a stage at each group's tail. `startModSynth` (and `onFree`'s restart) adds the mod synth at the tail of `processGroup` as now and moves the voice's mix group just before it in the same bundle; a re-gated mod synth stays where it is, already after its group. A group never ends, so these are plain messages, and a grain synth started into a group (`\addToHead` of `mixGroups[v]`) is never placed next to a synth that may have freed: `startPlacedGrainSynth`'s move before the mod synth and `gateVoice`'s move of a re-gated mod synth after the grain synth both go (the second would now put the mod synth before the stage).

#### 0c.3 Lifecycle

- **Persistent**, like the effects chain and the ring writers: created in the mix area's `start`, freed (stages, then groups) in its `stop`, before the voices area frees `processGroup`. Keys `voiceMixKey(v)` (`\voiceMix0` .. `\voiceMix6`) are `effectKeys`, so messages to them are plain. No voice command creates, frees, releases, cuts, moves or re-gates a stage: a voice has exactly one from start to stop, so replace-never-stack and the fading and cutting sets are unchanged, and none of `sendToSynth`'s cases applies.
- **Values.** The mix area's `grainParamHandlers` take `filterType`, `filterFreq`, `filterRes`, `filterMix`, `delaySend` and `reverbSend`: keep the value in `grainControls[voice]` (as now), set the stage, return true. `setVoiceControl` also sets the stage with every control `\strataVoiceMix` has (`amp`, `modBus1`-`3`, `modAmpAmt1`-`3`), whatever the source, while the voice runs (as it reaches a live grain synth now; a stopped voice's values reach the stage at its next `voiceOn`): only the other path uses them, as only buffer and input grain synths did. `voiceOn` sends the stage its reset `voiceControls` (amp, silent mod buses, 0 amounts) in the bundle that starts or re-gates the grain synth, so the level and the first grain land in one block. The stage starts with its mod buses on `silentBus`, never control bus 0. `retrigger` sends `velocity` and `t_trig` to the voice synth and, while the voice runs, the stage, and `t_trig` alone to the grain synth (one grain in triggered mode), so a release tail is never accented, as now. Lua changes nothing: the same commands, grain param names (the stub reads the handlers' keys) and values.
- **voiceOn and voiceOff** touch the stage only through those values; the release tails of the voice's grain synths go through it, and its filter keeps its state.
- **Source switches** change nothing on the stage: the new grain synth writes its kind's path; the old one fades on its own.
- **Scene recalls** switch sources and send values as before; the stage just follows.
- **The effect bypass:** the stage reads `fxMix` and writes the send buses as every grain synth does now; nothing else changes.
- **Bad values.** Today a grain synth whose filter goes bad (NaN or inf) is gone with the synth; a persistent filter would stay bad for good. So both `In.ar`s go through `zeroBadValues` (per channel), and so do the two filter banks' outputs, so nothing bad ever leaves the stage (the mirror, the tap and the record synths included); a `CheckBadValues` (`post: 0`, as `zeroBadValues` has it, so idle banks log no denormals) on the banks' raw outputs, ahead of that guard, drives a `SendReply` (`'/strataVoiceMixBad'`, the voice) on codes 1 and 2 only (NaN and inf; code 3 is a denormal, which a bank decaying to silence produces and the guard just zeroes, so it never resets a stage), on which sclang replaces the stage in place (`Synth.replace`: one server step, the same mix group and key) from `stageControls` and the voice's kept values, at most one replacement in flight per voice, posting `strata: voice <n> mix stage reset (bad values)`. The check: `"mix: a stage whose filter goes bad is replaced in place, plays again and lets nothing bad out"` (a NaN set on the stage's node directly).
- **CPU.** The stages run while their voices are stopped (§7).

#### 0c.4 Is it the same sound?

Sharing one filter needs every grain synth of the voice to use the same settings at the same moment. Today that does not quite hold: `setSynth` reaches only the live grain synth, so one in a switch's 2 s crossfade, its 50 ms cut or a release keeps the values it had when it stopped being live. Lua sends nothing to a stopped voice (param actions reach the engine only while it plays), so release tails always agree, and a scene recall switches sources at its transition's end, after its continuous keys have arrived. A value change or an accent during a crossfade or a cut is the one case: with the level in the stage, `setVoiceControl` and `retrigger` reach the fading buffer or input tail too. Nothing changes the filter per grain: the variation draws (`grainRate`, `grainDur`, `grainPan`, reverse, intervals) touch rate, length and pan only; the pan wander and `panBalance` act before the filter in both designs; 3.4's cutoff offset is per voice (§0c.6).

**Exactly 3.2's** (to float rounding, 1e-6), measured by the `mix` checks (§0c.5):
- every steady state (each grain synth at E = 1): every source, filter type and mix, cutoff and res, sends and effect mixes, accents, crossmod and amp moves;
- with the voice filter bypassed (mix 0, the default), at every moment when no value changes and no accent arrives during a crossfade or cut: the voice's attack and release, switches with their crossfades and cuts, the slot and ring fades;
- a switch between the synth source and another, on the separate paths, at bypass, and once its fades end.

**Exceptions, and how each is handled:**
1. **A per-synth fade while the voice filter is on** (Question A3). E now acts before the filter: about -54 to -58 dB of the output's peak at the default cutoff and res, -40 to -44 dB at 1 kHz, down to -18 dB at 200 Hz with res 0.9 (the table in §0c.1), only while a fade runs (0.1 s attack, 2 s release or crossfade, 50 ms cut, slot or ring fade). Accepted: it is the price of one filter per voice, the steady sound is the same, and the `mix` check bounds it at the defaults, by the figure 0c's first step measures on grains under 3.13 (§0c.5; about -50 dB is the estimate), and prints it at 200 Hz.
2. **A value change or an accent during a switch's crossfade or cut** (Question A4), filter on or bypassed. The fading buffer or input tail now follows the voice's current filter, sends and level: amp moves, crossmod routing and depth changes and every accent reach it (buffer → buffer, buffer → input, input → buffer, and the old buffer or input tail after a switch to the synth source), where today only the live synth heard them. With a pattern running, every such switch's old tail is accented for its 2 s. Accepted as the intended behavior (one voice, one set of values); the `mix` check prints the difference, and the changelog says so. Release tails are unaffected: nothing reaches the stage of a stopped voice.
3. **Float rounding.** Seven stages add into `mainBus` where up to 21 grain synths did, and a voice's two paths add before the sends: about 1e-7 of the signal.

#### 0c.7 The interface 3.4 consumes

3.4's fast modulation (`docs/design/3.4-design.md` §3) put all five offsets in every grain synth. With the stage, two of them belong to it:
- **`vfilt` (cutoff, channel 3) and `amp` (channel 4) apply in `\strataVoiceMix`**, not in grain synths. 0c builds the stage's graph from an Event of its controls through a neutral hook, `voiceMixMod(controls) -> controls` (`StrataMix.sc`), called before the filter banks and `voiceLevel`, and keeps the stage's start controls in `areaState[\mix][\stageControls][v]` (a Dictionary) so a later area adds a control there in its `allocState`, as 3.4 puts `\fastModIn` into `grainControls`. 3.4 declares `fastModIn` on the stage and fills the hook: `filterFreq' = (filterFreq × 31.62 ^ m3).clip(20, 20000)` before both filter banks, and the level `(amp × (1 + modSig + m4) × (1 + accent)).clip(0, 2)` on the other path and `(1 + m4).clip(0, 2)` on the synth path (3.4 §3.3: amp scales every source, not gated by `modLevel`). `voiceLevel(amp, modSig, accent)` is the one place the level is computed. The hook runs before the stage computes `modSig`, and the synth path takes no level, so the hook alone cannot apply the amp offset (3.4's Question 40, decided 2026-11-09): 3.4's 0c moves the neutral `voiceMixMod` to `StrataModulation.sc`, deleting 3.3's from `StrataMix.sc` in the same commit, and changes the stage's two level lines there, neutrally (`other = fo × voiceLevel(amp, modSig + controls[\ampOffset], accent)` and `sig = fs × voiceLevel(1, controls[\ampOffset], 0) + other`; at an `\ampOffset` of 0 they fold to 3.3's graph).
- **Grain synths read four channels** (pitch, position, size and amp; Decided 2026-11-08): pitch, position and size as 3.4 specifies, and amp read but unused until 3.5, when per-note amp becomes a per-synth gain before the voice filter. 3.4's `fastMod(controls)` no longer touches `filterFreq`; the voice-level amp offset stays in the stage, so 3.3's stage keeps the voice-level gain after its filter. The bus layout (five channels per voice, `fastModGroup` at the head of `context.xg`) is unchanged, and `fastModGroup` runs before every stage.
- 3.4's memory: about 31.5 KB in grain synths (21 × 1.5), about 16 KB in stages (7 × 2.25, the cutoff and amp offsets, so a stage is about 37.25-39.25 KB, 35-37 + 2.25, at worst 0.75 KB under its 40 KB check, which stays; 3.4's 1a reports it if it measures above 40), 21 KB of LFO nodes, 18 KB of scatter and 10 KB for the matrix's offset node: **about 97 KB** with the stage (3.4's §3.6).
- 3.4's design is re-planned from 3.3's measured figures (the implementation plan's Wave 3 opens the issue), which now include this interface.

### 1. Recording

#### 1.1 The settings

New params, in a group `strata_record` **(new group, registered by `lib/record.lua`)** after `strata_buffers`. `record_duration` (s) and `record_level` stay where they are. PSETs store all of them; scenes do not (Question 13).

| Param | Kind, range | Default | Engine argument |
|---|---|---|---|
| `record_source` | option: input, pre-master, post-master, voice 1-7 | input | `source` 0, 1, 2, 3-9 |
| `record_feedback` | control 0-100% | 0% | `feedback` 0-1 |
| `record_loop` | option off, on | off | `loop` 0, 1 |
| `record_threshold` | option off, -48, -42, -36, -30, -24, -18, -12, -6 dB | off | `threshold` (linear; 0 = off) |
| `record_sync` | option off, beat, bar | off | `delay` (s, worked out in Lua) |
| `record_beats` | number 1-64 | 16 | `seconds` (beats × beat length, while sync is on) |

- **The length.** With sync off a recording lasts `record_duration` seconds; with sync on, `record_beats` beats at the tempo when it is sent. Either is capped at `S.performance.buffer_seconds`; BUFFER shows the cap (`16b max`).
- **The kind** follows from the settings: feedback 0 and loop off is a **replace take**; anything else is a **playable recording**.

#### 1.2 Replace takes and playable recordings

| | Replace take | Playable recording |
|---|---|---|
| When | feedback 0, loop off; or any recording into a slot whose content is at another sample rate (Question 3) | feedback > 0, or loop on, into an empty slot or one at the server rate |
| Before it starts | the slot reads 0 frames (its voices fade), is zeroed after the fade (`afterSlotFade`), then a sync; at once, or for a synced take at `delay - (bufferFadeWait + recordPrepMargin)` (Question 4) | a slot that holds audio: nothing. An empty slot: zeroed after the fade, then a sync (an empty slot may still hold a canceled take) |
| While it records | `bufferState` reads 0 s; the voices are silent | the slot keeps playing; at its start (armed → recording) the valid length becomes `max(V, D)` (V the slot's length, D the record length) and is reported at once |
| What it writes | `[0, D)`, once | `[0, D)` once (feedback > 0), or wrapping over `[0, D)` until stopped (loop): each frame becomes `old × keep + new × rec` |
| When it ends | after D, or `stopRecord`: the length is the frames recorded, as in 3.2 | after D (one-shot), or `stopRecord`: the length stays `max(V, D)` |
| Afterward | the peaks file, then the length is reported, then the scratch take (§3) | the peaks file, then the scratch take; `recordState` reports the end |

- The valid length of a slot that holds audio never shrinks during a playable recording (Question 6). So a slot's frames past its valid region stay zero whenever it holds audio: every path that sets a non-zero length zeroes the slot first (load, replace take, playable recording on an empty slot), and a playable recording writes only inside `[0, max(V, D))`. A playable recording that extends a slot (D > V) writes over zeros.
- **Fade before zeroing holds.** The only zeroing is the existing `afterSlotFade` path; a playable recording on a slot with audio zeroes nothing.
- **`slotChanges` holds.** A recording's command calls `slotChanged` as 3.2's does, and every later step (the zeroing, the start, the valid-length update at the start, the end) runs only if the slot's count is unchanged, so a later load, clear or recording always wins.
- **Replace, never stack.** A new recording, load or clear on the slot frees its record synth first (`stopRecording`, at once, as 3.2 does), including one still in its stop fade. Only `stopRecord` and a one-shot's own end fade it (§1.4).
- **Other sample rates** (Question 3). A slot loaded from a file at another rate plays at `bufRateScale`, while the record synth writes at the server rate, so a playable recording there would play the old audio and the new at different pitches. Such a recording is made a replace take instead (the engine decides from `bufferSampleRates[i]`, and BUFFER flashes `buf <n>: replaced (rate)` from Lua's own check of the slot's rate); the take leaves the slot at the server rate.

#### 1.3 Record sources, buses and node order

All record synths go to a **tap group** (`areaState[\record][\tapGroup]`), added at the tail of `context.xg` in the record area's `start` (order 130, after the effects area has added the master), so it runs after everything else in the engine. The record area's `start` also starts one persistent tap synth at its head.

```
context.xg: voiceGroup -> processGroup -> strataFilter x3 -> strataDelay -> strataReverb -> strataMaster
            -> tapGroup (strataTapPreMaster, then the record synths)
```

| Source | `source` | Bus the record synth reads | Written by |
|---|---|---|---|
| input | 0 | `inputBus` | `\strataInput`, in `voiceGroup` (as 3.2) |
| pre-master | 1 | `areaState[\record][\preMasterBus]` (stereo) | `\strataTapPreMaster` **(new)**: `Out.ar(preMasterBus, this.masterSum(filterBus, delayBus, reverbBus, fxMix))`, the master's own sum: after the filter, delay and reverb, before drift, limiter and saturation |
| post-master | 2 | `context.out_b` (hardware 0-1) | `\strataMaster`: what you hear from Strata (Question 25) |
| voice v | 3 + (v - 1) | `voiceTaps[v - 1]` (stereo) | the voice's mix stage (`\strataVoiceMix`, §0c.2): the whole output of all its grain synths (live, releasing, fading and cutting alike, so a switch records seamlessly), after the voice filter and level, before the sends |

- Every reader runs after its writer in the same cycle, so no source is a block late and none reads silence (the stages run in `processGroup`, before the tap group).
- A source outside 0-9 records the input.
- `\strataTapPreMaster` never ends on its own, so its key joins `effectKeys` (plain messages); it is freed in the record area's `stop`. A bypassed delay or reverb leaves its bus unwritten, which `In.ar` reads as silence, exactly as the master does.
- 3.2's record synths ran after `inputSynth` in `voiceGroup`. They now run in the tap group, reading the same samples of `inputBus` in the same cycle, so input takes are unchanged. The ring writers stay at the tail of `voiceGroup`; the record synths write no bus they read.
- Recording a voice, or the output, into a slot that a voice is playing is allowed: the loop goes through the buffer, never through the graph, so it is resampling, not feedback.

#### 1.4 The record synth

`\strataRecord` is rebuilt (same name). Controls: `in`, `bufnumL`, `bufnumR`, `level=1`, `feedback=0`, `threshold=0`, `loop=0`, `frames` (D), `peakFrames` (the valid length the peak bins span), `peakBuf`, `framesOut`, `headOut`, `gate=1`, `replyID` (the slot).

1. **Source.** `In.ar(in, 2) * level`.
2. **Arming.** `armed` latches to 1 at the synth's first block when `threshold` is 0, else at the first block where `Amplitude.kr` of either channel (attack 1 ms, release 50 ms) passes `threshold`. Until then it writes nothing and `headOut` reads -1.
3. **Pre-roll.** With a threshold the written signal is the source through a fixed 10 ms `DelayN` (`recordPrerollSeconds`), so the take starts 10 ms before the crossing. Without one it is the source itself (a `Select`, so threshold-less takes are sample-exact).
4. **The head.** A `Phasor.ar` reset at arming, running over `[0, frames)`: it wraps in loop mode; in a one-shot the synth ends once it has written `frames` (counted by a `Sweep` from arming, reported on `framesOut` as 3.2's is, for `stopRecord`).
5. **The write.** Per channel, `BufRd` reads the old sample at the head and `BufWr` writes `old × keep + new × rec`, where `rec = env` and `keep = 1 - env × (1 - feedback)`, and `env` is a 5 ms ASR on `gate × armed` (`recordFadeSeconds`). So a recording fades in over 5 ms, and fades out over 5 ms when `gate` goes to 0, leaving no step in the audio (Question 10). A one-shot starts its release 5 ms before `frames`, so it ends on its length.
6. **The head bus.** `Out.kr(headOut, [phase, frames])` while armed (-1 and 0 before), two channels for the head guard (§1.5) and nothing else. The engine sets it back to -1 when the synth frees.
7. **Started.** `SendReply.kr` on the arming trigger (`'/strataRecord'`, `replyID`), which sclang turns into `recordState` "recording" and, for a playable recording, the valid-length update.
8. **Peaks.** A running maximum of `|sig|` across both channels, reset at each bin boundary (`floor(phase × waveformPoints / peakFrames)`); as the head leaves a bin it writes `min(1, old peak × keep + new peak)` into `peakBuf` (`BufWr.kr`). sclang reads the 64 values (§3.1).

`stopRecord` sets `gate 0` through `sendToSynth`; the synth frees itself after the fade. The synth stays in `recordSynths[i]` until it frees, and its frame count is read in its `onFree` (the frames bus keeps its last value; `/c_get` after the `/n_end`), not right after a free as 3.2's `stopRecordSlot` does, so the count includes the fade. The take is kept only if `slotChanges[i]` is still the stop's count; a new recording, load or clear meanwhile frees the fading synth at once (`stopRecording`) and the take is dropped. An armed recording that has not started is freed at once and keeps nothing. Every message to a record synth goes through `sendToSynth`, as 3.2's do.

#### 1.5 Grains steer around the record head

While a slot records playably, each `\strataGrainBuf` reading it reads the head from `recHeadBus` (`In.kr`; -1 means no recording, and the guard passes `start` through). For a grain about to start at frame `s` (from `slotGrainPos`), with read rate `r` (frames of buffer per frame, signed), a life of `L` output frames and head `h`, the head would meet the grain during its life when `s` falls in the guard zone:

```
forward (r >= 0):  [h - m - max(r - 1, 0) * L,   h + m + max(1 - r, 0) * L]
reverse (r < 0):   [h - m,                        h + m + (|r| + 1) * L]
```

`L = durFrames / |r|`: the hook's `durFrames` is the frames the grain reads (`grainFrames` in `slotGrainReader`), while the head moves one frame per output frame, so the zones need the grain's life in output frames (`|r|` floored at 0.001, as `readRate` is). `m` is `recordGuardFrames` (5 ms plus one block, for the block-rate head). Distances are taken modulo D for starts inside `[0, D)` (the head wraps there in loop mode); a start past D is never in danger. A start in the zone moves to its lower edge, just behind the head: the newest audio, as a ring grain reads (3.2 §2.3). The moved start is clipped to `slotGrainPos`'s range for its direction, so it never leaves the valid region.

- A grain then reads only freshly written audio or only audio the head has not reached, never the seam between them. The exception is a loop shorter than the guard zone: every start is in the zone, so the seam can't be avoided, and the grains keep sounding inside the valid region.
- Grains that are already playing when a recording starts are not moved; their seam is softened by the 5 ms fade-in at the head.
- In a one-shot, the head stops at D; when it ends the bus goes to -1 and the guard stops.
- **Where it runs.** Inside `slotGrainPos` (`lib/engine/StrataLoop.sc`), through the `slotHeadGuard(frame, lowest, highest, rate, durFrames, recHead)` hook 0a adds: the start is built at audio rate (from `TRand.ar` and the scan's `Phasor.ar`), so the guard runs at audio rate too, and it needs `slotGrainPos`'s own `lowest` and `highest` starts (locals there) to clip the moved start. The head bus is two channels, the head frame and D (`In.kr(recHead, 2)`), so the guard knows where the head wraps. Its block-rate head is `K2A`'d; the margin covers the block.
- Cost: budget about 8 KB in each `\strataGrainBuf` (about 30 audio-rate UGens; §7). The memory area's all-buffer worst case (21 `\strataGrainBuf`) measures it.

#### 1.6 Clock-synced starts and lengths in beats

With `record_sync` on, `record_buffer` (Lua) works out the next quantum q (1 beat, or `RECORD_SYNC.beats_per_bar` beats) at least `RECORD_SYNC.lead_seconds` (0.25 s) ahead:

```
now = clock.get_beats();  lead = lead_seconds / clock.get_beat_sec()
target = ceil((now + lead) / q) * q
delay  = (target - now) * clock.get_beat_sec()
seconds = record_beats * clock.get_beat_sec()   (capped at buffer_seconds)
```

and sends them in one `recordBuffer`. The engine prepares the slot (the fade and zeroing of a replace take or an empty slot) at `delay - (bufferFadeWait + recordPrepMargin)` after the command (`recordPrepMargin` 0.15 s, which covers zeroing a 300 s slot on a Pi 4; at once when that is not positive), through `SystemClock.sched` guarded by `slotChanges`, so the slot goes silent about 0.2 s before the take, not up to a bar before (Question 4). It starts the synth with a bundle timestamped `delay` after the command arrived (`context.server.makeBundle`), so the server starts it on the sample it names. If the preparation outlasts the delay (it should not with a 0.25 s lead), it starts as soon as the preparation is done and posts `strata: buffer <n> recording started <ms> ms late`.

- With a threshold too, the sync sets when arming begins; the level then starts it.
- A tempo change during the delay or the take is not followed: the length was fixed in seconds when it was sent.
- Without sync, `delay` is 0 and nothing changes.

#### 1.7 The `recordState` poll

Not periodic (`addPoll(\recordState, func, false)`), like `bufferLoadFailed`. One message per change of a recording's state:

```
value = ((record_number * 4) + state) * numBuffers + slot_index
state: 0 armed (waiting for its delay or level), 1 recording, 2 ended (completed or stopped: kept), 3 canceled (nothing kept)
```

`record_number` counts the slot's `recordBuffer` commands from 1, in the engine (`areaState[\record][\recordCounts]`) and in Lua (`buffers[slot].records_sent`) alike, so Lua ignores every report but its newest recording's, in whatever order they arrive. It is exact up to 524,288 recordings per slot (float32 integers to 2^24). A recording with neither delay nor threshold reports "recording" only. A load, clear or newer recording that cancels one reports "canceled" for it.

#### 1.8 Lua side (`lib/record.lua`)

- `S.lib.record.state(slot)`: nil when idle, else `{number, kind = "take" | "playable", phase = "armed" | "recording", started_at, seconds, loop}`.
- `record_buffer(slot)` (global, as today): while the slot is armed or recording it sends `stopRecord` (K3 stops or cancels); else it builds the arguments from the params (§1.1, §1.6) and sends `recordBuffer`.
- **Replace takes** keep 3.2's settling (`finish_buffer_take`, `settle_buffer_slot`, the 5 s fallback), except that the take timer starts at the "recording" report instead of at the send, so a delayed or level-armed take times right. Before that report the slot shows `ARM`.
- **Playable recordings** never go through the take settling: the slot stays loaded and shows its length (reported at the start) and `DUB` (one-shot) or `LOOP`. At "ended" it becomes `take <n>`, with the scratch take as its path (its content is no longer the file it was loaded from); at "canceled" it keeps what it was. A canceled armed **replace take** leaves the slot empty: its preparation already emptied and zeroed it.
- The header's indicator reads `REC` while any slot records and `ARM` while any is armed and none records (§8.2).

### 2. Files

#### 2.1 A random file, and filling empty slots

`lib/files.lua` **(new)**:
- **The folder** for a slot (`S.lib.files.folder_for(slot)`): the folder of its current path, else the folder of the last file chosen in BUFFER's file selector (`S.lib.files.last_folder`, remembered for the session), else `_path.audio` (Question 22).
- **The files**: `util.scandir(folder)` (not recursive), keeping names ending in `.wav`, `.aif`, `.aiff`, `.flac` or `.ogg` (case-insensitive), skipping folders (`ls -p` marks them with `/`) and anything starting with `.`.
- `S.lib.files.random_into(slot)`: a random file other than the slot's current one (when there are two or more), through `load_buffer(slot, path)`. BUFFER flashes `buf <n>: <name>`; an empty folder flashes `no files in <folder>`.
- `S.lib.files.fill_empty()`: every empty slot (not loaded, not recording, not armed) gets a distinct random file from the cursor slot's folder, until the files run out. One flash: `filled <k> slots`.
- Random loads start at 0 (§2.2).

#### 2.2 The start point of a long file

- `engine.loadBuffer(slot, path, start)`: `start` is seconds into the file at its own rate. The engine clips it to the file, seeks there (`SoundFile.seek`) for the decode check, and reads from that frame (`readChannel(path, startFrame, frames, ...)`), at most `bufferSeconds` from it.
- Each slot keeps its start (`buffers[slot].start`, seconds, 0 by default), sent with every load of the slot, restored with its path, and set back to 0 by a load of another file, a recording or a clear. `S.lib.buffers.set_start` does nothing while the slot records.
- **BUFFER's `start` field** shows the cursor slot's start (`12.0s`), or `-` when its file fits the slot (`audio.file_info` says how long it is). E3 moves it in steps of a tenth of a second (ALT+E3 a hundredth) between 0 and the file's length less the slot's, and reloads the file from the new start once E3 has rested for `FILES.restart_seconds` (0.3 s), so turning through a long file costs one load, not one per detent. The reload fades the slot's voices as any load does.

#### 2.3 Trim: save the loop region

- `engine.saveBuffer(slot, path, start, length)`: `start` and `length` are fractions of the slot's valid region (0 and 1 save it all, as 3.2 does). `writeSlot` writes frames `[start × V, (start + length) × V)` (`Buffer.write`'s `startFrame` and `numFrames`), under the same `.part`, generation and short-write rules. A region of less than one frame saves nothing and posts why.
- `save_buffer(slot)` keeps saving the whole slot. `save_buffer_loop(slot)` **(new, global)** saves the loop region of the selected voice if it reads the slot, else of the lowest-numbered voice that reads it, else the whole slot, to `_path.audio .. "strata/<YYYYMMDD-HHMMSS>-buf<n>-loop.wav"`. BUFFER flashes `saved buf <n> loop`.
- Trim leaves the slot as it is (Question 21).

#### 2.4 `buffers.data`

`BUFFER_DATA_VERSION` 2: `{version = 2, paths = {...}, starts = {...}}` (starts in seconds, per slot). A version 1 file loads with every start 0. `load_buffers_from_disk` passes each slot's start to `load_buffer`.

### 3. Waveforms

#### 3.1 Computing the peaks

The engine keeps each slot's overview, `waveformPoints` (64) values of the largest absolute sample across both channels per bin, each 0-1 (clipped), in sclang (`areaState[\waveform][\peaks][slot]`, a `FloatArray`) and in the slot's 64-frame peak buffer on the server.

| What changed the slot | How the peaks are made |
|---|---|
| a load | `fileDecodes` (already reading every frame for the decode check) reads **bin-aligned chunks**: each chunk is at most `loadCheckFrames` and ends at the next bin boundary, so no chunk spans two bins, and each goes into a fresh array (`freshReadArray`, #131). A bin's peak is the largest `chunk.peak` over its chunks: `Signal`'s `peak`, one C primitive per chunk, with no per-sample `abs` and no slices (each of which would allocate another array). Never `maxItem` and `minItem`: they are `Collection` methods that run a sclang function per sample (#131; a 300 s load's chunks took 3.56 s with them against 0.023 s with `peak` on 3.13). That adds at most 64 short reads to the check, a few milliseconds to a 16 s file, and stays within the long check's yields. After the read completes, `b_setn` puts the peaks in the peak buffer. |
| a replace take | the record synth's bins (§1.4) over D. A take stopped early at F frames uses its first `ceil(64 × F / D)` bins, spread over 64 (each new bin the largest of the bins it covers). |
| a playable recording | the record synth updates the peak buffer as it goes, starting from the slot's peaks (`peakFrames` = the valid length at its start). sclang reads them (`/b_getn`, 64 floats) every `peakRefreshSeconds` (0.5 s) while it records, and once more at its end. |
| a clear | all zeros; no file is written (Lua draws nothing for an empty slot). |
| a failed load | unchanged. |

**Allocation is the risk here, not time.** #131 showed that sclang 3.13 (norns' version) aborts in its garbage collector under array churn in exactly these long-file paths, and fresh arrays only made it rare (1 crash in 8 runs). So the peak scan must allocate nothing beyond the decode check's own chunk arrays: no `abs` copies, no `copyRange` slices, no per-bin arrays, and its 64 results in one `FloatArray` made once per load. The `waveform` check runs a 300 s load and a 300 s save back to back several times on CI's 3.13 and fails on any sclang abort.

#### 3.2 The peaks file

`<settings folder>/buf<n>.peaks`: the folder `allocSettingsDirectory` names, the one this engine used at alloc (`settingsDir` in the engine check, else `~/dust/data/strata/`, which is Lua's `S.lib.scenes.scene_data_dir`), so the engine check's settings folder keeps tests out of `~/dust` even after the build restores `settingsDir` (Question 14). Text, one key per line:

```
strata-peaks 1
slot 3
frames 768000
rate 48000
generation 41
peaks 0.012 0.250 0.731 ... (64 values, three decimals)
```

- `rate` is the slot's own (`bufferSampleRates[index]`), so `frames / rate` is the length the slot reports, for a file at another sample rate too.
- `generation` is an engine-wide count of peaks files written, so Lua skips a file it has already read.
- **Written before the length is reported.** `writeSlotPeaks(index, frames, action)` writes `<path>.<instanceTag>-<generation>.part`, renames it with `mv -f` through `unixCmd` (asynchronous, so sclang never waits on the subprocess), and runs `action` (the `bufferChanged` that reports the length) once the rename ends, only if `slotChanges` is unchanged. So every `bufferState` change that announces audio finds its peaks file in place. The periodic refresh of a playable recording writes the same way, with no report.
- A failed write posts `strata: cannot write <path>` and still reports the length (the waveform is a view, never a reason to hold up audio).
- `deleteStaleParts` already covers the settings folder (`stalePartDirs`).

#### 3.3 Reading them in Lua (`lib/waveforms.lua` **(new)**)

- **When.** On a `bufferState` report whose seconds changed to a non-zero value (`lib/buffers.lua` calls `S.lib.waveforms.stale(slot)`), and every `WAVEFORMS.refresh_seconds` (0.5 s) while the slot records playably.
- **How.** Never in a draw or a poll callback: `stale` marks the slot, and one `clock.run` loop (started by a start step, canceled by a cleanup step) reads at most one marked slot per tick of `1 / FRAMES_PER_SECOND`. A read is `io.open`, a few hundred bytes and a parse.
- **Valid only if** the header says version 1 and slot n, there are 64 values in 0-1, and `frames / rate` is within 0.01 s of the slot's reported seconds. Otherwise the slot has no waveform (BUFFER draws a flat line) until the next report.
- `S.lib.waveforms.peaks(slot)` → the 64 values or nil; `S.lib.waveforms.bins(slot, n)` → n values, each the largest of the points it covers (the grid's 16 or 8 columns).
- A redraw is requested (and the grid redrawn while a grid shows page 5) when a read changes the values.

### 4. Playheads

`lib/playheads.lua` **(new)** estimates, for every running buffer voice, where its grains start, as a fraction of the slot's valid region:

```
phase(t) = phase(t0) + (scan_effective) * (t - t0)        (wrapped 0-1)
playhead = loop_start + ((buf_pos + phase) wrap 0..1) * loop_length
scan_effective = engine_param_value("v<i>_buf_scan") * (1 - frozen) * (1 - scan_held) + scrub_rate
```

- `buf_pos` is `engine_param_value("v<i>_buf_pos")` (so LFO offsets count), the loop is the voice's loop params, `frozen` its 3.2 freeze, `scan_held` and `scrub_rate` what `lib/positions.lua` last sent (§5).
- It integrates on a 15 Hz clock (`PLAYHEADS.update_hz`), started by a start step and canceled by a cleanup step, so a scan that an LFO moves is followed within a tick.
- **Resets** set the phase to 0: a grain restart (`S.register_grain_restart`: a `voiceOn`, a source switch) and a jump (§5.2). The engine restarts the scan at exactly those moments (§5.1, Question 24), so the estimate starts where the engine does. Message latency (a few ms) is the only error between resets.
- `S.lib.playheads.position(voice_idx)` → fraction or nil (not a running buffer voice); `S.lib.playheads.spread(voice_idx)` → the width grains are spread over (`posSpread × loop_length`); `S.lib.playheads.record_head(slot)` → the record head's fraction of the valid region, from the "recording" report's arrival time, D and the valid length (wrapping in loop mode), or nil.
- `S.lib.playheads.column(voice_idx, columns)` → the column (1..columns) the playhead is in, for the grid. The clock redraws the grid only when some grid shows page 5 and a playhead's or record head's column changed, and marks BUFFER dirty through its `animates()` while BUFFER shows a slot a running voice reads or that records.

### 5. Positions

#### 5.1 Engine

Three grain controls, read in `slotScanPhase` (StrataPositions.sc) for `\strataGrainBuf`:

| Control | Meaning | Sent by |
|---|---|---|
| `t_scanReset` | a trigger: the scan phase restarts at 0 | Lua's jumps (`setGrainParam(v, "t_scanReset", 1)`), and the engine itself at every re-gate (below) |
| `scrubRate` | added to the scan rate (fractions of the loop per second) | Lua's arc glide (§5.5) |
| `scanHold` | 1 stops the voice's own scan (not the glide) | Lua's ALT hold and the grid's hold keys |

```
scan rate = this.scanRate(bufScan, freeze) * (1 - scanHold) + scrubRate
phase     = Phasor.ar(t_scanReset, scan rate * SampleDur.ir, 0, 1)
```

- `t_scanReset` has a grain-param handler (`grainParamHandlers`) that sends it and stores nothing, so a replacement grain synth never starts with a stale trigger. `scrubRate` and `scanHold` are plain grain controls, kept in `grainControls`, so a switch mid-glide keeps gliding.
- **Every grain start restarts the scan.** A new grain synth's `Phasor` starts at 0 already. `gateVoice` (`lib/engine/StrataLifecycle.sc`), which re-gates a releasing grain synth instead of starting one, now sends `t_scanReset 1` with the re-gate, so a voice restarted during its release starts its scan from `buf_pos` too (Question 24).
- These are **transient performance state**, the one exception to "the `voices` table is the only source of values": they are not params, scenes and PSETs never hold them, and `lib/positions.lua` sends `scrubRate 0` and `scanHold 0` in each voice's sync (its registered record's `sync`), so a started voice never inherits a glide.

#### 5.2 Jumps and two-key loops (`lib/positions.lua` **(new)**)

Positions on the grid are fractions of the slot's valid region (absolute), so a row always shows the whole slot.
- `S.lib.positions.jump(voice_idx, p)`: if `p` is inside the voice's loop (half-open with a 1e-6 tolerance, `loop_start - 1e-6 <= p < loop_start + loop_length - 1e-6`, since the params return values a rounding away from a cell's fraction), `buf_pos` becomes `(p - loop_start) / loop_length`; otherwise the loop becomes the whole slot (`loop_start` 0, `loop_length` 1) and `buf_pos` becomes `p` (Question 17). Then `t_scanReset`, and the playhead resets. Everything but the reset goes through `params:set`, so scenes, LFOs and the screen follow.
- `S.lib.positions.set_loop(voice_idx, a, b)` (a ≤ b, fractions): `loop_start` a, then `loop_length` b - a (the order 3.2's clamps need), then a jump to a.
- **On a position row of n cells** (16, or 8 on the 64's short rows): a press on cell c jumps to `(c - 1) / n`. While one key of the row is held, a press on another sets the loop from the lower cell's start to the higher cell's end, `[(lo - 1) / n, hi / n]`. Releases do nothing but end the hold.
- Only buffer voices respond; a key on another voice's row does nothing. A stopped voice's position still changes (it applies when it starts).

#### 5.3 Bookmarks

- **Params.** `v<i>_bookmark_1` .. `v<i>_bookmark_4` (`NUM_BOOKMARKS`), controls 0-1, defaults 0, 0.25, 0.5, 0.75: positions in the slot (Question 12). One registered voice-param record (`bookmarks`, count 4, `fader = false`), so they are in PSETs and scenes, where they **crossfade** like any continuous registered key (Question 2: the registry's discrete keys must be whole numbers in a whole `{min, max}`, and `validate_scene` rejects a non-integer discrete extra, so 0.25-0.75 cannot be discrete; a bookmark changes nothing until recalled), never randomized or mutated, and shown on VOICE in a `bookmarks` section for buffer voices (`voice_rows`, `when` = uses a buffer), where E3 edits them like any row.
- `S.lib.positions.recall_bookmark(voice_idx, n)`: `jump(voice_idx, bookmark n)`, and the voice's last recalled bookmark becomes n (`S.lib.positions.last_recalled(voice_idx)`, for LEDs and the screen).
- `S.lib.positions.store_bookmark(voice_idx, n)`: bookmark n becomes the voice's playhead (`S.lib.playheads.position`), or its `buf_pos` mapped into the loop when it is not running.
- Recalled from the grid (§9), from BUFFER's `bm` field (§8.1) and, in 3.4, from pattern steps.

#### 5.4 The interface 3.4 consumes

3.4's step locks call exactly two functions, on a thin module `lib/bookmarks.lua` **(new, 1f's)**, `S.lib.bookmarks`, built on `S.lib.positions`:
- `S.lib.bookmarks.recall(voice_idx, n)`: `S.lib.positions.recall_bookmark(voice_idx, n)`. A no-op when `is_set` is false.
- `S.lib.bookmarks.is_set(voice_idx, n)`: true when `voice_idx` is 1..`NUM_VOICES`, `n` is a whole number 1..`NUM_BOOKMARKS`, and the voice reads a slot that holds audio (`voice_buffer_slot(voice_idx)` and its reported seconds > 0).

Neither ever creates engine nodes (a lock fires from a pattern step, which must never create nodes); `recall` sends only `params:set`s and one `setGrainParam`. 3.3's tests pin both names and their behavior, including `is_set` false for a synth or input voice, an empty slot, n 0 or 5 and a fractional n. Besides them, 3.4's beat-quantized actions may call `S.lib.positions.jump(voice_idx, p)` and `S.lib.positions.set_loop(voice_idx, a, b)`, and may read `S.constants.NUM_BOOKMARKS` and the params `v<i>_bookmark_<n>`.

#### 5.5 Arc scrub and friction

- **Scrub.** BUFFER's ring 1 moves the selected voice's `buf_pos` through `arc_sweep_param`, as 3.2's ring 1 does.
- **Friction** (`scrub_friction`, 0-100%, default 100%, in a group `strata_positions` **(new)**): at 100% the ring is direct, as now. Below it, ring 1 releasing (no tick for `SCRUB.release_seconds`, 0.08 s) starts a glide at the ring's speed over its last 0.1 s, in loop fractions per second: Lua sends it as `scrubRate` and decays it every 1/15 s by `exp(-dt / tau)`, with `tau = SCRUB.max_glide_seconds × (1 - friction)` (4 s at 0%), sending 0 once it is below 0.001. A new turn of ring 1, a jump or a source switch ends the glide at once.
- **ALT stops the scan.** The first ALT+ring 1 tick on BUFFER sends `scanHold 1` for the selected voice; K1's release sends `scanHold 0` (Question 19). A glide may still run meanwhile (fling with the tape held).
- The scrub works on the selected voice whatever slot BUFFER's cursor shows.

### 6. Commands and polls (AGENTS.md)

Three changed signatures, `stopRecord` extended, no new command, one new poll. Rows as they go into AGENTS.md's table:

```
| `engine.loadBuffer(slot, path, start)` | `"isf"` | Read a file into slot 0-7 from `start` seconds into it (at the file's rate; clipped to the file; 0 = the beginning): at most `bufferSeconds` (16 s standard, up to 300 s high) at the server rate (a 96 kHz file keeps half as many seconds on a 48 kHz server). Voices reading the slot fade first. A file longer than 16 s at the server rate is checked in a routine that yields between chunks: it takes effect only after the check, and is dropped silently if a record, clear or later load that took effect arrived meanwhile. A missing, unreadable, empty or damaged file (checked by decoding it) leaves the slot unchanged but cancels a recording in it; a server read that fails anyway (the file vanished) leaves it empty. Both are reported by the `bufferLoadFailed` poll. The slot's peaks file is written before its length is reported |
| `engine.recordBuffer(slot, seconds, level, feedback, source, threshold, loop, delay)` | `"ifffifif"` | Record `source` (0 input, 1 pre-master, 2 post-master, 3-9 voice 1-7; others input) × `level` into slot 0-7, from frame 0, for `seconds` (at most `bufferSeconds`). `feedback` 0 with `loop` 0 is a replace take: the slot fades, empties and reads 0 s until it ends. Otherwise the slot keeps playing (grains avoid the head), its length becomes the longer of its own and `seconds` when recording starts, and each frame keeps the old audio × `feedback`; `loop` 1 wraps over the first `seconds` until `stopRecord`. `threshold` > 0 waits for the source's level (linear), with a 10 ms pre-roll. `delay` (s) starts it that long after the command (clock sync). States on `recordState`. At its end writes the peaks file and the scratch take `buf<n>.wav` |
| `engine.stopRecord(slot)` | `"i"` | End a recording now, with its 5 ms fade, and keep what was captured; an armed recording that has not started is canceled (keeps nothing). No-op if the slot is not recording or armed |
| `engine.saveBuffer(slot, path, start, length)` | `"isff"` | Write `[start, start + length)` of the slot's valid region (fractions; 0, 1 = all of it) as a stereo 16-bit WAV (creates the directory). Samples beyond ±1 clip symmetrically |
```

`setGrainParam`'s row adds `t_scanReset` (a jump's scan restart, not stored), `scrubRate` and `scanHold` (transient, §5.1). `clearBuffer` is unchanged (it also cancels an armed recording).

**Poll `bufferState`**: unchanged encoding; its text changes to "A slot recording a replace take reads 0 s; one recording playably reads its valid length".

**Poll `recordState`** (new) text for AGENTS.md: "(engine → Lua, not periodic): one message per change of a recording's state, as `((record_number * 4) + state) * 8 + slot_index` (`NUM_BUFFERS` = 8, 0-based slot; state 0 armed, 1 recording, 2 ended and kept, 3 canceled; `record_number` counts the slot's `recordBuffer` commands from 1, in the engine and in `record_buffer` alike). Lua acts only on its newest recording's reports, in whatever order they arrive."

The engine stub reads the new signatures and the poll from the source, the sync check finds every call, and the table check one row per command, as today.

## Cost if wrong

- **The high floor (Question 1).** If 4096 KB must stay, 3.3 and 3.4 together need about 230-370 KB back on high. The levers, largest first: the lowpass-only voice filter on high (about 88 KB at 21 synths), sharing the guard's arithmetic with `slotGrainPos`'s clips (perhaps half its 8 KB per buffer synth: up to about 84 KB at the all-buffer case), dropping the pre-roll (4 KB per recording), and a cheaper 3.4. Even all of them leave the all-buffer case short, which is why the recommendation lowers the floor. If 3.5 later needs the room back, it is in the grain graph, where every KB counts 21 times per note.
- **Playable recordings (Question 5).** If performers want overdub to silence the slot instead, the kind rule changes in one place (`recordSlot`'s branch), and the guard goes unused.
- **From frame 0 (Question 6).** Recording into a voice's loop needs a start frame argument (a ninth `recordBuffer` argument) and the guard's modulo over the region; nothing else changes.
- **The delay for sync (Question 8).** If timed bundles drift against Link, Lua can send at `clock.sync` with `delay` 0 and accept the preparation's lateness on replace takes.
- **5 ms fades (Question 10).** If a replace take must be bit-identical to 3.2's, the fade becomes a playable-only feature (`recordFadeSeconds` 0 for takes).
- **Positions-only bookmarks (Question 12).** If 3.4 finds it needs loops in bookmarks, the record adds `bookmark_<n>_start`/`_length` params (hidden) and `recall_bookmark` sets them; the interface keeps its names.
- **Bookmarks crossfade (Question 2).** If a step lock recalling a bookmark mid-transition should land on the old or new value, not between, 0b adds the `held` set (non-integer keys switched at a transition's end) and the bookmark record lists them; nothing else changes. If a recall replacing them at all surprises, drop them from the scene defaults (they stay in PSETs).
- **Other-rate slots become replace takes (Question 3).** If overdubbing a 44.1 kHz file in place matters, a later release can resample the slot to the server rate on load (a pitch-exact `b_gen` copy is not available; it would be an sclang pass, with #131's allocation risk).
- **Prepare late for synced takes (Question 4).** If `recordPrepMargin` proves short on a busy Pi (the zeroing outlasting it), the take starts late and posts so; the margin is one constant.
- **Peaks file location (Question 14).** One method (`peaksPath`) and one Lua function.
- **Grid page access (Questions 15-16).** Each layout's file alone; the 128v's columns can become one voice at a time if the 16-row columns prove hard to read.
- **ALT scan hold (Question 19).** If it should engage on K1 alone, `hold_scan` moves to BUFFER's ALT press.
- **Holds on release (Question 20).** If acting on release feels slow for clear and save, the holds move to grid-only or to fields with K3, and clear and save go back to presses.
- **Re-gated scan restart (Question 24).** If carrying on mid-scan was wanted, the engine stops sending the reset and Lua's estimate restarts from a stored phase instead (a re-gate keeps it).
- **The mix stage (amendment).** If a per-synth fade through the filter (Question A3) is heard (an attack or a cut at a low, resonant cutoff), the fix is local to `\strataVoiceMix`: give it the voice's ASR (the grain synth's own attack and release would then only free it), which leaves the switch crossfade and the 50 ms fades. If the second path proves not worth 7 KB a voice (Question A2), dropping it costs exactness in synth-to-other crossfades only. If the stages' idle CPU matters on a CM3, a stage whose voice holds no grain synth is paused (`/n_run 0` on the stage, in the bundle after its last grain synth frees, `/n_run 1` in the bundle that starts one); a paused filter keeps a decayed state. If 3.5 wants a filter per note after all, it is a third path per note, not a return to filters in grain synths.

## Calls made in this design

- (Amendment, 2026-11-08) One persistent `\strataVoiceMix` per voice, in a mix group just before its mod synth, with a synth path and an other path: the voice filter, the voice-level gain (amp, amplitude crossmod, accent), the sends, the mirror (other path only) and the voice tap move out of every grain synth; each grain synth keeps its grains, rate, pan and own fades and writes one bus. Package 0c, after 0a. Exactly 3.2's sound in every steady state and at bypass; per-synth fades with the filter on, and a value change or accent during a crossfade or cut (which now reaches the fading tail), are the accepted exceptions; a stage that goes bad is replaced in place.

- Three changed commands (`loadBuffer` "isf", `recordBuffer` "ifffifif", `saveBuffer` "isff"), `stopRecord` extended to armed recordings, no new command, one new poll (`recordState`).
- Replace takes keep 3.2's fade-zero-silent path; every other recording is playable, writes in place from frame 0 and never shrinks a slot.
- An empty slot is zeroed before a playable recording (it may hold a canceled take); a slot with audio is never zeroed by one.
- The valid length of a playable recording changes at its start (the arming), not at the command, so a canceled armed recording changes nothing.
- One record SynthDef for every kind, `Phasor`/`BufRd`/`BufWr` (not `RecordBuf`), with 5 ms ASR fades on record and keep levels, a 10 ms pre-roll only when arming by level, and a head bus.
- All record synths in a tap group at the tail of `context.xg`; the pre-master tap is a persistent synth calling `masterSum`; post-master reads norns' output bus; a voice tap is an `Out` in the voice's mix stage (§0c).
- Grains avoid the record head by moving their start just behind it; grains already playing are not moved.
- Clock sync through a `delay` argument and a timed bundle, with a 0.25 s lead; a bar is four beats; beats become seconds at the send.
- Record settings are global params in their own group, in PSETs, not scenes.
- Peaks: 64 points, the largest absolute sample across both channels, from the decode check for loads and from the record synth (a 64-frame server buffer) for recordings; a text file in Strata's data folder, written through a `.part` before the length is reported, refreshed every 0.5 s during a playable recording; read in Lua by one clock loop, never in a draw.
- Playheads integrated in Lua at 15 Hz from `engine_param_value`; the engine restarts the scan at every grain start, including re-gates.
- Jumps, scrubbing and holds through three grain controls (`t_scanReset` with a handler that stores nothing, `scrubRate`, `scanHold`), as transient state outside the `voices` table.
- Grid position rows show the whole slot; two keys set a loop; a press outside the loop resets it.
- Bookmarks: four positions per voice, voice params, crossfading scene fields (the registry's discrete keys are whole numbers), VOICE rows, defaults at the quarters; the 3.4 interface is `S.lib.bookmarks.recall` and `is_set` (§5.4).
- A playable recording into a slot at another sample rate becomes a replace take; a synced replace take prepares its slot `bufferFadeWait + recordPrepMargin` before it starts; a stopped recording's count is read in its `onFree`.
- The head guard lives inside `slotGrainPos`, at audio rate, with a two-channel head bus (head, D); budget 8 KB per buffer grain synth; the memory check adds the all-buffer worst case.
