## Must not break

- **The freeze is `\strataDelay`'s `frozen`** (3.8 design §7.3), never a feedback of 1 through `setDelay`: `setDelay`'s feedback stays clipped to 0-0.95 (its literal, the stub's limit and Lua's param), and only `frozen` takes the loop's gain to 1.
- **The loop's gain reaches 1 exactly**: `hold` is `Slew.kr(frozen.clip(0, 1), delayFreezeRate, delayFreezeRate)`, a linear ramp over `delayFreezeSeconds` (20 ms) that lands on its target, and the gain is `feedback.clip(0, 0.95).max(hold)`. A one-pole lag only approaches 1, and a loop a little under 1 fades: the 3.8 facts' prototype lost 0.2-0.4 dB over 9 s with one.
- **The input closes with the same ramp** (`In.ar(in, 2) * liftFade(fadeIn) * (1 - hold)`): nothing enters a frozen loop.
- **The time is held before its lag and rounded to whole samples while frozen**: `Gate.kr(time.clip(0.01, 5.4), 1 - isFrozen).lag(0.1)`, then `(held * SampleRate.ir).round / SampleRate.ir` while frozen. The gate holds what `setDelay` sends meanwhile (a tempo move under sync, a time change), so the loop keeps its length until the release, which glides to the new time as any time change does. The rounding keeps the loop bright: `BufDelayC`'s cubic interpolation at a fractional delay is a gentle lowpass, and a loop that passes it at every repeat lost 10.7-11 dB of its 6-24 kHz band by 9 s at 0.30928 s (the facts). The loop's period is then the rounded time plus the feedback's one block, both whole samples. The clip stays a literal on the control (`time.clip(0.01, 5.4)`): the Lua stub reads `setDelay`'s time limit there, and Lua's `DELAY.max_seconds` must equal it (a test).
- **Ping-pong is snapped while frozen**: the cross-feed moves with `hold` from the lagged `pingPong` to `pingPong >= delayPingPongSnap` (0.5), lagged as `pingPong` is, so it is 0 or 1 in a frozen loop. A cross of 0.5 averages the two lines at every pass and the loop loses its width (the facts' side read -999 dB); at 0 or 1 the loop is a permutation of the two lines and keeps both.
- **Unfrozen and uncleared the graph plays what it played before, bit for bit**: `hold` 0 makes every new product a multiply by 1 or an add of 0, the clear's gains are exactly 1, and the gate passes the time (the effects area's `checkLineAsDelayC` still reads a largest difference of 0 against 3.7's `DelayC` copy).
- **Clear mutes, it never zeroes the line** (design §7.2): `t_clear` opens `Trig1.kr(t_clear, delayTime + delayClearMargin)`, which closes the feedback at once (one control block) and the return over `delayClearFade` (3 ms). When the window ends, everything the line held before the clear has been read and not fed back, so the line holds only what came in since (silence with the input stopped or a frozen, closed loop); the return's fade out sits inside the 5 ms margin. Both open again over `delayClearOpenSeconds` (20 ms), a raised cosine of a linear `Slew` (`delayClearGain`): a gain stepped back up in one block wrote a step into the line, heard one delay time later (#308's kind of click), and stepped the return itself (the check read sample steps of 0.0070 and 0.0041 against the steady signal's 0.0032; with the fade 0.0019 and 0.0027). The gain is exactly 1 while nothing is cleared (`Slew` starts at its input; `cos(pi)` is -1 in single precision). The buffers are zeroed only by a bypass's free (`emptyDelayLine`).
- **A time lengthened inside a clear's window can bring back audio from before the clear**: the window's length is the delay time when it opens (plus the margin), and a longer time set inside it reads further back into the line, past what the window muted, once it ends.
- **`frozen` is a kept control, `t_clear` is not**: `setDelayFreeze` keeps it (`keepEffectControls`) and `startDelay` passes it, but **a freed delay is never frozen** (#889): the bypass's free sets the kept value to 0 with the line it empties (`emptyDelayLine`), and a freeze sent while no node runs keeps 0, so a lift that re-creates the node starts it unfrozen at its kept time. A node born frozen would close its input on an empty line and hold a time of 0 (`Gate.kr` starts closed at 0). Lua's bypass also releases a freeze before it bypasses and refuses one while bypassed (`docs/notes/lua/fx-hold.md`): Freeze by bypass, below, says which side guards what. `clearDelay` sends nothing while the delay is bypassed (`effectLive`: in the fade too) and is never kept.
- **Every `Synth(\strataDelay` passes `bufL` and `bufR`** (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); this area's own nodes start in one place, `startCheckDelay`, on lines of their own.
- The new controls are `arg`s with literal clips (`frozen.clip(0, 1)`), where the Lua stub reads them (the plan's ruling 24); the constants are named once (`delayFreezeSeconds`, `delayFreezeRate`, `delayPingPongSnap`, `delayClearMargin`, `delayClearFade`, `delayClearOpenSeconds`).

## The freeze and the clear (`StrataDelay.sc`)

`\strataDelay` (`in, out, bufL, bufR, time=0.375, feedback=0.4, mix=0, pingPong=0, fadeIn=0, frozen=0, t_clear=0, shiftL=0, shiftR=0, shimmer=0, shimmerRatio=2`) is the effects area's node (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); this file covers 3.8's two additions. The delay has no engine area of its own: `addDelayCommands` (called from `addEffectCommands`) adds the two commands beside `setDelay`.

- `setDelayFreeze(frozen)`, `"i"`: 0 releases, 1 freezes; any other value (2, -1, a float) is ignored with one console line (`fastModIndicesValid`, which posts through the modulation area's `postFastModIgnored`: `strata: setDelayFreeze ignored: frozen 2 is not a whole number from 0 to 1`). It keeps the value and sets the node; with no node (the bypass has freed it) it keeps 0 and sets nothing, so a lift never starts a frozen node (#889; a freeze set in the bypass's fade is set on the fading node and kept, and dropped at its free: Freeze by bypass, below).
- `clearDelay()`, `""`: no argument (norns takes an empty format, as `Engine_PolySub.sc` does). Sets `t_clear` 1 on the running node; nothing while bypassed.
- Released, the loop decays at the feedback the node holds (`setDelay`'s, kept through the freeze): 0.4 is about -8 dB a pass, under -40 dB 2 s after the release at 0.375 s.
- Frozen and cleared, the loop empties and stays frozen on silence (the input is closed), until a release opens the input again. That is the engine's answer to the pair; Lua no longer sends it: a clear on a frozen delay releases the freeze first, `setDelayFreeze(0)` then `clearDelay()` (#912; Freeze by bypass, below, row 7).
- The node took 16.9 KB of the pool on 3.14.1 and on 3.13 with the freeze and the clear (the memory area's twenty-node probe; 7.5 KB before them, 14.3 KB before the clear's fade back in), and 24.4 KB with 3.8's shimmer (its feedback blend: `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`), under its 30 KB limit (`delayDefLimitKB`, 20 before shimmer).

**The node's states.** They live in the graph (`hold`, the `Slew` of `frozen`; the clear's `Trig1` window; the `Gate` on the time), and sclang keeps only the `frozen` it last sent (`keepEffectControls`), so the engine reports none of them. A running node is unfrozen, frozen (with `hold` ramping either way over 20 ms) or in a clear's window, a frozen one too:

| State | Event | Next | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| unfrozen | `setDelayFreeze(1)` | frozen | over 20 ms: the loop's gain to 1, the input closed, the time held (rounded to whole samples) and ping-pong snapped |
| frozen | `setDelayFreeze(0)` | unfrozen | over 20 ms: the input open again, the time gliding to what `setDelay` last sent (0.1 s), the loop decaying at the kept feedback |
| unfrozen | `setDelay` | unfrozen | the time glides over 0.1 s, the rest lagged as before |
| frozen | `setDelay` | frozen | kept and set; the gate holds the time until the release |
| unfrozen | `clearDelay` | clearing | the feedback closed in one block and the return over 3 ms, for the delay time and 5 ms |
| clearing | the window's end | unfrozen | both open again over 20 ms; the line holds only what came in since |
| clearing | `setDelay` with a longer time | clearing | once the window ends, the longer time reads audio from before the clear |
| frozen | `clearDelay` | frozen, on silence | the loop empties and the closed input keeps it empty until a release (Lua releases first, #912: row 7 below) |
| bypassed (fading or freed) | `clearDelay` | as it was | nothing sent (`effectLive`) |
| any | a bypass, a lift | Freeze by bypass, below | |

**What drives its rows** (`test/engine_check/delay.scd`, on nodes of the check's own but for the commands): the freeze, `"delay: a loop frozen at 1 s (%) keeps its level and its 6-24 kHz band within 0.5 dB from 2 to 9 s ..."`, `"delay: frozen at ping-pong 0.5 (snapped to 1), % keeps its width ..."`, `"delay: the input is closed while frozen ..."` and, on the engine's node, `"delay: setDelayFreeze sets the node's frozen and keeps it ..."`; the release, `"delay: a release decays at the kept feedback 0.4 ..."`; `setDelay` while frozen, `"delay: a time change waits for the release ..."`; the clear, `"delay: a clear mutes the return from 10 ms after it to one delay time ..."` and `"delay: with the input stopped, a clear empties the line ..."`; the window's end, `"delay: a clear ends without a step ..."`; frozen and cleared, `"delay: frozen and cleared, the loop is empty ..."`; a clear while bypassed, `"delay: clearDelay sets the running delay's t_clear, keeps nothing, and sends nothing while bypassed ..."`. `setDelay` unfrozen is the effects area's (`"effects: a time of 5.4 s is reached ..."` among others). No check lengthens the time inside a clear's window.

## Freeze by bypass

The freeze and the bypass meet in four states of the delay: running (unfrozen or frozen), bypassed and fading (`setBypass`'s level at 0, the node still there for `bypassFadeTime`), and freed (no node, the line emptied: `docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); row 7 is the freeze's other coupling, with a clear. Each guard is Lua's (`lib/fx_hold.lua` and the bypass params' action, `docs/notes/lua/fx-hold.md`), the engine's (this file and `setEffectBypass`), or both; the engine's hold for any client, the check's commands among them.

| # | the delay | arrives | what happens | guarded by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | running, frozen | a bypass | Lua sends `setDelayFreeze(0)` before every `setBypass(0, 1)` (`S.lib.fx_hold.release`), so the node fades unfrozen; a bypass sent alone fades the node frozen, and row 4 still holds | Lua |
| 2 | bypassed, fading | a freeze | Lua refuses it (the param set back to 0); sent alone, the engine sets it on the fading node and keeps it | Lua |
| 3 | bypassed, fading | a lift | the free is canceled: the same node, its loop and its `frozen` play on at the kept level | nothing to guard (the node and the kept value agree) |
| 4 | bypassed, fading | the free | the node freed, its line emptied and its kept `frozen` set to 0 (`emptyDelayLine`, #889) | the engine |
| 5 | freed | a freeze | Lua refuses it; sent alone, the engine keeps 0 (`setDelayFreeze`: no node, no loop to hold) | both |
| 6 | freed | a lift | a new node from the kept controls, unfrozen at the kept time, its line empty, its input fading in (`startDelay` with `fadeIn` 1) | the engine (rows 4 and 5) |
| 7 | running, frozen | a clear | Lua releases the freeze first, `setDelayFreeze(0)` then `clearDelay()` (#912), so the line empties and refills unfrozen; sent alone, the engine empties the loop and keeps it frozen on silence (above) | Lua |

`"delay: a bypass lifted with frozen kept starts the delay unfrozen at its kept time"` sends rows 1 and 5 alone, then a tone at the lift: the node's and the kept `frozen` are 0, and the return is silent until the delay time and back after it.

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/delay.scd`)

The signal checks run nodes of the engine's own `\strataDelay` on buses and lines of their own (`startCheckDelay`), each driven by `\strataCheckDelayStep` synths mapped to its `frozen`, `time` or `t_clear` and measured by `\strataCheckDelayWindows` in up to eight windows of server time (per window: the energy, its 6-24 kHz band, the side and the mid, the peak and the largest difference from a reference node), all created in one bundle and read after the analyses' `/n_end`, so a busy machine changes nothing. A window ends 10 ms before a step it must not see: on SuperCollider 3.13 a `Sweep.kr` (the steps) runs a block ahead of a `Sweep.ar` (the windows), since 3.14 changed when `Sweep` adds its increment, and a window ending on the release took the release's first block on 3.13 only:
- a held loop (steady pink noise, feedback 0.4, 0.375 s and 0.30928 s, ping-pong 0, 1 and 0.5, frozen at 1 s): in windows from 2 to 9 s the energy and the 6-24 kHz band stay within 0.5 dB of the first window's (each window is a whole number of the loop's periods, about 1 s, so each holds the same audio: the readings are 0.0 dB); at ping-pong 0.5 the side stays above -20 dB of the mid (-6 to -9 dB; the noise stops at 1.5 s there, so a loop that was not held would lose its width). Without the rounding the band falls 4.6 dB by 9 s at 0.30928 s, and without the snap the side is -300 dB;
- the input closed (a 1 kHz sine started after the freeze: nothing in the return, against its level unfrozen), a time change waiting for the release (a node whose time goes to 0.2 s while frozen plays what one left at 0.375 s plays, to the sample, until the release), a release decaying at the kept feedback (held within 1 dB until it, -42 dB 2 s after it), a clear (the return under -60 dB from 10 ms after it to one delay time, its new input back one delay time later; with the input stopped, and frozen, nothing after the window), and a clear ending without a step (`checkClearEdge`: a steady sine of eleven cycles a loop period, about 29 Hz, so the repeats add in phase and the refill is never louder than the steady return: across the window's end and one delay time after it the return's largest sample step stays within the steady largest, `clearStepBound` 1);
- the commands on the engine's node: `setDelayFreeze` setting and keeping `frozen`, kept through a bypass's fade and a lift inside it (the same node) and dropped at its free (#889: the lifted node unfrozen, at its kept time, whether the freeze came before the bypass or while the delay was freed), 2 and -1 ignored with one line each; `clearDelay` setting `t_clear` on the running node, keeping nothing and sending nothing in a bypass's fade or after its free; a fresh engine's delay unfrozen (the node and the kept value).

It builds one standard engine of its own (the fresh-engine check) and no high one, and runs in about 23 s, in the `record,waveform,onsets,delay,shimmer` CI shard. `runCases` allocates an input bus only for a case without `inputFrom` (a case that shares an input allocates none, as `shimmer.scd`'s copy does), and the area ends by checking that the server's audio bus allocator holds what it held when the area began, plus the fresh engine's context (its two inputs and its silent output, four channels the harness frees only at the run's end; #807: before, each shared case leaked two channels).
