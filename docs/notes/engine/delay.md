## Must not break

- **The freeze is `\strataDelay`'s `frozen`** (3.8 design §7.3), never a feedback of 1 through `setDelay`: `setDelay`'s feedback stays clipped to 0-0.95 (its literal, the stub's limit and Lua's param), and only `frozen` takes the loop's gain to 1.
- **The loop's gain reaches 1 exactly**: `hold` is `Slew.kr(frozen.clip(0, 1), delayFreezeRate, delayFreezeRate)`, a linear ramp over `delayFreezeSeconds` (20 ms) that lands on its target, and the gain is `feedback.clip(0, 0.95).max(hold)`. A one-pole lag only approaches 1, and a loop a little under 1 fades: the 3.8 facts' prototype lost 0.2-0.4 dB over 9 s with one.
- **The input closes with the same ramp** (`In.ar(in, 2) * liftFade(fadeIn) * (1 - hold)`): nothing enters a frozen loop.
- **The time is held before its lag and rounded to whole samples while frozen**: `Gate.kr(time.clip(0.01, 5.4), 1 - isFrozen).lag(0.1)`, then `(held * SampleRate.ir).round / SampleRate.ir` while frozen. The gate holds what `setDelay` sends meanwhile (a tempo move under sync, a time change), so the loop keeps its length until the release, which glides to the new time as any time change does. The rounding keeps the loop bright: `BufDelayC`'s cubic interpolation at a fractional delay is a gentle lowpass, and a loop that passes it at every repeat lost 10.7-11 dB of its 6-24 kHz band by 9 s at 0.30928 s (the facts). The loop's period is then the rounded time plus the feedback's one block, both whole samples. The clip stays a literal on the control (`time.clip(0.01, 5.4)`): the Lua stub reads `setDelay`'s time limit there, and Lua's `DELAY.max_seconds` must equal it (a test).
- **Ping-pong is snapped while frozen**: the cross-feed moves with `hold` from the lagged `pingPong` to `pingPong >= delayPingPongSnap` (0.5), lagged as `pingPong` is, so it is 0 or 1 in a frozen loop. A cross of 0.5 averages the two lines at every pass and the loop loses its width (the facts' side read -999 dB); at 0 or 1 the loop is a permutation of the two lines and keeps both.
- **Unfrozen and uncleared the graph plays what it played before, bit for bit**: `hold` 0 makes every new product a multiply by 1 or an add of 0, the clear's gains are exactly 1, and the gate passes the time (the effects area's `checkLineAsDelayC` still reads a largest difference of 0 against 3.7's `DelayC` copy).
- **Clear mutes, it never zeroes the line** (design §7.2): `t_clear` opens `Trig1.kr(t_clear, delayTime + delayClearMargin)`, which multiplies the feedback at once and the return through a `delayClearFade` (3 ms) lag. When the window ends, everything the line held before the clear has been read and not fed back, so the line holds only what came in since (silence with the input stopped or a frozen, closed loop); the return's fade sits inside the 5 ms margin, so neither edge clicks. The buffers are zeroed only by a bypass's free (`emptyDelayLine`).
- **`frozen` is a kept control, `t_clear` is not**: `setDelayFreeze` keeps it (`keepEffectControls`) and `startDelay` passes it, so a lift re-creates the node with it; Lua's bypass releases a freeze before it bypasses (`docs/notes/lua/fx-hold.md`), so the kept value is 0 at a lift from Lua. `clearDelay` sends nothing while the delay is bypassed (`effectLive`: in the fade too) and is never kept.
- **Every `Synth(\strataDelay` passes `bufL` and `bufR`** (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); this area's own nodes start in one place, `startCheckDelay`, on lines of their own.
- The new controls are `arg`s with literal clips (`frozen.clip(0, 1)`), where the Lua stub reads them (the plan's ruling 24); the constants are named once (`delayFreezeSeconds`, `delayFreezeRate`, `delayPingPongSnap`, `delayClearMargin`, `delayClearFade`).

## The freeze and the clear (`StrataDelay.sc`)

`\strataDelay` (`in, out, bufL, bufR, time=0.375, feedback=0.4, mix=0, pingPong=0, fadeIn=0, frozen=0, t_clear=0`) is the effects area's node (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); this file covers 3.8's two additions. The delay has no engine area of its own: `addDelayCommands` (called from `addEffectCommands`) adds the two commands beside `setDelay`.

- `setDelayFreeze(frozen)`, `"i"`: 0 releases, 1 freezes; any other value (2, -1, a float) is ignored with one console line (`fastModIndicesValid`, which posts through the modulation area's `postFastModIgnored`: `strata: setDelayFreeze ignored: frozen 2 is not a whole number from 0 to 1`). It keeps the value and sets the node (`setSynth` skips a missing node: a freeze set while bypassed is kept for the lift).
- `clearDelay()`, `""`: no argument (norns takes an empty format, as `Engine_PolySub.sc` does). Sets `t_clear` 1 on the running node; nothing while bypassed.
- Released, the loop decays at the feedback the node holds (`setDelay`'s, kept through the freeze): 0.4 is about -8 dB a pass, under -40 dB 2 s after the release at 0.375 s.
- Frozen and cleared, the loop empties and stays frozen on silence (the input is closed), until a release opens the input again.
- The node takes 14.3 KB of the pool on 3.14.1 and on 3.13 (the memory area's twenty-node probe, under its 20 KB limit, `delayDefLimitKB`; 7.5 KB before the freeze and the clear).

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/delay.scd`)

The signal checks run nodes of the engine's own `\strataDelay` on buses and lines of their own (`startCheckDelay`), each driven by `\strataCheckDelayStep` synths mapped to its `frozen`, `time` or `t_clear` and measured by `\strataCheckDelayWindows` in up to eight windows of server time (per window: the energy, its 6-24 kHz band, the side and the mid, the peak and the largest difference from a reference node), all created in one bundle and read after the analyses' `/n_end`, so a busy machine changes nothing. A window ends 10 ms before a step it must not see: on SuperCollider 3.13 a `Sweep.kr` (the steps) runs a block ahead of a `Sweep.ar` (the windows), since 3.14 changed when `Sweep` adds its increment, and a window ending on the release took the release's first block on 3.13 only:
- a held loop (steady pink noise, feedback 0.4, 0.375 s and 0.30928 s, ping-pong 0, 1 and 0.5, frozen at 1 s): in windows from 2 to 9 s the energy and the 6-24 kHz band stay within 0.5 dB of the first window's (each window is a whole number of the loop's periods, about 1 s, so each holds the same audio: the readings are 0.0 dB); at ping-pong 0.5 the side stays above -20 dB of the mid (-6 to -9 dB; the noise stops at 1.5 s there, so a loop that was not held would lose its width). Without the rounding the band falls 4.6 dB by 9 s at 0.30928 s, and without the snap the side is -300 dB;
- the input closed (a 1 kHz sine started after the freeze: nothing in the return, against its level unfrozen), a time change waiting for the release (a node whose time goes to 0.2 s while frozen plays what one left at 0.375 s plays, to the sample, until the release), a release decaying at the kept feedback (held within 1 dB until it, -42 dB 2 s after it), a clear (the return under -60 dB from 10 ms after it to one delay time, its new input back one delay time later; with the input stopped, and frozen, nothing after the window);
- the commands on the engine's node: `setDelayFreeze` setting and keeping `frozen`, kept through a bypass and its lift and set while bypassed, 2 and -1 ignored with one line each; `clearDelay` setting `t_clear` on the running node, keeping nothing and sending nothing in a bypass's fade or after its free; a fresh engine's delay unfrozen (the node and the kept value).

It builds one standard engine of its own (the fresh-engine check) and no high one, and runs in about 18 s, in the `record,waveform,onsets,delay` CI shard.
