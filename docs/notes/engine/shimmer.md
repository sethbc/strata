## Must not break

- **At amount 0 each effect is its def without shimmer, bit for bit** (3.8 design §7.4): the delay's feedback is `delayed + ((shifted - delayed) × amount)`, an exact `+ 0` at 0 (the rings hold finite values), and each reverb's loop is multiplied by the amount before its `LocalOut`, so its input gains an exact 0. The engine check compares each def at 0 with a check-only copy of it as it was before shimmer (1b's delay with its freeze and clear, 1a's reverbs): largest difference 0.0. Keep every new product a multiply by an amount and every new sum an add of the loop.
- **One audio-rate `LocalIn` a SynthDef** (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`): `\strataReverb` gains its one `LocalIn.ar(2)`; the FDN's `LocalIn.ar(8)` is widened to ten (channels 8 and 9 the loop, entering before the diffusers); the delay's loop is its existing feedback. Never a second `LocalIn`.
- **Every node of `\strataDelay`, `\strataReverb` and `\strataReverbFDN` passes `shiftL` and `shiftR`** (and a delay `bufL` and `bufR`, #807), whatever form starts it (`Synth(`, `Synth.new(`, `Synth.head`, `tail`, `before`, `after`, `replace`, `Synth.basicNew` with `newMsg`, an `s_new` written out), the engine's (`startDelay`, `startReverb`, so the chain's start and a bypass's lift) and every check's private node (a ring pair of its own, through one helper an area: `allocLine` in `effects.scd` and `delay.scd`, `startCheckNode` in `shimmer.scd`, the size probes in `memory.scd`, `rt60s` and `poolKB` in `effects.scd`). A bufnum left at its default 0, or a negative or too large one, is buffer 0 (`GET_BUF` clamps), a real buffer of another area (today the wave type's first wavetable), which the shifter's `BufWr` would write. A test (`test/unit/shimmer_test.lua`) reads every such site in the engine and the check, and a self-test of it holds each form. No default makes a node without its rings write nowhere: the only bufnum `BufWr` skips is one never allocated, and none is reserved.
- **The rings**: four mono buffers of `shimmerRingFrames` (16384, ordinary RAM, counted in `fixedBufferBytes`), `areaState[\effects][\shimmerRings]` (the delay's left and right, the reverb's left and right; `shimmerRingsFor(key)`), allocated in the effects area's `allocState`, freed in its `free` (kept in the list, freed, so the structure area's `engineLeft` counts them); their bufnums never change. A bypass's free zeroes its effect's two in the same bundle (`emptyShimmerRings`), since a `BufRd` reads whatever its ring holds: a lifted node never plays a ring's old audio.
- **The reverb's loop is bounded, never by the output's level alone**: it clips softly at `shimmerCeiling` (0.25, `(x / ceiling).tanh × ceiling`) and is ducked when the output passes `shimmerOverInput` times what the reverb is sent plus the halo (below), so a loud return keeps its shimmer in proportion and a loop that would grow holds at that bound, then as a quiet halo once the send stops. A bound on the output's absolute level (2a's first law) took the shimmer from every loud return. The send (before the reverb and its level follower), the output (before the loop's follower and the return) and the loop (after its amount and duck) pass `zeroBadValues`, in both reverbs: a NaN or inf on the send never latches in FreeVerb2's combs, the FDN's lines or a follower, and the reverb plays on as it would without it. `zeroBadValues` is a `Select` that passes every normal finite sample unchanged and zeroes NaN, inf and subnormals (`CheckBadValues`' codes 1-3), so at amount 0 the guards change no normal sample (a subnormal, under 1.2e-38, becomes 0; the check's largest difference is 0.0).
- **The delay's loop gain is still the feedback's**: the shifter's two heads are a convex mix (Hann gains that sum to exactly 1), never above the ring's peak, so the loop is bounded at feedback 0.95 and frozen (gain exactly 1) at every interval.
- **The controls are `arg`s with literal clips** (`shimmer.clip(0, 1)`, `shimmerRatio.clip(0.5, 4)`, in each def's body), where the Lua stub reads `setShimmer`'s limits; `setShimmer` clips nothing itself.
- No delay line or table sized from a control (the rings are buffers of a constant size), no `LocalBuf`, no new magic number where a name exists (`shimmerCeiling`, `shimmerLowpassHz`, `shimmerHighpassHz`, `shimmerWindowSeconds`, the duck's `shimmerOverInput`, `shimmerHaloRms`, `shimmerHaloLag`, `shimmerSendAttack`, `shimmerSendRelease`, `shimmerDuckRange`; Lua's `SHIMMER.ratios`).
- **Every random source of the check's bound, halo and heard cases is seeded** (each input's noise on a generator of its own, the FDN's drift through a seeder before each node), so a run's figures are every run's: the halo is a deterministic figure, never a peak that varies by run.

## The shifter (`StrataEffects.sc`)

`shimmerShift { arg sig, bufs, ratio; }` (stereo in and out; `bufs` the two mono rings): each channel is written into its ring with `BufWr` at a `Phasor` over `shimmerRingFrames`, and read by two cubic `BufRd` heads (`loop` 1, so the read frames wrap). The heads' delays are `span × saw + span + lead` frames, `span` half of `shimmerWindowSeconds` (0.1 s) in frames and `saw` an `LFSaw` at `(1 - ratio) / window` Hz, the two heads half a cycle apart (`iphase` 0 and 1): the delay sweeps the window at a rate of `1 - ratio` seconds a second, which shifts by `ratio`. `lead` is `shimmerLeadBlocks` (2) control blocks, so a read never meets a frame not yet written whichever of `BufWr` and `BufRd` the graph runs first in a block (cubic interpolation reads a frame ahead). The first head's gain is `0.5 + 0.5 × cos(pi × saw)`, the second's `1` less it (a Hann pair: 0 where a head jumps, summing to exactly 1). At ratio 1 the saw stands still (a plain delay). The shifted copy is 2.7-102.7 ms later than its source (the heads' delay). The 3.8 facts' prototype measured 12.6 KB for the stereo shifter with a `.wrap` on each read phase; this one shares the read phases and the gains between the channels.

## In the delay (`StrataDelay.sc`)

`\strataDelay` gains `shiftL=0, shiftR=0, shimmer=0, shimmerRatio=2`. The shifter runs on `delayed` (the line's output), and the feedback is `fed = delayed + ((shifted - delayed) × shimmer.clip(0, 1).lag(fxMixLag))`, then `fed × feedback.clip(0, 0.95).max(hold) × the clear's gain` into `LocalOut`; the return stays `delayed`. Each repeat steps by the ratio; at shimmer 1 a frozen loop is all shifted. `startDelay` passes the delay's two rings and the kept `shimmer` and `shimmerRatio`.

## Around each reverb (`StrataEffects.sc`)

Both reverb defs gain the same four args. `shimmerLoop { arg wet, sent, bufs, amount, ratio; }` takes the reverb's stereo output and what the reverb is sent (`sent`: its send and the delay's share, before the loop), each with its bad values already zeroed, shifts the output, low-passes it at `shimmerLowpassHz` (6 kHz) and high-passes it at `shimmerHighpassHz` (100 Hz), clips it softly at `shimmerCeiling` (0.25), multiplies it by the amount and by the duck and zeroes any bad value: what goes back into the reverb's input. `\strataReverb` adds it to FreeVerb2's two inputs through its one `LocalIn.ar(2)`; `\strataReverbFDN` adds it to the diffusers' input through channels 8 and 9 of its `LocalIn.ar(10)` (before the diffusers). Each def's output is `wet` with its bad values zeroed (`zeroBadValues` a channel) times its level.

**The duck** (the design's "soft clip or limiter", left to the builder). The clip bounds what the loop feeds in, not what comes out: the reverb's own gain is far above 1 at a large size (FreeVerb2 at size 1, damp 0: a loop at the clip drove its output to a peak of 10.8, and the FDN's to 1.3, with the clip alone), and a loop leaking a hundredth of the clip sustains it. So the loop's gain falls linearly from 1 to 0 as the output's RMS rises from `bound` to `(1 + shimmerDuckRange)` (1.25) times it, where `bound` is `shimmerOverInput` (1.5) times the send's level, plus `shimmerHaloRms` (0.07, -23 dB). The output's level is its RMS: both channels' mean square lagged `shimmerHaloLag` (0.5 s to -60 dB, a time constant of 72 ms). The send's is its mean square rising over `shimmerSendAttack` (0.05 s) and falling over `shimmerSendRelease` (2 s) (`LagUD`, 60 dB times; `shimmerLevel`), so it rides the send's peaks and reads above its RMS: 1.35 times on a steady sine, about 1.6 times on pink noise (an instrumented copy read the bound at 0.43 on a sine of RMS 0.177, and at 0.15-0.19 on pink noise of RMS 0.033-0.05). So the output is held to about 2.5 times the send's RMS on a sine and 3.0 times on pink noise (1.25 × 1.5 × 1.35 or 1.6), plus 0.09 of halo: a held runaway on a steady sine of 0.25 read 2.4 times the send's RMS on the FDN and 3.05 on FreeVerb2. So:
- a loud return keeps its shimmer in proportion: a reverb at size 0.8 shimmering a sine or a band of noise at 0.5 (its output -11 to -18 dB RMS) puts as much into the shifted band, level for level, as at a tenth of that level (within 1-4.4 dB), at least 20 dB over the same case at amount 0, and its level over 100 ms moves no more than the reverb's own without shimmer (the check's "heard" cases);
- a loop that would grow while the send plays holds near that bound (the bound cases' peaks 0.4 to 0.8 at pink noise of 0.25, at every ratio, amount and size); a send near full scale (pink noise at 0.9, size 1, amount 1, the worst ratio) peaks 1.1 dB (FreeVerb2) and 0.5 dB (the FDN) over the same send at amount 0, at 1.98 and 2.81 (1.75 and 2.66 at amount 0), before the master's limiter. At size 1, damp 0, the reverb's own build-up on a steady dense send (5.6-5.9 times its RMS) reaches the bound by itself, and the duck then holds the loop all but shut: a reverb at full size on a steady noise shimmers little;
- once the send stops, the bound falls over 2 s to the halo, and a runaway holds there: about -22 dB RMS, peaks of -8 to -9 dBFS, until the amount or the size comes down. A shimmer tail that is no runaway keeps its own decay under that bound.
`shimmerOverInput` is the smallest multiple that kept the peaks of the bound cases under 0.8 with room (2 gave 0.996); `shimmerHaloRms` the largest halo whose peaks stay 2 dB under the brief's -6 dBFS (the halo's crest is about 13-14 dB: a -20 dB RMS halo peaks at -6 dBFS). The first law keyed on the output's RMS alone (the duck from 0.08, -22 dB): a loud return (its output over about -20 dB RMS) lost its shimmer while it played, and a peak follower tried before it (attack 10 ms, release 0.1 s, then 1 s) let a halo's beating peaks reach 1.1.

## The command (`addEffectCommands`)

`setShimmer(delayAmount, reverbAmount, ratio)`, `"fff"`: a non-finite argument is ignored with one console line (`fastModValuesFinite`, which posts through the modulation area's `postFastModIgnored`: `strata: setShimmer ignored: ratio inf is not a finite number`); otherwise it keeps `shimmer` and `shimmerRatio` for both effects (`keepEffectControls`: the delay's `delayAmount`, the reverb's `reverbAmount`, the one ratio) and sets both nodes (`setSynth`, which skips a missing node; a node in its bypass fade takes it). The kept controls start at the defs' defaults (`startEffectsChain`'s `effectDefaults` lists), so a lift creates the node with the last values sent, bypassed or not.

## Memory and CPU

The rings are ordinary RAM (256 KB, `fixedBufferBytes`, `docs/notes/engine/performance.md`); the graphs are the pool's. Measured with the memory area's twenty-node probes (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`): `\strataDelay` 24.4 KB (16.0 before shimmer; limit 30), `\strataReverb` 125.6 KB (103.6 before; limit 130, raised from 125 in 2a's fix round 2: two probe steps of room was too thin for a CI gate), `\strataReverbFDN` 229.2 KB at 3.8 (208.1 before shimmer), 103.6 KB since 3.9's reverb freeze moved its lines into buffers (limit 110, `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`). CPU, measured in NRT on x86 (sixteen instances for 300 s, user time less the source's): one stereo shifter alone is about 0.84 × FreeVerb2's; two run always, at amount 0 too (a CM3 figure is a device check, #6).

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/shimmer.scd`)

Nodes of the engine's own defs on buses, rings and lines of their own (`startCheckNode`), driven by check-only inputs (seeded noise, a sine), steps and a time LFO, measured on the server in windows of server time (`\strataCheckShimmerWindows`: per window the energy, the peak, whether a bad value reached it and the largest difference from a reference; `\strataCheckShimmerBands`: a band's energy, the whole energy and the largest and smallest level over 100 ms), run as plans in four batches of under 5 MB of the pool each (`runPlans`) and read after the analyses' `/n_end`, so a busy machine changes nothing:
- shimmer 0 as before: each def at amount 0 (ratio 3) against its check-only copy (`\strataCheckShimmerDelay18`, `\strataCheckShimmerVerb18`, `\strataCheckShimmerFdn18`) on the same pink noise for 5 s: the delay at feedback 0.4, ping-pong 0.5, at 0.30928 s, on a 0.5 Hz time LFO and frozen at 1 s; FreeVerb2 and the FDN at size 0.8, damp 0.5 (the FDN's drift draws from the default random generator, so a seeder, `\strataCheckShimmerSeed`, reseeds it at every control block just before each of the two nodes). Largest difference 0.0 in all five;
- the delay's loop: a 0.2 s pink burst at feedback 0.95, time 0.375 s, each ratio at shimmer 1, unfrozen and frozen at 0.25 s: the last window of 9 s (three loop periods) at most 1 dB over the first after the burst (about -8.6 dB unfrozen, 0.0 dB frozen);
- each reverb's loop: every ratio × amount {0.5, 1} × size {0.8, 1} at damp 0, 3 s of pink noise at 0.25 then silence: the peak under 1.0 (at most 0.80) and no bad value over 9 s, and at ratio 1.5, amount 1, size 1 the halo from 5 to 9 s: its peak under -6 dBFS and its RMS under `haloRmsDb` (-15 dB) (FreeVerb2 -8.9 dBFS and -22.2 dB, the FDN -8.4 and -22.5, the same in every run), and, the duck regulating it alone, its level over 100 ms from 6 to 8.9 s moving at most `haloPumpDb` (3 dB; 2.05 and 1.52 dB); a check-only case at ratio 1.02 (amount 1, size 1) holds too. With the clip alone, eleven cases fail (peaks up to 10.8);
- shimmer heard: each effect on a sine of 440 Hz at 0.1, amount 0.5, ratio 2 and 1.5: the band at 880 or 660 Hz at least `presentDb` (30 dB) over amount 0 and `shiftDb` (20 dB) over the band the other ratio would make (34.9-52.3 dB, the delay at ratio 1.5 the lowest, and 34.2-39.5 dB; two band-passes pass an unshifted 440 Hz about 10.2 dB more at 660 than at 880 Hz, so a loop that ignored the ratio would come within 0.2 dB of a bound of 10); each reverb at size 0.8 on a sine and on noise low-passed at 400 Hz, at 0.5 against 0.05: the shifted band (880 Hz; above 1200 Hz) within `proportionalDb` (6 dB) of 20 dB over the quiet case's (15.6-19.0 dB), and over amount 0 at least `presentDb` on the sine (41.5-50.0 dB) and `noisePresentDb` (15 dB) on the noise (20.8-21.3 dB: the band above 1200 Hz holds the reverb's own share of the low-passed noise); and, the duck leaving them alone (the bound far above them), their level over 100 ms from 2 to 5 s moving at most `pumpDb` (1.5 dB) more than at amount 0 (at most 0.01 dB more);
- the duck holding a runaway (`planHeld`): each reverb at size 1, damp 0, amount 1, its worst ratio (FreeVerb2 4, the FDN 1.5), on a steady sine of 440 Hz at 0.25 (the duck regulating: an instrumented copy read its gain at 0.15-0.2 on average): its RMS from 4 to 8.9 s at most `heldSineTimes` (4) times the send's (3.05 and 2.39) and its level over 100 ms moving at most `heldPumpDb` (2.5 dB) more than at amount 0 (FreeVerb2 1.71 dB more; the FDN's drift makes its own spread 10.6 dB at amount 0, so the FDN's pumping is read on its halo); on steady pink noise at 0.25 (the reverb's own build-up at the bound) at most `heldOverDb` (3 dB) over amount 0 (1.56 and 0.0 dB) and no more pumping; and pink noise at 0.9: no bad value, its peak at most `loudOverDb` (6 dB) over amount 0 (1.1 and 0.5 dB);
- a NaN in the send: each reverb at amount 0 and at amount 1 (size 0.8, ratio 2) with one control block of inf and NaN on its input at 1 s: the input carries them, the output none, and from 1.5 s it plays as the same case without them (0.0 dB; before the send was zeroed, FreeVerb2's combs and the FDN's lines latched the NaN and the output was silent for good);
- the commands on the engine's nodes: `setShimmer(0.6, 0.4, 3)` sets and keeps both, a bypass of both and a lift keep them (the lifted nodes read 0.6, 0.4 and 3), one sent while bypassed is kept for the lift; a NaN or infinite value in each place ignored with one line each;
- the rings: four of 16384 frames, one channel, distinct, the delay's node on the first two and the reverb's on the last two through a bypass and its lift; with shimmer on and noise on both sends they hold audio, and a bypass's free empties them;
- a fresh standard and a fresh high engine at shimmer 0 and ratio 2 (nodes and kept values), the high engine's FDN on the reverb's two rings, keeping `setShimmer` through a lift.

It builds a standard engine and a high one of its own, so it never shares the memory area's shard, and runs in about 40 s (39.5 s on 3.14.1 and on 3.13, ten runs each), in the `record,waveform,onsets,delay,shimmer` CI shard. Mutations checked to fail it: an offset of 1e-7 on FreeVerb2's loop input and a gain of 1.0000001 on the delay's feedback (the four delay and FreeVerb2 as-before checks: 7.5e-8 to 8.7e-7); the duck removed (fourteen bound and halo checks); `shimmerShift` ignoring its ratio (every heard check, and six bound cases); the amount forced to 0 (every heard check); the duck's range at 1 % (FreeVerb2's held sine, 3.43 dB more, and its halo, 3.27 dB); a 3 s output follower with that range, which makes both ducks oscillate (FreeVerb2's held sine and halo, and the FDN's halo, 4.28 dB). The range at 1 % alone leaves the FDN's duck steady (its halo 1.49 dB, its held sine unchanged).
