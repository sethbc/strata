## Must not break

- **Limiter before the tanh saturator** in `\strataMaster`. The engine check asserts the order.
- **Sends at 1 are 3.0's chain.** `voiceOutput` writes dry `(1 − s_d·m_d)(1 − s_r·m_r)`, delay send `s_d`, reverb send `s_r·(1 − s_d·m_d)`; the delay returns `m_d·D`, the reverb `m_r·R` from its send plus the delay's return, and `masterSum` adds dry + (1 − m_r)·delay return + reverb return. Every reader of a mix lags it `fxMixLag` (0.05 s, 3.0's delay lag; 3.0's reverb mix was not lagged, so a moving reverb mix glides where 3.0's stepped), and `fxMix` and the delay's and reverb's own `mix` controls are set in one bundle so every reader moves in the same block. The engine check compares this against check-only copies of 3.0's chain (-60 dB).
- **Bypass is explicit**: only `setBypass` (Lua's `delay_bypass`/`reverb_bypass`, never a scene field) frees or creates the delay or reverb node. A mix of 0 (LFO, scene fade, PARAMS, CC) only silences and never frees or creates a node.
- **Replace, never stack, and node order**: a bypass waits `bypassFadeTime` (the lag plus one block) after broadcasting the mix as 0 and then frees the node; a lift inside the wait cancels the free (the same node); a later lift creates it at its place (`\addAfter` `\filterReverb` for the delay, `\addBefore` the master for the reverb), so the chain stays filter ×3 → delay → reverb → master and there is never more than one delay or reverb node.
- **`effectKeys`**: the chain's synths (`\filter`, `\filterDelay`, `\filterReverb`, `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`) never end on their own (the engine frees and creates the bypassed ones itself), so `setSynth` sends to them plainly and skips a missing key.
- **NaN guards**: each `\strataFilter` replaces bad values on its bus (`zeroBadValues`) before the delay and the reverb. The master filters are never freed: freeing one would leave its output unwritten.
- **`\strataFilter` and the voice filter**: never compare a UGen with `==` (that is object identity); use `BinaryOpUGen('==', ...)`. A type change crossfades over 20 ms.
- The filter, delay and reverb SynthDef defaults equal the Lua param defaults (the script never bangs its params); the kept controls start from the SynthDefs' defaults.
- A grain synth gets its engine's send and mix buses through `grainControls` (`mixBusControls`), never only from its SynthDef defaults (they are the buses of the last engine built).
- Never size a delay line from a control: the FDN's lines, diffusers and the delay are sized by constants.

## The mix area (`StrataMix.sc`, order 120)

`areaState[\mix]` holds the buses: `\delaySend` and `\reverbSend` (stereo audio, every grain synth writes them), their filtered copies `\delaySendPost` and `\reverbSendPost`, `\fxMix` (control, 2 channels: the delay mix and the reverb mix as broadcast), and the effects area's buses under the mix's names: `\dry` (`mainBus`, every grain synth's `out`), `\dryPost` (`filterBus`), `\delayReturn` (`delayBus`), `\reverbReturn` (`reverbBus`). `mixBus(name)` reads one. The area frees only its own four send buses and `fxMix`.

- **`voiceFilter(sig, filterType, filterFreq, filterRes, filterMix)`**: inside every grain synth, after the pan and before the level (`grainBody`, `docs/notes/engine/grains.md`). It is `multimodeFilter`, the master filter's design: `RLPF`/`RHPF`/`BPF` at rq `1 − res × 0.95`, each weighted by `BinaryOpUGen('==', type, index).lag(0.02)`, the cutoff lagged 0.05 s and clipped to 20-20000 Hz, the mix lagged 0.05 s; `filterMix` 0 (the default) passes the signal unchanged. Each grain synth filters only itself, so a fading synth keeps its own filter state and a switch never steps it. Lua's params: `docs/notes/lua/voice-mix.md`.
- **`voiceOutput(sig, out, mirrorOut, mirrorLevel, delaySend, reverbSend)`**: four stereo `Out`s. `out` stays the dry output (the engine sets `mainBus`; the engine check moves it to measure one grain synth). The sends are clipped to 0-1 and lagged `fxMixLag`; the mixes (`In.kr(fxMixIn, 2)`) are only lagged (`liveMix` clips them before they are broadcast). The mirror is the whole signal times `mirrorLevel`, whatever the sends, so crossmod follows the voice as before. The send and mix buses are named controls (`delaySendOut`, `reverbSendOut`, `fxMixIn`), not in the arg lists Lua reads; `allocMixBuses` puts this engine's indices into every voice's `grainControls`, so every grain synth the engine starts (and re-gates) gets them.
- **Real-time memory**: the voice filter and sends add about 57 UGens, about 12-13 KB, to every grain synth: with 2b's draws `\strataGrainRing` measures 69.1 KB and `\strataGrainBuf` 74.2-75.0 KB (the memory area's limit is 80 KB). The high tier's worst case (spectral freeze on every voice, the FDN) leaves about 4.24-4.26 MB free, about 4.13-4.15 MB counting the spectral writers' late allocations, so the three-type voice filter stays; a lowpass-only fallback would save about 4.2 KB per grain synth (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`).

With every send at 1 this is term for term 3.0's output, `(1 − m_d)(1 − m_r)·F(x) + m_d(1 − m_r)·Delay(F(x)) + m_r·Rev((1 − m_d)·F(x) + m_d·Delay(F(x)))`, at constant mixes and while the delay mix moves (3.0's delay lagged its mix 0.05 s, as every 3.2 reader does). Where it can't match: while the reverb mix moves (3.0 sent it to `FreeVerb2` unlagged, so it stepped where 3.2's readers glide over 0.05 s; the engine check sweeps only the delay mix); after an explicit bypass (the effect restarts empty); while the delay mix moves with the master filter on, the filter sees `(1 − m_d(t))·x` instead of filtering `x` and then scaling (milliseconds, the filter's impulse response: the engine check's swept run lands about -71 dB); and with sends other than 1, which 3.0 never had. With sends below 1, the delay feeds the reverb as one source (`m_d·D`) and returns through the global `(1 − m_r)`, so a voice with reverb send 0 still has its echoes reverberated and one with delay send 0 still hears the other voices' echoes in the reverb. A voice whose sends are 0 plays dry at full level at any global mix.

## The effects area (`StrataEffects.sc`, order 60)

The chain, at the tail of `context.xg` in signal order: `\filter` (`mainBus` → `filterBus`), `\filterDelay` (delay send → its copy), `\filterReverb` (reverb send → its copy), `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`.

- `\strataFilter` (`in, out, freq=5000, res=0.5, filterType=0, mix=0`): the NaN guard on its input (`zeroBadValues` per channel), then `multimodeFilter` with the cutoff `freq.lag(0.05).clip(20, 20000)`, `res.clip(0, 1)` and `mix.lag(0.05).clip(0, 1)` (written in the SynthDef, where the Lua stub reads the limits). The filter is linear, so its three instances on the three parts equal one on their sum, exactly where 3.0 had it. `setFilter` sets all three in one bundle (the Lua stub reads the FX controls from the literal `\filter` line; the other two follow in a loop).
- `\strataDelay` (`in, out, time=0.375, feedback=0.4, mix=0`): `DelayC` (max 2 s, `time.lag(0.1)`) fed by the filtered delay send plus `LocalIn`, the delayed signal times `feedback.clip(0, 0.95)` sent back; it returns the delayed signal only, times `mix.clip(0, 1).lag(0.05)` (`m_d·D`).
- `\strataReverb` (`in, delayIn, out, mix=0.3, size=0.8, damp=0.5`): the standard tier's. `FreeVerb2` at mix 1 on the filtered reverb send plus the delay's return, returned times its lagged mix (`m_r·R`). `FreeVerb2`'s mix is exactly the linear crossfade (checked), so this equals 3.0's reverb.
- `\strataReverbFDN` (the same controls and return): the high tier's (`startReverb` picks it by `tier`), a feedback delay network of core UGens, so CI and every norns run the same thing. Four `AllpassN` diffusers per channel (`fdnDiffuserSeconds`, 5.3-27.1 ms, decay 19 × the delay, a gain of about 0.7); eight `DelayL` lines (`fdnLineSeconds`: 1487, 1759, 2063, 2543, 2927, 3499, 4271 and 5119 samples at 48 kHz, primes of about 31-107 ms) scaled by `0.6 + 0.4 × size` and shortened by the `LocalIn`/`LocalOut` block; a Householder matrix (`line − 2/8 × Σ lines`, lossless); a `OnePole` per line at `damp × 0.4` (FreeVerb's damping per pass); and per-line feedback `(0.7 + 0.28 × size) ** (length / 1378 samples)`, FreeVerb's comb feedback per its mean comb length, so its decay per second at low frequencies is FreeVerb's. Lines 3 and 7 drift slowly (`LFNoise1`, 0.5-0.6 ms) against metallic ringing; left is lines 1, 3, 5, 7 summed, right 2, 4, 6, 8 (input gain 0.25, about FreeVerb2's level, within 1-2 dB from size 0.2 to 1). Measured on the check (the impulse response, counted in server blocks): RT60 about 2.10-2.18 s against FreeVerb2's 2.17 s at size 0.8, a ratio of about 0.97-1.01 (12 runs at once and alone) (the check requires 30%; only the FDN varies, with its drift's random start), and about 202 KB of the pool against FreeVerb2's 100 KB (each line's `DelayL` is sized by its own length, not the longest).
- `\strataMaster` (`in, delayIn, reverbIn, fxMix, out, amp=0.7, drift=0.01`): `masterSum` (the filtered dry bus, the delay's return times `1 − m_r` from `fxMix`, and the reverb's return), then drift, `Limiter.ar(sig, 0.95)` to catch filter resonance and delay feedback peaks, then the tanh saturation. The limiter must stay before the saturator: `(sig * 2).tanh * 0.5` bounds its output to ±0.5, so a limiter after it could never act (the engine check asserts the order). Drift (master and Sub) is an LFO at a fixed slow rate scaled by the drift amount: tape drift 0 = none; the Sub drift param's minimum stays 0.001. Its `PitchShift` draws its time dispersion from the server's shared random generator, so two masters never match sample for sample: the engine check compares the 3.0 copies against `masterSum`.

**Commands.** `setDelay` and `setReverb` keep their values (`areaState[\effects][\controls]`, starting from the SynthDefs' defaults), then in one bundle set the node (the mix times `effectLive`: 0 while bypassed) and broadcast both mixes on `fxMix` (`broadcastFxMix`, `liveMix`: the kept mix, 0 while bypassed). `setMasterDrift` sets the master.

**Bypass** (`setBypass(effect, bypassed)`, `"ii"`: 0 delay, 1 reverb, `bypassKeys`; anything else ignored), `setEffectBypass`:
- Bypass: mark it bypassed, set the node's mix and the broadcast to 0 in one bundle (the dry gains return to full and the return fades with the same lag, so nothing jumps), then after `bypassFadeTime` (0.05 s + one block) free the node, if this bypass's token is still the pending one (`areaState[\effects][\pendingFrees]`). Freeing `\strataDelay` returns its 1 MB `DelayC` to the pool (the check measures about 1 MB).
- Lift: if the free is still pending, cancel it (the node is still there); otherwise create the node in its place from the kept controls at mix 0 (`startDelay`, `startReverb` take a mix). In the same bundle, set the kept mix and broadcast it, which fades in through the lag. The new line starts silent. A free in flight is sent before the creation, so the server never holds two.
- `setDelay`/`setReverb` while bypassed keep their values (`setSynth` skips the missing node, or sets the fading one at mix 0) and the broadcast stays 0.
- Stopping the engine clears the pending frees (`stopEffects`).
- The master filters are never freed: they are cheap, carry the NaN guard, and their outputs must be written.
- Free and re-create was chosen over `/n_run 0` (design Decision 3): a paused delay line would keep minutes-old audio and play it on resume. Should it change, the change is local to this file.

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/effects.scd`)

The limiter order; the filter and send controls reaching a UGen in both grain defs, and their four stereo `Out`s; 3.2's chain (a stand-in grain synth through `voiceOutput`, the engine's filters, delay, reverb and `masterSum`) against check-only copies of 3.0's filter, delay and reverb at the defaults and with the filter on and the delay mix swept every 50 ms (-60 dB); a voice with sends 0 dry at full level with the mixes at 0.8 and 0.9; the routing, the chain's order and the mix buses; the defaults; `setFilter` on all three filters; the master and voice filter type crossfades (the largest sample step on a 1 kHz sine at the cutoff; both filters in their own groups at once); NaN and inf on each of the three buses zeroed; plain sends to the chain (`setFilter`'s messages reach all three filters with no `/error -1`); the bypass (what it sends, the free after the fade, the re-creation in place with the kept controls, a lift inside the fade, ignored values), the 1 MB returned (allocation probes, as `memory.scd`), 20 Hz toggling for 2 s (at most one delay node, none once bypassed) and 15 Hz toggling (each bypass outlasts the fade, so the delay is freed and created again each time), a mix swept through 0 (the node never freed), `setDelay` during the fade and while bypassed, the reverb's bypass; the FDN's RT60 against FreeVerb2's at size 0.8 within 30% (T30 of the impulse response's energy envelope, measured on the server: one unit impulse on one bus that both reverbs read, created in one bundle with them and with `\strataCheckDecayTimes`, which counts the control blocks each envelope spends above -5 and -35 dB of its running peak for a fixed 3 s of blocks and then frees itself; the wait ends on its `/n_end` and one `/c_getn` reads the counts, so a busy machine changes nothing, #176. A noise burst's random spectrum moved FreeVerb2's T30 between about 1.6 and 2.1 s) and its pool size, and a high engine running the FDN after a bypass is lifted; and the mix buses freed with the engine. It ends with the Lua defaults and nothing bypassed.
