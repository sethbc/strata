## Must not break

- **Limiter before the tanh saturator** in `\strataMaster`. The engine check asserts the order.
- **Plain sends** (3.8 design §7.1). Each voice's stage (`voiceOutput`, `docs/notes/engine/mix.md`) writes its signal times `dry` to the dry bus and times each send to its send bus, the sends taken before the dry level (a voice at dry 0 is fully wet). The returns are levels: the delay returns its delayed signal times its `mix`, the reverb FreeVerb2 (or the FDN) fully wet on its filtered send plus `delayToReverb` of the delay's return, times its `mix`. `masterSum` is the filtered dry bus plus both returns, times `masterSumGain` (0.7), and the pre-master record tap reads it. Every send, dry level, return level and `delayToReverb` is clipped to 0-1 and lagged `fxMixLag` (0.05 s) in its own node; no bus carries a level (3.7's `fxMix` bus, its broadcast and its one-bundle rule are gone). The defaults sound as 3.7.0's: with the reverb's level at 0.43 (0.3 / 0.7) the sum is `0.7·x + 0.301·R(x)` against 3.7's `0.7·x + 0.3·R(x)`; away from the defaults the balance differs from 3.7's. The engine check compares the chain with a check-only reverb and the sum with the three buses written out, and the defaults with a copy of 3.7's law (-40 dB).
- **Bypass is explicit**: only `setBypass` (Lua's `delay_bypass`/`reverb_bypass`, never a scene field) frees or creates the delay or reverb node. A level of 0 (LFO, scene fade, PARAMS, CC) only silences and never frees or creates a node.
- **Replace, never stack, and node order**: a bypass waits `bypassFadeTime` (the lag plus one block, counted on `SystemClock` from the handler's own time, which is when it sends the level: see Bypass below) after setting the node's level to 0 and then frees the node; a lift inside the wait cancels the free (the same node); a later lift creates it at its place (`\addAfter` `\filterReverb` for the delay, `\addBefore` the master for the reverb), so the chain stays filter ×3 → delay → reverb → master and there is never more than one delay or reverb node. Each effect's bypass, pending free and synth are one of three legal triples (running, fading, freed), held by the bookkeeping sweep (`sweepBookkeeping`, after every area of the engine check: `docs/notes/test-harness/engine-check-runner.md`).
- **`effectKeys`**: the chain's synths (`\filter`, `\filterDelay`, `\filterReverb`, `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`) never end on their own (the engine frees and creates the bypassed ones itself), so `setSynth` sends to them plainly and skips a missing key.
- **NaN guards**: each `\strataFilter` (one on the dry bus and one on each send) replaces bad values on its bus (`zeroBadValues`) before the delay and the reverb, and since 3.8's shimmer both reverbs' outputs and their shimmer loops do too (a loop the effect builds can make a bad value after the filters: `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`). The master filters are never freed: freeing one would leave its output unwritten.
- **`\strataFilter` and the voice filter**: never compare a UGen with `==` (that is object identity); use `BinaryOpUGen('==', ...)`. A type change crossfades over 20 ms.
- **The filter runs `RLPF` and `BPF` only; the highpass is the input less both, never an `RHPF`** (`multimodeFilter`). The three share their poles and their numerators add up to the denominator, so the complement is RHPF's response (within 6e-8, -140 dB) without RHPF's state, which grows as 1 / cutoff² and bursts when the cutoff jumps up (#305). Keep the grouping `low × (w0 − w1) + band × (w2 − w1) + sig × w1`: a settled lowpass or bandpass is then `RLPF` or `BPF` plus exact zeros, bit for bit.
- **A cutoff rises at most `cutoffRiseRate` (100 octaves a second) through `riseLimitedCutoff`, inside `multimodeFilter`**, so both callers get it with no change at a call site: `min(cutoff, ceiling)`, the ceiling a `Slew` of the cutoff in octaves (up 100, down unlimited) times 1.0001. A cutoff that falls, holds or rises more slowly (a step up to about a factor of 1.5 through the 0.05 s lag) passes bit for bit; the engine check's step from 20 Hz to 20 kHz peaks 2.18 at res 1 where it peaked 2883.
- **The master's drift shifter has no time dispersion and a window of at most 0.02 s** (`PitchShift.ar(sig, 0.01, driftMod, 0, 0)`, #306): a dispersion makes every steady tone flutter by 25-30 dB, at drift 0 too, and a long window combs the treble once the ratio leaves 1 (the first notch at 2 / ((ratio − 1) × window) Hz: 5 kHz at the default drift's edge with 0.2 s). The engine check reads both values from the graph.
- **A node a bypass lift creates fades its input in** (`liftFade(fadeIn)`: `Line.ar(1 − fadeIn, 1, fxMixLag)`, audio rate; `fadeIn` 1 only from `setEffectBypass`'s lift, #308); every other node takes its input whole (`fadeIn` 0, a gain of exactly 1.0), which the check's impulse measurements and its comparison of the plain chain rely on. Keep the `Line` audio rate: on SuperCollider 3.13 a control-rate `Line`'s first block is already a step up, a step of up to 2.7 % of the signal at the node's first sample.
- The filter, delay and reverb SynthDef defaults equal the Lua param defaults (the script never bangs its params); the kept controls start from the SynthDefs' defaults. The Lua test compares the SynthDefs' defaults with the params' (and the scale and bypass params' defaults with the engine's start state, #460); the engine check reads a fresh engine's nodes before any command reaches them (#364).
- A stage gets its engine's send buses through `stageControls` (`mixBusControls`: `delaySendOut`, `reverbSendOut`), never only from its SynthDef defaults (they are the buses of the last engine built).
- Never size a delay line from a control: the FDN's lines (sixteen buffers of `fdnLineFrames`, 3.9), diffusers and the delay are sized by constants. The delay's line is two mono buffers of `delayLineFrames` (2^18 frames, 5.46 s at 48 kHz; 3.8 design §0.2), allocated once with the effects area's state and read with `BufDelayC`, which clips a delay to the largest power of two in the buffer less one frame, so its time is clipped to 5.4 s. The buffers are ordinary RAM (2 MB, counted in `fixedBufferBytes`, `docs/notes/engine/performance.md`), not the real-time pool; before 3.8 the line was a `DelayC` of 2.7 s (two lines of 2^17 samples, 1 MB of the pool). The engine stub reads `setDelay`'s time limit from the literal `time.clip(0.01, 5.4)` (3.8: clipped before the freeze's gate and the lag, `docs/notes/engine/delay.md`), and Lua's `DELAY.max_seconds` must equal it (a test).
- **Every `Synth(\strataDelay` passes `bufL` and `bufR`**: a bufnum left at its default 0, or a negative one, is buffer 0 (`GET_BUF`), so the node would write into whatever buffer 0 is. A check's own delay node gets a line of its own, never the engine's, which it would write into while the engine's node runs. **Every `Synth(\strataDelay`, `Synth(\strataReverb` and `Synth(\strataReverbFDN` passes `shiftL` and `shiftR`**, its two shimmer rings, for the same reason (3.8; a source test reads every site: `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`), and **every `Synth(\strataReverbFDN` passes `lineBuf`**, the first of its sixteen line buffers (3.9, the same test: `docs/notes/engine/reverb.md`).
- **A lifted delay starts empty**: the bundle that frees the delay node zeroes both buffers (`emptyDelayLine`, `/b_zero`), so nothing of the old line comes back after a lift. (A new `BufDelayC` also reads 0 for every frame it has not written yet, for its first pass of the buffer, so the lift is silent either way; the zero keeps the buffers empty for any other reader of them.)
- **One audio-rate `LocalIn` a SynthDef** (SuperCollider's `LocalIn` help: a def holds at most one): a def's feedback paths (the delay's two lines, the FDN's eight and its shimmer loop's two, FreeVerb2's shimmer loop) widen its one `LocalIn`/`LocalOut` pair, never add a second (#777).
- **Shimmer 0 is each effect without it, bit for bit** (3.8's shimmer, `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`): the delay's feedback blend adds an exact 0 and each reverb's loop is an exact 0 into its input; `shimmer` and `shimmerRatio` are kept with each effect's other controls, through a bypass.
- **Ping-pong at 0 is 3.6's graph, exactly** (3.7): `pingPong` (default 0, `pingPong.clip(0, 1).lag(0.05)`) only crossfades the two existing lines' inputs: no new line. `setDelay`'s four values are kept together, so a lift re-creates the node with the kept `pingPong`.
- **The freeze and the clear** (3.8) are `\strataDelay`'s `frozen` and `t_clear` (`setDelayFreeze`, `clearDelay`): `frozen` is kept with `setDelay`'s four and passed by `startDelay`, but a bypass's free sets the kept value to 0 with the line it empties and a freeze sent while no node runs keeps 0, so a lift never starts a frozen node (#889); `t_clear` is never kept; a freeze never goes through `setDelay`'s feedback, and a clear never zeroes the line. `docs/notes/engine/delay.md`.

## The effects area (`StrataEffects.sc`, order 60)

The delay's line, `\strataDelay`, `startDelay` and `setDelay` are in `StrataDelay.sc` (3.8), which has no area of its own: the effects area owns the node and its place in the chain and calls `allocDelayLine` from its `allocState` (`allocEffectBuses`), `addDelayDef` from `addEffectDefs` (directly, never inside a function: the structure area walks each SynthDef function's home frame up to `areaCall` through method frames only), `addDelayCommands` from `addEffectCommands`, `startDelay` from the chain's start and a lift, `emptyDelayLine` with a bypass's free and `freeDelayLine` from `freeEffects`, after every stop. `delayLine` is `areaState[\effects][\delayLine]`, `[left, right]`; the bufnums never change. The FDN's lines and `setReverbFreeze` are in `StrataReverb.sc` (3.9, `docs/notes/engine/reverb.md`), the same way: `allocReverbLines` from `allocEffectBuses`, `addReverbCommands` from `addEffectCommands`, `emptyReverbLines` with a reverb's bypass free, `freeReverbLines` from `freeEffects`; the def stays here with the standard tier's.

The chain, at the tail of `context.xg` in signal order: `\filter` (`mainBus` → `filterBus`), `\filterDelay` (delay send → its copy), `\filterReverb` (reverb send → its copy), `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`.

- `\strataFilter` (`in, out, freq=5000, res=0.5, filterType=0, mix=0`): the NaN guard on its input (`zeroBadValues` per channel), then `multimodeFilter` with the cutoff `freq.lag(0.05).clip(20, 20000)`, `res.clip(0, 1)` and `mix.lag(0.05).clip(0, 1)` (written in the SynthDef, where the Lua stub reads the limits). `multimodeFilter` runs `RLPF` and `BPF` at rq `1 − res × 0.95` on the rise-limited cutoff (`riseLimitedCutoff`) and makes the highpass as the input less both, each type weighted by `BinaryOpUGen('==', type, index).lag(0.02)`. A cutoff that jumps up by more than about a factor of 1.5 glides at 100 octaves a second (the whole range in 0.1 s); a highpass moved fast differs from 3.0's `RHPF` in its transients (-40 dB of its level under a 1 Hz sweep at res 0.5, -22 dB under a 6 Hz sweep, -10 dB at res 0.9) and, slammed down at high res, rings as the lowpass does on the same move; in steady state it is `RHPF` within 6e-8. There is one instance on each bus it filters, before the effects (dry, delay send, reverb send; never the returns): each filters what goes to one place. `setFilter` sets all three in one bundle (the Lua stub reads the FX controls from the literal `\filter` line; the other two follow in a loop).
- `\strataDelay` (`in, out, bufL, bufR, time=0.375, feedback=0.4, mix=0, pingPong=0, fadeIn=0, frozen=0, t_clear=0, shiftL=0, shiftR=0, shimmer=0, shimmerRatio=2`): `BufDelayC` on the line's two buffers (`bufL`, `bufR`; the time `time.clip(0.01, 5.4)` through the freeze's gate, then `.lag(0.1)`) fed by the filtered delay send times `liftFade(fadeIn)` plus `LocalIn` (the feedback path is not faded), the delayed signal times `feedback.clip(0, 0.95)` sent back; it returns the delayed signal only, times its level, `mix.clip(0, 1).lag(this.fxMixLag)`. With `p` the lagged `pingPong`, the left line takes `inL + p·inR + (1 − p)·fbL + p·fbR` and the right `(1 − p)·inR + (1 − p)·fbR + p·fbL`: at 0 each line its own channel and feedback, at 1 both channels summed into the left line, the left's output feeding the right line and the right's the left, so the repeats alternate sides. It plays what 3.7's `DelayC` lines played, sample for sample (the check's largest difference is 0 at a fixed and a moving time: both interpolate a power-of-two line with the same cubic), and takes 24.4 KB of the pool with 3.8's freeze and clear (`frozen`, `t_clear`: `docs/notes/engine/delay.md`; 7.5-8.4 KB before them, 16.9 KB with them) and its shimmer (the feedback a blend of `delayed` and its shifted copy, by `shimmer`: `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`), under its 30 KB limit (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`).
- `\strataReverb` (`in, delayIn, out, mix=0.43, size=0.8, damp=0.5, delayToReverb=1, fadeIn=0, shiftL=0, shiftR=0, shimmer=0, shimmerRatio=2`): the standard tier's. `FreeVerb2` at mix 1 on the filtered reverb send plus the delay's return times `delayToReverb.clip(0, 1).lag(this.fxMixLag)` (1, the default, reverberates the echoes as 3.7 did; 0 leaves a voice with no reverb send dry echoes), times `liftFade(fadeIn)`, plus its shimmer loop (its one `LocalIn.ar(2)`: `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`), returned with its bad values zeroed times its lagged level (`mix`). The clips are literals in the def, where the Lua stub reads `setReverb`'s and `setShimmer`'s limits. 125.6 KB of the pool with shimmer (103.6 KB before it, both by the memory area's twenty nodes), under 130 KB (125 until 2a's fix round 2: two probe steps of room was too thin for a CI gate).
- `\strataReverbFDN` (the same controls, `delayToReverb` and shimmer's four among them, input, fade and return; its shimmer loop on channels 8 and 9 of its `LocalIn.ar(10)`, entering before the diffusers): the high tier's (`startReverb` picks it by `tier`), a feedback delay network of core UGens, so CI and every norns run the same thing. Four `AllpassN` diffusers per channel (`fdnDiffuserSeconds`, 5.3-27.1 ms, decay 19 × the delay, a gain of about 0.7); eight lines (`fdnLineSeconds`: 1487, 1759, 2063, 2543, 2927, 3499, 4271 and 5119 samples at 48 kHz, primes of about 31-107 ms) scaled by `0.6 + 0.4 × size` and shortened by the `LocalIn`/`LocalOut` block, each two buffers read on the same time by a `BufDelayL` and a `BufDelayN` (3.9, #819: `docs/notes/engine/reverb.md`; before, each a `DelayL` of its own pool memory); a Householder matrix (`line − 2/8 × Σ lines`, lossless); a `OnePole` per line at `damp × 0.4` (FreeVerb's damping per pass); and per-line feedback `(0.7 + 0.28 × size) ** (length / 1378 samples)`, FreeVerb's comb feedback per its mean comb length, so its decay per second at low frequencies is FreeVerb's. Lines 3 and 7 drift slowly (`LFNoise1`, 0.5-0.6 ms) against metallic ringing; left is lines 1, 3, 5, 7 summed, right 2, 4, 6, 8 (input gain 0.25, about FreeVerb2's level, within 1-2 dB from size 0.2 to 1). Measured on the check (the impulse response, counted in server blocks): RT60 about 2.10-2.18 s against FreeVerb2's 2.17 s at size 0.8, a ratio of about 0.97-1.01 (12 runs at once and alone; 0.984 with 3.9's lines) (the check requires 30%; only the FDN varies, with its drift's random start). Its pool: 229.2 KB by the memory area's twenty nodes while its lines were `DelayL`'s memory (208.1 before shimmer, against FreeVerb2's 103.6 KB), 103.6 KB with 3.9's lines in buffers and its freeze (under `fdnDefLimitKB`, 110 KB; this check's probes read about 101 KB). **The freeze is `docs/notes/engine/reverb.md`'s** (3.9, #819): 3.8's package 3a built one on the `DelayL` lines and cut it (its band above 6 kHz fell 2.4 to 3.2 dB in six seconds: a control-rate time that has moved never lands on a whole sample again, and the interpolation darkens the loop at every pass); #819 holds its figures and causes.
- `\strataMaster` (`in, delayIn, reverbIn, out, amp=0.7, drift=0.01`): `masterSum` (the filtered dry bus and both returns, summed, times `masterSumGain`, 0.7: 3.7's dry share at its default reverb mix, so the limiter and the saturator see what they saw; `amp`'s 0.7 is another, at the output), then drift, `Limiter.ar(sig, 0.95)` to catch filter resonance and delay feedback peaks, then the tanh saturation. The limiter must stay before the saturator: `(sig * 2).tanh * 0.5` bounds its output to ±0.5, so a limiter after it could never act (the engine check asserts the order). Drift (master and Sub) is an LFO at a fixed slow rate scaled by the drift amount (the ratio `1 + LFNoise1(0.2 Hz) × drift × 0.2`: ±0.2 % at the default 0.01, ±2 % at 0.1): tape drift 0 = none; the Sub drift param's minimum stays 0.001. The master's `PitchShift` has a 10 ms window and no time dispersion (#306): at drift 0 it is a two-sample delay, exactly (the check compares the output with the limiter and saturation alone on the input, the filtered dry bus times `masterSumGain`, two samples late: within 1.5e-7), and a steady 1 kHz tone keeps its level within 0.2 dB at drift 0, 0.01 and 0.1 (it moved 23-29 dB with 3.0's 0.2 s window and 10 ms dispersion). What is left at the largest drift is the interpolation's loss and a comb above about 3 kHz (a 5 kHz tone dips about 3 dB, slowly). Taking the flutter out gave back the 5.7 dB above 1 kHz it cost every patch: all seven voices at the default drift came out 1.8 dB louder (RMS; 4.4 dB above 1 kHz, peaks 0.298 to 0.324), FM, Pulse, Karplus and Ring 3.7 dB, Resonator and Sub 1.4 dB. The shifter's delay lines are sized by the window (about 16 KB of the pool, where the 0.2 s window took 256 KB). The engine check compares the plain chain at `masterSum`, before the shifter.

**Commands.** `setDelayFreeze` and `clearDelay` (3.8) are `docs/notes/engine/delay.md`'s, `setShimmer` (3.8) `docs/notes/engine/shimmer.md`'s. `setDelay` first ignores a call with any non-finite argument, with one console line (`fastModValuesFinite`, as 3.4's commands do: a NaN in the node's lag or feedback loop would stay there until a bypass re-created it; the final review's engine Minor 3). `setDelay` and `setReverb` keep their values (`areaState[\effects][\controls]`, starting from the SynthDefs' defaults; the reverb's four, `mix`, `size`, `damp` and `delayToReverb`), then set the node (the level times `effectLive`: 0 while bypassed); no bus is set. `setMasterDrift` sets the master. The ranges: `setDelay`'s time 0.01-5.4 s, level and `pingPong` 0-1; `setReverb`'s level, size, damping and `delayToReverb` 0-1 (`"ffff"`, 3.8: the fourth widened it as 3.7's `pingPong` widened `setDelay`; it prints no line for a bad value); `setFilter`'s type 0 lowpass, 1 highpass, 2 bandpass, and its mix 0 leaves the filter out (the three nodes keep running).

**Bypass** (`setBypass(effect, bypassed)`, `"ii"`: 0 delay, 1 reverb, `bypassKeys`; anything else ignored; `bypassed` 1 bypasses, 0 lifts), `setEffectBypass`:
- Bypass: mark it bypassed, set the node's level to 0 (the return fades over its lag, so nothing jumps; the dry sound and the other effect are untouched), then after `bypassFadeTime` (0.05 s + one block) free the node, if this bypass's token is still the pending one (`areaState[\effects][\pendingFrees]`). The free's bundle also zeroes the delay's line (`emptyDelayLine`) or the FDN's sixteen lines (`emptyReverbLines`, none on the standard tier), dropping the kept `frozen` with them, and the effect's two shimmer rings (`emptyShimmerRings`). Freeing `\strataDelay` returns its CPU and its few KB of the pool; its line is buffers and stays (before 3.8 the free returned the 1 MB of its `DelayC` lines).
- When the free lands (#308's risk, settled by a check, no change): `SystemClock.sched(bypassFadeTime, ...)` counts from the handler's own time, `thisThread.seconds`. Every command reaches the engine as an OSC message (matron's, through CroneEngine's `OSCdef`s), and sclang runs an OSC handler with its thread's time set to when the handler runs, not when the message arrived: measured on 3.13 and 3.14.1 with the interpreter kept busy for 200 ms, the handler's `thisThread.seconds` was 200 ms after the send and a `sched(0.05)` from it fired 50 ms after the handler ran. So the wait always counts from the message that sets the level to 0, however late the handler runs; a free whose own time comes while sclang is busy only runs later (the node lives a little longer, and a lift meanwhile still cancels it). Both go to the server as immediate messages in that order, so on the server the free follows the level's 0 by the wait less at most one block, which the extra block in `bypassFadeTime` covers. `checkBypassFreeTiming` sends `/command/setBypass` to sclang over OSC with the interpreter busy for 100 ms: the step on the delay's return stays at its steady size (0.0042 against 0.0041; a 5 ms wait gives 0.06-0.32). Keep the free scheduled from the handler's time: a time taken from the message's arrival, or a caller in a Routine whose logical time lags the real one (only the engine check calls commands that way), would shorten the wait.
- Lift: if the free is still pending, cancel it (the node is still there: nothing is created or faded); otherwise create the node in its place from the kept controls at level 0 and with `fadeIn` 1 (`startDelay`, `startReverb` take a mix and a fade), so its input rises over 0.05 s (`liftFade`) and the first echo or the tail starts without a step: the sends carry audio the whole time, and an empty line that took its input whole recorded a step and played it one delay time later as a click, repeated by the feedback (#308; the check's largest sample step on the return after a lift is 0.0044 against 0.0041 in steady state, where it was 0.25). In the same bundle, set the kept level (the node clips it), which fades in through the lag. A free in flight is sent before the creation, so the server never holds two. `startEffectsChain` starts both with `fadeIn` 0.
- `setDelay`/`setReverb` while bypassed keep their values, all four of each (`setSynth` skips the missing node, or sets the fading one at level 0), and the lift creates the node with them.
- Stopping the engine clears the pending frees (`stopEffects`).
- The master filters are never freed: they are cheap, carry the NaN guard, and their outputs must be written.
- Free and re-create was chosen over `/n_run 0` (design Decision 3): a paused delay line would keep minutes-old audio and play it on resume. Should it change, the change is local to this file.

**The bypass's three states.** Each effect's bypass is held in three records, `areaState[\effects][\bypassed][key]`, the pending free's token (`\pendingFrees`, an `Object` the timer compares by identity) and the node (`synths[key]`), and they form one of three triples, never a name of its own; no other area reads them (`effectLive` reads `bypassed` for the commands that keep values while bypassed):

| State | `bypassed` | token | node |
|---|---|---|---|
| running | false | none | yes |
| fading | true | set | yes, its level 0 |
| freed | true | none | none |

| State | Event | Next | Effect |
|---|---|---|---|
| running | a bypass | fading | the level set to 0 (the return fades over its lag), a new token, the free scheduled `bypassFadeTime` on |
| running | a lift | running | nothing (the value is unchanged) |
| fading | a bypass | fading | nothing |
| fading | a lift | running | the token dropped, the free canceled: the same node, its kept level restored |
| fading | the timer, its token still the pending one | freed | the node freed, in one bundle with the delay's line or the FDN's lines emptied and its kept `frozen` set to 0 (`emptyDelayLine`, #889; `emptyReverbLines`, #819) and the effect's shimmer rings zeroed (`emptyShimmerRings`) |
| fading | the timer of an older token (a lift and a bypass inside the wait) | fading | nothing: the newer token's timer frees it |
| freed | a bypass | freed | nothing |
| freed | a lift | running | a new node in its place from the kept controls, at level 0 and with `fadeIn` 1, then the kept level (`startDelay`, `startReverb`, #308) |
| fading | the engine stops (`stopEffects`) | (the area's end) | the pending frees forgotten, so no timer frees a node the area's own stop has |

How a freeze meets these three: `docs/notes/engine/delay.md`, Freeze by bypass.

**What drives its rows** (`test/engine_check/effects.scd`): running's bypass, `"effects: setBypass first sets the delay's level to 0, keeping the node for the fade and setting no bus ..."` and `"effects: setBypass sets the reverb's level to 0 for its fade, setting no bus ..."`; fading's lift, `"effects: a bypass lifted inside its fade cancels the free (the same node, its mix restored)"`; fading's timer, `"effects: setBypass frees the delay after the mix fade ..."`, `"effects: setBypass frees the reverb after its fade and leaves the delay ..."`, `"effects: a bypass's free empties the delay's line ..."` and `"effects: a bypass whose handler runs 100 ms late frees the delay after its fade ..."`; freed's lift, `"effects: setBypass frees the delay after the level's fade and re-creates it when lifted ..."`, `"effects: lifting the reverb's bypass re-creates it before the master ..."`, `"effects: a lifted delay starts empty ..."` and `"effects: a lifted % starts without a step ..."`; an older token's timer, the toggling checks, `"effects: 20 Hz bypass toggling for 2 s keeps at most one delay node and none once bypassed ..."` and `"effects: 15 Hz bypass toggling frees and re-creates the delay, never two nodes and none once bypassed ..."`; the kept `frozen` dropped at the free, `test/engine_check/delay.scd`'s `"delay: frozen is kept through a bypass's fade and a lift inside it ..."`. No check sends a repeated value alone (running's lift, fading's and freed's bypass), nor stops the engine in a fade.

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/effects.scd`)
