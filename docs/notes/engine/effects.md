## Must not break

- **Limiter before the tanh saturator** in `\strataMaster`. The engine check asserts the order.
- **Sends at 1 are 3.0's chain.** Each voice's stage (`voiceOutput`, `docs/notes/engine/mix.md`) writes dry `(1 − s_d·m_d)(1 − s_r·m_r)`, delay send `s_d`, reverb send `s_r·(1 − s_d·m_d)`; the delay returns `m_d·D`, the reverb `m_r·R` from its send plus the delay's return, and `masterSum` adds dry + (1 − m_r)·delay return + reverb return. Every reader of a mix lags it `fxMixLag` (0.05 s, 3.0's delay lag; 3.0's reverb mix was not lagged, so a moving reverb mix glides where 3.0's stepped), and `fxMix` and the delay's and reverb's own `mix` controls are set in one bundle so every reader moves in the same block. The engine check compares this against check-only copies of 3.0's chain (-60 dB).
- **Bypass is explicit**: only `setBypass` (Lua's `delay_bypass`/`reverb_bypass`, never a scene field) frees or creates the delay or reverb node. A mix of 0 (LFO, scene fade, PARAMS, CC) only silences and never frees or creates a node.
- **Replace, never stack, and node order**: a bypass waits `bypassFadeTime` (the lag plus one block, counted on `SystemClock` from the handler's own time, which is when it sends the mix: see Bypass below) after broadcasting the mix as 0 and then frees the node; a lift inside the wait cancels the free (the same node); a later lift creates it at its place (`\addAfter` `\filterReverb` for the delay, `\addBefore` the master for the reverb), so the chain stays filter ×3 → delay → reverb → master and there is never more than one delay or reverb node.
- **`effectKeys`**: the chain's synths (`\filter`, `\filterDelay`, `\filterReverb`, `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`) never end on their own (the engine frees and creates the bypassed ones itself), so `setSynth` sends to them plainly and skips a missing key.
- **NaN guards**: each `\strataFilter` replaces bad values on its bus (`zeroBadValues`) before the delay and the reverb. The master filters are never freed: freeing one would leave its output unwritten.
- **`\strataFilter` and the voice filter**: never compare a UGen with `==` (that is object identity); use `BinaryOpUGen('==', ...)`. A type change crossfades over 20 ms.
- **The filter runs `RLPF` and `BPF` only; the highpass is the input less both, never an `RHPF`** (`multimodeFilter`). The three share their poles and their numerators add up to the denominator, so the complement is RHPF's response (within 6e-8, -140 dB) without RHPF's state, which grows as 1 / cutoff² and bursts when the cutoff jumps up (#305). Keep the grouping `low × (w0 − w1) + band × (w2 − w1) + sig × w1`: a settled lowpass or bandpass is then `RLPF` or `BPF` plus exact zeros, bit for bit.
- **A cutoff rises at most `cutoffRiseRate` (100 octaves a second) through `riseLimitedCutoff`, inside `multimodeFilter`**, so both callers get it with no change at a call site: `min(cutoff, ceiling)`, the ceiling a `Slew` of the cutoff in octaves (up 100, down unlimited) times 1.0001. A cutoff that falls, holds or rises more slowly (a step up to about a factor of 1.5 through the 0.05 s lag) passes bit for bit; the engine check's step from 20 Hz to 20 kHz peaks 2.18 at res 1 where it peaked 2883.
- **The master's drift shifter has no time dispersion and a window of at most 0.02 s** (`PitchShift.ar(sig, 0.01, driftMod, 0, 0)`, #306): a dispersion makes every steady tone flutter by 25-30 dB, at drift 0 too, and a long window combs the treble once the ratio leaves 1 (the first notch at 2 / ((ratio − 1) × window) Hz: 5 kHz at the default drift's edge with 0.2 s). The engine check reads both values from the graph.
- **A node a bypass lift creates fades its input in** (`liftFade(fadeIn)`: `Line.ar(1 − fadeIn, 1, fxMixLag)`, audio rate; `fadeIn` 1 only from `setEffectBypass`'s lift, #308); every other node takes its input whole (`fadeIn` 0, a gain of exactly 1.0), which the check's impulse measurements and its comparison with 3.0's chain rely on. Keep the `Line` audio rate: on SuperCollider 3.13 a control-rate `Line`'s first block is already a step up, a step of up to 2.7 % of the signal at the node's first sample.
- The filter, delay and reverb SynthDef defaults equal the Lua param defaults (the script never bangs its params); the kept controls start from the SynthDefs' defaults. The Lua test compares the SynthDefs' defaults with the params' (and the scale and bypass params' defaults with the engine's start state, #460); the engine check reads a fresh engine's nodes before any command reaches them (#364).
- A stage gets its engine's send and mix buses through `stageControls` (`mixBusControls`), never only from its SynthDef defaults (they are the buses of the last engine built).
- Never size a delay line from a control: the FDN's lines, diffusers and the delay are sized by constants.

The voice filter, the sends and `voiceOutput` run in each voice's mix stage since 3.3: `docs/notes/engine/mix.md`.

## The effects area (`StrataEffects.sc`, order 60)

The chain, at the tail of `context.xg` in signal order: `\filter` (`mainBus` → `filterBus`), `\filterDelay` (delay send → its copy), `\filterReverb` (reverb send → its copy), `\delay`, `\reverb`, `\master`.

- `\strataFilter` (`in, out, freq=5000, res=0.5, filterType=0, mix=0`): the NaN guard on its input (`zeroBadValues` per channel), then `multimodeFilter` with the cutoff `freq.lag(0.05).clip(20, 20000)`, `res.clip(0, 1)` and `mix.lag(0.05).clip(0, 1)` (written in the SynthDef, where the Lua stub reads the limits). `multimodeFilter` runs `RLPF` and `BPF` at rq `1 − res × 0.95` on the rise-limited cutoff (`riseLimitedCutoff`) and makes the highpass as the input less both, each type weighted by `BinaryOpUGen('==', type, index).lag(0.02)`. A cutoff that jumps up by more than about a factor of 1.5 glides at 100 octaves a second (the whole range in 0.1 s); a highpass moved fast differs from 3.0's `RHPF` in its transients (-40 dB of its level under a 1 Hz sweep at res 0.5, -22 dB under a 6 Hz sweep, -10 dB at res 0.9) and, slammed down at high res, rings as the lowpass does on the same move; in steady state it is `RHPF` within 6e-8. The filter is linear, so its three instances on the three parts equal one on their sum, exactly where 3.0 had it. `setFilter` sets all three in one bundle (the Lua stub reads the FX controls from the literal `\filter` line; the other two follow in a loop).
- `\strataDelay` (`in, out, time=0.375, feedback=0.4, mix=0, fadeIn=0`): `DelayC` (max 2 s, `time.lag(0.1)`) fed by the filtered delay send times `liftFade(fadeIn)` plus `LocalIn` (the feedback path is not faded), the delayed signal times `feedback.clip(0, 0.95)` sent back; it returns the delayed signal only, times `mix.clip(0, 1).lag(0.05)` (`m_d·D`).
- `\strataReverb` (`in, delayIn, out, mix=0.3, size=0.8, damp=0.5, fadeIn=0`): the standard tier's. `FreeVerb2` at mix 1 on the filtered reverb send plus the delay's return, times `liftFade(fadeIn)`, returned times its lagged mix (`m_r·R`). `FreeVerb2`'s mix is exactly the linear crossfade (checked), so this equals 3.0's reverb.
- `\strataReverbFDN` (the same controls, input fade and return): the high tier's (`startReverb` picks it by `tier`), a feedback delay network of core UGens, so CI and every norns run the same thing. Four `AllpassN` diffusers per channel (`fdnDiffuserSeconds`, 5.3-27.1 ms, decay 19 × the delay, a gain of about 0.7); eight `DelayL` lines (`fdnLineSeconds`: 1487, 1759, 2063, 2543, 2927, 3499, 4271 and 5119 samples at 48 kHz, primes of about 31-107 ms) scaled by `0.6 + 0.4 × size` and shortened by the `LocalIn`/`LocalOut` block; a Householder matrix (`line − 2/8 × Σ lines`, lossless); a `OnePole` per line at `damp × 0.4` (FreeVerb's damping per pass); and per-line feedback `(0.7 + 0.28 × size) ** (length / 1378 samples)`, FreeVerb's comb feedback per its mean comb length, so its decay per second at low frequencies is FreeVerb's. Lines 3 and 7 drift slowly (`LFNoise1`, 0.5-0.6 ms) against metallic ringing; left is lines 1, 3, 5, 7 summed, right 2, 4, 6, 8 (input gain 0.25, about FreeVerb2's level, within 1-2 dB from size 0.2 to 1). Measured on the check (the impulse response, counted in server blocks): RT60 about 2.10-2.18 s against FreeVerb2's 2.17 s at size 0.8, a ratio of about 0.97-1.01 (12 runs at once and alone) (the check requires 30%; only the FDN varies, with its drift's random start), and about 202 KB of the pool against FreeVerb2's 100 KB (each line's `DelayL` is sized by its own length, not the longest).
- `\strataMaster` (`in, delayIn, reverbIn, fxMix, out, amp=0.7, drift=0.01`): `masterSum` (the filtered dry bus, the delay's return times `1 − m_r` from `fxMix`, and the reverb's return), then drift, `Limiter.ar(sig, 0.95)` to catch filter resonance and delay feedback peaks, then the tanh saturation. The limiter must stay before the saturator: `(sig * 2).tanh * 0.5` bounds its output to ±0.5, so a limiter after it could never act (the engine check asserts the order). Drift (master and Sub) is an LFO at a fixed slow rate scaled by the drift amount (the ratio `1 + LFNoise1(0.2 Hz) × drift × 0.2`: ±0.2 % at the default 0.01, ±2 % at 0.1): tape drift 0 = none; the Sub drift param's minimum stays 0.001. The master's `PitchShift` has a 10 ms window and no time dispersion (#306): at drift 0 it is a two-sample delay, exactly (the check compares the output with the limiter and saturation alone on the input two samples late: within 1.5e-7), and a steady 1 kHz tone keeps its level within 0.2 dB at drift 0, 0.01 and 0.1 (it moved 23-29 dB with 3.0's 0.2 s window and 10 ms dispersion). What is left at the largest drift is the interpolation's loss and a comb above about 3 kHz (a 5 kHz tone dips about 3 dB, slowly). Taking the flutter out gave back the 5.7 dB above 1 kHz it cost every patch: all seven voices at the default drift came out 1.8 dB louder (RMS; 4.4 dB above 1 kHz, peaks 0.298 to 0.324), FM, Pulse, Karplus and Ring 3.7 dB, Resonator and Sub 1.4 dB. The shifter's delay lines are sized by the window (about 16 KB of the pool, where the 0.2 s window took 256 KB). The engine check compares the 3.0 copies against `masterSum`, since 3.0's master had the flutter.

**Commands.** `setDelay` and `setReverb` keep their values (`areaState[\effects][\controls]`, starting from the SynthDefs' defaults), then in one bundle set the node (the mix times `effectLive`: 0 while bypassed) and broadcast both mixes on `fxMix` (`broadcastFxMix`, `liveMix`: the kept mix, 0 while bypassed). `setMasterDrift` sets the master.

**Bypass** (`setBypass(effect, bypassed)`, `"ii"`: 0 delay, 1 reverb, `bypassKeys`; anything else ignored), `setEffectBypass`:
- Bypass: mark it bypassed, set the node's mix and the broadcast to 0 in one bundle (the dry gains return to full and the return fades with the same lag, so nothing jumps), then after `bypassFadeTime` (0.05 s + one block) free the node, if this bypass's token is still the pending one (`areaState[\effects][\pendingFrees]`). Freeing `\strataDelay` returns its 1 MB `DelayC` to the pool (the check measures about 1 MB).
- When the free lands (#308's risk, settled by a check, no change): `SystemClock.sched(bypassFadeTime, ...)` counts from the handler's own time, `thisThread.seconds`. Every command reaches the engine as an OSC message (matron's, through CroneEngine's `OSCdef`s), and sclang runs an OSC handler with its thread's time set to when the handler runs, not when the message arrived: measured on 3.13 and 3.14.1 with the interpreter kept busy for 200 ms, the handler's `thisThread.seconds` was 200 ms after the send and a `sched(0.05)` from it fired 50 ms after the handler ran. So the wait always counts from the bundle that sets the mix to 0, however late the handler runs; a free whose own time comes while sclang is busy only runs later (the node lives a little longer, and a lift meanwhile still cancels it). Both go to the server as immediate messages in that order, so on the server the free follows the mix by the wait less at most one block, which the extra block in `bypassFadeTime` covers. `checkBypassFreeTiming` sends `/command/setBypass` to sclang over OSC with the interpreter busy for 100 ms: the step on the delay's return stays at its steady size (0.0042 against 0.0041; a 5 ms wait gives 0.06-0.32). Keep the free scheduled from the handler's time: a time taken from the message's arrival, or a caller in a Routine whose logical time lags the real one (only the engine check calls commands that way), would shorten the wait.
- Lift: if the free is still pending, cancel it (the node is still there: nothing is created or faded); otherwise create the node in its place from the kept controls at mix 0 and with `fadeIn` 1 (`startDelay`, `startReverb` take a mix and a fade), so its input rises over 0.05 s (`liftFade`) and the first echo or the tail starts without a step: the sends carry audio the whole time, and an empty line that took its input whole recorded a step and played it one delay time later as a click, repeated by the feedback (#308; the check's largest sample step on the return after a lift is 0.0044 against 0.0041 in steady state, where it was 0.25). In the same bundle, set the kept mix and broadcast it, which fades in through the lag. A free in flight is sent before the creation, so the server never holds two. `startEffectsChain` starts both with `fadeIn` 0.
- `setDelay`/`setReverb` while bypassed keep their values (`setSynth` skips the missing node, or sets the fading one at mix 0) and the broadcast stays 0.
- Stopping the engine clears the pending frees (`stopEffects`).
- The master filters are never freed: they are cheap, carry the NaN guard, and their outputs must be written.
- Free and re-create was chosen over `/n_run 0` (design Decision 3): a paused delay line would keep minutes-old audio and play it on resume. Should it change, the change is local to this file.
