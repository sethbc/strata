## Must not break

- **A voice's synth is its type** (3.8, design §0.3): `voiceTypeDefs` lists each type's voice SynthDef in type order, one name a line, and a type package only appends its line (Lua's types, package 0d, are registered in the same order). Voice v starts on type v (`areaState[\voices][\types]`, set in `allocVoiceState`), so the list has at least `numVoices` entries (the stub's parse fails otherwise). Every reader asks `voiceDefFor(voice)`: `startVoiceSynth`, `gateVoice`'s re-gate, an extra note's `startNoteSynth`, `notesUnfrozen`; `ownNotePairs` asks `voiceTypeDefs.includes(defName)`. `voiceSynthDefs`, declared in `lib/Engine_Strata.sc`, is no longer assigned, and nothing reads it.
- **`setVoiceType(voice, type)` ignores anything else without a line**: a voice outside 0..`numVoices` - 1 or a type outside 0..`numVoiceTypes` - 1, or either not an integer (`validVoice`, `validVoiceType`), as the other voice commands do (`docs/notes/engine/voices.md`); it is no guarded command. The voice's own type again is a no-op.
- **A stopped, buffer or input voice only records its type**: its next start on the synth source (`voiceOn`, or `setGrainSource(v, 0)` on a running voice) plays the type's def. So does a running voice whose voice synth failed to start (`synths[voiceKey]` nil): the watcher's restart (`voiceSynthFreed`) or the next start takes the type.
- **A switch cuts, never releases**: on a running synth voice `replaceVoiceType` runs `switchVoiceType` in one bundle: it frees the voice's cutting set (`freeCuttingSynths`), cuts its voice synth (`cutVoiceSynth`: the kill fade, `switchCutTime`), starts the new def at the head of `voiceGroup` (`startVoiceSynth`) and restarts its live voice synth notes (`restartVoiceNotes`). So type switches leave a voice two voice synths at most, the new and the cut, however fast they come (the source-switch cycles keep their own bound, three: `docs/notes/engine/lifecycle.md`). Both methods keep closures (the bundle's function, the new synth's `onFree`), so both are called only through `areaCall` and are `AREA_CALL_METHODS` entries (`test/unit/structure_test.lua`).
- **`gateVoice` re-gates a releasing voice synth only of the voice's current type** (ruling 15): one of another type (switched while the voice was stopped) is cut (`freeCuttingSynths`, then `cutVoiceSynth`) before the new def starts, so a stopped voice's long release never comes back as the old type. No state is needed: one def a type, so the releasing synth's `defName` is its type.
- **A switch leaves the freeze alone**: a frozen synth voice keeps its ring writer (paused, or the spectral writer on high) and its `\stream` notes, whose grains read the ring and never name the def; only the voice synth changes. Held (hold), the voice synth is not heard at all. Spectral, the writer holds the magnitudes (`PV_MagFreeze`) while the phases go on coming from the voice bus, so the new type's voice synth sets the held spectrum's phases: the spectrum stays the old type's, its level moves about a decibel against the same freeze without a switch (3.13). A buffer or input voice has no voice synth to switch.

## Owns

`lib/engine/StrataVoices.sc` (the voices area): `voiceTypeDefs`, `numVoiceTypes`, `validVoiceType`, `voiceType(voice)` (0-based, into the list), `voiceDefFor(voice)` (the def name), `areaState[\voices][\types]` and the command `setVoiceType` (beside `setGrainSource`). `lib/engine/StrataLifecycle.sc`: `replaceVoiceType`, `switchVoiceType` and `gateVoice`'s cut. `lib/engine/StrataNotes.sc`: `restartVoiceNotes`. `noteMainRanges` (`lib/engine/StrataVoiceDefs.sc`) holds one entry a line, in type order, so a type package appends one line there too.

## The switch

`setVoiceType` records the type (`areaState[\voices][\types][voice] = type`), then, on a running synth-source voice with a live voice synth, calls `replaceVoiceType` through `areaCall`. `switchVoiceType`, inside that bundle:

1. `freeCuttingSynths(voice)`: whatever an earlier cut or switch left in its kill fade goes at once (a source switch frees it the same way, so a cutting grain synth loses the rest of its fade as it does there).
2. The live voice synth leaves `synths` and is cut (`cutVoiceSynth`, kept in `cuttingSynths[voice]` until it frees).
3. `startVoiceSynth(voice)`: the new def from `voiceControls` with the own note's park and expression (`ownNotePairs`), so a parked own note stays parked. The voice keeps every value in `voiceControls`, those of the old type included: a name two types share (FM's and Ring's `ratio`) reaches the new def until Lua sends the new type's values (Lua's sync after a switch, package 0d).
4. `restartVoiceNotes(voice)`: each live extra note of kind `\voice` is cut and started again on the new def at its kept `freq`, its order kept, silent (ruling 45). A releasing note keeps releasing on the old def (it is in the pool until it frees, and a slot still holds one live or releasing and one cutting synth at most); a `\stream` note of a frozen voice is left as it is.

## Wave

`lib/engine/StrataTypeWave.sc` (3.8, package 1f; the area `typeWave`, order 21): `\strataWave`, a wavetable voice, and the bank it reads.

- **The bank is 0b's reservation and nothing more**: `waveBankSize` (8) tables of `waveTableFrames` (`wavetableBankFrames div: waveBankSize`, 4096 frames: a wavetable of 2048 samples in VOsc's format), so the whole bank is `wavetableBankFrames` (32768 frames, 128 KB of ordinary RAM, which `fixedBufferBytes` already counts: `docs/notes/engine/performance.md`); a change to the bank's size is a change to that method alone. `allocWaveBank` (the area's `allocState`) allocates it once with `Buffer.allocConsecutive`, so its bufnums are consecutive (`VOsc3` reads a table and the next) and never change, and fills each table on the server in its `b_alloc`'s completion message (`/b_gen sine1`, normalized, wavetable, cleared): no sclang loop over the samples. `freeWaveBank` (its `free`) frees every buffer of it. The state is `areaState[\typeWave][\bank]` (`waveBank`).
- **The eight tables** (`waveBankSpectra`, harmonic amplitudes, darkest first): a sine; two, four and eight harmonics falling as 1/k; sixteen odd ones (a soft square); thirty-two as 1/k (a soft saw); twelve peaked around the sixth (a formant); sixty-four as 1/sqrt(k). A table is not band-limited: the brightest aliases at high pitches, as the prototype did (`docs/design/3.8-facts/engine-voices.md`).
- **The tables play at matched level** (the controller's ruling at 1f's review: `morph` is the main control and its default wander sweeps it, so a level that followed the table was heard as the voice fading as it brightened, 12.9 dB across the bank when each table was normalized to its peak). `waveBankAmplitudes` scales each spectrum so the root of its squared amplitudes' sum is 1, and `sine1` fills the tables with its normalize flag off: every table has a unit sine's RMS (a table's peak may pass 1, which the def's 0.3 keeps the voice under: 0.48 at most measured). The eight tables then play within 0.7 dB of each other at 220 Hz, and the defaults' morph 0.3 about 2 dB louder than before (RMS 0.073 against 0.057).
- **The def** takes every shared and note control and plays, on each side of a `Balance2`, one `VOsc3` of three oscillators at the pitch and detuned above it (left) or below it (right) by `detune` and twice `detune`. The position is `noteMainValue(\strataWave, morph, noteMain)` plus `LFNoise1.kr(morphRate) * morphDepth` plus `modSig * 0.2`, clipped to 0-1 and scaled by `waveBankSize - 1.001`, so the table after the one read stays in the bank. No delay line, no table sized from a control, no re-excitation on a trigger, and no subsonic block (a table of sines has no energy at 0 Hz; the check measures it). Its expression range is `noteMainRanges`' `strataWave: [\morph, 0, 1]`.
- **The bank's first bufnum is built into the def**, as `scaleBuffer`'s is into the grain defs (`docs/notes/engine/variation.md`): every engine built on a server re-adds `\strataWave` with its own bank, so after a second engine was built and freed (the structure, files, memory and other areas' `h.startEngine`) the def reads that engine's freed bank, which `VOsc3` plays as silence. On norns one engine runs at a time. A check that plays the wave voice on `h.engine` first rebuilds it (`areaCall(\addWaveDef)` on `h.engine`), as `types/wave.scd` does.

The check, `test/engine_check/types/wave.scd` (loaded by the types area): the bank (eight consecutive bufnums, each of `waveTableFrames`, summing to `wavetableBankFrames`, as the server reports them, each filled), a second engine's def reading that engine's bank (its first bufnum, not 0, a constant of the def), its free leaving none of its bank's buffers (every `Buffer` freed, the server reporting 0 frames at each bufnum), the level at the defaults and at each own key's range ends at 110 and 2000 Hz (finite, peaks 0.11-0.48 against the floor 0.005 and the ceiling 1, 3.14.1), below 8 Hz under 0.15 of the peak (0.0 measured), the eight tables at 220 Hz with no wander within 3 dB of the loudest (0.7 dB measured), morph 0 against morph 1 at 220 Hz with no wander by their spectrum, not their level (the share of energy above the fourth harmonic, two 990 Hz highpasses: 0.0 against 0.53, at least 0.25 apart), and a held three-note chord on a wave voice (three `\strataWave` note synths, each finite and above the floor, and `morph` sent voice-wide reaching all three). Its pool size is the memory area's (`checkVoiceSynthSizes`).

## The additive type

`lib/engine/StrataTypeAdditive.sc` (3.8, package 1g; the Lua record is `lib/types/additive.lua`): the area `strataAreaTypeAdditive` (order 22) whose `addDefs` adds `\strataAdditive` (`addAdditiveDef`, through `areaCall`) and returns no names (the voices area's `voiceTypeDefs` names the def). Its controls: the shared and note controls, `tilt` (0-3, the expression control, `noteMainRanges` `[\tilt, 0, 3]`), `stretch` (-1-1), `oddEven` (0-1) and `wander` (0.01-1 Hz); envelope 4 and 8 s.

- **The partials**: `additivePartials` of them (16), partial k at freq × k ^ (1 + `additiveStretchRatio` (0.05) × stretch), at a level of k ^ -tilt times its odd or even level (`oddEven` 0 the odd only, 0.5 all, 1 the even only; the fundamental always at 1). One `LFNoise1.kr(wander)` serves every partial: it moves the tilt `additiveWanderDepth` (0.3) either way, so the color breathes (design §3: no oscillator a partial). Each partial is worked out in logarithms (its log frequency and log level each one `MulAdd` of the control-rate slope or power and the constant log k, then an `exp`), muted by a comparison of its log frequency with log(`additiveMuteRatio` (0.45) × the sample rate), which follows bend, crossmod and the note's bend, lagged `additiveMuteLag` (0.03 s) so a sweep across the limit fades a partial and the normalization instead of stepping them, and summed by its `SinOsc`'s own `MulAdd` into its side's running sum: nine UGens a partial, 209 at 16. The sum is normalized by the levels' sum, which bounds each side's peak whatever the tilt; it is no loudness match (a flat tilt spreads the level over many partials: at tilt 0 the RMS is about 13 dB under tilt 3's). **The voice is centered at pan 0** (fix round 1 of the package's review): the fundamental's one `SinOsc` starts both sides' sums, and the upper partials spread in pairs (2 left, 3 and 4 right, 5 and 6 left, ...), so the sides' RMS stay within about 1 dB of each other at every tilt and `oddEven` (0.83 dB at most in the check, 1.5 its bound); a mono sum on both sides was not needed. The first build put the fundamental and 2 on the left only (1 and 2 left, 3 and 4 right), which left the voice 9 dB to the left at its defaults and 29 dB at tilt 3. `Balance2` then pans the pair with the level (the levels' sum's reciprocal, the envelope and the voice level), as the other stereo types' does. A sum of sines has nothing near 0 Hz: no `subsonicBlock`.
- **The count is measured** (design §3: 16 if it is within the voice def limit, 52 KB, else 12, else 8): the memory area's `checkVoiceSynthSizes` line, alone, on 3.14.1 and on 3.13 in Docker. With the mute's lag (209 UGens): 47.3 and 47.2 KB at 16 partials. Before it (193 UGens): 43.8 and 43.8 KB at 16, 36.5 and 37.1 at 12, 29.8 and 30.3 at 8 (about 1.7 KB a partial on a base of about 16.5; the lag adds about 0.2 KB a partial). So 16 is kept. `\strataKarplus` (48.1-48.9 KB in the same runs) stays the costliest def, by under a KB: the worst cases are unchanged, and a def that grows past Karplus becomes the one every worst case casts.
- **The check**: `test/engine_check/types/additive.scd`, run by the types area, measures each side of the voice bus by itself (its peak, its peak below 8 Hz, its peak above the limit and its mean square): the def follows the built-in types in `voiceTypeDefs`; it has one `SinOsc` a partial; a voice on the type plays it; at its defaults and at each own key's range ends, at 110 and 2000 Hz, each side is finite and peaks above 0.005 and under 1 (measured 0.069 to 0.198); at pan 0 the two sides' RMS over 1.5 s are within `balanceLimitDb` (1.5 dB) of each other at 110 and 2000 Hz at the defaults, tilt 0 and 3 and `oddEven` 0 and 1, and at 12 kHz, where only the fundamental sounds (0.83 dB at most; the first build failed it by 9 to 29 dB and with the right side silent at 12 kHz); below 8 Hz (two 8 Hz lowpasses) each side under 0.15 of its peak over 6 s at its defaults at 110 Hz; at 2000 Hz with stretch 1 and tilt 0 each side above 0.45 × the sample rate (eight highpasses there) is under -60 dB of its peak (-87.5 dB measured; with the mute taken out, -17.6 dB, so the check fails it); three extra notes of a held chord are three `\strataAdditive` synths beside the own note's. `noteMain` ±1 reaching the range's ends is the voices area's check of every `noteMainRanges` entry.

## Noise (`\strataNoise`, package 1h)

`lib/engine/StrataTypeNoise.sc`, the area `typeNoise` (order 23), whose `addDefs` only adds the def (`voiceTypeDefs` already names it); its Lua record is `lib/types/noise.lua` (`docs/notes/lua/types.md`). Keys `freq color grit dust q bank amp pan`; `q` is the main and the expression control (`noteMainRanges` `strataNoise: [\q, 0.005, 1]`); attack 3 s, release 6 s.

- **The graph**: pink noise × (1 - `grit`) plus `Dust2` at `dust` (× `noiseDustSpread`, 1.1, on the right) × `grit` × `noiseDustLevel` (4), a channel each; through `subsonicBlock` (pink noise has energy near 0 Hz: `docs/notes/engine/voices.md`, the subsonic rule), then an `LPF` at `color` mapped exponentially over `noiseColorRange` (200-16000 Hz). The bank is four `Resonz` at the voice's frequency × `noiseSpread` (1.5) ** k, at `noiseBankLevels` (1, 0.6, 0.4, 0.25), each at rq `q` (through `noteMainValue`) and kept below Nyquist (`filterCeiling`); a narrower `Resonz` rings quieter, so the sum is × `q`'s reciprocal square root, and × a slow swell (`noiseSwellFloor`, 0.6, to 1, at `noiseSwellRate`, 0.1 Hz). `bank` mixes the bank against the plain noise (× `noisePlainLevel`, 0.3).

- **Its cost**: 31.5 KB of the pool on 3.14.1 and 31.2 KB on 3.13 (111 UGens; the memory area's figure, under the 52 KB limit; Karplus stays the costliest def; the allocation probes' 0.85 KB steps let one def read a step apart from one run to the next). Seven voices on a norns are a device question (#6).
- **The check** (`test/engine_check/types/noise.scd`, run by the types area): its place in `voiceTypeDefs` is Lua's (the place of `lib/types/noise.lua`'s include among `strata.lua`'s type includes, less one, after the built-in types); at the defaults and each own key's range ends, at 110 and 2000 Hz, finite with a peak above 0.005 and under 1 (0.059 to 0.64 measured); the RMS against Pulse's within 3 dB; below 8 Hz under 0.15 of the whole peak over 10 s at the defaults and at `bank` 0 (0.01 and 0.05 measured); "noise: q 0.005, grit 1, accents every 0.25 s for 4 s stay under 1" (amp 1, dust 50, bank and color 1, full amplitude and frequency crossmod, at 20, 110 and 2000 Hz, centered and panned hard either way: peaks 0.37 to 0.9, the limiter's level); three extra notes of a held chord start three synths of the def. Its pool size is the memory area's (`checkVoiceSynthSizes`); the expression range, the ASR defaults and the velocity rule are the voices area's and the Lua tests', for every def of the list.

## Saw

`lib/engine/StrataTypeSaw.sc`, the area `typeSaw` (order 24; its `addDefs` adds `\strataSaw` through `areaCall` and returns no names, since the voices area returns `voiceTypeDefs`). `\strataSaw` (3.8 package 1i, design §3): three `VarSaw`s a side at the pitch times 1, 1 + `detune` and 1 + 2 × `detune` (upward on the left, downward on the right), summed and divided by three, at the width `noteMainValue(\strataSaw, width, noteMain)` (`noteMainRanges`' `strataSaw: [\width, 0.05, 0.95]`); then Pulse's filter, `RLPF` at the cutoff times a 0.1 Hz `LFNoise1` between 0.5 and 2, clipped to 100-8000 Hz, with rq `1.1 - res.clip(0.1, 1)` (Pulse's mirror on purpose: `PULSE_RES.mirror`, which `types_saw_test.lua` compares, so one `res` sounds alike on both types); `Balance2` and the output × 0.5. Attack 3 s and release 6 s by default. A `VarSaw`'s mean is 0 at any width, so it needs no `subsonicBlock`. No delay line, no table, nothing re-excited on a trigger (`t_trig` reaches only `voiceAccent`).

Its check, `test/engine_check/types/saw.scd`: `\strataSaw` in `voiceTypeDefs` after the built-in voices' types with its `noteMainRanges` entry; at its defaults and at each own key's range ends (width 0.05 and 0.95, detune 0 and 0.02, cutoff 100 and 8000, res 0.1 and 1), at 110 and 2000 Hz, a synth of its own on a private bus is finite, peaks above 0.005 and under 1 a channel and leaves under 0.15 of its peak below 8 Hz (two 8 Hz lowpasses), with Pulse at its defaults at 110 Hz as the level the message reports; a held chord's three extra notes on a saw voice are three `\strataSaw` synths. Its pool size is the memory area's (`checkVoiceSynthSizes`). Measured (3.14.1): 0.27 at its defaults at 2000 Hz, 0.107 at 110 Hz (Pulse's 0.186 there, -4.8 dB); res 1 at 2000 Hz peaks 0.85 when the wandering cutoff crosses the pitch; cutoff 100 at 2000 Hz is 0.0002 to 0.0007 (the lowpass four octaves under a triangle's fundamental).
