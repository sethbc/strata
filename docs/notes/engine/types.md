## Must not break

- **A voice's synth is its type** (3.8, design §0.3): `voiceTypeDefs` lists each type's voice SynthDef in type order, one name a line, and a type package only appends its line (Lua's types, package 0d, are registered in the same order). Voice v starts on type v (`areaState[\voices][\types]`, set in `allocVoiceState`), so the list has at least `numVoices` entries (the stub's parse fails otherwise). Every reader asks `voiceDefFor(voice)`: `startVoiceSynth`, `gateVoice`'s re-gate, an extra note's `startNoteSynth`, `notesUnfrozen`; `ownNotePairs` asks `voiceTypeDefs.includes(defName)`. `voiceSynthDefs`, declared in `lib/Engine_Strata.sc`, is no longer assigned, and nothing reads it.
- **`setVoiceType(voice, type)` ignores anything else without a line**: a voice outside 0..`numVoices` - 1 or a type outside 0..`numVoiceTypes` - 1, or either not an integer (`validVoice`, `validVoiceType`), as the other voice commands do (`docs/notes/engine/voices.md`); it is no guarded command. The voice's own type again is a no-op.
- **A stopped, buffer or input voice only records its type**: its next start on the synth source (`voiceOn`, or `setGrainSource(v, 0)` on a running voice) plays the type's def. So does a running voice whose voice synth failed to start (`synths[voiceKey]` nil): the watcher's restart (`voiceSynthFreed`) or the next start takes the type.
- **A switch cuts, never releases**: on a running synth voice `replaceVoiceType` runs `switchVoiceType` in one bundle: it frees the voice's cutting set (`freeCuttingSynths`), cuts its voice synth (`cutVoiceSynth`: the kill fade, `switchCutTime`), starts the new def at the head of `voiceGroup` (`startVoiceSynth`) and restarts its live voice synth notes (`restartVoiceNotes`). So type switches leave a voice two voice synths at most, the new and the cut, however fast they come (the source-switch cycles keep their own bound, three: `docs/notes/engine/lifecycle.md`). Both methods keep closures (the bundle's function, the new synth's `onFree`), so both are called only through `areaCall` and are `AREA_CALL_METHODS` entries (`test/unit/structure_test.lua`).
- **`gateVoice` re-gates a releasing voice synth only of the voice's current type** (ruling 15): one of another type (switched while the voice was stopped) is cut (`freeCuttingSynths`, then `cutVoiceSynth`) before the new def starts, so a stopped voice's long release never comes back as the old type. No state is needed: one def a type, so the releasing synth's `defName` is its type.
- **A switch leaves the freeze alone**: a frozen synth voice keeps its ring writer (paused, or the spectral writer on high) and its `\stream` notes, whose grains read the ring and never name the def. Held (hold), the voice synth is not heard at all, so the switch is made at once. A buffer or input voice has no voice synth to switch.
- **Under a spectral freeze the switch waits for the unfreeze** (3.8, package X7): the spectral writer holds the magnitudes (`PV_MagFreeze`) while the phases go on coming from the voice bus, so a new voice synth would set the held spectrum's phases. Measured by package X6, the held level then fell by a mean of 2.4 to 2.6 dB and by about 8 at worst (the new partials set the held bins' phases and the overlapping frames partly cancel; every type as wide), against within about a decibel with no switch (#798). So `setVoiceType` on a running synth voice whose ring a spectral writer holds (`spectralHoldsVoice`, the freeze area's: `\mode` `\spectral` and `\source` 0) records the type and leaves the voice synth running: `voiceType` and `voiceDefFor` answer the new type at once, and the running voice synth is the old def. Nothing records the wait: it is derived, the live voice synth's `defName` against `voiceDefFor` (`voiceTypeWaits`), as `gateVoice`'s cut is. A second switch under the freeze replaces the first, and one back to the running type cancels it. Every path that ends a spectral freeze goes through `unfreezeVoice`, which applies a waiting switch (`applyWaitingVoiceType`, the one place) after it replaces the writer: the freeze param's unfreeze (in `unfreezeWithControl`'s bundle), a `freezeMode` change while frozen (unfreeze, then freeze in hold), and a freeze on another mode or source. A source switch also ends it, but its voice synth fades out and the return to the synth starts the type's def; `voiceOff` leaves the freeze and the old synth releasing, and the next `voiceOn` cuts it (`gateVoice`'s rule) and starts the type's def under the freeze. The engine's `free` applies nothing.

## Owns

## The switch

`setVoiceType` records the type (`areaState[\voices][\types][voice] = type`), then, on a running synth-source voice with a live voice synth whose ring no spectral writer holds, calls `replaceVoiceType` through `areaCall`. Under a spectral freeze it stops there, and `unfreezeVoice` calls `applyWaitingVoiceType`, which calls `replaceVoiceType` the same way when the voice is still on its synth source and its live voice synth is not of its type. The freeze param's unfreeze makes it inside `unfreezeWithControl`'s bundle, so the new voice synth, the old one's `kill` 1, the plain writer that replaces the spectral one and the grain synth's `freeze` 0 land in one block: the first block the writer records the live voice bus again holds the new type and the old one's kill fade, as a switch made unfrozen does, never a block of the old type alone. The notes area's `notesUnfrozen` then runs in the same bundle and restarts the `\stream` notes as voice synths of the new def (`startNoteSynth` reads `voiceDefFor`). `switchVoiceType`, inside the bundle:

1. `freeCuttingSynths(voice)`: whatever an earlier cut or switch left in its kill fade goes at once (a source switch frees it the same way, so a cutting grain synth loses the rest of its fade as it does there).
2. The live voice synth leaves `synths` and is cut (`cutVoiceSynth`, kept in `cuttingSynths[voice]` until it frees).
3. `startVoiceSynth(voice)`: the new def from `voiceControls` with the own note's park and expression (`ownNotePairs`), so a parked own note stays parked. The voice keeps every value in `voiceControls`, those of the old type included: a name two types share (FM's and Ring's `ratio`) reaches the new def until Lua sends the new type's values (Lua's sync after a switch, package 0d).
4. `restartVoiceNotes(voice)`: each live extra note of kind `\voice` is cut and started again on the new def at its kept `freq`, its order kept, silent (ruling 45). A releasing note keeps releasing on the old def (it is in the pool until it frees, and a slot still holds one live or releasing and one cutting synth at most); a `\stream` note of a frozen voice is left as it is.

**Lua under a waiting switch** (no Lua change): Lua sends `setVoiceType`, then, on a running voice, its sync, which sends the new type's keys with `setVoiceParam` and no longer the old type's (a key's sender reaches the engine only while the voice's type has it, `docs/notes/lua/types.md`), so nothing moves the held sound's source. `setVoiceControl` keeps each value in `voiceControls` and sends it to the running voice synth; an `/n_set` of a name the old def lacks changes nothing and reports nothing (scsynth sets a named control only when the synth's graph has it), and a name both defs share (`freq`, `amp`, `pan`) reaches the old one as it would unfrozen. The voice synth the unfreeze starts takes every kept value (`startVoiceSynth`: `controlPairs` of `voiceControls`).

## Wave

`lib/engine/StrataTypeWave.sc` (3.8, package 1f; the area `typeWave`, order 21): `\strataWave`, a wavetable voice, and the bank it reads.

- **The bank is 0b's reservation and nothing more**: `waveBankSize` (8) tables of `waveTableFrames` (`wavetableBankFrames div: waveBankSize`, 4096 frames: a wavetable of 2048 samples in VOsc's format), so the whole bank is `wavetableBankFrames` (32768 frames, 128 KB of ordinary RAM, which `fixedBufferBytes` already counts: `docs/notes/engine/performance.md`); a change to the bank's size is a change to that method alone. `allocWaveBank` (the area's `allocState`) allocates it once with `Buffer.allocConsecutive`, so its bufnums are consecutive (`VOsc3` reads a table and the next) and never change, and fills each table on the server in its `b_alloc`'s completion message (`/b_gen sine1`, normalized, wavetable, cleared): no sclang loop over the samples. `freeWaveBank` (its `free`) frees every buffer of it. The state is `areaState[\typeWave][\bank]` (`waveBank`).
- **The eight tables** (`waveBankSpectra`, harmonic amplitudes, darkest first): a sine; two, four and eight harmonics falling as 1/k; sixteen odd ones (a soft square); thirty-two as 1/k (a soft saw); twelve peaked around the sixth (a formant); sixty-four as 1/sqrt(k). A table is not band-limited: the brightest aliases at high pitches, as the prototype did (`docs/design/3.8-facts/engine-voices.md`).
- **The tables play at matched level** (the controller's ruling at 1f's review: `morph` is the main control and its default wander sweeps it, so a level that followed the table was heard as the voice fading as it brightened, 12.9 dB across the bank when each table was normalized to its peak). `waveBankAmplitudes` scales each spectrum so the root of its squared amplitudes' sum is 1, and `sine1` fills the tables with its normalize flag off: every table has a unit sine's RMS (a table's peak may pass 1, which the def's 0.3 keeps the voice under: 0.48 at most measured). The eight tables then play within 0.7 dB of each other at 220 Hz, and the defaults' morph 0.3 about 2 dB louder than before (RMS 0.073 against 0.057).
- **The def** takes every shared and note control and plays, on each side of a `Balance2`, one `VOsc3` of three oscillators at the pitch and detuned above it (left) or below it (right) by `detune` and twice `detune`. The position is `noteMainValue(\strataWave, morph, noteMain)` plus `LFNoise1.kr(morphRate) * morphDepth` plus `modSig * 0.2`, clipped to 0-1 and scaled by `waveBankSize - 1.001`, so the table after the one read stays in the bank. No delay line, no table sized from a control, no re-excitation on a trigger, and no subsonic block (a table of sines has no energy at 0 Hz; the check measures it). Its expression range is `noteMainRanges`' `strataWave: [\morph, 0, 1]`.
- **The bank's first bufnum is built into the def**, as `scaleBuffer`'s is into the grain defs (`docs/notes/engine/variation.md`): every engine built on a server re-adds `\strataWave` with its own bank, so after a second engine was built and freed (the structure, files, memory and other areas' `h.startEngine`) the def reads that engine's freed bank, which `VOsc3` plays as silence. On norns one engine runs at a time. A check that plays the wave voice on `h.engine` first rebuilds it (`areaCall(\addWaveDef)` on `h.engine`), as `types/wave.scd` does.

The check, `test/engine_check/types/wave.scd` (loaded by the types area): the bank (eight consecutive bufnums, each of `waveTableFrames`, summing to `wavetableBankFrames`, as the server reports them, each filled), a second engine's def reading that engine's bank (its first bufnum, not 0, a constant of the def), its free leaving none of its bank's buffers (every `Buffer` freed, the server reporting 0 frames at each bufnum), the level at the defaults and at each own key's range ends at 110 and 2000 Hz (finite, peaks 0.11-0.48 against the floor 0.005 and the ceiling 1, 3.14.1), below 8 Hz under 0.15 of the peak (0.0 measured), the eight tables at 220 Hz with no wander within 3 dB of the loudest (0.7 dB measured), morph 0 against morph 1 at 220 Hz with no wander by their spectrum, not their level (the share of energy above the fourth harmonic, two 990 Hz highpasses: 0.0 against 0.53, at least 0.25 apart), and a held three-note chord on a wave voice (three `\strataWave` note synths, each finite and above the floor, and `morph` sent voice-wide reaching all three). Its pool size is the memory area's (`checkVoiceSynthSizes`).

## The additive type

`lib/engine/StrataTypeAdditive.sc` (3.8, package 1g; the Lua record is `lib/types/additive.lua`): the area `strataAreaTypeAdditive` (order 22) whose `addDefs` adds `\strataAdditive` (`addAdditiveDef`, through `areaCall`) and returns no names (the voices area's `voiceTypeDefs` names the def). - **The partials**: `additivePartials` of them (16), partial k at freq × k ^ (1 + `additiveStretchRatio` (0.05) × stretch), at a level of k ^ -tilt times its odd or even level (`oddEven` 0 the odd only, 0.5 all, 1 the even only; the fundamental always at 1). One `LFNoise1.kr(wander)` serves every partial: it moves the tilt `additiveWanderDepth` (0.3) either way, so the color breathes (design §3: no oscillator a partial). Each partial is worked out in logarithms (its log frequency and log level each one `MulAdd` of the control-rate slope or power and the constant log k, then an `exp`), muted by a comparison of its log frequency with log(`additiveMuteRatio` (0.45) × the sample rate), which follows bend, crossmod and the note's bend, lagged `additiveMuteLag` (0.03 s) so a sweep across the limit fades a partial and the normalization instead of stepping them, and summed by its `SinOsc`'s own `MulAdd` into its side's running sum: nine UGens a partial, 209 at 16. The sum is normalized by the levels' sum, which bounds each side's peak whatever the tilt; it is no loudness match (a flat tilt spreads the level over many partials: at tilt 0 the RMS is about 13 dB under tilt 3's). **The voice is centered at pan 0** (fix round 1 of the package's review): the fundamental's one `SinOsc` starts both sides' sums, and the upper partials spread in pairs (2 left, 3 and 4 right, 5 and 6 left, ...), so the sides' RMS stay within about 1 dB of each other at every tilt and `oddEven` (0.83 dB at most in the check, 1.5 its bound); a mono sum on both sides was not needed. With the mute's lag (209 UGens): 47.3 and 47.2 KB at 16 partials. ## Saw

`lib/engine/StrataTypeSaw.sc`, the area `typeSaw` (order 24; its `addDefs` adds `\strataSaw` through `areaCall` and returns no names, since the voices area returns `voiceTypeDefs`). `\strataSaw` (3.8 package 1i, design §3): three `VarSaw`s a side at the pitch times 1, 1 + `detune` and 1 + 2 × `detune` (upward on the left, downward on the right), summed and divided by three, at the width `noteMainValue(\strataSaw, width, noteMain)` (`noteMainRanges`' `strataSaw: [\width, 0.05, 0.95]`); then a resonant lowpass on Pulse's range, `RLPF` at the cutoff times a 0.1 Hz `LFNoise1` between 0.5 and 2 (the prototype's wander; Pulse's cutoff wanders at 0.2 Hz), clipped to 100-8000 Hz, with rq `1.1 - res.clip(0.1, 1)` (Pulse's mirror on purpose: `PULSE_RES.mirror`, which `types_saw_test.lua` compares, so one `res` sounds alike on both types); `Balance2` and the output × 0.5. Attack 3 s and release 6 s by default. A `VarSaw`'s mean is 0 at any width, so it needs no `subsonicBlock`. No delay line, no table, nothing re-excited on a trigger (`t_trig` reaches only `voiceAccent`).

## Formant (`\strataFormant`, 3.8, package 1j)

`lib/engine/StrataTypeFormant.sc`, the `typeFormant` area (order 25), whose `addDefs` only adds the def (the voices area's `voiceTypeDefs` names it). Lua's record: `lib/types/formant.lua` (key `formant`, list name `vox`).

- **The graph**: per channel (the right one `formantDetune`, 1.003, above), two `Formant` oscillators at the voice's pitch, the second at `formantSecondLevel` (0.5) of the first, summed and scaled by `formantGain` (0.5): a burst is up to 2, so the sum stays under 1.5 before the level. `vowel` (0-1) places both formants, wandering `formantWanderDepth` (0.2) either way at `formantWanderRate` (0.1 Hz, the prototype's `wander` at its default, fixed) and clipped to 0-1; the first moves exponentially over `formantFirstRange` (300 to 800 Hz), the second over `formantSecondRange` (2300 down to 1100). `formant` (0.5-2, the expression control: `noteMainValue(\strataFormant, formant, noteMain)`, `noteMainRanges` `[\formant, 0.5, 2]`) scales both, and `bw` (0.1-1) is each formant's bandwidth as a fraction of its frequency. The ASR's defaults are 3 and 6 s, the record's envelope.
- **A narrow bandwidth at a high pitch is clamped, never undefined**: `Formant` takes the larger of the pitch and the bandwidth as its burst rate (`Formant_next`, `server/plugins/OscUGens.cpp`). It does not band-limit, so a formant scale of 2 at 2000 Hz aliases; its level stays in range (the check below).
- **`subsonicBlock` on each channel's sum, before the pan** (#309's rule, `docs/notes/engine/voices.md`): a burst lasts one cycle of the burst rate, so where a formant falls near or under the pitch (the first formant at 150 Hz under a 300 Hz pitch at formant 0.5 and vowel 0; every formant at 2000 Hz) a burst holds about half a cycle of its sine and carries DC, which the vowel's wander moves. Without the block the check measured 0.090 below 8 Hz of a 0.212 peak at 300 Hz (formant 0.5, vowel 0, bw 1) and 0.082 of 0.179 at 2000 Hz (formant 2, vowel 0, bw 1), over the 0.15 ceiling; with it, under 0.0001 of every peak (0.00001 below 8 Hz at most, the larger channel's: the check measures each channel; 3.14.1 and 3.13).
- **No delay line, no table, no `LocalBuf`, no re-excitation on a trigger**: velocity reaches only the accent (`voiceModulation`).

**The check** (`test/engine_check/types/formant.scd`, run by the types area): the def in `voiceTypeDefs` after the types the voices start on; each voice bus at the defaults and at each own key's range end (formant 0.5 and 2, vowel 0 and 1, bw 0.1 and 1), at 110 and 2000 Hz, finite with a peak above 0.005 and under 1 (0.06 to 0.28 measured), and `"formant: 2000 Hz at formant 2 is finite and under 1"` (the formant scale and the bandwidth at their highest, at vowel 0: the first formant at the bottom of its range, the second at the top of its); below 8 Hz under 0.15 of the peak at the defaults and at the widest bandwidth at the formant scale's ends (110, 300 and 2000 Hz); a held chord's three extra notes as three synths of the def, the server agreeing. The pool size is the memory area's (`checkVoiceSynthSizes`), noteMain's ±1 the voices area's.
