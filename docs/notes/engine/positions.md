## Must not break

- `slotScanPhase(bufScan, freeze, scrubRate, scanHold, t_scanReset, speed, state)` runs at `(scanRate(bufScan, freeze) × speed × (1 − scanHold)) + scrubRate` spans a second; in wrap it is `Phasor.ar(t_scanReset + t_scanSync, rate × SampleDur.ir, 0, 1, ...)`, so over a span of 1 s at the server's rate (`slotScanSpeed` 1) with the controls at rest it is 3.2's scan, exactly (`"positions: slotScanPhase is 3.2's scan, a speed over a span of 1 s"`; 3.7 re-based it, ruling 29).
- **The scan is a speed** (3.7, ruling 29): `speed` is `slotScanSpeed(bufRateScale, spanFrames)` = `bufRateScale × SampleRate.ir / spanFrames.max(1)`, `spanFrames` the region's or the loop's length in the slot's frames, so a scan of 1 crosses any span in its own length (`"positions: a scan of 1 crosses a 2 s region in 2 s, and a 0.5 s region in 0.5 s"`). It applies to every `\strataGrainBuf`, a check-only reader's too; `scrubRate` stays in spans a second (Lua's glide moves a position, not audio). A frozen ring's scan (`ringScanOffset`) is untouched.
- **The modes act on `bufPos + the phase`, never on the phase alone** (`slotScanPosition`): wrap sums position, phase and spread and wraps (3.6's expression, exactly); bounce folds the sum over the span; one shot clips it; in both the spread is added last and clipped, never wrapped, and every clip is audio rate (`Clip.ar`; `docs/notes/engine/loops.md`). Bounce's `Phasor` runs to 2; one shot's phase is a `Sweep` (unbounded, double), never a `Phasor` with a far end, which wraps at once on a negative rate and loses its precision (`"positions: bounce turns round at both edges"`, `"positions: one shot holds its position at the edge, forward and backward"`).
- **A one-shot note that has left its span is silent, never ended**: the reader's level is multiplied by 0 (lagged 5 ms, `oneShotLevel`) once `bufPos + phase` is past 1 (strictly: at exactly 1, the span's last start, it sounds, so a one shot at `bufPos` 1 with the scan at 0 sounds while it is held, design §3.5, and a backward one shot from 1 opens at full level; `docs/decisions/3.7.md`) or below 0, until a restart; the engine never releases a note for it (`"positions: one shot holds at the edge and is silent past it, forward and backward"`, `"positions: one shot sounds at exactly 1 with the scan at 0 and is silent past 1, or below 0"`, `"positions: a backward one shot from bufPos 1 opens at full level, with no fade-in"`).
- **The restarts 3.7 adds are one synth's each**: a `retrigger` on a one-shot voice (`grainControls[voice][\scanMode]` 2) sends `t_scanReset` to the own grain synth alone (this area's `voiceHit` hook, `positionsHit`); a `noteOn` on a live extra slot of a one-shot buffer voice restarts that note's synth (`extraNoteOn`, `docs/notes/engine/notes.md`); `regionLength` restarts the own grain synth's, in the message that sets it (`"positions: a retrigger restarts a one-shot voice's scan and not a wrapping one's"`, `"positions: regionLength restarts the own note's scan and no extra note's"`). Lua's voice-wide `t_scanReset` keeps its meaning.
- **`syncScan` moves the own grain synth's phase and nothing else**: `scanSyncPhase` and `t_scanSync 1` on the voice's own `\strataGrainBuf` (none for a ring or a stopped voice), the phase wrapped into the mode's range (0-1 wrap, 0-2 bounce); one shot ignores it. Both controls and `slotGain` are the engine's (`engineOwnedControls`): `setGrainParam` refuses them and nothing stores them (`"positions: syncScan sets the own note's phase, wraps it in wrap, and makes no node"`, `"positions: syncScan leaves a one-shot scan where it is and sends nothing to a ring voice or a stopped one"`).
- **`t_scanReset` is never stored.** Its `grainParamHandlers` entry sends it to the voice's live grain synth (through `setSynth`, so `sendToSynth`) and returns true, so no replacement grain synth starts with a stale trigger (`"positions: t_scanReset restarts the phase and is not stored"`, hazard 5 of the 3.3 plan).
- `scrubRate` and `scanHold` are plain grain controls, stored and sent, so a source switch mid-glide keeps gliding (`"positions: scrubRate and scanHold survive a source switch"`). With `t_scanReset` they are **transient performance state**, the one exception to "the `voices` table is the only source of values": not params, never in a scene or PSET; `lib/positions.lua` sends `scrubRate 0` and `scanHold 0` in each voice's sync, so a started voice never inherits a glide.
- **Every grain start restarts the scan.** A new `\strataGrainBuf` starts its `Phasor` at 0. `gateVoice` (`lib/engine/StrataLifecycle.sc`) adds `\t_scanReset, 1` to the re-gate of a releasing `\strataGrainBuf` (`"lifecycle: a re-gated buffer voice restarts its scan"`). A `voiceOn` on a voice that is running is no start: its scan goes on (`"lifecycle: a voiceOn on a running buffer voice keeps its scan"`). Lua's playhead resets on the same events (`docs/notes/lua/playheads.md`).

## Owns

`lib/engine/StrataPositions.sc` (`strataAreaPositions`, order 150): `slotScanPhase`, `slotScanSpeed`, `slotScanState`, `slotScanPosition` and `oneShotLevel`, called by `slotGrainReader` (`StrataGrainDefs.sc`) and `slotGrainPos` (`StrataLoop.sc`); the `t_scanReset` and `regionLength` handlers; the `syncScan` command; the `voiceHit` hook (`positionsHit`). Its state is `areaState[\positions][\received]` (what `syncScan` last took, for the check). Every `\strataGrainBuf` has the controls; `\strataGrainRing` declares them and ignores them, so a jump on a ring voice does nothing.

## 3.7's scan controls

| Control | Default | What it does | Set by |
|---|---|---|---|
| `scanMode` | 0 | `scanMode.round.clip(0, 2)`: 0 wrap, 1 bounce, 2 one shot (the stub reads it as an enumeration); a retrigger's restart reads the kept value the same way (`oneShotVoice`), so an out-of-range value plays and restarts as the mode it clips to (the final review's engine Minor 4) | `setGrainParam`, voice-wide |
| `regionStart`, `regionLength` | 0, 0 | while `regionLength` > 0 the region (each clipped 0-1) takes the loop's place (`slotSpan`, `docs/notes/engine/loops.md`); per-note controls (`perNoteGrainControls`) | the own note's: `setGrainParam` (`regionLength`'s handler: kept, then `regionLength` and `t_scanReset 1` to the own grain synth in one message); an extra note's: `noteRegion` |
| `followRate` | 1 | the read rate × `followRate.clip(0.25, 4).lag(0.05)`, after the quantize (design §2.4) | `setGrainParam`, voice-wide, every note |
| `slotGain` | 1 | the reader's level × `slotGain.clip(0, 16).lag(0.05)` (design §4.4) | the buffers area (`setSlotGain`, `slotLengthPairs`) |
| `t_scanSync`, `scanSyncPhase` | 0, 0 | a sync of the wrap or bounce phase to `scanSyncPhase` | `syncScan` |

`slotScanState(controls, loopStart, loopLength)` turns the def's Event of these into what the reader, `slotScanPhase` and `slotGrainPos` share: `mode`, `oneShot` (`mode > 1.5`), the span's `first` and `last`, the follow rate, the gain and the sync, and (the reader puts it there) `sum`, `bufPos` + the phase, which the one-shot position and level read once. A check-only reader (`slotGrainReader` with its fifteen arguments, ruling 6) passes no state: 3.6's wrap, the loop, no follow rate, gain or sync, at the speed.

## The scan phase

`scanRate(bufScan, freeze)` is the freeze area's hook: `bufScan × (1 − freeze)`, a speed (3.7): × `slotScanSpeed`, it is spans a second (`slotGrainPos` spreads the phase over `loopLast - loopFirst`, the span's legal starts). The phase's rate is that rate × `(1 − scanHold)` plus `scrubRate`:

| Control | Effect | Sent by |
|---|---|---|
| `t_scanReset` | a trigger: the scan phase restarts at 0 | Lua's jumps (`setGrainParam(v, "t_scanReset", 1)`), `gateVoice` at every re-gate of a releasing slot reader, and 3.7's restarts (`regionLength`, a one-shot voice's retrigger, `noteRegion`, a one-shot voice's live extra `noteOn`) |
| `scrubRate` | added to the scan rate; neither `freeze` nor `scanHold` stops it (a fling goes on with the tape held) | Lua's arc glide |
| `scanHold` | 1 stops the voice's own scan, not the glide | Lua's ALT hold and the grid's hold keys |

The phase's reset is the `Phasor`'s own trigger input (`t_scanReset + t_scanSync`, the sync's position its `resetPos`, 0 on a reset), and the `Sweep`'s (`t_scanReset` alone) in one shot, so a reset falls in the block the message lands in. The two rate controls enter the rate as control-rate signals; a glide is as smooth as Lua's 1/15 s decay of `scrubRate`. Both phases run in every mode and a `Select.ar` picks one, so a change of mode never restarts the scan: a one-shot's `Sweep` that ran past its span while the voice wrapped stays silent until the next restart, which Lua sends with the mode (2a's).

## The check

`test/engine_check/positions.scd` has two kinds of assertion. The hook runs beside a `Phasor` of the rate it should have and the largest difference (`Peak.ar`, on the server) is read back once: 0 for the scan alone, the scrub alone, both, a hold, a hold with a glide and a freeze with a glide, and across a reset. The real `\strataGrainBuf` runs end to end on a slot of known contents: its left channel is a staircase of its position (64 steps, `Buffer.fill`) and its right channel is 1, so `RunningSum(left) / RunningSum(right)` of the voice's other path is where the grains read, read back with one `/c_get` beside the server's own time (a `Sweep`), so a slope never depends on sclang's clock. The voice runs with `panSpread` and `panJitter` at 0: `panSpread`'s slow `Balance2` drift moves the ratio by far more than the scan does. `test/engine_check/lifecycle.scd` uses the same slot for `gateVoice`'s re-gate. 3.7's checks: the hook in bounce and one shot over a span of 1 s (`\strataCheckScanModes`: the server's time at the top and the bottom, within a block of 1 s and 2 s), and on the real synth the one shot's level (the mean of the right channel, which is 1), a retrigger's restart, `oneShotVoice` against `slotScanState`'s one-shot reading for scanMode -1, 0, 1, 1.4, 1.6, 2, 2.6, 3 and 7, the speed over the 2 s loop and a 0.5 s region, `syncScan` (positions, the node count, the refusal of its controls, its ignored lines through the modulation area's `postIgnored` hook), `regionLength`'s restart (an extra note's messages, read from a `makeBundle(false)`), and each new control's feed and default in both defs.
