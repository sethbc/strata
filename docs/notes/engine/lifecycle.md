## Must not break

- **Replace, never stack.** A source switch replaces the grain synth. Earlier fading synths are cut with `cutSynth` (the `kill` fade), never a second `release` (EnvGen ignores it). New grain synths are placed with `startPlacedGrainSynth`, never relative to a releasing synth.
- `voiceOn` never stacks synths: it re-gates what the voice still has, and a voice holds at most its live synths, one fading set and one cutting set.
- **A type switch cuts** (3.8, `docs/notes/engine/types.md`): `setVoiceType` on a running synth voice frees its cutting set, cuts its voice synth (`cutVoiceSynth`, never a release) and starts the new type's def in one bundle, at once or, under a spectral freeze, at the unfreeze (`replaceVoiceType`, `switchVoiceType`, both through `areaCall`), the third replacement beside the source switch's; `gateVoice` re-gates a releasing voice synth only of the voice's current type and cuts one of another type (`freeCuttingSynths`, then `cutVoiceSynth`). So type switches, however fast, and a start after one leave a voice two voice synths at most (the new and the cut); the source-switch cycles keep their own bound, three voice synths and three grain synths (Stopping, below).
- **`openedGate(gate)`** wraps every ASR gate, so a synth gated off in its first block still starts and frees. Never use `gate.max(...)`: it breaks forced releases.
- **Messages to synths that can end on their own** (releasing, fading or cutting synths; a voice's live synths in `setSynth` with a voice, grain or mod key, `releaseVoice` and switches; record synths) go through `sendToSynth` (a bundle that opens with `/error -1` and closes with `/error -2`), never `synth.set`/`release`/`free`/`moveBefore` directly: the synth may have freed a moment ago, and the server's `FAILURE ... Node not found` line fails the engine check. The effects chain's synths (`effectKeys`: the three master filters `\filter`, `\filterDelay`, `\filterReverb`, then delay, reverb, master) never end on their own, so `setSynth` sends to them plainly: a failure there is a real bug and must stay reported. The engine frees and re-creates the delay and the reverb itself (bypass), so they stay `effectKeys`; `setSynth` skips a missing key.
- A broken bookkeeping invariant posts one `Strata invariant broken` line (`warnInvariant`), which fails the engine check.
- **A start counts once the server confirms it** (#497). `watchVoiceSynth` keeps each new voice, grain and mod synth in `unconfirmedStarts` (`areaState[\voices][\unconfirmed]`) with its start time until its `/n_go`. A synth whose `/s_new` failed (no room in the real-time pool, a missing def) never sends `/n_go` or `/n_end`. Each start has a timer of its own (`watchStart`'s Routine, stopped at its `/n_go` or `/n_end`, `endFailedStart`'s included): still unconfirmed `startConfirmWait` (0.5 s) after it, the engine syncs with the server (`serverAnswers`: a `/n_go` sent before the sync is handled first, so a busy sclang or a loaded server never makes a live synth look failed; with no answer within `judgeSyncSeconds`, 1 s, its responder and timer are freed and nothing is judged) and, still unconfirmed and not past stop, forgets it wherever it is kept, with no command needed (`startJudged`, #1019: out of `synths` under any key first, so nothing restarts it, then `endFailedStart`, which runs its `onFree` as an `/n_end` would: the releasing, fading and cutting sets, `ringReaders`, `grainSlots`, `inputReaders`, a note's slot). The voice is without that synth until its next `voiceOn`, where `gateVoice` starts what it is missing. Before #1019 only the voice's next `voiceOn` or cut judged it (`forgetFailedStarts`, which still runs there), and every record kept the dead node meanwhile. `freeCuttingSynths` forgets them first too, since a synth it frees leaves every set: a failed grain start that source switches release, cut and free before any `voiceOn` is forgotten there, never left as a ring reader or unconfirmed start (the check switches such a voice three times). Within `startConfirmWait` an unconfirmed synth is re-gated as before, so a fast stop and start (or the check's commands sent without a yield) never starts a second one.
- **A synth that frees itself is started again, but never in a loop** (#940). `voiceSynthFreed` starts a voice's live synth again when it frees (a late gate), at most `restartLimit` (32) times within `restartWindow` (1 s) for one voice, between two `voiceOn`s (`restartAllowed`, the times in `areaState[\voices][\restarts]`, at most 33 a voice; every `voiceOn` empties them in `gateVoice`). Past it the restart is refused and the voice is left without that synth until its next `voiceOn` (which starts what a voice is missing), with one `Strata invariant broken` line for the whole run of restarts, not one a restart. Why 32: a real loop (a synth that frees itself as it starts; #458's ran at 20 Hz through this path) turns over at the server's round trip, a hundred times a second or more, so 32 ends it within a fraction of a second; a player trilling a key in release mode meets a late gate a few times a second, which a lower limit would silence. Nothing restarts once the engine is past stop (`pastStop`, `docs/notes/engine/structure.md`). The lifecycle check drives the rule from the voice's restart times, set by hand, so it never rests on how many round trips fit in a second: 32 times older than the window let a self-freed voice synth start again (and are dropped), and 31 just now let one of the voice's three synths, freed together, start again and refuse the other two with one line; the next `voiceOn` starts them and empties the times.
- **`ringReaders`** holds every `\strataGrainRing` (live, releasing, fading or cutting) with its voice until it frees: `startGrainSynth` adds it, `watchVoiceSynth`'s `onFree` removes it, as `grainSlots` does for `\strataGrainBuf`.
- `replaceGrainSynth` first calls `unfreezeVoice(voice)` and bumps `ringChanges[voice]`, so a frozen voice is unfrozen and a ring copy still waiting for its fade never runs after a switch.
- **A voice's extra notes are the notes area's, never its own synths** (3.5, `docs/notes/engine/notes.md`): a note synth is in no set of `synths`, `releasingSynths`, `fadingSynths` or `cuttingSynths`, is watched by `watchNoteSynth` (nothing restarts it) and is bounded by its slot (one live and one cutting synth). `releaseVoice` releases the voice's live extra notes with it (`releaseNotes`); `voiceOn` resets the own note (`resetOwnNote`), and every own synth started or re-gated takes `ownNotePairs`.
- **Every grain start restarts the scan.** `gateVoice`'s re-gate of a releasing `\strataGrainBuf` carries `\t_scanReset, 1`, as a new grain synth starts its scan at 0; a `voiceOn` on a voice already running sends no reset (`docs/notes/engine/positions.md`).

## The synth sets

A voice's own synths are its voice synth (the synth source only), its grain synth and its mod synth, under `voiceKey`, `grainKey` and `modKey`. Each one is in exactly one of five sets at a time; whether the voice runs is derived (`voiceRunning`: a live voice or grain synth). A start the server has not yet confirmed (`unconfirmedStarts`, Watching, below) is a second fact beside the set, never a sixth set. The extra notes are the notes area's, in none of these sets (`docs/notes/engine/notes.md`).

| Set | Held in | On the server | Back to live |

| live | `synths[key]` | gate 1; the voice's sends reach it (`setSynth`, `setVoiceSynths`, `setGrainSynths`) | — |
| releasing | `releasingSynths[voice][key]`; a grain synth's source in `releasedGrainSource[voice]` | gate 0: its ASR's release | by the next `voiceOn`, if it still fits |
| fading | `fadingSynths[voice]` (an `IdentitySet`) | a forced release (`release(grainRelease)`, 2 s, linear), or a superseded grain synth's own release | never |
| cutting | `cuttingSynths[voice]` (an `IdentitySet`) | `kill` 1: the kill fade, `switchCutTime` (0.05 s) | never |
| gone | in no set | freed | a new synth starts under the key |

- **The commands that change no set**: `setGrainSource` with the voice's own source (Lua sends it at every start), and on a stopped voice, where it only records the source (the next `voiceOn` judges the releasing synths by it); `setVoiceType` with the voice's own type, and on a stopped, buffer, input or voice-source voice, where it only records the type (the next `voiceOn` cuts a releasing voice synth of another type), or under a spectral freeze, where the unfreeze makes the switch (`applyWaitingVoiceType`, `docs/notes/engine/types.md`); `retrigger`, `setVoiceParam`, `setGrainParam` and the crossmod commands, which send to the live set only (but a `freeze` or `freezeMode` that ends a spectral freeze with a type switch waiting, which makes that switch: the `setVoiceType` row, `applyWaitingVoiceType`); the slot-0 note commands, which park and unpark the live synths (`docs/notes/engine/notes.md`).
- **The states no event leaves**: one synth in two sets; a mod synth fading or cutting (`\strataMod` has no kill fade; a releasing mod synth is always re-gated); a releasing synth beside a live one under the same key (`releaseVoice` and `cutFadingSynths` warn if they ever meet one, the two warnings in the table); a second fading set (every move into fading first cuts the set there). So a voice holds at most its live synths, one fading set and one cutting set (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`), however fast the commands come; there is no Lua-side debounce, and switches inside one `switchCutTime` (a chord of scene buttons) still leave one cutting set.
- **What checks the rows**: `test/engine_check/lifecycle.scd` drives most of them by example: the source switches, bursts and spaced switches (the `setGrainSource` row, "source switching leaves no extra nodes"), a `voiceOn` within and after the release ("voiceOn during the release re-gates the same synths", "voiceOn late in the release brings back one trio"), a superseded synth at a start (`checkSupersededAtStart`: "voiceOn hands the previous source's releasing grain and voice synths to fadingSynths", "the next start for another source cuts them"), the fast stop / source / start / switch cycles (`checkStopStartCycles`), the two invariant warnings with stand-in synths, the starts the server never made ("lifecycle: after a stop, a voice whose grain synth never started gets one at its next start (#497 ...)", "lifecycle: a grain synth that never started, switched away three times before a voiceOn, is forgotten", and, with no command, "lifecycle: a synth whose /s_new failed is in no record past startConfirmWait, with no command sent: left running, stopped, switched away twice (#1019 ...)": each kind's `/s_new` held back from what the engine sends: a grain synth left live, one releasing and one cutting, a voice synth's and a mod synth's restart after an `/n_free` behind sclang's back, and an extra note's synth, judged against the check's own record of the synths it held back, and the sweep's unconfirmed starts), the late-gate restart and its limit ("lifecycle: a voice whose live synth frees itself more than restartLimit times in a second stops restarting, with one invariant line (#940 ...)"); `test/engine_check/types.scd` the `setVoiceType` row and the type cut at a start ("types: a releasing voice synth of another type is cut, never taken back"); `test/engine_check/structure.scd` the past-stop flag ("structure: pastStop is false while the engine runs and true once free has begun the stop phase"). And the seeded walk walks the rows of the commands together (`checkSynthSetWalk`, "lifecycle: a seeded walk of 2000 commands ... keeps every voice's synth sets as the table has them after each"): on a high engine of its own (a Pi 4's settings, so the spectral freeze is in it), voices 1-3 with two slots loaded and a pool of 6, 2000 commands of one seed (9) drawn from `voiceOn`, `voiceOff`, `setGrainSource` (the synth, two slots, the input, two voice sources), `setVoiceType`, `freeze`, `freezeMode` and `noteOn` (the own note and three extra slots), with `checkOwnSynths` after every command: the four sets are disjoint and hold none of the voice's note synths, no mod synth fades or cuts, nothing releases under a live key, at most one voice synth and one grain synth fade, and the live set is the source's (none on a stopped voice; a running one's grain and mod synths, its voice synth on the synth source only, its grain synth the source's def). Whether a voice runs, its source and its live extra notes are the walk's own record of its commands (a `voiceOn` runs the voice, a `voiceOff` stops it and releases its notes, a `setGrainSource` records the source, an extra note on a running voice is live while the pool has room), never read from the engine, so a `voiceOff` that releases nothing or a `voiceOn` that starts nothing fails at that command. The ignored lines are checked by text: an extra note to a stopped voice ("voice <v> is stopped") and one with the pool full of live notes ("the pool of 6 is full of live notes") post one line each, and any other line, or a missing one, fails. It fails unless it reaches the rows it names: a re-gate, a superseded synth handed to fading and a releasing voice synth of another type cut at a `voiceOn`, a release (counted once the grain synth has left the live set for the releasing one), a running source switch, a running type switch, a freeze, an extra note started, and the spectral rows: a running voice's type switch waiting for its spectral freeze's unfreeze, the waiting switch made at a `freeze` or `freezeMode` command, and a type switch of a stopped voice under a spectral freeze (#979). Seed 9 reaches each (on 3.14.1 the waiting switch made 5 times; seeds 1, 3, 4 and 7 of the first fourteen never make it). The commands go without a yield, so no `/n_end` or `/n_go` comes in and the sets move by the command rows alone, but for a sync each time half of `startConfirmWait` has passed (a walk longer than that, with no `/n_go` handled, would forget its own starts as failed: no sync in 2000 commands at 0.17-0.19 s on 3.14.1 and 0.15-0.23 s on 3.13, one when a loaded machine takes past 0.25 s); the `/n_end`, `/n_go`, confirm-timeout and stop rows are the examples' above. Its `freeze` and `freezeMode` commands come in bursts between syncs: before #1000's fix such a burst could pause a ring writer twice before its first block, and the run after crashed scsynth (the walk on the unchanged engine crashed it in 1 of 3 runs on 3.14.1); `freeze.scd` checks the smallest such burst by the messages the engine sends ([freeze.md](freeze.md)).
- The extra notes move with these rows: `voiceOff` releases them, a running switch cuts and restarts them, a type switch restarts the live voice synth notes (`docs/notes/engine/notes.md`, The cuts and restarts).

### Replace, never stack

**Replace, never stack.** A running switch's new grain synth is created with the voice's current `grainControls` (and the voice-level controls) and the old one gets its forced release in the same call, so one voice never runs two grain paths beyond that release, and once a switch has been processed (within `switchCutTime`) a voice has at most one releasing grain synth and one releasing voice synth, however fast or slowly the sources are scrolled (one switch per E3 detent). The voice synth that leaves the synth source releases with the old grain synth (a forced release: its sound only reached the output through that grain synth, so it does not run its own release, up to 30 s, silently); a return to the synth source starts a new voice synth from `voiceControls`. `voiceOn` for a voice whose source is not 0 builds its `\strataGrainBuf` (or its `\strataGrainRing`) directly and no voice synth. The switch itself is in Audio Sources, `docs/notes/engine/grains.md`. New grain synths are placed by `startPlacedGrainSynth` (see Node Ordering in `docs/notes/engine/structure.md`), never next to a releasing synth: that one may have freed on the server already, and an `/s_new` to a missing target creates nothing.

### Cutting: the kill fade

**Cutting is `cutSynth`, the kill fade, never a second forced release**: EnvGen starts a forced release only when its gate crosses -1 from above (`gate <= -1 && prevGate > -1`), so a synth already in `release(grainRelease)` (gate -3) ignores `release(0.05)` (gate -1.05) and would run its full 2 s. Every voice and grain SynthDef multiplies `killFade(kill)` into its ASR: `EnvGen.kr(Env([1, 0], [switchCutTime]), kill, doneAction: 2)`, which holds level 1 until the `kill` control rises above 0 and then fades linearly to 0 in `switchCutTime` (0.05 s; built into the SynthDefs in `alloc`, so changing it later has no effect) and frees the synth, whatever the ASR is doing. `kill` is that EnvGen's trigger, not its gate (`Env.cutoff` on `1 - kill` would never start in a synth killed in its creation block and would play on at full level). `cutSynth(synth)` sends `\kill, 1` to a synth that `isPlaying` (`cutVoiceSynth` keeps it in `cuttingSynths` until it frees, so the next cut can free it at once); a killed synth is never re-gated (it has already left `synths` and `releasingSynths`). `doneAction: 2` frees the synth after its release, or after its kill fade.

### Starting

`voiceOn` resets `voiceControls`, merges its grain values into `grainControls` and calls `gateVoice`, which never stacks synths: what the voice still has is re-gated, the `voiceOn` row of the table. A releasing synth's liveness is `isPlaying`, and its re-gate restarts the ASR's attack from the current level. The voice synth's release is its `releaseTime`, up to 30 s (`releaseTimeRange`, `#[0.01, 30]`, Lua's `v<i>_release`), but the grain synth's is 2 s and the mod synth's 0.5 s, so late in a long release only the voice synth is re-gated. Controls not in the voiceOn reset keep their old values on a re-gated synth until Lua sends them: a start sends its grain values before `voiceOn` (kept in `grainControls`, so the re-gated grain synth takes them with its gate) and its voice-level values right after it (#325: the area starts a buffer voice in that order and checks that no first grain reads the last run's position or plays in a mode chosen while stopped, and that a re-gate takes values sent during the release). A re-gated buffer grain synth also gets the slot's current `bufnumL/R`, `bufFrames` and (with audio) `bufRateScale` (`bufferSlotPairs`), since `bufferChanged` sends a releasing grain synth only `bufFrames 0` and the slot may have been loaded, recorded or cleared during the release. A releasing voice synth of another type (3.8: `setVoiceType` while the voice was stopped; its `defName` is not `voiceDefFor(voice)`) is cut, never taken back, before the type's def starts, so the old type's tail never comes back (ruling 15, `docs/notes/engine/types.md`). A releasing synth that no longer fits the source (it changed while the voice was stopped) is treated like a synth a switch replaces, before anything new starts (`fadeSupersededSynths`, after the cutting synths are freed and the fading ones cut: `replaceFadingSynths`), so the next switch, or the next such start, cuts it (#46). Buffer-, input- and voice-sourced voices re-gate their grain and mod synths the same way.

### Stopping

`voiceOff` (`releaseVoice`) gates the trio off and keeps it releasing for `voiceOn` to re-gate (a buffer- or input-sourced voice has only a grain and a mod synth). `gateVoice` leaves nothing releasing beside running synths, so no different synth should already be releasing under the same key; one that is joins the fading set as `gateVoice`'s do (`fadeSupersededSynths`), so no releasing synth goes untracked (voice and grain keys only: a releasing mod synth is always re-gated, never superseded). Fast voiceOff / source change / voiceOn / switch cycles inside one 50 ms cut never pile up synths. The engine check runs six such cycles on one voice, through the synth, buffer and input sources, twice. First it counts the engine's own tracking (live, releasing, fading and cutting synths) after every command, with all 24 commands sent without a yield: no `/n_end` is handled meanwhile, so the count does not depend on server timing. It finds at most three grain synths and three voice synths, and two voice synths at once after a stop on the synth source, a start on the input and a switch back to the synth (so the count measures something; the engine before #46 held five grain synths there). Then it counts the server's node tree after every step, as an upper bound only. Should a superseded synth ever be found in `releasingSynths` (by `releaseVoice`, or by `cutFadingSynths` for a running voice), the engine handles it as the table says and posts one `WARNING: Strata invariant broken: ...` line (`warnInvariant`), which shows in maiden and fails `test/run_engine_check.sh`. The engine check provokes both cases with stand-in synths. It captures the lines through `postInvariantWarning` (nil by default: `String.warn`) so its own run does not fail, and checks that the script's failure pattern matches them.

### Gates

Gate of every ASR: `openedGate(gate)` = `Select.kr(Impulse.kr(0) > 0, [gate, 1])`, used by every voice, grain and mod SynthDef. EnvGen starts only when its gate rises above 0, so a synth created and gated off in one control block (a MIDI note on and note off in one ~1.3 ms block, `voiceOn` + `voiceOff` in one bundle) would never start its ASR and never free. The opened gate is 1 in the synth's first block and the plain `gate` control after it, so that synth starts and releases from its second block, and normal releases, forced releases (negative gates; never `gate.max(...)`, which clamps them to a plain gate 0 and turns the 2 s cut into the full natural release) and re-gates are unchanged.

### Watching

Every voice, grain and mod synth is created through `startVoiceSynth`/`startGrainSynth`/`startModSynth`, which call `watchVoiceSynth` (`register(true)`, so `isPlaying` holds from creation, then `onFree`, the `/n_end` row). A synth still in `synths` when it frees was re-gated just as its release ended (the gate arrived too late); it is started again so the voice is never left without it (the engine check frees each of the three behind sclang's back, #505). `free` empties `synths` after freeing them, and nothing restarts once it has begun its stop phase (`pastStop`, #942); a voice restarts at most `restartLimit` (32) times a second between two `voiceOn`s (#940, above). Until its `/n_go` a synth is unconfirmed (`unconfirmedStarts`); one whose `/s_new` failed is forgotten at its own timer, `startConfirmWait` after its start (#1019), and at the voice's next `voiceOn` past it (#497); that none is older than twice `startConfirmWait` is held by the bookkeeping sweep (`sweepBookkeeping`, after every area of the engine check: `docs/notes/test-harness/engine-check-runner.md`). The engine check makes that failure by holding a grain synth's `/s_new` back from a `voiceOn`'s bundle (`makeBundle(false)`), the same for sclang as a failed one and with no `FAILURE` line, and expects a grain node after a stop and start and after a `voiceOn` on the running voice.

### Sending to a voice's synths

- Per-voice commands go through `setSynth(key, ...)`, which ignores voices that are not running, and voice indices are range-checked with `validVoice`
- **Messages to synths that can end on their own go through `sendToSynth`**: a bundle that opens with `/error -1` and closes with `/error -2`. The server counts them: `-1` turns its error reports off until the end of the bundle it is in, `-2` turns them back on, so the reports are off for `sendToSynth`'s own messages only. That matters because `makeBundle` (the `voiceOn` handler's) flattens every `sendToSynth` into one outer bundle: with `-1` alone, a re-gate early in `voiceOn` silenced the `/s_new` of a voice or grain synth and the mod synth's bundle after it, so a start that failed there (the pool full) posted no `FAILURE` line and sent no `/fail` (#498). Such a synth may have freed on the server a moment ago with its `/n_end` not yet in sclang (`isPlaying` still true), and a message to it would post `FAILURE IN SERVER ... Node not found` (which `test/run_engine_check.sh` fails on: a flake) and send a `/fail`. The racy sends: `gateVoice` re-gating releasing synths (`set`, the mod synth's too) and giving a superseded voice synth its forced release; `cutSynth` on fading synths; `freeCuttingSynths`; `bufferChanged` on releasing and fading grain synths; and, for a voice's live synths (which free when their release ended just as `voiceOn` re-gated them), `setSynth` with a voice, grain or mod key, `releaseVoice` and `replaceGrainSynth`'s forced releases; `stopRecording` freeing a record synth whose take may have just ended (`freeMsg`, then `free(false)` to mark it freed). No synth is ever moved: grain synths are placed at the head of a group (`startPlacedGrainSynth`), and the one move, `startModSynth`'s `/n_before` of the voice's mix group just before its new mod synth, goes in a plain bundle, since it moves a group and a group never ends (#507). The engine check provokes each: it frees the synths on the server behind sclang's back, sends the command at once and expects no `/fail`. The effects chain's synths (`effectKeys`) never end on their own, so `setSynth` sends to them with a plain `set`: a failure there is a real bug, which the server must report (the engine check captures what `setFilter` sends and finds no `/error -1` around it, while a voice's `setVoiceParam` has one). It also takes the bundle of a `voiceOn` that re-gates a releasing voice synth (`makeBundle(false)`, then sends it) and finds both `/s_new`s where its `-1`s and `-2`s balance (#498)

## Rings and the lifecycle

- `startGrainSynth` builds `\strataGrainRing` for the synth and input sources and `\strataGrainBuf` for a slot, each writing its source kind's path (`\out`, `voicePathBus`). The synth source sets `rateLevel` and `pan` 0 with its mod buses on `silentBus`; the input and buffer sources `rateLevel` 1. `grainControlPairs` carries the ring and its history: it ends with the freeze area's `grainReadPairs` (`bufL`, `bufR`, `phaseBus`, `history`: the synth source's voice ring at history 0, the input ring at the voice's history, a frozen input voice's copy at history 0; nothing for a slot), last so it wins over the kept history, so a start and `gateVoice`'s re-gate read the same thing (`docs/notes/engine/freeze.md`). There is no `ringPairs`. `startGrainSynth` and the re-gate then call `noteInputReader` (a synth reading the copy joins `\inputReaders`), and `onFree` calls `forgetInputReader` beside `ringReaders.removeAt`. `startPlacedGrainSynth` puts a synth-source grain synth at the head of `processGroup` and any other at the head of the voice's mix group; `startModSynth` adds the mod synth at the tail of `processGroup` and moves the voice's mix group just before it in the same bundle (`gateVoice` no longer moves a re-gated mod synth: it is already after its group). See `docs/notes/engine/mix.md`.
- The ring writers are persistent like the effects (`effectKeys`): no voice command ever releases, cuts or re-gates them, and they are not among a voice's synths. Placement (`startPlacedGrainSynth`), `gateVoice`, the fading and cutting sets, `freeCuttingSynths` and `sendToSynth` are as before: rings add no synth that can end on its own.
- The engine check's lifecycle area runs the switch and recall stress over the new defs and asserts that `ringReaders` maps every ring grain synth to its voice and empties as they free. Since 3.5 it runs the burst, the spaced switches and the fast stop / source / start / switch cycles again with a chord of four held on five voices (the own note and three extra notes each: 15 extra notes, within the pool of 16, where all seven voices' 21 would not fit; the cycled voice plays its chord again at each start): the own synths, counted without the note synths, keep the bounds above, every slot holds at most one live and one cutting synth, the live notes stay three a voice, within the pool, and after each stress the note synths the server runs are exactly the slots' (`h.noteNodesAgree`).

## Notes and the lifecycle (3.5)

- `startGrainSynth(voice, target, addAction, notePairs, watch)`: with `notePairs` and `watch` nil it builds the voice's own grain synth, watched by `watchVoiceSynth`, with the own note's park and expression when it sounds the own note (`ownNotePairs`: a buffer or input voice's grain synth; the synth-source one takes the neutral values). The notes area's `startNoteSynth` passes a note's pairs (pitch, accent, park open, expression: last, so they win) and its own watcher (`watchNoteSynth`). The bookkeeping (`grainSlots`, `ringReaders`, `noteInputReader`) is the same for both.
- `startVoiceSynth` adds `ownNotePairs` too, and `gateVoice` sends them with each re-gate of the voice and grain synths (`voiceOn` has reset the own note by then, so a re-gate unparks it and puts its expression back to neutral).
- `watchStart(synth)` is what `watchVoiceSynth` and `watchNoteSynth` share: `register(true)`, the entry in `unconfirmedStarts` until the synth's `/n_go` (#497) and the timer that judges it at `startConfirmWait` (`startJudged`, #1019). `forgetGrainReader(synth)` is what their `onFree` share: out of `grainSlots`, `ringReaders` and the freeze area's `inputReaders`.
- `releaseVoice` ends with `releaseNotes(voice)`: every live extra note of the voice gets `gate 0` (through `sendToSynth`) and its slot is releasing until the synth frees.
- `replaceGrainSynth` runs its work (`switchGrainSynth`) inside one `makeBundle`, ending with `restartNotes(voice)`: every extra note of the voice is cut and each that was live restarts on the new source from its kept values (its kind by the voice's state after the unfreeze), keeping its order; a releasing one is not restarted. The own synths' crossfade is as before, so a slot holds a live and a cutting synth at most, and the own synths' bounds hold with a chord held (the lifecycle check's chord stress).
