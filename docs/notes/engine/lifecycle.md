## Must not break

- **Replace, never stack.** A source switch replaces the grain synth. Earlier fading synths are cut with `cutSynth` (the `kill` fade), never a second `release` (EnvGen ignores it). New grain synths are placed with `startPlacedGrainSynth`, never relative to a releasing synth.
- `voiceOn` never stacks synths: it re-gates what the voice still has, and a voice holds at most its live synths, one fading set and one cutting set.
- **`openedGate(gate)`** wraps every ASR gate, so a synth gated off in its first block still starts and frees. Never use `gate.max(...)`: it breaks forced releases.
- **Messages to synths that can end on their own** (releasing, fading or cutting synths; a voice's live synths in `setSynth` with a voice, grain or mod key, `releaseVoice` and switches; record synths) go through `sendToSynth` (a bundle with `/error -1`), never `synth.set`/`release`/`free`/`moveBefore` directly: the synth may have freed a moment ago, and the server's `FAILURE ... Node not found` line fails the engine check. The effects chain's synths (`effectKeys`: the three master filters `\filter`, `\filterDelay`, `\filterReverb`, then delay, reverb, master) never end on their own, so `setSynth` sends to them plainly: a failure there is a real bug and must stay reported. The engine frees and re-creates the delay and the reverb itself (bypass), so they stay `effectKeys`; `setSynth` skips a missing key.
- A broken bookkeeping invariant posts one `Strata invariant broken` line (`warnInvariant`), which fails the engine check.
- **`ringReaders`** holds every `\strataGrainRing` (live, releasing, fading or cutting) with its voice until it frees: `startGrainSynth` adds it, `watchVoiceSynth`'s `onFree` removes it, as `grainSlots` does for `\strataGrainBuf`.
- `replaceGrainSynth` first calls `unfreezeVoice(voice)` and bumps `ringChanges[voice]`, so a frozen voice is unfrozen and a ring copy still waiting for its fade never runs after a switch.
- **Every grain start restarts the scan.** `gateVoice`'s re-gate of a releasing `\strataGrainBuf` carries `\t_scanReset, 1`, as a new grain synth starts its scan at 0; a `voiceOn` on a voice already running sends no reset (`docs/notes/engine/positions.md`).

## Replace, never stack

**Replace, never stack.** `setGrainSource` on a running voice calls `replaceGrainSynth`: the new grain synth is created with the voice's current `grainControls` (and voice-level controls) and the old one gets a forced release (`release(grainRelease)`, 2 s, linear) in the same call, so one voice never runs two grain paths beyond that release. First `cutFadingSynths(voice)` cuts everything an earlier switch (or a `voiceOn` for another source, see Synth Lifecycle) left releasing (`fadingSynths[voice]`, an `IdentitySet` emptied by the cut and by `onFree`), and any grain or voice synth still in `releasingSynths` (none should be: `voiceOn` re-gates them or hands them to `fadingSynths`), so once a switch has been processed (within `switchCutTime`) a voice has at most one releasing grain synth and at most one releasing voice synth, however fast or slowly the sources are scrolled (one switch per E3 detent); there is no Lua-side debounce. A switch first frees at once whatever an earlier cut left still in its kill fade (`freeCuttingSynths`), so even switches faster than `switchCutTime` leave at most one cutting set per voice (see `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`). **Cutting is `cutSynth`, the kill fade, never a second forced release**: EnvGen starts a forced release only when its gate crosses -1 from above (`gate <= -1 && prevGate > -1`), so a synth already in `release(grainRelease)` (gate -3) ignores `release(0.05)` (gate -1.05) and would run its full 2 s. Every voice and grain SynthDef multiplies `killFade(kill)` into its ASR: `EnvGen.kr(Env([1, 0], [switchCutTime]), kill, doneAction: 2)`, which holds level 1 until the `kill` control rises above 0 and then fades linearly to 0 in `switchCutTime` (0.05 s; built into the SynthDefs in `alloc`, so changing it later has no effect) and frees the synth, whatever the ASR is doing. `kill` is that EnvGen's trigger, not its gate (`Env.cutoff` on `1 - kill` would never start in a synth killed in its creation block and would play on at full level). `cutSynth(synth)` sends `\kill, 1` to a synth that `isPlaying`; a killed synth is never re-gated (it has already left `synths` and `releasingSynths`). Leaving the synth source releases the voice synth with `release(grainRelease)` (a forced release: its sound only reached the output through the old grain synth, so it does not run its own up-to-12 s release silently) and adds it to `fadingSynths`; returning to the synth source starts a new voice synth from `voiceControls`. New grain synths are placed by `startPlacedGrainSynth` (see Node Ordering in `docs/notes/engine/structure.md`), never next to a releasing synth: that one may have freed on the server already, and an `/s_new` to a missing target creates nothing. `voiceOn` for a voice whose source is not 0 builds `\strataGrainBuf` (or the input's `\strataGrainRing`) directly and no voice synth. `setGrainSource` with the current source does nothing (Lua sends it on every voice start).

## Synth Lifecycle

Synths use `gate` with ASR envelopes:
- Starting: `voiceOn` resets `voiceControls`, merges its grain values into `grainControls` and calls `gateVoice`, which never stacks synths. Synths the voice still has are **re-gated**: a running voice's only take the voiceOn values (`set`); a released voice's synths still in their release (`releasingSynths[voice]`, liveness = `isPlaying`) get `\gate, 1` (the ASR restarts its attack from the current level) plus the voiceOn values with `set` (a `\strataGrainBuf` also `\t_scanReset, 1`, so its scan starts over as a new synth's does); the ones that have already freed are created (`Synth.new` with `gate=1`). The voice synth's release is 2-12 s but the grain synth's is 2 s and the mod synth's 0.5 s, so late in a release only the voice synth is re-gated. Controls not in the voiceOn reset keep their old values on a re-gated synth until Lua's `sync_voice_to_engine` (always right after `voiceOn`) sends them; a re-gated buffer grain synth also gets the slot's current `bufnumL/R`, `bufFrames` and (with audio) `bufRateScale` (`bufferSlotPairs`), since `bufferChanged` sends a releasing grain synth only `bufFrames 0` and the slot may have been loaded, recorded or cleared during the release. A releasing synth that no longer fits the source (it changed while the voice was stopped) is treated like a synth a switch replaces, before anything new starts (`fadeSupersededSynths`): a grain synth built for another source (`releasedGrainSource[voice]`) finishes its release and a voice synth when the source is no longer the synth is given a forced release (`release(grainRelease)`), both as the voice's `fadingSynths`, after the cutting synths are freed and the fading ones cut (`replaceFadingSynths`). So the next switch, or the next such start, cuts them, and a voice never holds more than its live synths, one fading and one cutting set (#46; the new grain synth is placed by `startPlacedGrainSynth`). Buffer- and input-sourced voices re-gate their grain and mod synths the same way
- Stopping: `releaseVoice` sets `\gate, 0` (release phase), removes the trio from `synths` (a buffer- or input-sourced voice has only a grain and a mod synth) and keeps it in `releasingSynths[voice]` (the grain's source in `releasedGrainSource`) for `voiceOn` to re-gate. `gateVoice` leaves nothing releasing beside running synths, so no different synth should already be releasing under the same key; one that is joins the fading set as `gateVoice`'s do (`fadeSupersededSynths`), so no releasing synth goes untracked (voice and grain keys only: a releasing mod synth is always re-gated, never superseded, and `\strataMod` has no kill fade). Fast voiceOff / source change / voiceOn / switch cycles inside one 50 ms cut never pile up synths. The engine check runs six such cycles on one voice, through the synth, buffer and input sources, twice. First it counts the engine's own tracking (live, releasing, fading and cutting synths) after every command, with all 24 commands sent without a yield: no `/n_end` is handled meanwhile, so the count does not depend on server timing. It finds at most three grain synths and three voice synths, and two voice synths at once after a stop on the synth source, a start on the input and a switch back to the synth (so the count measures something; the engine before #46 held five grain synths there). Then it counts the server's node tree after every step, as an upper bound only. Should a superseded synth ever be found in `releasingSynths` (by `releaseVoice`, or by `cutFadingSynths` for a running voice), the engine handles it as above and posts one `WARNING: Strata invariant broken: ...` line (`warnInvariant`), which shows in maiden and fails `test/run_engine_check.sh`. The engine check provokes both cases with stand-in synths. It captures the lines through `postInvariantWarning` (nil by default: `String.warn`) so its own run does not fail, and checks that the script's failure pattern matches them
- Gate of every ASR: `openedGate(gate)` = `Select.kr(Impulse.kr(0) > 0, [gate, 1])`, used by every voice, grain and mod SynthDef. EnvGen starts only when its gate rises above 0, so a synth created and gated off in one control block (a MIDI note on and note off in one ~1.3 ms block, `voiceOn` + `voiceOff` in one bundle) would never start its ASR and never free. The opened gate is 1 in the synth's first block and the plain `gate` control after it, so that synth starts and releases from its second block, and normal releases, forced releases (negative gates; never `gate.max(...)`, which clamps them to a plain gate 0 and turns the 2 s cut into the full natural release) and re-gates are unchanged
- Watching: every voice, grain and mod synth is created through `startVoiceSynth`/`startGrainSynth`/`startModSynth`, which call `watchVoiceSynth` (`register(true)`, so `isPlaying` holds from creation, then `onFree`). A freed synth is dropped from `releasingSynths`. A synth still in `synths` when it frees was re-gated just as its release ended (the gate arrived too late); it is started again so the voice is never left without it. `free` empties `synths` after freeing them so nothing restarts
- Switching source: `replaceGrainSynth` (see Audio Sources in `docs/notes/engine/grains.md`); the synths it releases are tracked in `fadingSynths[voice]` and cut (`cutVoiceSynth`: `cutSynth`, the kill fade, recorded in `cuttingSynths[voice]`) by the next switch, which also frees at once any synth still in its kill fade from the switch before (see `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`)
- `doneAction: 2` frees the synth after release (or after its kill fade)
- Per-voice commands go through `setSynth(key, ...)`, which ignores voices that are not running, and voice indices are range-checked with `validVoice`
- **Messages to synths that can end on their own go through `sendToSynth`**: a bundle with `/error -1`, which turns the server's error reports off for that bundle only. Such a synth may have freed on the server a moment ago with its `/n_end` not yet in sclang (`isPlaying` still true), and a message to it would post `FAILURE IN SERVER ... Node not found` (which `test/run_engine_check.sh` fails on: a flake) and send a `/fail`. The racy sends: `gateVoice` re-gating releasing synths (`set`), giving a superseded voice synth its forced release and moving a re-gated mod synth (`moveAfter`); `cutSynth` on fading synths; `freeCuttingSynths`; `bufferChanged` on releasing and fading grain synths; and, for a voice's live synths (which free when their release ended just as `voiceOn` re-gated them), `setSynth` with a voice, grain or mod key, `releaseVoice`, `replaceGrainSynth`'s forced releases and `startPlacedGrainSynth`'s `moveBefore` the mod synth; `stopRecording` freeing a record synth whose take may have just ended (`freeMsg`, then `free(false)` to mark it freed). The engine check provokes each: it frees the synths on the server behind sclang's back, sends the command at once and expects no `/fail`. The effects chain's synths (`effectKeys`) never end on their own, so `setSynth` sends to them with a plain `set`: a failure there is a real bug, which the server must report (the engine check captures what `setFilter` sends and finds no `/error -1` around it, while a voice's `setVoiceParam` has one)

## Rings and the lifecycle

- `startGrainSynth` builds `\strataGrainRing` for the synth and input sources and `\strataGrainBuf` for a slot, each writing its source kind's path (`\out`, `voicePathBus`). The synth source sets `rateLevel` and `pan` 0 with its mod buses on `silentBus`; the input and buffer sources `rateLevel` 1. `grainControlPairs` carries the ring and its history: it ends with the freeze area's `grainReadPairs` (`bufL`, `bufR`, `phaseBus`, `history`: the synth source's voice ring at history 0, the input ring at the voice's history, a frozen input voice's copy at history 0; nothing for a slot), last so it wins over the kept history, so a start and `gateVoice`'s re-gate read the same thing (`docs/notes/engine/freeze.md`). There is no `ringPairs`. `startGrainSynth` and the re-gate then call `noteInputReader` (a synth reading the copy joins `\inputReaders`), and `onFree` calls `forgetInputReader` beside `ringReaders.removeAt`. `startPlacedGrainSynth` puts a synth-source grain synth at the head of `processGroup` and any other at the head of the voice's mix group; `startModSynth` adds the mod synth at the tail of `processGroup` and moves the voice's mix group just before it in the same bundle (`gateVoice` no longer moves a re-gated mod synth: it is already after its group). See `docs/notes/engine/mix.md`.
- The ring writers are persistent like the effects (`effectKeys`): no voice command ever releases, cuts or re-gates them, and they are not among a voice's synths. Placement (`startPlacedGrainSynth`), `gateVoice`, the fading and cutting sets, `freeCuttingSynths` and `sendToSynth` are as before: rings add no synth that can end on its own.
- The engine check's lifecycle area runs the switch and recall stress over the new defs and asserts that `ringReaders` maps every ring grain synth to its voice and empties as they free.
