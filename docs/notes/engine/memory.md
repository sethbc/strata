## Must not break

- norns' scsynth pool is 8192 KB. No grain SynthDef has a `PitchShift` or a delay line, and every `GrainBuf` uses `maxGrains` (128); never size a delay line or table from a control.
- A voice never holds more than its live synths, one fading and one cutting set, however fast switches, stops and starts arrive (`freeCuttingSynths`, `fadeSupersededSynths`).
- The worst case (spaced, in a burst and for fast A -> B -> A -> C recalls) leaves at least **3 MB** of the pool free on the standard tier (twelve 256 KB probes). On the high tier it leaves at least **4 MB** (sixteen probes, with spectral freeze engaged on every voice: about 4.8 MB free before 2d, about TBD MB with 2d's voice filter, sends and FDN reverb; a spectral writer taking about 80 KB; `docs/notes/engine/freeze.md`). Each spectral writer's `PV_MagFreeze` takes its last ~16 KB lazily, once its first frame is analyzed (about 21 ms in): after the probes have allocated but while they are still held, so an allocation that did not fit fails the run (`alloc failed`), while the printed free figure may be up to about 16 KB per writer (112 KB for seven) too high. The probes start inside the kill fade, while the cutting synths are still there (the check asserts it).
- Each grain synth takes under 80 KB of the pool (about 61 and 64 KB with 3.2's voice filter and sends).
- **Buffer memory** (ordinary RAM, not the pool): 8 × 2 mono slot buffers of `bufferSeconds` (16 s on standard, ≈ 49 MB; up to 300 s on high, set by the performance area: [performance.md](performance.md)), 7 × 2 voice rings of 4 s (10.8 MB) and an input ring pair of `historySeconds` + 5 s (8.1 MB standard, 25 MB high). Never allocate longer ones. Allocated once; bufnums never change.

## Real-time memory

Every UGen's delay lines and tables come from scsynth's real-time pool, allocated when a synth starts. norns never raises it: `Crone.sc` boots `Server.local` with `memSize` left at the `ServerOptions` default, **8192 KB** (its `memSize = 2**16` line is commented out). A UGen that cannot get its memory prints `<UGen>_Ctor: alloc failed` and outputs zeros, and a synth whose own node cannot be allocated fails with `/s_new out of real time memory`: either way that voice goes silent with nothing on screen. Buffers (`b_alloc`: the slots and the rings) are not in this pool.

What a grain synth takes, at 48 kHz:
- `GrainBuf` grain tables: about 100 bytes per grain, `maxGrains` (128) per `GrainBuf`, two per synth: about 26 KB per synth (measured: 13.6 KB for one 128-grain `GrainBuf`, whatever its channel count). `maxGrains` is 128 (the UGen's default is 512): the largest overlap Lua can ask for is grain size 0.5 s × density 100 × master density 2 = 100 grains, and a `GrainBuf` runs at most `maxGrains - 1` (beyond that it drops grains and prints `Too many grains!`, on which `test/run_engine_check.sh` fails).
- the rest of the graph: about 250 bytes per UGen (unit structs, inputs and outputs). `\strataGrainRing` has 85 UGens and `\strataGrainBuf` 103.
- 3.1's `\strataGrain` and `\strataGrainBuf` also each had a `PitchShift` (128 KB at its fixed 0.1 s window) and `\strataGrain` a position-spread `DelayC` (128 KB). 3.2 removed both: pitch is the grain rate and the spread is a backward position offset in the ring (`docs/notes/engine/rings.md`).

**Budget** (used KB of the 8192 KB pool; the per-synth and worst-case figures were first read with `/rtMemoryStatus` on 3.14.1 and match what `test/engine_check/memory.scd`'s allocation probes measure, which its check lines print):

| | 3.0.2 / 3.1 (scsynth 3.14) | 3.2 standard (scsynth 3.14.1, local) |
|---|---|---|
| engine idle (effects chain, input, writers; delay 2 s stereo = 1 MB, master `PitchShift` 0.2 s = 256 KB, `FreeVerb2`) | about 1460 | about 1500-1526 |
| one synth grain synth | 274 (`\strataGrain`) | 47.6-47.7 (`\strataGrainRing`) |
| one buffer grain synth | 152 | 51.4 |
| the worst case (below), with its cutting synths | about 6920 (1270 free) | 2758-2786 (about 5.4 MB free), standard and high alike until 2a and 2d add spectral freeze and the FDN reverb |

CI runs the same check on SuperCollider 3.13.0 (norns' version); its lines carry its own figures.

- **The worst case**: seven voices at grain size 0.5 and density 200, each switched synth -> buffer -> input -> synth with each switch processed before the next (as E3 detents arrive), then stopped and started. At the last switch every voice holds its live voice, grain and mod synths, the input grain synth in its 2 s release and the buffer grain synth in its 50 ms cut: 21 grain synths and 7 voice synths.
- **Bursts**: switches that arrive inside one 50 ms cut (a chord of grid scene buttons recalls each scene at once and every recall switches each voice; PMAP-mapped `v<i>_source` params) would leave a synth in its kill fade per switch. Every step that makes a new fading set therefore first frees, at once (`freeCuttingSynths`: `/n_free` in a bundle with `/error -1`, since one may have just ended its fade on the server), the synths an earlier cut left in their fade (`cuttingSynths[voice]`, filled by `cutVoiceSynth`, emptied by `onFree`), then cuts the current fading set (`replaceFadingSynths`): a switch (`cutFadingSynths`), and a `voiceOn` that meets releasing synths built for another source (`gateVoice` hands them to `fadingSynths` through `fadeSupersededSynths`, before it starts anything; `releaseVoice` does the same with a superseded synth, which `gateVoice` no longer leaves). A synth freed this way stops without the rest of its 50 ms fade; that only happens to a synth already fading out under a newer one.
- **Recalls** (#46): scenes A = on/synth, B = off/input, C = on/buffer recalled A -> B -> A -> C at once (a grid chord, a fast scene sequencer). The voice holds its live synths, one fading set (at most one grain and one voice synth) and one cutting set, however switches, stops and starts interleave, and A -> B -> A -> C ends with the same synths as the worst case above: it is the real bound. Lua also stops a voice a recall stops before setting its source (see `docs/notes/lua/scenes.md`).

The engine check boots its server with `memSize = 8192` (norns' value, set explicitly), runs the worst case, the burst and the A -> B -> A -> C recalls, and each time sends twelve 256 KB probes (`\strataCheckMemProbe`, a `DelayN` that passes 1 or reads 0 when its allocation failed, on a zeroed bus) right after the last command, so they start inside the 50 ms kill fade, then measures what is left and asks (`/n_query`, errors off) which cutting synths still exist: every synth in a cutting set must still be there (the probes meet the real peak), and all probes must allocate, so the worst case leaves at least 3 MB free.

**Measuring without `/rtMemoryStatus`.** SuperCollider 3.13 (norns' and CI's version) has no `/rtMemoryStatus` (it is new in 3.14, and an unknown command posts a `FAILURE` line, which fails the check), so the area measures only with allocation probes, on every version. A failing `DelayN` probe prints `alloc failed`, so those are used only where all must fit (the floors). The figures come from a flood of small graph probes (`\strataCheckPoolProbe`: about 17 KB of controls, nothing that allocates in a UGen) sent in one bundle with `/error -1`: a probe whose graph does not fit is simply not created, quietly, so the number created times a probe's size is the free pool to within one probe. A small probe's size is calibrated on the running server against four `DelayN` probes (1 MB). A `DelayN`, `GrainBuf` or `Pluck` takes its memory when it first runs (the next block) while a graph takes its own at `/s_new`, so a flood waits a sync and 10 ms after everything sent before it. Two 64 KB `DelayN` probes (`\strataCheckReserveProbe`) held around each flood (and counted as free; 256 KB each until 2d, when beside sixteen probes the high worst case left too little room for them and the spectral writers' lazy allocations) keep the server from ever running with a full pool, so replies and anything an engine synth allocates meanwhile find room. Per grain synth: the small probes that fit with and without twenty of them (within 1 KB). On 3.14.1 the probes give the same figures as `/rtMemoryStatus` did (48.0 and 51.4 KB per synth, about 5.4 MB free at the worst case). The high run then replaces the default engine with a high one (`h.replaceEngine`: a `high` setting, a Pi 4 model, a large `MemAvailable`; one engine alone in the pool), asserts it runs tier high, runs the same three cases with sixteen probes (4 MB), and brings the default engine back for the areas that follow. (Before this, the probes started after syncs and a status read, about 145 ms after the cut: the 50 ms fades had ended, so the figure, about 2400 KB, left the cutting synths out.) It also measures each grain SynthDef (under 80 KB each) and the idle engine's free pool. `test/run_engine_check.sh` also fails on any `alloc failed` in the server output. A new UGen with a delay line or table in a per-voice SynthDef multiplies by up to three grain (or two voice) synths per voice: rerun the check, and keep the headroom.

## The floors, and what 3.5 needs

The floors replace 3.1's 1 MB rule, sized for 3.5's note streams (`docs/design/3.2-design.md` §2.7): a note stream is one grain synth, and under today's lifecycle rule (a switch keeps a full fading set) each note beyond the first adds 21 grain synths and about 14 voice synths across the seven voices.
- The design estimated about 35 KB per stream (24 KB of grain tables plus 11 KB of graph). Measured on scsynth 3.14.1 it is about 48-51 KB (26 KB of tables plus about 21-25 KB of graph), so each extra note costs about 21 × 50 + 250 ≈ 1.3 MB rather than 1.0 MB.
- **Standard** (2 notes): 1.3 MB plus the 1 MB rule = 2.3 MB, inside the 3 MB floor; the measured worst case leaves about 5.4 MB free.
- **High**: both floors are kept. Under today's lifecycle rule high fits three 3.5 notes (2 × 1.3 + 1 = 3.6 MB, inside the 4 MB floor); four (3 × 1.3 + 1 = 4.9 MB) need a lifecycle change (a switch that does not keep a full fading set of notes) or a smaller grain graph. The design (§2.7) records this; the 3.5 design re-measures on norns' own scsynth (the hardware check, #6) before it settles the note count.
- **The mix area (2d)** adds each grain synth's voice filter and sends (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`): about 57 UGens, about 13 KB per synth, so `\strataGrainRing` measures about 60.7 KB and `\strataGrainBuf` 64.0 KB (the design budgeted about 10 KB for the filter, the sends and 2b's draws together), and the FDN reverb about 100 KB more than `FreeVerb2` on high (about 200 KB: each line's `DelayL` sized by its own length). The worst case then leaves about 5.07 MB free on standard and about 4.33 MB on high with spectral freeze on every voice and the FDN (up to about 112 KB less once the spectral writers' lazy allocations are counted: about 4.2 MB, about 120 KB above the 4 MB floor plus its measurement reserve). 2b's draws (about 200 KB estimated) would take high to about 4.0 MB; the voice filter's lowpass-only fallback saves about 4.2 KB per grain synth (about 88 KB). A 3.5 stream is about 63 KB and each extra note about 21 × 63 + 250 ≈ 1.6 MB: high's three notes would need 2 × 1.6 + 1 = 4.2 MB, just over the 4 MB floor, so the 3.5 design must count the voice filter (or make it cheaper) when it settles the note count.
- If a floor ever fails, find which estimate was wrong before cutting anything.
