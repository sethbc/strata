## Must not break

- **The stage is persistent and never re-gated.** Each voice has exactly one `\strataVoiceMix` at the tail of its mix group from `start` to `stop`: no voice command creates, frees, releases, cuts, moves or re-gates it. Its key (`voiceMix0`..`voiceMix6`) is one of the `effectKeys`, so messages to it are plain. A switch, a stop or a recall changes nothing on it; the check holds every stage's node ID through them.
- **The voice-level gain stays after the voice filter** (on the other path): the filter does not commute with a gain that moves within its memory, so `V = voiceLevel(amp, modSig, accent)` multiplies the filtered other path, never its input (`docs/design/3.3-design.md` §0c.1). The synth path takes no level.
- **The mirror is the other path only** (`Out.ar(mirrorOut, other)`: filtered and after the level). The synth path never mirrors: `\strataMod` already follows the voice synth on `granBuses[i]`. The tap is the whole output (`sig`), before the dry level and the sends (3.8 plan ruling 30): a voice at dry 0 still records on its voice source and feeds 1d's output ring at full level.
- **Grain synths of the buffer and input sources start at the head of their mix group**, synth-source ones at the head of `processGroup` (`startPlacedGrainSynth`), never relative to another synth (one may have just freed).
- **The mix group sits just before its voice's mod synth.** `startModSynth` adds the mod synth at the tail of `processGroup` and moves the voice's group before it in the same bundle (also on `onFree`'s restart), so the mirror reaches `\strataMod` in the same block. A re-gated mod synth is already after its group; nothing moves it.
- **The stage's inputs and filter outputs stay guarded.** Both `In.ar`s and both filter banks' outputs go through `zeroBadValues` per channel, so nothing bad ever leaves the stage (mirror, tap and sends included). A NaN or inf on a bank's raw output (codes 1 and 2 of `CheckBadValues`) replaces the stage in place (`replaceBadVoiceMix`: `Synth.replace`, same group and key, from `stageControls` and the voice's kept values, at most one replacement in flight per voice, posting `strata: voice <n> mix stage reset (bad values)`). A denormal (code 3), which a filter bank decaying to silence produces, is zeroed by the guard and never replaces it. The input guards are what keep a bad input from replacing the stage over and over while the input stays bad (its outputs clean all the while): the check puts 50 ms of inf and NaN on both paths and asserts the same node, nothing bad on the dry bus, the sends, the mirror or the tap, and the voice playing afterward (#367).
- **Plain sends** (3.8 design §7.1, `voiceOutput`): the dry bus takes the signal times `dry`, each send bus the signal times its send, the sends taken before the dry level (a voice at dry 0 is fully wet); no level of the effects reaches the stage (3.7's `fxMix` bus and its readers are gone). `dry` is the stage's arg (`dry=1`) and a grain param (`grainParamHandlers`, kept in `grainControls`), so a replaced stage restarts with it.
- **The stage gets its buses from `stageControls`**, never only from its SynthDef defaults (they are the buses of the last engine built); `\tapOut` is added in `start`, after the record area has allocated the taps. Its mod slots start on `silentBus` and `voiceOn` resets them there, never control bus 0.
- `grainParamHandlers` is written as a literal `grainParamHandlers: (...)` Event, so the Lua stub reads its keys.

## The mix area (`StrataMix.sc`, order 120)

`\strataVoiceMix` (one per voice, created in the mix area's `start`) holds what all of a voice's grain synths share: the voice filter, the voice-level gain, the dry level, the sends, the mirror and the tap. A grain synth keeps the grains, their rate, the pan and its own fades (ASR, kill, slot and ring fades, `grainSignal`) and writes one bus, its source kind's path (`docs/notes/engine/grains.md`): the voice's synth path for the synth source, its other path for a slot or the input.

`areaState[\mix]` holds the buses: `\delaySend` and `\reverbSend` (stereo audio, every stage writes them), their filtered copies `\delaySendPost` and `\reverbSendPost`, and the effects area's buses under the mix's names: `\dry` (`mainBus`, every stage's `out`), `\dryPost` (`filterBus`), `\delayReturn` (`delayBus`), `\reverbReturn` (`reverbBus`). `mixBus(name)` reads one. Per voice: `\synthPaths` and `\otherPaths` (stereo audio, `voicePathBus(voice, source)`), `\mixGroups` (`voiceMixGroup(voice)`, created in `start` at the tail of `processGroup` in voice order) and `\stageControls` (the Dictionary a stage starts with: its buses, `\mirrorOut`, `\modBus1`-`3` on `silentBus`, the send buses; `\tapOut` is added in `start`, since the record area's allocState runs later; the modulation area's allocState adds `\fastModIn`, the voice's fast-modulation block, `docs/notes/engine/modulation.md`). `\replacing` is the at-most-one-in-flight flag per voice. The area frees its four send buses and the path buses; `stop` frees the stages, then the groups.

The stage, per block (`addVoiceMixDef`):

```
s = guard(In.ar(synthIn, 2))        o = guard(In.ar(otherIn, 2))        guard = zeroBadValues per channel
raw = voiceFilter(s ++ o, ...)      (one set of lagged cutoff, res, type weights and mix; four channels: two banks)
fs, fo = guard(raw's first and last pair)   a CheckBadValues on raw drives SendReply on codes 1 and 2 only
modSig = (In.kr(modBus_i) × modAmpAmt_i).sum.clip(-2, 2)
accent = EnvGen.kr(Env.perc(0.005, 0.4), t_trig, levelScale: velocity)
other = fo × voiceLevel(amp, modSig + ampOffset, accent)        sig = (fs × voiceLevel(1, ampOffset, 0) + other) × voiceGate
voiceOutput(sig, out, dry, delaySend, reverbSend)   Out.ar(mirrorOut, other)        Out.ar(tapOut, sig)
```

- **`voiceFilter(sig, filterType, filterFreq, filterRes, filterMix)`** is `multimodeFilter`, the master filter's design (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`): `RLPF` and `BPF` at rq `1 − res × 0.95`, the highpass as the input less both (never an `RHPF`, whose state bursts when the cutoff jumps up, #305), each type weighted by `BinaryOpUGen('==', type, index).lag(0.02)`, the cutoff lagged 0.05 s, clipped to 20-20000 Hz and then rising at most `cutoffRiseRate` (100 octaves a second, `riseLimitedCutoff`; 3.4's `voiceMixMod`, which rewrites `filterFreq` before the banks, is covered too), the mix lagged 0.05 s; `filterMix` 0 (the default) passes the signal unchanged. On four channels it is two filter banks with one set of lagged controls. Lua's params: `docs/notes/lua/voice-mix.md`.
- **`voiceOutput(sig, out, dry, delaySend, reverbSend)`**: three stereo `Out`s, 3.8's plain sends: `out` (the dry output; the engine sets `mainBus`, the engine check moves it to measure one stage) takes `sig × dry`, the delay send `sig × delaySend` and the reverb send `sig × reverbSend`, each `.clip(0, 1).lag(fxMixLag)`. The send buses are named controls (`delaySendOut`, `reverbSendOut`), set from `stageControls` (`mixBusControls`).
- **`voiceLevel(amp, modSig, accent)`** is `(amp × (1 + modSig) × (1 + accent)).clip(0, 2)`, the one place the level is computed.
- **`voiceMixMod(controls)`** is the modulation area's (`StrataModulation.sc`, `docs/notes/engine/modulation.md`), called before the banks and `voiceLevel`: it declares the stage's `fastModIn`, `seqGate`, `gateSeconds`, `gateRelease` and `voiceMute`, reads the voice's fast-modulation block, and gives the Event the cutoff offset (`filterFreq × 31.62 ^ m3`, clipped to 20-20000, before both banks), `ampOffset` (m4, through `voiceLevel` on both paths) and `voiceGate` (the step gate times the lagged mute, on the whole output). With every channel at 0, `seqGate` 0 and `voiceMute` 0 the two level lines play 3.3's `other = fo × voiceLevel(amp, modSig, accent)` and `sig = fs + other` (the modulation check compares the stage with a copy of 3.3's within 1e-6). The mirror writes `other`, outside the gate factor. A stage measures 40.4 KB of the pool with them (35.4 before 3.4), under the memory area's check, which is 42 KB since 3.4 (40 KB in 3.3): `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`. 3.5's `t_gate` (ruling 54: a trigger control and its sum with `t_trig`, two UGens) takes it from 40.87 to 41.29 KB with the memory area alone on 3.14.1 and on 3.13, one probe step. 3.8's plain sends take it to 39.6 KB on both (the `fxMix` reader and the crossfade's products gone, `dry` added; `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`).
- **Lifecycle**: persistent, like the effects chain and the ring writers, and none of `sendToSynth`'s cases applies (a stage never ends on its own; a replacement is one server step, same key). `setStageParam` (the seven `grainParamHandlers`: `filterType`, `filterFreq`, `filterRes`, `filterMix`, `dry`, `delaySend`, `reverbSend`, and the modulation area's four: `seqGate`, `gateSeconds`, `gateRelease`, `voiceMute`) keeps the value in `grainControls[voice]` and sets the stage. `setVoiceControl` also sets the stage with every control `\strataVoiceMix` has (`amp`, `modBus1`-`3`, `modAmpAmt1`-`3`), whatever the source, while the voice runs (a stopped voice's reach the stage at its next `voiceOn`); only the other path uses them. `voiceOn` sends the stage its reset `voiceControls` (`controlPairs`) in the bundle that starts or re-gates the grain synth, so the level and the first grain land in one block. `retrigger` sends `velocity` and `t_trig` to the voice synth and, while the voice runs, the stage, and `t_trig` alone to the grain synth (one grain in triggered mode), so a release tail is never accented, as before.

Since 3.8 the output is plain sends (3.8 design §7.1): the master's input is `0.7 × (dry·F(x) + l_d·D(s_d·F(x)) + l_r·R(s_r·F(x) + k·l_d·D(...)))`, the levels `l_d` and `l_r` the delay's and the reverb's (their `mix`), `k` the reverb's `delayToReverb` (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`). At the defaults (dry 1, sends 1, the delay at 0, the reverb at 0.43) it is 3.7.0's `0.7·F(x) + 0.3·R(F(x))` within -50 dB (0.301 against 0.3 on the reverb); away from them the balance is the plain law's, which 3.7's crossfade (`(1 − s_d·m_d)(1 − s_r·m_r)` dry, its reverb send through `(1 − s_d·m_d)`, from the `fxMix` bus) was not. The stage measures 1-2 KB less without that bus's readers and products (`docs/notes/engine/memory.md`).

## Is it the same sound?

The `mix` check area compares every voice against references that keep 3.2's tail in the grain synth (3.2's filter, level, sends and mirror, with 3.8's plain output law: the runner's `voiceOutput32` is the plain law with a `dry` argument, 1 in the verbatim 3.2 defs, which had none; so each comparison holds the same law on both sides and stays exact, and the steady states include a buffer voice at dry 0.5), on the server (`docs/design/3.3-design.md` §0c.4-0c.5): a split reference per kind (`\strataCheckGrainRing32Split`, `\strataCheckGrainBuf32Split`, built from the engine's own reader and `grainSignal` and 3.2's tail) and the verbatim 3.2 grain defs (`h.def32`). 3.2's tail holds 3.2's own `multimodeFilter` (`RLPF`, `RHPF` and `BPF`, in the runner's `addDefs32`), so each filter type is compared with 3.2's filter, never with the engine's code (#365): the lowpass and bandpass read 0.0, the highpass about 1.5e-8 (the complement against `RHPF`). Each gets the gate, a forced release and a kill in the same bundle as the engine's grain synth; a check synth writes `Peak.ar` of the largest difference over dry, delay send, reverb send and mirror and the reference's own peak to two control buses, and every comparison also asserts the reference's peak is above -30 dBFS, so silence never passes.

**Exactly 3.2's (within 1e-6):** every steady state (each grain synth at its full level), for the synth, buffer and input sources, every filter type and mix, accents, crossmod and amp moves; and, with the voice filter bypassed, a voice's attack and release and synth -> buffer -> input switches with their crossfades and cut, when no value changes and no accent arrives during them. The two exceptions:

1. A per-synth fade while the voice filter is on (Question A3 of the design): the fade now acts before the filter. The bound in `mix.scd` is the worst figure Step 1b measured at 5 kHz, res 0.5, on 3.13 (`\strataCheckTone` and a loaded noise slot; three runs): -36.6 dB of the reference's peak (a slot fade on noise; -39.4, -36.6 and -38.6 dB in the three runs, -36.2 dB on 3.14.1), plus 6 dB: **-30.6 dB**. It is the slot fade, a 50 ms `Lag` (`hasAudio`) with an instantaneous first slope, that sets it; the design's -50 dB estimate took the linear 50 ms fades. The other 5 kHz figures in that measurement: attack -60 to -63 dB, release about -74 dB, a switch's crossfade about -75 dB, a cut about -53 dB, the input's ring fade about -52 dB; at 200 Hz, res 0.9: attack -29 dB, release -38 dB, crossfade -38 dB, a cut about -16 dB, a slot fade about -6 dB, a ring fade about -12 dB (printed, not bounded, as the check prints them). Exact when the filter is bypassed and once a fade ends.
2. A value change or an accent during a switch's crossfade or cut: the fading buffer or input tail now follows the voice's current filter, sends and level (`mix` prints the difference and asserts it is finite and the reference above -30 dBFS).
