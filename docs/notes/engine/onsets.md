## Must not break

- **No sclang function per sample** (#131, [files.md](files.md)): the chunk loop of `onsetStrengths` and every method of `StrataOnsets.sc` it calls (`hopEnergies`, `onsetChunkStrengths`) hold no `do`, `collect`, `fill`, `inject`, `.sum(`, function literal with arguments, `maxItem` or `minItem`; each hop's energy and strength are C primitives over a chunk (`squared`, `copySeries`, Signal arithmetic, `thresh`, `max`, `min`, `overWrite`). A source scan in `test/unit/structure_test.lua` enforces it (`"the onsets pass runs no sclang function per sample (#131)"`).
- **Every `readData` reads into a fresh array** (`freshReadArray`), and **the chunk loop waits through `yieldToCommands`** after each chunk, never a plain `wait` ([files.md](files.md)). A 300 s pass lets a command in within a chunk's time (`"onsets: a 300 s slot's pass yields to commands"`).
- **Nothing grows**: the kept candidates are in two arrays made once with room for `onsetCandidates` (63); the hops' strengths are one `Signal` made once with room for every hop of the slot; a chunk's arrays are its own.
- **The guard**: every step of a pass (after the server's writes, after each chunk, before the file, after its rename) runs only while `slotChanges[index]` is what it was at the command, so a load, a clear or a recording sent meanwhile wins: the pass stops at its next step, deletes its temporary files and reports failed, and leaves no file (one renamed into place after a change is deleted, but only while the pass is the slot's newest analysis, so it never deletes a newer pass's file, #888). The steps before the file also run only while the pass is the slot's newest (`analyses[index]` is its count), so a newer `analyzeBuffer` of the slot ends it the same way at its next chunk (#872). A slot with no audio (`bufferFrames` 0, a load still under way included) or one that records (`slotRecording`) is refused at once with a failed report.
- **The report comes after the file**: `bufferOnsets` is updated for a pass that wrote its file only from `renameIntoPlace`'s `finish`, once the file is in place, as a load's length follows its peaks file. Every `analyzeBuffer` with a slot in range is reported exactly once, written or failed; an engine that has been freed (`stop`) reports nothing more.
- **File writes** go to `buf<n>.onsets.<instanceTag>-<count>.part` in the settings folder (`allocSettingsDirectory`) and are renamed through `renameIntoPlace` (the waveform area's mover), never a `unixCmd` of their own; the temporary float files are `strata-buf<n>-<instanceTag>-onsets<count>-<L|R>.wav` in `Platform.defaultTempDir`, which `deleteStaleParts`' pattern (`strata-buf*-*.wav`) already sweeps after a crash.
- `bufferOnsetsValue` stays one line of the shape `name { arg ...; ^expr }`: the docs test evaluates it to feed Lua's handler, as it does the other polls'.
- **A strength is the rise in level** (log2 of the ratio over the four hops before, less 1, scaled so the strongest is 1), never the plain ratio, so a hit after silence never flattens the others to `0.000` (`"onsets: a strength is the rise in level: ..."`).
- The candidates of a pass are transient: never params, scene fields or PSET values (design 3.7 §3.3).

## Owns

`lib/engine/StrataOnsets.sc` (`strataAreaOnsets`, order 180): the `analyzeBuffer` command and the `bufferOnsets` poll. Its state, `areaState[\onsets]`: `\analyses` (per slot, the `analyzeBuffer` commands received, counted from 1 as Lua counts them; the count names the report), `\poll`, `\chunks` (the chunks every pass has read since alloc, for the check), `\chunkWait` (nil on norns: the check slows a pass with it); a pass reads the engine's `pastStop` (3.9, #942, `StrataUtil.sc`; before, the area's own `\stopped`, true from its `stop`), so once `free` has begun its stop phase a pass still running reports nothing, and the area has no `stop` hook.

Its literals: `onsetHopFrames` 256 (a power of two, below), `onsetCandidates` 63 (with the marker at 0, at most 64 slices), `onsetGapSeconds` 0.03, `onsetChunkFrames` 16384 (64 hops), `onsetFloor` 1e-6 × the hop's frames, `onsetStrengthCeiling` 1e9 (a NaN or an infinity in the audio never reaches the scaling).

## The pass

`analyzeBuffer(slot)` checks the slot (out of range: one line through the modulation area's `fastModIndicesValid`, nothing counted), counts the analysis and, unless it refuses the slot, has the server write the valid region of both channels to temporary float files (`/b_write`, as a save's first stage, `writeSlot`), then forks `runOnsetPass` on `SystemClock` through `areaCall`. After `context.server.sync` the routine reads the two files in chunks of `onsetChunkFrames`:

1. **Each hop's energy** (`hopEnergies`): the chunk's samples `squared`, then summed in pairs eight times over (`copySeries` of the even and the odd elements, added), so each of the 64 sums is one hop's. Signal's `integral` is one sum of the whole Signal (`_SignalIntegral` returns a Float), not a running sum, so it cannot give the hops' ends; the pairwise sums give the same energies with no running total's rounding. The two channels' energies are added. The last part hop of the slot is left out.
2. **Each hop's strength** (`onsetChunkStrengths`): a hop is a candidate when its energy is above `onsetFloor` (`thresh`) and more than twice the mean of the four hops before it (the four before a chunk are carried from the one before it; before the slot's first hop, its own energy stands in, so it is never a candidate). The mean counts as at least half the floor, so the divide after silence is finite and a hop above the floor there is a candidate. **A strength is the rise in level**: log2 of the ratio (the hop's energy over that mean), less 1 (the 2x threshold), `max` 0, `min` `onsetStrengthCeiling` (Signal's `log2`, a C primitive), and in the file over the strongest's. On that scale a hit after digital silence (a ratio of 1e4 to 1e6, a strength of 12 to 19) and a hit inside a texture (a ratio of 3 to 20, a strength of 0.6 to 3.3) stay apart and in order once the strongest is 1, where a plain ratio printed every hit in the texture as `0.000` beside one after silence. Lua's sensitivity (design §3.1) is a threshold on this scale (decisions 3.7, the onsets pass). The chunk's strengths are written into the slot's strengths Signal (`overWrite`).
3. **The kept** (`pickOnsets`): the strongest hop (Signal's `peak`, its index by `indexOf`), then every hop nearer than the gap (`onsetGapSeconds` at the slot's rate, rounded up to whole hops: 6 at 48 kHz) cleared, and again, at most `onsetCandidates` times: one sclang step per candidate kept, the rest C primitives over the hops. Each is placed at its hop's first frame.
4. **The file** (`onsetsText`, `writeOnsetsFile`): `<settings folder>/buf<n>.onsets`, the frames in ascending order and each strength over the strongest's, with three decimals (`peakText`, so the strongest is `1.000`); both lists end at their key when there is none:

   ```
   strata-onsets 1
   slot 3
   frames 768000
   rate 48000
   onsets 14336 28160 ...
   strengths 0.412 1.000 ...
   ```

   `frames` is the slot's valid length and `rate` its own rate, so Lua can tell a file for other audio. The rename's end reports (`bufferOnsetsValue`: `((count * 2) + failed) * numBuffers + index`).

A second `analyzeBuffer` of the same slot while a pass runs starts a pass of its own and ends the older one at its next step (#872): the older deletes its temporary files and reports failed, so from the older pass's next step on (up to a chunk's time, or its `server.sync`, after the newer command, whose server write starts at once) only the newer pass's pair is there, and Lua acts on the newest report only. An older pass whose rename was already handed to the mover reports as before: written once its file is in place if the slot is unchanged (the newer pass's file replaces it), failed if the slot changed; it then deletes the path only while no newer analysis of the slot has come (#888), since the mover renames in order and the path may by then hold the newer pass's file. One edge stays (#963): a rename that waited past `peaksMoverTimeout` in a stuck mover goes by subprocess, so it can land after a newer pass's rename by subprocess (one the full backlog sent that way); after a change to the slot the path then holds the older pass's file, which describes the audio before the change.

## Times

Measured on a desktop at 3.7, on SuperCollider 3.14.1 and 3.13 alike (the check prints both): a 16 s slot's pass reports in about 0.08 s, a 300 s slot's in 0.6-0.7 s, most of it the server's write of the temporary files (115 MB of float for 300 s); every message sent during the 300 s pass (one each 10 ms) was handled within 0.8 ms. Not measured on a Pi.

## The check

`test/engine_check/onsets.scd` (about 7 s, in the `record,waveform,onsets,delay,shimmer` CI shard) loads float WAVs of single-sample clicks at known frames and levels: eight at 0.25 s steps, each found at its hop's first frame with its strength its rise in level; one hit after silence and four inside a steady tone at known rises (3, 9, 5 and 17 times the mean before), whose strengths stay distinct, non-zero and in order; each hop's energy on noise, different on the two channels, against each hop's own `copyRange(...).squared.integral`; silence and a steady tone (none); 200 clicks 10 ms apart of rising level (no two kept nearer than 30 ms, the strongest kept, against the same greedy order worked out in the check); 100 clicks 40 ms apart, each after silence (the last 63 kept). It reads the report's arrival with an `OSCFunc` on `/poll/value` (the file is there by then), interrupts slowed passes with a clear, a load and a recording (failed, stopped at once, nothing left), ends one with a second `analyzeBuffer` (the first failed and stopped at its next chunk, then only the newer pass's pair of temporary files, #872), holds the mover (`kill -STOP`) while an older pass's rename and a newer one's wait behind a change to the slot, then lets both land while sclang waits (the newer pass's file stays, #888), checks the refusals, a 300 s pass on a high engine of its own with a message every 10 ms, and an index out of range. It starts the `bufferOnsets` poll for its time and stops it again, so the runner's poll check sees the registry as it was.
