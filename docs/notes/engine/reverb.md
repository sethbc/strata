## Must not break

- **Every `Synth(\strataReverbFDN` passes `lineBuf`**, the first of its sixteen line buffers, as every delay passes its line and every reverb its shimmer rings: a bufnum left at its default 0 is buffer 0 (`GET_BUF`), whatever another area keeps there, which the node's sixteen `BufDelay` UGens would write. The engine's node takes the engine's lines (`reverbLineBuf`, in `startReverb`'s one `args` literal); a check's own node takes a set of its own (`newReverbLines`), never the engine's. A source test reads every site (`test/unit/shimmer_test.lua`: a literal `\strataReverbFDN` site, and a site whose def is a variable and whose arguments name `\delayIn`).
- **The lines are sixteen mono buffers on consecutive bufnums**, two a line (`fdnLineFrames`: 2048, 2048, 4096, 4096, 4096, 4096, 8192 and 8192 frames), line `i`'s interpolated read's at `lineBuf + 2i`, its whole-sample read's at `lineBuf + 2i + 1`: each `BufDelay` UGen writes its own input into its own buffer, so no two UGens write one buffer. They are the effects area's, allocated once on the high tier (`allocReverbLines`, from `allocEffectBuses`, after the performance area has set the tier) and freed with it (`freeReverbLines`, from `freeEffects`); their bufnums never change. The standard tier allocates none (`reverbLineBuf` 0: `\strataReverb` has no `lineBuf`, and the server ignores a control a def lacks).
- **Unfrozen, the FDN is 3.8.0's, bit for bit**: at `frozen` 0 every new product is times an exact 1 or plus an exact 0 (`hold` is an exact 0, `1 - hold` an exact 1, the `Gate`s pass their inputs, the held drift's gap is an exact 0), the loop's `OnePole` and the tap's compute the same thing, and `BufDelayL` plays what `DelayL` played. The check compares it with a check-only copy of 3.8.0's def, the size still and moving, at shimmer 0 and 0.5: largest difference 0.
- **The loop's frozen read is the whole-sample one**: the loop crossfades its interpolated read to its `BufDelayN` read by `hold`, through a `OnePole` at `damping × (1 - hold)`, and every line's gain is `.max(hold)`, so a frozen loop is whole-sample reads, no damping, the lossless Householder matrix and a gain of exactly 1. A loop read through linear interpolation darkens at every pass (3.8's cut freeze lost 2.4 to 3.2 dB above 6 kHz in six seconds, #819): no control-rate time ever lands on a whole sample again once it has moved.
- **The taps keep the interpolated read at the live damping**, frozen or not (their own `OnePole`), so the output keeps what the loop drops (the step's cause in 3.8) and `damp` shades a frozen tail without touching its loop.
- **`hold` reaches 0 and 1 exactly** (`Slew.kr(frozen.clip(0, 1), reverbFreezeRate, reverbFreezeRate)`, 20 ms, the delay's ramp): a one-pole lag only approaches 1, and a loop a little under 1 fades.
- **The input closes after the diffusers** (each diffuser chain's output times `1 - hold`), and shimmer's loop, which enters before them, with it: no sound and no shimmer enters a frozen reverb (an accumulating freeze grows without bound).
- **The size and the two drifts are held while frozen**: the size through `Gate.kr(size.clip(0, 1), 1 - (frozen.clip(0, 1) > 0))` before its lag, so a size sent meanwhile waits and the release glides to it; each drift through `fdnHeldDrift`, held at its value at the close and gliding back at no more than `fdnDriftReturnRate` (0.002 s a second) after the release, never jumping.
- **A reverb node is never born frozen, and a reverb is never bypassed and frozen** (as the delay, #889): `setReverbFreeze` keeps 0 with no node, the bypass's free sets the kept `frozen` to 0 with the lines it zeroes (`emptyReverbLines`), and a lift starts unfrozen. A node born frozen would close its input on empty lines and hold a size of 0 (`Gate.kr` starts closed at 0).
- **The freeze is the high tier's alone**: on the standard tier `setReverbFreeze` keeps 0 and sends nothing, and `\strataReverb`'s `frozen` is inert (it is there so both reverb defs keep the same controls: the kept defaults are read from the standard def, and the Lua stub maps the reverb's controls to it).
- **`setReverbFreeze` sets the node in its own body**, as `setDelayFreeze` does: the docs test that checks each FX command's controls against its params' defaults reads command bodies only (#25), so it checks `reverb_freeze`'s 0 against the def's `frozen`.
- No delay line is sized from a control and no `LocalBuf` is used: the lines are buffers of literal sizes, and the def keeps its one audio-rate `LocalIn` (ten channels: eight lines and shimmer's two).

## The lines (`StrataReverb.sc`)

Before 3.9 each line was a `DelayL` with its own memory in the real-time pool (each the next power of two above its longest delay, its size-1 length, the drift's room and a block: 144 KB of the def's 229 KB). 3.9 moves them into buffers, as 3.8's package 0b moved the delay's line: `newReverbLines` takes sixteen consecutive bufnums (`server.nextBufferNumber(16)`) and allocates each buffer at its line's frames, so the def takes one bufnum. They are ordinary RAM, 73728 frames (288 KB, 294912 bytes), which `highMemoryBytes` counts (`docs/notes/engine/performance.md`); the pool gets the 144 KB back, less what the freeze adds to the graph (the def takes 103.6 KB on 3.14.1 and on 3.13, the memory area's twenty-node probe, 229.2 before; `fdnDefLimitKB` 110, `docs/notes/engine/memory.md`). Freeing each `Buffer` frees the block's bufnums (the first frees the allocator's block, the others are no-ops there), as the window bank's `allocConsecutive` buffers are freed.

The def reads each line twice on the same control-rate time (the length less the `LocalIn` block, as before): `BufDelayL.ar(lineBuf + 2i, input, time)` and `BufDelayN.ar(lineBuf + 2i + 1, input, time)`, each fed the line's input. `BufDelayN` reads whole samples whatever the time's fraction, so nothing rounds a time. The frames are 48 kHz literals, sized for norns' fixed 48 kHz rate: at a higher server rate the longest lines would be clipped to their buffers (`BufDelay` reads at most a buffer less a frame), where the old `DelayL`s were sized in seconds.

## The freeze

`\strataReverbFDN` (`in, delayIn, out, mix=0.43, size=0.8, damp=0.5, delayToReverb=1, fadeIn=0, shiftL=0, shiftR=0, shimmer=0, shimmerRatio=2, lineBuf=0, frozen=0`) is the effects area's node (`docs/notes/engine/effects.md`); this file covers 3.9's two additions. The reverb has no engine area of its own: `addReverbCommands` (called from `addEffectCommands`) adds the command.

- `setReverbFreeze(frozen)`, `"i"`: 0 releases, 1 freezes; any other value (2, -1, a float) is ignored with one console line (`fastModIndicesValid`: `strata: setReverbFreeze ignored: frozen 2 is not a whole number from 0 to 1`). Then, on the high tier, it keeps the value with the reverb's other controls and sets the node; with no node (a bypass has freed it) it keeps 0 and sets nothing. On the standard tier it keeps 0 and sends nothing. `startReverb` passes the kept value.
- Frozen, the tail holds: over 60 s its level and its band above 6 kHz moved by 0.01 to 0.15 dB in every case the check measures (below), and the level's step at the freeze, against an unfrozen twin in the same window, was -0.24 to +0.50 dB, shimmer at 0 and at 1 (3.14.1 and 3.13 within 0.02 dB of each other).
- Released with the input stopped, the tail decays as an unfrozen one (size 0.8: -80 dB 3 s after); released with the input running, the level is back at the twin's within 0.01 dB 3 s after.
- `damp` stays live on the output: damp 0 to 1 while frozen lowers the band above 6 kHz by about 4 dB, and back at 0 it is where it was (0.04 dB). The loop keeps its gain of 1 and no damping, so nothing is lost; at the release the loop takes the damp it has then.
- `fdnHeldDrift(drift, open)`: `held = Gate.kr(drift, open)`, `gap = Slew.kr(held - drift, rate, rate)`, the result `Select.kr(open, [held, drift + gap])`. Closed, exactly the value at the close; open, the drift plus a gap that Slew takes to an exact 0 at `fdnDriftReturnRate`, faster than either drift moves (at most about 0.0004 s a second), so it meets the drift; never closed, the gap is an exact 0 and the result exactly the drift.
- The band's own step at the freeze, against the window before it (the design asked the builder to look; above 1.5 dB the rising gain of #819's `fdn-step4.scd` was to be tried): -0.63 to +0.56 dB (-0.61 to +0.56 on 3.13), so the gain stays as the design has it.

**The node's states.** They live in the graph (`hold`; the `Gate`s on the size and the drifts; the held drift's `Slew`), and sclang keeps only the `frozen` it last sent (`keepEffectControls`), so the engine reports none of them. A running node is unfrozen or frozen, with `hold` ramping either way over 20 ms:

## Freeze by bypass

As the delay's (`docs/notes/engine/delay.md`, Freeze by bypass, rows 1 to 6): the reverb is running (unfrozen or frozen), bypassed and fading (`setBypass`'s level at 0, the node still there for `bypassFadeTime`), or freed (no node, the lines zeroed). Each guard is Lua's (`lib/fx_hold.lua`, its next package), the engine's, or both.

| # | the reverb | arrives | what happens | guarded by |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | running, frozen | a bypass | Lua sends `setReverbFreeze(0)` before `setBypass(1, 1)`, so the node fades unfrozen; a bypass sent alone fades the node frozen, and row 4 still holds | Lua |
| 2 | bypassed, fading | a freeze | Lua refuses it; sent alone, the engine sets it on the fading node and keeps it | Lua |
| 3 | bypassed, fading | a lift | the free is canceled: the same node, its tail and its `frozen` play on at the kept level | nothing to guard |
| 4 | bypassed, fading | the free | the node freed, its sixteen lines zeroed and its kept `frozen` set to 0 (`emptyReverbLines`, with the shimmer rings' `emptyShimmerRings`) | the engine |
| 5 | freed | a freeze | Lua refuses it; sent alone, the engine keeps 0 (the command: no node, no tail to hold) | both |
| 6 | freed | a lift | a new node from the kept controls, unfrozen, its lines empty, its input fading in (`startReverb` with `fadeIn` 1) | the engine (rows 4 and 5) |

`"reverb: frozen is kept through a bypass's fade and a lift inside it"` drives row 3, `"reverb: a freed reverb is never frozen"` rows 4 to 6 (the free keeps 0, a freeze sent with no node keeps 0, the lifted node has `frozen` 0), and `"reverb: a bypass's free zeroes the sixteen lines"` row 4's zeroing (the lines filled with 1 in the fade, every sample 0 after the free).

## The engine check (`test/engine_check/reverb.scd`)

The signal checks run nodes of the engine's own `\strataReverbFDN` on buses, lines and rings of their own (`startCheckNode`, the one place the area starts one), each pair's drift reseeded before each node (`\strataCheckReverbSeed`, as `shimmer.scd` does; at its `step` 0, the seed the same every control block, so `LFNoise1` draws the same target at every segment and a seeded drift stops moving after its first, about 2.3 and 3.2 s; at `step` 1 the seed is the seed plus the block's count, the same for a pair's two nodes but new at every segment, so the drifts keep moving) and each input's noise on a generator of its own, driven by `\strataCheckReverbSteps` and `\strataCheckReverbSweep` synths mapped to its controls and measured by `\strataCheckReverbWindows` in up to eight windows of server time (per window: the energy, its band above 6 kHz, the peak, the largest difference from a reference node, a bad value), all created in one bundle and read after the analyses' `/n_end`, so a busy machine changes nothing:
- **the graph**: eight `BufDelayL`, eight `BufDelayN` and no `DelayL` (by class, exactly: `BufDelayL` is a kind of `BufDelayN`);
- **unfrozen is 3.8.0's**: four pairs (size 0.8 still and swept 0.3 to 0.9 by a bus both nodes map, each at shimmer 0 and 0.5) against `\strataCheckReverbFDNBefore`, a check-only copy of 3.8.0's def on its shimmer loop through the engine's own methods, for 6 s: largest difference 0;
- **the bar** (the design's Decided 6), thirteen pairs in one batch of 72 s: twelve with a node frozen at 12 s (the network has reached its level, size 1 included) and an unfrozen twin on the same input and drift, the input stopping at 13 s (so an unfrozen node decays: the twins read -300 dB a minute later). Settings as size, damp, noise, shimmer: (0.5, 0, pink, 0), (0.5, 0.5, pink, 0), (0.8, 0, pink, 0), (0.8, 0.5, pink, 0), (1, 0.5, pink, 0), (0.2, 1, pink, 0), (0.5, 1, white, 0), (0.8, 0.5, white, 0), (1, 1, white, 0), (0.2, 0, white, 0), (0.8, 0.5, pink, 1), (1, 0.5, pink, 1); pink noise high-passed twice at 100 Hz (`PinkNoise` reaches below 1 Hz, and its few cycles a window move a window's energy), shimmer's ratio 2. The twelve's drifts have stopped by the freeze (the seeder's `step` 0), so a thirteenth pair (size 0.8, damp 0.5, pink, shimmer 0; `step` 1, its drifts moving) freezes at 1.5 s, its input stopping at 2.5 s, its windows as the others' moved with its freeze: the bar holds a drift frozen while it moves (step 0.03, level 0.11, band 0.07 dB on 3.14.1). Each case is a check of its own with its three figures (the step in [12.1, 13.0] s against the twin's, the level and the band in [71.1, 72.0] against [12.1, 13.0], all within 1 dB) and the band's own step against [11.1, 12.0]; then the three checks the design names. 26 nodes of 18 buffers each, about 2.7 MB of the pool: they fit the check server's 1024 buffers and 8192 KB beside the standard engine;
- **the behavior**, nine nodes in one batch of 9 s: the input closed, a release with the input stopped and running, a size change, damp, the freeze toggled ten times in 2 s (the rows above);
- **the held drift**: `fdnHeldDrift` on a 0.5 Hz sine of 0.0005 in `\strataCheckReverbDrift`, recorded a control block a frame into a buffer: the sine while open, constant while closed, then its distance to the sine falling by at most `fdnDriftReturnRate` a second until it meets it exactly (0.243 to 0.245 s after), and no step;
- **the commands** on the main (standard) engine and a high engine of the area's own: the engine's own node toggled ten times in 2 s by `setReverbFreeze` with noise in its send (one `\strataReverbFDN` in the engine's context after every command, the same node ID, no bad value in its return); `setReverbFreeze` setting and keeping the high node's `frozen`, 2, -1 and 0.5 ignored with one line each; nothing sent and 0 kept on the standard tier; kept through a bypass's fade and a lift inside it; a freed reverb never frozen; the lines zeroed by the free; the lines sixteen buffers of `fdnLineFrames` on consecutive bufnums the bypass and the lift keep, the node's `lineBuf` the first; a fresh high engine's reverb unfrozen.

Each check failed first: the graph's on 3.8.0's def, the bar's and the behavior's with the freeze's checks run on the check-only copy (no freeze: the level after 60 s read -300 dB), the drift's on a plain `Gate` (no glide), the thirteenth pair's not: with a plain drift in place of `fdnHeldDrift` (the final review's mutation) it read step -0.17, level -0.44 and band -0.28 dB, inside the bar, since a drift moving under the whole-sample frozen loop costs little level in a minute; that mutation fails the behavior's damp case instead (back at 0 by -0.64 dB, against 0.5), the commands' with no `setReverbFreeze`, and the engine node's toggle against a stand-in that started a second reverb node in the middle (node IDs two, two `\strataReverbFDN` nodes). It builds one high engine of its own and runs in about 101 s on 3.14.1 and on 3.13, in the `modulation,freeze,envelopes,tuning,reverb` CI shard, and ends by checking that the server's audio bus allocator holds what it held when the area began, plus the high engine's context.
