## Must not break

- MOD draws and edits a modulation source only through its record (`S.lib.mod_sources`: `name`, `describe()`, `row` with `enc`, `fine_enc`, `press`, `alt_press`, `hints`), never through the module that registered it, so a source registered later (3.5's MPE, 3.6's crow and CC inputs, a test's) shows with no change here. A source without `row` is read-only (E3, K3 and ALT+K3 do nothing and the hint row is empty), unless it has 3.7's `fields`: then K3 opens its settings and the hint is `K3:edit`. The page knows two sections by their sources: the macros (K3 opens the target view, the value right-aligned, ALT+E2 and the button change the bank) and the gestures (the arc button presses a recorder).
- The rows are built from the registry (`rows_from_registry`: `MOD_PAGE.sections` in order, then every other section in the order its first source registered; a section with no row is left out) and kept until what they are built from changes: `build_rows` keys them on one visibility flag per registered source (`visible()`, so the macros' shown bank; the key's length follows a registration), so a frame (`animates`, the draw, the hints, the held hint) builds them once at most and an idle frame not at all, and a source registered later or a bank change from any surface shows at the next use. The cursor follows its row's id (`S.mod_page.row_id`): the row keeps the cursor when rows come and go, and where the id is gone (a bank change) the cursor keeps its index, so a bank change puts it on the same row of the new bank. Always move the cursor through the page (`place`, E2, K2, closing a view), never by setting `S.mod_page.row`.
- Every encoder edit goes through `enc_param_delta(3, id, d)` (center detents; ALT+E3 fine on a control param) or a source's own `enc`/`fine_enc`; a binary param (scatter's toggles, a routing's on/off) through `set_binary_param` (#538). The target view's min and max are not params: they go through `S.lib.macros.set_target`, the routing view's source and destination through `S.lib.matrix.edit_routing` (its 25 % rule for a routing it completes), never `set_routing`.
- Learn (ALT+K3: `S.lib.macros.learn` on a target row, `S.lib.matrix.learn` on the routing view's `dest` row) waits across page changes (ruling 80, #595: no surface on MOD touches a target or a destination, so the touch is made on another page; the record has no `leave`). It ends on the touch, K2 in the view (which closes it), a new learn, the modules' own 10 s and, for a routing, when the routing view moves to another routing (`move_routing`, arc ring 4), so learn never waits for a routing other than the one the view shows. While it waits the header says so on every page: `learn_notice`, a standing notice of `S.lib.ui_flash` (`add_notice`, `docs/notes/lua/ui/header-and-flashes.md`), `learn M<m> row <k>` or `learn R<k> dest` (at most 67 px, inside the flash's 74), under any flash (a refused touch's `not a target` shows over it, and it is back when that ends) and gone the moment learn ends; every way learn ends already asks for a redraw.
- The routing view's source choices are every registered source, visible or not (`S.lib.mod_sources.registered()`, ruling 77): all sixteen macros, whatever bank the list shows, so a stored source always has its place among them (E3's neighbors, ring 2's position). The list itself shows only the visible sources (`list`).
- ALT+K2 goes through the shared handler (`S.lib.ui_alt.k2_up(record)` at the release, #557): `randomize_section()` gives the scatter section's spec (`scatter_depth` a whole percent 0-30, `scatter_rate` even in octaves 0.02-0.5 Hz) and the matrix section's (the depth of every complete routing, a whole percent −50..50, on or off; when none is complete, a spec with no draws while a learn's undo is held there, #927, else nil), a spec with no draws on a random sequence's row and in its settings (`sequence_spec(key)`, one per source key, made once; its section is the key, so a quick press only undoes a held `new` of that sequence, #922), and nil in the macros, morph, gestures, lfos and any other registered section (a macro apply writes up to eight params the undo would not hold). The target view follows the macros, the routing view the matrix. Every param a spec draws calls `note_param_edit` from its action (`lib/scatter.lua`, `lib/matrix.lua`).
- `alt_hold()` clears only what is under the cursor and only when it holds something (`hold_action`): a target row with an id, a routing with a part, a depth other than 0 or off (list or view: `S.lib.matrix.clear`), a source's `alt_press(2)` (a recorder that is not empty; any other source with `alt_press` is taken at its word); elsewhere nothing, and `alt_hold_hint()` then says `cancel`, else `clear`.
- With ALT held the hint names only keys that act (#419). Its K2 part says what ALT+K2 does on the row (ruling 76, `k2_part`; the shared `k2_hint` is unchanged for the other pages): where `randomize_section()` gives a spec, the shared hint (`K2:rand`, `K2:undo` while that section's undo is held; once held 0.5 s, `K2:clear` or `K2:cancel` through `alt_hold_hint`); where there is no spec but the hold would clear something, `K2:clear` from the start (a recorder that holds a loop, a target row with a target, a routing that holds something while none is complete); where ALT+K2 does nothing, no K2 part, held or not (a macro, the bank row, the morph, an LFO, an empty recorder, an empty target row). K1 let go while K2 is down: that K2 part alone. ALT+K3 only on a routing (on/off), a source with `alt_press` (its own `hints(true)`: a playing recorder's `K3:dub`, `K3:dub off` while it overdubs), and in the views (learn, on the routing view's `dest` row only).
- **The settings view knows nothing of its source** (3.7, design §0.4, plan ruling 14): it draws and edits a source's `fields` only through the record (each field's `label`, `value()`, `enc`, `fine_enc`, `press`), read at each draw and each key or turn, so 3.7's envelopes and sequences (and a test's source) need no change here. K3 opens it only on a source with `fields` and no `row.press` (a source's own press keeps K3: an LFO row still goes to the LFO page). Every branch on the view knows `"settings"`: the draw, the encoders, K2 and K3, ALT+K3 (nothing), the hold (nothing to clear), the section randomize (none, but a random sequence's `sequence_spec`, #922), the hints, `animates` (true: a field may show a live value), `section()` (the source's) and the rings (`lib/arc/mod.lua`).
- The rings read and move the page's state only through `S.lib.pages_mod` (`docs/notes/lua/arc-pages.md`); the page sets no arc field. The arc button is the record's `arc_button`.
- Every row stays inside 128×64 at its widest: values are cut to the room left of them and a row's text to the room before its value (`S.lib.ui_text.fit_text`, after `printable`), so a source's long describe, a later release's long name and a stored unknown key or id never cross the value or leave the screen (the layout states).

## Owns

- `lib/pages/mod.lua` (`S.lib.pages_mod`): the MOD record, `S.MOD_PAGE` (the sections, layout, levels, the bound steps, the randomize ranges) and the page state `S.mod_page` (`row`, `row_id`, `view`, `views`, `macro`, `target_row`, `target_column`, `routing`, `routing_row`, and 3.7's `settings`, the settings view's source key, and `settings_row`). Module fields: `place(id)`, `view()`, `section()`, `cursor_routing()`, `routing()`, `macro()`, `target_row()`, `target_bounds()`, `nudge_bound(which, amount)`, `move_target_row(steps)`, `move_routing(steps)` (learn armed for the routing it leaves is canceled), `step_source(steps)`, `step_dest(steps)`, `source_position()`, `dest_position()`, `routing_plays(k)`, `carry_context(with_routing)`, and 3.7's `open_settings(key)` (the settings view of a source with `fields`, on its first row; false, the view unchanged, for a source without fields, one whose `fields` function returns no row, or an unknown key; the page shown is not changed), `settings_row()`, `settings_count()`, `move_settings_row(steps)` and `turn_setting(steps)` (the row's `enc`, its `fine_enc` with ALT).
- The sources, macros, scatter, morph, recorders, LFOs and the matrix are their modules' (`docs/notes/lua/mod-sources.md`, `macros.md`, `scatter.md`, `morph.md`, `gestures.md`, `lfos.md`, `matrix.md`).
- Its unit tests: `test/unit/pages_mod_test.lua`. Its rings: `lib/arc/mod.lua` (`docs/notes/lua/arc-pages.md`).

## The list

Five rows from y 18, 8 px apart (`S.lib.ui_field_list`, the scroll marker at x 127). A section label at x 0, level 2. A row: its label at x 6, its text from x 24 (or 4 px after a label wider than 14 px), its value right-aligned at x 124. Levels: the cursor's row 15, the others 8; a routing that is off or incomplete 4 (10 under the cursor).

- E2 moves over the rows (clamped). K2 jumps to the next section's first row, after the last to the first. ALT+E2 on a macro row shows the next bank (with E2 back, the bank before), the cursor on the same row of it. ALT+E3 is the row's `fine_enc` or a fine `enc_param_delta`.
- ALT+K3: a routing's on/off; a source's `alt_press(3)` (a recorder's overdub). ALT+K2 held 0.5 s: `alt_hold()`.
- A routing row draws its source by name (`S.lib.mod_sources.get(key).name`) and its destination by its LFO destination label (from `S.LFO_DESTINATIONS`), with `~` when the param it resolves to now is one of the five fast ones (`S.lib.lfos.fast_destination`; a voice's main entry follows its source); a key or id this build lacks as stored, cut to the room.
- The morph's rows say when it cannot move (#896): while `S.lib.morph.corners_ready()` is false, a corner row whose corner `S.lib.morph.corner_valid(corner)` refuses (its slot empty, or populated with a scene that fails `validate_scene`) draws the slot's label as its text and `MOD_PAGE.empty_corner` (`[empty]`) as its value, so the mark is never cut, and the morph row draws `MOD_PAGE.morph_needs` with `S.lib.morph.MORPH.min_corners` (`[need 2]`) beside its percent. With enough corners the rows are as before. Both are the morph's pure readers (params and `S.scenes` only): never `plan_ready()`, which rebuilds a stale plan and so would forget what the morph owns and flash again from a draw (fix round 1), and never a test of the page's own.
- A routing row, its depth ring (`routing_plays(k)`) and an L row are dim while `S.lib.matrix.status` of the routing or of the LFO's home (`home_slot(n)`) is neither `summed` nor `owned` (#881, #926): off, at depth 0, empty, incomplete or unknown. `routing_complete(k)` (the randomize's complete routings) is a status other than `empty`, `incomplete` and `unknown`. The page keeps no record of either.
- Hints: `K3:bank E3:bank`; the source's own (`K3:targets E3:value`, `K3:rec E3:quant`, `K3:lfo E3:depth`, `E3:gain`); `E3:scene` (A-D), `E3:morph` (morph and y), `E3:depth`, `E3:rate`; `K3:on|off E3:on/off`; `K3:edit E3:depth`. With ALT: the K2 part (above: `K2:rand` in scatter and the matrix, `K2:clear` on a recorder holding a loop, none on a macro, the morph, an LFO or an empty recorder), ALT+K3's (`K3:on|off`, `K3:dub`, `K3:dub off`), `E2:bank` on a macro row, `E3:fine` on a row that turns finely: `K2:rand E3:fine` on scatter's rate, `E2:bank E3:fine` on a macro, `K2:clear K3:dub` on a playing recorder, `K2:clear K3:dub off` on one that overdubs, an empty hint row on the bank row. K1 let go while ALT+K2 is down: the K2 part alone.

## The target view

K3 on a macro row. y 16: `M<m> targets` (15) and the macro's value right-aligned (8). Rows 1-8, five in view from y 24, 7 px apart: the number at x 0, the target's label from x 8 (`-` for an empty row; an id this build lacks as stored, at 4; `learn` while learn waits for the row), and `min>max%` right-aligned at x 124 (min and max in whole percents, a tenth shown where they have one; `90>20%` inverted). On the cursor's row the column under the cursor is at 15 and the rest at 8; the other rows at 4.
- E2 the row; K3 the next column (target, min, max, wrapping); E3 the column: the target steps through `S.lib.macros.target_ids()` (none first; ALT+E3 jumps a group: the LFO destinations' groups, then the LFOs' rate and depth, then the routings' depths), min and max 1 % a detent, ALT+E3 0.1 % (`MOD_PAGE.bound_step`, `bound_fine_step`), clamped to 0..1. An empty row takes no bound.
- K2 back (the list cursor on the macro's row). ALT+K3 learn. ALT+K2 held empties the row.
- Hints: `K2:back K3:col E3:target|min|max`; ALT `K3:learn E3:group|fine`, after `K2:clear` on a row with a target.

## The routing view

K3 on a routing row. y 16: `R<k> routing` and `on`/`off` right-aligned. Rows from y 24, 7 px apart: `source` (the name), `dest` (the label, `~` on a fast destination; `learn` while learn waits), `depth` (`+40%`), `on`; the cursor's row at 15, the others at 4.
- E2 the row; E3: the source through `S.lib.mod_sources.registered()` in registration order, hidden sources too (none first; ALT+E3 jumps a section), the destination through `S.lib.matrix.destination_ids()` (ALT+E3 jumps a group), the depth (ALT+E3 fine), on/off. A source or destination not in the list (a key or id this build lacks) steps from none. K3 on/off; K2 back (the list cursor on the routing's row). ALT+K3 on `dest`: learn. ALT+K2 held clears the routing.
- Hints: `K2:back K3:off|on E3:source|dest|depth|on`; ALT the K2 part (`K2:rand` while a routing is complete, else `K2:clear` while this one holds something) with `K3:learn E3:group` on `dest`, `E3:section` on `source`, `E3:fine` on `depth`.

## The settings view

3.7 (design §0.4). K3 on a source row whose record has `fields` and no `row.press`, or `S.lib.pages_mod.open_settings(key)`: `S.mod_page.view = "settings"`, `settings = key`, `settings_row = 1`. y 16: the source's name (15). The fields, five in view from y 24, 7 px apart (`S.lib.ui_field_list.draw`, the scroll marker at x 127): the `label` at x 0 and `value()` right-aligned at x 124, cut to the room left of the label; the cursor's row at 15, the others at 4. `fields` is the list, or what its function returns at each read (a field added or taken away shows at the next draw; the cursor is kept on a row).
- E2 the row (clamped); E3 the row's `enc(d)`, ALT+E3 its `fine_enc(d)` (its `enc` when it has none); K3 its `press()` (nothing without one); K2 back, the list cursor on the source's row (`source:<key>`). ALT+K2 and ALT+K3 do nothing (no randomize, nothing to clear or learn); the arc button toggles scatter, as in the target view.
- On the list, a source whose K3 opens its settings (`fields` that give a row, and no `row.press`) shows `K3:edit` before its row's own hint (`K3:edit E3:depth`), so the hint names the key. The own hint gives up its last whole parts until both fit the hint row (`MOD_PAGE.hint_width`, 128 px; a single part too long is cut with `fit_text`), so a later source's long hint never loses `K3:edit`; a test checks every source the script registers fits beside it uncut.
- A `fields` function that returns no row gives no settings: `open_settings` returns false and changes nothing, K3 on the row does nothing and its hint has no `K3:edit`, until the function returns a row.
- A field's label is drawn and named in the hint cut to `MOD_PAGE.field_label_max` (8) characters after `printable`: a `fields` function's list is not checked at registration, so a longer label never pushes the row or the hint off the screen.
- Hints: `K2:back E2:row E3:edit`; on a row with a `press`, `K2:back K3:<label> E3:edit` (a build ruling of 3.7: E2:row gives way, as the targets and routing views name no E2 either: with an 8-character label of the widest letter the four parts pass the hint row's 128 px, and this is 126). `E3:edit` only on a row with an `enc` (a random sequence's `new` row has a press alone: `K2:back K3:new`; the final review's Lua B, Important 1). With ALT: `E3:fine` on a row with `fine_enc`, else nothing; so a row that turns finely under ALT gives a `fine_enc` (the envelopes' control rows give their `enc`, which is `enc_param_delta` and reads ALT itself).
- Layout states draw EN7 at `999ms 999ms` and at `~999ms 49ms` (routed to a Lua destination), RS4 clocked by voice 7 at `16/16`, EN7's settings and RS4's on its `new` row (the final review's Lua B, Minor 6).
- The rings: ring 1 the row, ring 2 the value (the row's `enc`, `fine_enc` with ALT), both 20 ticks a step, ring 2's carry kept per row; rings 2 to 4 are dark (a field has no range to show), 3 and 4 do nothing (`docs/notes/lua/arc-pages.md`).

## Redraws

Every edit asks for a redraw (param actions, `enc`, `key`, the rings; the macros' `set_bank`, the matrix's and the recorders' own changes). `animates` is true while ALT+K2 is down (its hint turns at the hold time), while a recorder records (its row counts seconds), while a row of a section MOD does not list in `MOD_PAGE.sections` is in view (a live value: the input level) and in the settings view (3.7: a field's value may be live).

Every section's first and last row (the bank row; M16 of bank 4 with every macro's eight targets at the widest label and 100 %; morph A with a twelve-letter scene name; the morph at 100 %; the morph at 100 % with one corner holding a scene and three empty corners on twelve-letter names (#896); L2 on a 32-character home this build lacks (#881); morph C and y at 100 % with all four corners on twelve-letter scene names (3a); scatter on; scatter's rate with its depth and rate at the top (`100%`, `1.00 Hz`); scatter amp; G1 and G4 with the four recorders empty, playing, stopped and recording; L1 and L6 with six LFOs engine-run and modulated; R1 with an empty, an off, an incomplete and an unknown routing; R1 and R16 with all sixteen routings at their widest; IN), a section registered by a test, ALT+K2 held on scatter's depth (`K2:cancel E3:fine`, the state the #557 layout test draws) on a playing recorder (`K2:clear K3:dub`) and on an overdubbing recorder with a 10-minute loop (`play 600.0 s dub`, `K2:clear K3:dub off`, #879), the target view of M16 at rows 1 and 8 with each column, M1's target view learning, M16's learning row 8 and R16's learning its destination (the header's widest learn notices), the routing view of R16 at its widest with each row, and a routing with unknown parts; 3.7's later source with settings on its row in the list (its `K3:edit` hint), and its settings view with six rows at their widest (an 8-letter label of the widest letter, a 30-letter value, a press: the widest hint), each row selected.
