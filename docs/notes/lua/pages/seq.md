## Must not break

- The page file sets every record field but the arc fields; SEQ's rings and the step editor's are `lib/arc/seq.lua`'s (`docs/notes/lua/arc-pages.md`), which sets the editor's on its modal owner (`S.lib.pages_seq.step_editor_owner`). Avoid the arc field names in this file, even on the owner: a test reads every `arc_delta =` and `arc_redraw =` in a page file.
- Pattern edits go through the sequencer's functions (`toggle_pattern_step`, `clear_pattern`, `randomize_pattern` through the randomize, `S.lib.sequencer.euclid`, `set_step_attr`) or write `S.patterns[voice][step]` within bounds (voice 1..`NUM_VOICES`, step 1..`PATTERN_LENGTH`, level 0..`MAX_STEP_LEVEL`), and call `grid_redraw()`, so the grid follows. They request no screen redraw of their own: `enc` and `key` (strata.lua) request one after every handler, as `arc_delta` does after a ring. A level edit calls `S.lib.mutation.note_pattern_edit(voice)` (E3 here, the arc's ring 2, the grid's level gesture, `toggle_pattern_step`, `replace_pattern` and `set_step_attr` do), which ends a pattern undo of that voice (#557).
- An empty step (0) is drawn as an outline, never as a filled cell at level 0 or 1, so a level-1 step never reads as empty. A step past its voice's length is a 1×1 dot, set or not.
- Each row's playing column is `S.lib.sequencer.sounding_step(voice)`, the step its voice sounded last (nil while stopped and before its first step), the one every grid layout lights on that voice's row (`step_brightness`); never `S.pattern_position` (#113).
- While the patterns play, the frame clock redraws SEQ's screen every frame (screen only, never the arc; the rule is in `docs/notes/lua/ui/redraws.md`), so the columns move. `draw()` requests no redraw. When they stop or another page shows, nothing extra draws. While the norns menu is open the frame still calls the global `redraw` (norns.none then), so the page itself never draws and the arc gets no extra frames (tested).
- ALT+K2/ALT+K3 are the record's `alt_key` (reached through `alt_action`), never handled in `key`. ALT+K2 acts on release (`alt_key_release`, `S.lib.ui_alt.k2_up`): the press only calls `k2_down`. A quick press randomizes the selected voice's pattern (levels only) or undoes it, in both views; the record's `alt_hold` opens the edit view from the pattern view and resets the cursor's row in the edit view, and its `alt_hold_hint` names which (`K2:edit`, `K2:reset`), or `K2:cancel` on a row with nothing to reset (`euclid`, `clear`: `row_resets`, #596), where letting go only cancels the quick press. The step editor's held hint follows the same rule. ALT+E3 changes `tempo` through `enc_param_delta` in the pattern view (a number param: whole BPM).
- Every edit of a step's attributes, from the edit view's E3 and the step editor's rings alike, goes through `S.lib.pages_seq.turn_step(voice, step, key, d, opts)`, so the two agree on `off`, a start from off and the range's ends; a lock never touches its stored param (`set_step_attr` only).
- The step editor is open exactly while its owner is the modal one (the `step editor` invariant): it opens only through `open_step_editor` and closes only through `close_step_editor` (K2 in the editor calls it with the editor's port). It never changes `S.selected_voice`, `S.cursors.seq_step` or the page underneath. The four functions the grids (task 2c) call keep their names and shapes (The step editor, below).
- Every pattern replacement an action of the step editor makes (its `euclid` row, its `clear` row, ALT+K3, ALT+K2's randomize and undo) runs through `run_replacement`, which sets `step_editor_replacing()` true around it and back to false even when it raises; no other code sets it (ruling 71).
- The step editor's owner has `animates()` (#592): the frame clock draws every frame while its ALT+K2 is held or the patterns play, over any page, so its `K2:reset` hint and its strip's playhead never wait for an unrelated redraw. Its press is forgotten each time the norns menu closes (`forget_editor_k2_press`, a `S.register_menu_close` hook), since the menu takes any release made while it is open: a press left set would redraw at 15 fps and hold the hint at `K2:reset` until the editor closed.
- The arc button (`arc_button`) plays/stops, like K2 in the pattern view (design §7 lists SEQ play/stop for the button). The step editor has no button.

## Owns

- `lib/pages/seq.lua` (`S.lib.pages_seq`): the SEQ record (`S.registry.pages[UI_PAGES.index.SEQ]`) with `draw`, `hints`, `enc`, `key`, `alt_enc`, `alt_key`, `alt_key_release`, `randomize_section`, `alt_hold`, `alt_hold_hint`, `header_status`, `animates`, `arc_button` and `layout_states`; its layout constants are the local `SEQ_PAGE` (the pattern view) and `SEQ_EDIT` (the edit view) tables, its rows `EDIT_ROWS`.
- The module's fields: `open_step_editor(voice, step, port)`, `close_step_editor(port)`, `step_editor()`, `step_editor_replacing()` (the grids'), `step_editor_owner` (the modal owner, whose ring fields `lib/arc/seq.lua` sets), and for the rings `turn_step(voice, step, key, d, opts)`, `lock_param_id(voice, key)` and `has_bookmarks(voice)`; `forget_editor_k2_press()`, its menu-close hook.
- The step editor owner's `animates` (the frame clock reads a pushed owner's, `docs/notes/lua/ui/redraws.md`, #592). The `step editor` invariant. Its unit tests: `test/unit/pages_seq_test.lua`; the rings' in `test/unit/arc_pages_test.lua`. Its step cursor is `S.cursors.seq_step` (`lib/ui/cursors.lua`); the row is `S.selected_voice`. The edit view's row cursors (SEQ's and the step editor's) are local: each an anchor, the row key it was put on and its index, so a row that comes or goes (bookmark) never moves it to another row.

## The pattern view

Drawing (`redraw_pattern_view`, design §5, 8.6):
- One row per voice, rows 6 px apart from y 13 (the row's top). The voice number at x 0, baseline 5 px below the top, level 15 for the selected voice and 4 for the others; `+` (running) or `-` (stopped) right-aligned at x 127, level 4.
- Sixteen cells of 4×4 px, 6 px apart from x 10 (so x 10-103). A step at level L (1-15) is a filled rect at level L; an empty step a 4×4 outline at level 1. A step past the voice's length (`v<i>_seq_length`) is a 1×1 dot at level 1 (`SEQ_PAGE.dot_level`), 1 px into its cell.
- The playing column, per row (the voice's sounding step, while the patterns play): outlines at level 4, filled cells at `min(15, L + 4)`.
- A gated voice (`v<i>_seq_gate` on) shows `G` right-aligned at x 121, level 4, between its cells and its `+` or `-`. A letter with no descender, so two gated rows 6 px apart never touch.
- The cursor: a 6×6 outline at level 15 around the selected voice's step (`S.cursors.seq_step`, clamped to 1..`PATTERN_LENGTH`).
- `header_status()` returns `"<tempo> bpm"`; `draw_header` draws it at x 79 with the play mark at x 73 while the patterns play (`HEADER.status_x`, `HEADER.status_play_x`; a test in `test/unit/ui_layout_test.lua` compares the two).
- The renders stroke the cells' outlines on whole-pixel rects, as the other pages' lines do; on the OLED cairo centers a 1 px stroke on the path, which the 3.1 hardware check should look at.

Keys:
- E2: `S.lib.ui_cursors.move("seq_step", d)`. E3: the step's level, clamped to 0..`MAX_STEP_LEVEL`.
- K2: `pattern_toggle()`. K3: `toggle_pattern_step(S.selected_voice, step)` (on at 15, or off).
- ALT+E2: `S.lib.ui_keys.select_voice_by(d)` (MAIN's list and the MIDI bend follow). ALT+E3: `enc_param_delta(3, "tempo", d)`.
- ALT+K2, on release: a quick press undoes the randomize held for this voice's pattern (within 5 s) or randomizes it, through `S.lib.mutation.randomize` with the spec `randomize_section` returns (`{page = SEQ, section = "pattern", voice, pattern = voice}`; never locked: lock covers params). The undo restores the old steps through `replace_pattern`, also while the pattern clock plays. Held `ALT_HOLD_SECONDS`, its release opens the edit view (`alt_hold`, Question 12). The record animates while ALT+K2 is held (the hint turns to `K2:edit` at 0.5 s). ALT+K3: `clear_pattern(S.selected_voice)`, which ends the undo.
- Hints: `K2:play K3:step E3:level` (`K2:stop` while playing); ALT: `K2:rand K3:clr E3:bpm` (`K2:undo` while an undo is held, `K2:edit` after 0.5 s held). With K1 let go while ALT+K2 is down, `k2_hint(record)` alone, since the release still acts.

## The edit view

A field list (`S.lib.ui_field_list`) of the selected voice's step at `S.cursors.seq_step` (design 8.6, 8.12, 8.13), drawn by `draw_edit_view`:
- y 16: `v<voice> step <n>` at x 0 (level 10), and the voice's sixteen steps as 3×3 px cells 4 px apart from x 60 (tops at y 13): set steps filled at their level, empty ones outlined, past the length dots, the playing one brighter, the step outlined (5×5) at 15.
- The rows as VOICE's: 5 in view, 7 px apart from y 24; section labels at x 0 (level 2), fields at x 3 (15 under the cursor, 4 otherwise), values right-aligned at x 124; `field_list.draw`'s scroll marker at x 127.
- Section `voice`: `length` (`v<i>_seq_length`, `16`), `division` (`1/16`), `swing` (`58%`), `gate` (`off`, `on`), `gate len` (`0.50 st`), `gate rel` (`0.10 s`), `euclid` (`5/12 +2`: the last fill's hits, the voice's length and the rotation, `S.seq_euclid[voice]`; `-` before the first), `clear` (no value).
- Section `step <n>`: `level` (`9`), `prob` (`80%`; `100%` when not set), `ratchet` (`x2`; `x1` when not set), `gate` (`2.00 st`, `off`), `note` (`+3`, `-2`, `0`, `off`: `notes[1]`), `pos` (`0.42`, `off`), `pitch` (`1.50x`, `off`), `size` (`0.12 s`, `off`), `bookmark` (`2`, `off`), shown only while `has_bookmarks(voice)` (any `S.lib.bookmarks.is_set(voice, n)`: a buffer voice whose slot holds audio).
- Section `chain` (global): `chain` (`seq_chain`, `off`/`on`), `from`, `scenes`, `bars` (`seq_chain_from`, `seq_chain_scenes`, `seq_chain_bars`) and `quantize` (`quantize_actions`).

Keys:
- E2: the row (`field_list.move`; the anchor follows). E3: the row's value: a param through `enc_param_delta(3, id, d)` (a binary one, `chain`, on with a turn up and off with a turn down through `set_binary_param`), a step attribute or the level through `turn_step`, `euclid`'s hits.
- ALT+E2: the step (`S.lib.ui_cursors.move("seq_step", d)`; `seq_alt_enc`). ALT+E3: fine on a control row (`enc_param_delta` and `turn_step` read ALT: a tenth of the quantum), `euclid`'s rotation; a whole-number row steps as E3 does.
- K2: back to the pattern view (its cursor on the step edited). K3: `toggle_pattern_step(voice, step)`; on the `clear` row `clear_pattern(voice)`.
- ALT+K2: as the pattern view's on a quick press (the selected voice's pattern, with its undo); held, `reset_row`: a param to its default (`param_default`; `chain` through `set_binary_param`), a step attribute off (nil: `prob` back to 100 %, `ratchet` to one hit, a lock, the step's gate, the notes list and the bookmark off), the level to 0; `euclid` and `clear` keep everything. ALT+K3: `clear_pattern(voice)`.
- Hints: `K2:back K3:step E3:<label>` (on the `clear` row `K2:back K3:clr`); ALT: `K2:rand K3:clr E2:step` (`K2:undo`, `K2:reset` once K2 is held 0.5 s, `K2:cancel` instead on `euclid` and `clear`, which have nothing to reset).

`turn_step(voice, step, key, d, opts)` (the edit view's E3 and the step editor's rings): `level` clamped to 0..`MAX_STEP_LEVEL`; `prob` from 100 (nil) by whole percents, 0-100, 100 stored as nil; `ratchet` from 1 (nil), 1..`SEQ.max_ratchet`, 1 stored as nil; `notes` moves `notes[1]` and keeps further entries; `bookmark` (only while `has_bookmarks`); `pos`, `pitch`, `size` and a step's `gate` are continuous locks on a param's controlspec (`lock_param_id`: `v<i>_buf_pos`, `v<i>_grain_pitch`, `v<i>_grain_size` from the sequencer's `SEQ.lock_params`, and `v<i>_seq_gate_length`), moved `d` × the spec's quantum (a tenth with `opts.fine`) or, from the arc, `opts.raw` of the range. From `off` a lock starts at its param's stored value and a note or bookmark at 0, and the turn steps from there (one detent up from a stored 0.30 is 0.31); a turn below the range's start lands on it, and one from the start goes `off` (a note below -14, a bookmark below 1, a lock below its param's minimum); nothing passes the range's end. A lock keeps its raw place per voice, step and key (`lock_raws`), as a control param keeps its raw value, so ticks finer than a stepped spec's step (size's 0.01) add up. Returns whether the step changed.

## The step editor

The edit view for any voice and step, pushed as a modal owner (`S.lib.ui_modal`, design 8.7): the grids open it on a held step key and close it on the release (task 2c). The four functions the grids call:
- `open_step_editor(voice, step, port)`: a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES` or a step outside 1..`PATTERN_LENGTH` opens nothing and returns false; otherwise any open editor is popped (a second open, from any port, replaces the first), the editor's state is `{voice, step, port}`, every ring's carried ticks are forgotten (`forget_arc_ticks()`, as at a page change), the owner is pushed, and it returns true.
- `close_step_editor(port)`: closes the editor only when `port` opened it (an editor another port has opened since stays), forgets the carried ticks and pops the owner, so the page shows again.
- `step_editor()`: `{voice, step, port}` (a copy) or nil.
- `step_editor_replacing()` (ruling 71): true exactly while an action of the open editor is running a pattern replacement (`run_replacement`, around its `euclid` and `clear` rows, ALT+K3, and ALT+K2's randomize and undo: each goes through `S.lib.sequencer.replace_pattern`, which calls the grids' `end_step_gestures`), false at every other time and with no editor open. The rule it serves: a replacement made from inside the step editor keeps the editor open (its held key stays the editor's, and the key's release closes it and toggles nothing); a replacement from anywhere else (a recall, a chain link, a randomize or clear from another surface) closes it (a held key whose pattern changes under it from elsewhere must never stay held). The grids read it in `end_step_gestures`.
- While it is pushed, strata.lua and `lib/arc/core.lua` send it E2, E3, K2, K3 and the arc (E1 does nothing; K1 still sets ALT), and `redraw` draws it under the page's header. It draws and edits as the edit view, on its own voice and step: E2 the row (its own cursor, which opens on `level` the first time), E3 the value, ALT+E2 its step (1..`PATTERN_LENGTH`), ALT+E3 fine or the rotation; K2 closes it (`close_step_editor(editor.port)`); K3 the step on/off (the `clear` row clears); ALT+K3 clears its voice's pattern; ALT+K2 goes through `S.lib.ui_alt.k2_down` and `k2_up` with the editor's own record (`editor_alt`: `randomize_section` the editor voice's pattern spec, `alt_hold` the row's reset).
- Its hints are the edit view's. The `K2:reset` (or `K2:cancel`) hint comes from the editor's own press time (`ui_alt.k2_held` reads the page's press marks, which a modal key never sets). Its owner's `animates()` (`editor_animates`) is true while that press is held or the patterns play, and the frame clock reads it over any page (#592), so the hint turns at the hold time and the strip's playhead moves. The press is forgotten as the norns menu closes (`forget_editor_k2_press`), which may have taken its release. SEQ's own view needs no such hook: its hint and `animates` read `S.lib.ui_alt.k2_held()`, whose press marks (`S.key_press_page`, `S.key_press_alt`) `forget_held_keys` clears as the menu opens, and its `k2_up` runs only from a release `key()` routes after a new press.

`layout_states`: the cursor at steps 1 and 16 on voices 1 and 7, every row with levels while playing (`levels-playing`), 300 BPM while playing (`tempo-300`), short voices at three divisions with three gated rows while playing (`short-gated-playing`), the pattern view's ALT+K2 held (`K2:edit K3:clr E3:bpm`), the edit view's ALT+K2 held past the hold time (`ALT+K2 held past the hold time`: `K2:reset K3:clr E2:step`), the step editor on voice 7's step 16 over SEQ, and the edit view with every row at its widest value (`widest_values`) on voices 1 and 7 at step 16, with and without bookmarks, five windows each, so every row is drawn.
