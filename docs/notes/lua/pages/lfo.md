## Must not break

- The page has no clock of its own: its record's `animates` (`any_lfo_running`) is true while any LFO runs, so `draw_frame` marks the screen dirty on every frame then (15 fps) and the dots move; with all three off an idle frame draws nothing (#423). Everything else it shows asks for a redraw when it changes (every param action through `redraw_on_param_changes`, `enc`, `key`, the rings), turning the last LFO off included. `cleanup` stops the frame clock with `stop_frame_clock()`.
- The dot's height follows the depth: it swings around the row's middle line by `(raw - 0.5) × lfo_depth_<n> / 100` of the wave's height, so at depth 0 (which moves nothing; the library sends no step then) it sits on the line, whatever the last raw value (#520).
- Changing `S.selected_lfo` (K2/K3) calls `forget_arc_ticks("lfo:4")`, so ring 4's carried ticks never step another LFO's clocked rate. Only a real change forgets them (K3 on LFO 3 forgets nothing); the depth rings' keys (`lfo:1`-`lfo:3`) are never forgotten here.
- Every E3 edit goes through `enc_param_delta(3, id, d)` (center detents, ALT+E3 fine on a control param), except the on/off field and ALT+E3 on the destination.
- Turning an LFO on or off from the page (ALT+K3, E3 on the on/off field, ALT+K2 on it, the arc button) goes through `set_lfo_running`, a plain `params:set("lfo_<n>", ...)`. The offset survives because `lib/lfos.lua`'s wrapper of the state action restores it (#103), not because of anything on the page.
- `lfo_dest_group_starts()` is derived from `S.LFO_DESTINATIONS`, never hardcoded: none, the first global, each `v<i> main` (found by its label), then each registered destination's `v1 <label>` after `S.lib.lfos.BUILT_IN_DESTINATIONS` (found by its label), so each registration's seven voices are a group (#511). Groups only add jump targets: a registered destination never moves an index or a voice group's start.
- The strip measures with `screen.text_extents`, so it fits whatever the real font draws; the layout tests use the stub's widths (`stubs.text_width`, norns' font advances), never less than the ink width matron's `text_extents` returns, so the device shows at least as many fields as the renders.
- Rows and strip stay between y 10 and 56 (above the hint row's glyphs) for every selected LFO: `LFO_PAGE.row_first_y` 11, `row_spacing` 13, `strip_height` 8. The rows test in `test/unit/pages_lfo_test.lua` checks it.
- Rows are 13 px apart so the right-hand column keeps a blank pixel row between its strings: a destination (baseline top + 4) sits 6 px under the row above's `on|off` text, and 7 px over its own, which its descender clears (#422). At 12 px no destination position gives both: #422's top + 4 touched the row above, top + 5 put a descender on the line below. A test checks the 6 px for every selected LFO. A descender may still touch the line under it (the `y` of `density` over `on 25%`; a strip value with one, `up` or `dly fdbk`, over the hint row when LFO 3 is selected), as `assert_screen_layout`'s 7 px rule allows: the page has no room for 14 px rows.

## Owns

- `lib/pages/lfo.lua` (`S.lib.pages_lfo`): the LFO record, `redraw_lfo`, `lfo_shape_value`, `lfo_dest_group_starts`, `lfo_dest_group_jump` (global); `lfo_running`, `row_top`, `strip_y`, `layout` (`LFO_PAGE`) as module fields; `LFO_FIELDS`, `set_lfo_running`, `select_lfo_by`, `strip_view`, `any_lfo_running` (local). The strip cursor `S.lfo_field`.
- The LFOs themselves (`S.lfos`, `S.selected_lfo`, `S.LFO_DESTINATIONS`, `S.lfo_last_raw`, `lfo_phase_estimate`) are `lib/lfos.lua`'s (`docs/notes/lua/lfos.md`), as is the 25 % default depth.
- Its unit tests: `test/unit/pages_lfo_test.lua`. Its rings: `lib/arc/lfo.lua` (`docs/notes/lua/arc-pages.md`).

## The page

Rows (`draw_lfo_row`):
- One row per LFO. `row_top(n)` = `LFO_PAGE.row_first_y` (11) + (n - 1) × `row_spacing` (13), plus `strip_height` (8) for the rows under the selected LFO.
- `L<n>` at x 0 (baseline top + 7; level 15 for `S.selected_lfo`, 4 otherwise).
- One cycle of the shape as a 64-px polyline from x `wave_x` (12) to 75: value 1 at the row top and 0 at top + `wave_height` (10), level 6 running / 2 off, from `lfo_shape_value(shape, phase)` (a mirror of the library's shape math; random is drawn as two held steps).
- While the LFO runs and has sent a value, a 3×3 dot at x = 12 + `lfo_phase_estimate(n)` × 63, y = bottom - (0.5 + (`S.lfo_last_raw[n]` - 0.5) × depth / 100) × 10 (the middle line at depth 0).
- Right-aligned at x 127: the destination label (baseline top + 4, so a descender, the `y` of `density`, clears the line below, #422; with 13 px rows it is 6 px under the row above's `on|off`) and `on|off <depth>%` (top + 11).

The strip (`draw_strip`), at baseline `strip_y()` = `row_top(S.selected_lfo)` + `strip_baseline` (18):
- `LFO_FIELDS` in order: `dest` (`strata_lfo<n>_dest`, its label), `shape` (`lfo_shape_<n>`), `mode` (`lfo_mode_<n>`), `rate` (`lfo_free_<n>` as `%.1fs`, or `lfo_clocked_<n>`'s option string in clocked mode: the field's param follows the mode), `depth` (`lfo_depth_<n>`, `%.0f%%`), `offset` (`lfo_offset_<n>`, signed `+20%`), `on/off` (`lfo_<n>`). Each has a label (the hint), `param(n)` and `value(n)`.
- `strip_view(widths, cursor, marker_width)`: the smallest first field such that the fields from it to the cursor fit, packing `strip_gap` (6) apart from x 0 (or after a `<` and a gap when first > 1), each field before the last needing room for a gap and `>` before `strip_right` (127). The cursor's field at level 15, the others and the markers at 4; `>` right-aligned at 127.
- Offset: norns' LFO library (`lua/lib/lfo.lua` in norns) adds `(max - min) × offset` to `scaled`, and `apply_lfo` sends `scaled` × half the destination's range, so a 20 % offset moves the destination by 20 % of its range.

Input:
- E2 moves `S.lfo_field` (1-7, clamped). E3: `enc_param_delta(3, field.param(S.selected_lfo), d)`; the on/off field turns the LFO on for d > 0, off for d < 0.
- `alt_enc`: on the destination, ALT+E3 applies `lfo_dest_group_jump` once per detent and returns true; on any other field it returns false, so `enc` runs with ALT held (fine on the free period, whole steps on number and option params).
- `lfo_dest_group_jump(index, d)`: the first start above `index` (d > 0) or the last start below it (d < 0); `index` itself when there is none. The built-in starts are 1, 2, 13, 21, ..., 61; 3.2's four registered destinations add 69 (`v1 hist`), 76 (`v1 vfilt`), 83 (`v1 dsend`) and 90 (`v1 rsend`), so from v7 main (61) the last voice's reverb send (96) is four jumps (69, 76, 83, 90) and six detents.
- K2/K3: `select_lfo_by(-1|1)`, clamped to 1..`NUM_LFOS`.
- `alt_key`: ALT+K2 calls `S.lib.ui_alt.k2_down` and acts on release (`alt_key_release` calls `k2_up(record)`, #557); ALT+K3 toggles the selected LFO. A quick release runs `randomize_section`: the selected LFO's `lfo_shape_<n>` (uniform option) and its rate by mode, `lfo_free_<n>` 0.5-30 s even in octaves or `lfo_clocked_<n>` a uniform index 3-16 (`1/4` to `16` of norns' `lfo_rates_as_strings`); destination, mode, depth, offset and on/off are kept, so a running LFO keeps running (the Review Focus test). The spec's section names the LFO and its mode (`"lfo " .. n .. " " .. mode`, `lfo 2 free`) and its voice is nil: each LFO holds its own undo, a mode switch drops the undo of the rate no longer shown (as VOICE's `synth (buffer)` section), and no voice's mutation ends it. `alt_hold` (a press held `ALT_HOLD_SECONDS`) sets the field's param to `param_default(id)` (depth 25, destination none, free period 4 s, offset 0, on/off off). `animates` is also true while ALT+K2 is down, for the `K2:reset` hint. `arc_button` is the same toggle.
- Hints: `K2/K3:lfo E3:<label>`; with ALT `K2:rand|undo|reset K3:on|off` (`k2_hint`; `K2:reset` once K2 is held 0.5 s), plus ` E3:group` on the destination field (`K2:reset K3:off E3:group`, 103 px by norns' font advances, is the longest). With K1 let go while ALT+K2 is down, `k2_hint(record)` alone, since the release still acts.
- `layout_states` (the options and destinations looked up by name, #430; `descender density`, `descender v1 pos` and `descender dly fdbk` pin #422): `lfo1`-`lfo3` (each selected, running, on the last destination), `field-dest` ... `field-state` (LFO 2 with wide values: v7 scan, random, 300 s, 100 %, -50 %), `clocked` (LFO 3 at `5/16`), `free` (LFO 1 at 0.1 s), `ALT+K2 held past the hold time` (LFO 1 running, the cursor on its destination: the widest ALT hint, `K2:reset K3:off E3:group`).
