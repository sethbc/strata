## Must not break

- E3 edits through `enc_param_delta` (never `params:delta`), so ALT+E3 is fine and the center detents apply.
- K3 on a delay or reverb row toggles that effect's bypass param (`delay_bypass`, `reverb_bypass`, `S.lib.voice_mix.BYPASS`) through `set_binary_param`, so a controller mapped to it follows (norns' `Binary:set` sends no MIDI-map CC, #538), and never touches its level; only that param frees the engine node. The one exception is the delay's `clear` row (3.8): while the delay runs, K3 there calls `S.lib.fx_hold.clear()` and nothing else (one `clearDelay()`, no `setBypass`); while it is bypassed the clear would do nothing, so the row shows `--` and K3 is the bypass as on every other delay row. K3 on a filter row keeps 3.1's mix toggle (the filter is never freed).
- The filter's restore value is `S.fx_last_mix.filter`, written only in `lib/params_setup.lua`: `filter_mix`'s action sets it when the mix rises above the mix before (E3, PARAMS, scenes, PSET), and `S.lib.params_setup.toggle_filter_mix()` sets it to the mix at the press before turning a mix above 0 to 0. Never write it from the page. K3 on a filter row calls `toggle_filter_mix` and nothing else (#235). The delay and reverb have no restore mix: their bypass params keep it.
- Section labels (`selectable = false` rows) take no cursor stop: always move through `S.lib.ui_field_list` (`move`, `clamp`), and clamp `S.fx_row` before use. Select a row by its param id (the layout states use `row_of`, a hold row `hold_row_of(effect, hold)`), never by a hard-coded index (#114).
- The rows are written by hand in `S.FX_PAGE.rows` (the plan's ruling 29); a registered fx record adds params, never rows. Every row has a `section`, which ALT+K2 randomizes; K3 and ALT+K3 act on the row's `effect` (master and shimmer rows have none and do nothing; their hint is `E3:value`).
- **The freezes are reached only through `S.lib.fx_hold`** (the plan's ruling 27): a hold row (`hold = "freeze"` or `"clear"`) has no `param_id`; its param is `S.lib.fx_hold.FX_HOLD[effect]`'s `freeze_id` or `clear_id`, and none while `S.lib.fx_hold.offered(effect)` is false. Then the row shows `S.FX_PAGE.unavailable_text` (`--`), E3 and ALT+K3 send nothing, and no draw, hint or key reads the param (3.8 cut the reverb's freeze, #819: there is no `reverb_freeze` param, and a test checks nothing reaches for one). Never decide from a param's presence or `S.performance.tier`. A section row's `frz` reads `S.lib.fx_hold.frozen(effect)`, which is false for an effect not offered.
- ALT+K3 (`alt_key(3)`, through `alt_action`) freezes or releases the cursor row's effect through `S.lib.fx_hold.toggle(effect)`, only while it is offered and not bypassed (a bypassed effect has no node, and its freeze would be refused); the shimmer, filter and master rows do nothing. E3 on a freeze row (a row's `binary`) turns it on with a turn up and off with a turn down through `set_binary_param`.
- The hint names only keys that act (#419): `K3:<effect> bypass|on`, `K3:clear` on a running delay's clear row, or `E3:value` while ALT is up. With ALT held: the shared `S.lib.ui_alt.k2_hint(record)` (`K2:rand`, `K2:undo`, or `K2:cancel` once K2 is held 0.5 s, FX having no `alt_hold`), then ` K3:freeze` (` K3:unfreeze` while frozen) on a row of an effect ALT+K3 freezes, then ` E3:fine` on a row that turns finely, a control param (none on an option, a binary row or the clear). The widest, `K2:cancel K3:unfreeze E3:fine`, is 122 px. With K1 let go while ALT+K2 is down, `k2_hint(record)` alone, since the release still acts.
- ALT+K2 (#557) acts on release through `S.lib.ui_alt` (`alt_key` calls `k2_down`, `alt_key_release` calls `k2_up(record)`); the record has `randomize_section` and `animates` (while K2 is down) but no `alt_hold`. `randomize_section` returns the prebuilt spec of the cursor row's `section`: density 0.5-1.5 and drift 0-0.03; filter type (uniform option), freq 100-10000 Hz and res 0-0.7; delay time 0.05-1.0 s, feedback 0-0.7 and level 0-0.5; reverb level 0-0.5, size 0.3-0.95, damp 0-1 and `delay_to_reverb` 0.3-1; shimmer interval (uniform option), delay 0-0.5 and reverb 0-0.4. Freq and time are even in octaves. Kept: `filter_mix`, both bypasses, the freezes and the clear, never in a spec. The specs are built once, since the hint asks on every draw while ALT is held. Each drawn param's action calls `note_param_edit` (params_setup's, voice_mix's `delay_to_reverb`, shimmer's three), so an edit ends the undo.
- Every row stays inside 128×64 at both ends of its range and every row position, `byp` and `frz` included (the FX layout test and `layout_states`).
- The delay's rows (3.7, ruling 15): K3 stays the bypass on every delay row but the clear, `sync`, `ping` and `freeze` too. E3 on `sync` turns it on and off as on a freeze row. While the delay plays a division (`S.lib.delay_sync.playing()`), the `time` row (its `synced = "delay_division"`) shows `S.lib.delay_sync.label()` and E3 and the ALT hint act on `delay_division`, an option (no `E3:fine`); `delay_time` is left alone.

## States

The filter's restore mix (`S.fx_last_mix.filter`, and the mix before the last change, local to `lib/params_setup.lua`, starting at 0). The filter mix itself is a param; no invariant is registered.

| Event | Mix | Restore mix | Mix before |
|-------|-----|-------------|------------|
| `filter_mix` set higher than the mix before (E3, PARAMS; a PSET load or a scene recall counts as a rise only when it raises the mix) | x | x | x |
| `filter_mix` set equal or lower (a turn down, a fade, a PSET bang of the same value; a PSET load or scene recall of a lower mix is a fall too, decision 1's accepted cost for any fall that is not K3's) | x | unchanged | x |
| K3 (`toggle_filter_mix`) with a mix above 0 | 0 | the mix at the press | 0 |
| K3 with a mix of 0 | the restore mix | unchanged | the restore mix (set through the action) |

Known cost (decision 1): turned down from 80% to 30% and later to 0, K3 restores 80%.

## The page

`redraw_fx`, `fx_enc`, `fx_key`, `fx_alt_key`:
- `S.FX_PAGE.rows` (3.8, design §7.6): five section labels (master, filter, delay, reverb, shimmer; no `param_id`, `selectable = false`, drawn at level 2 at x=0; the delay's and reverb's carry `bypass = "<effect>"` and `freeze = "<effect>"`) and 21 param rows `{label, section, param_id, format, effect}`: density, drift; filter type/freq/res/mix; delay time/fdbk/level/sync/ping/freeze/clear (3.7: `delay_sync` with `binary = true` and `on`/`off`, `delay_pingpong` in percent; the time row's `synced`; 3.8: `level` is `delay_mix`, the return's level; `freeze` and `clear` are hold rows); reverb level/size/damp/`from delay` (`delay_to_reverb`)/freeze; shimmer interval (`shimmer_interval`, the option's name)/delay/reverb (`shimmer_delay`, `shimmer_reverb`). `format` is a `string.format` pattern, a function of the value (`percent`, `on_off`), or nil for the param's own string. A test lists all 26 in order.
- Filter mix, delay feedback, level and ping-pong, reverb level, damping and `from delay`, and the shimmer amounts show in percent (`50%`). A freeze row shows `on`/`off`, or `--` (`unavailable_text`) while not offered; the clear row `K3` (`clear_text`), or `--` while the delay is bypassed.
- A bypassed effect's section row shows `S.FX_PAGE.bypass_text` (`byp`) right-aligned at the value column, a frozen one's `frozen_text` (`frz`) in its place, both at `bypass_level` (4); a bypass ends a freeze, so they never meet. The level row keeps showing the level.
- `S.FX_PAGE.visible_rows` (5) rows at y=18-50 (`first_row_y` 18, `row_spacing` 8), drawn through `S.lib.ui_field_list.draw`. Param labels at x=6, value right-aligned at x=124. The selected row at `selected_level` (15), the others at `row_level` (4). The view keeps one row above the cursor. A 1 px scroll marker at x=127 (from the helper).
- E2 moves over selectable rows (clamped), E3 is `enc_param_delta(3, <row param>, d)` (the synced time row's `delay_division`; `set_binary_param` on `sync` and a freeze; nothing on the clear or a freeze not offered).
- K3: on a delay or reverb row, `params:set(<effect>_bypass, 1 - current)` (on a running delay's clear row `S.lib.fx_hold.clear()`); on a filter row, `S.lib.params_setup.toggle_filter_mix()` (the mix between 0 and `S.fx_last_mix.filter`, initially 1). ALT+K3: `S.lib.fx_hold.toggle(effect)` on a delay or reverb row while it is offered and not bypassed.
- Hint: `K3:<effect> bypass` while the effect is on (delay and reverb: not bypassed; filter: mix above 0), else `K3:<effect> on`; `K3:clear` on a running delay's clear row; master and shimmer rows `E3:value`. A delay or reverb level of 0 is still "on". The texts are `S.FX_PAGE.hints`'.
- `layout_states`: first row, last row (the shimmer section at its widest: the interval's widest name, `oct down`, 38 px, and both amounts at 100%), filter freq at 20000 Hz, the delay and reverb both bypassed with the cursor on delay time (the delay's section row shows `byp`) and on reverb level (`reverb bypassed in view`: the reverb's; the delay's seven rows keep the two out of one view), the delay synced and halved (`delay synced and halved`: 1 bar at 20 BPM, the widest label `1 bar>1/4`), ALT+K2 held `ALT_HOLD_SECONDS` on filter freq (`ALT+K2 held past the hold time`: `K2:cancel E3:fine`), and 3.8's: `delay frozen` (`frz` on the delay's section row), `reverb frozen on high` (the cursor on reverb level: `frz` on the reverb's section row where its freeze is offered; on this build the section with no mark), `the clear row` (`K3:clear`), `ALT on a frozen delay row` (ALT+K2 held: the widest hint, `K2:cancel K3:unfreeze E3:fine`) and `shimmer at oct+5th and 100%`. The states freeze through `S.lib.fx_hold.toggle`, so a state reads no param a build lacks.
- The tests reach a reverb freeze behind the same seam a later package would fill: they plant `S.lib.fx_hold.FX_HOLD.reverb = {freeze_id = <id>}` and a binary param of that id in the stub session (`plant_reverb_freeze` in `test/unit/pages_fx_test.lua`; the header test the same), so ALT+K3 freezing an offered reverb, its `frz`, its hint and the `reverb frozen on high` state (on a high tier) are covered.
