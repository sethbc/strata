## Must not break

- **One way in** (plan's Global Constraints): a slice is played only through `S.lib.slices.play(voice, n, note)`; it alone gives `S.lib.notes.play` a note with a slice's region. The sequencer's steps that take the own note (one, off, and overlap's first) set the own region through `S.lib.notes.set_own_region` (the region rides the step's hit, as a lock does); every other player (MIDI, a grid's slice keys, an overlap step's slice in an extra slot) calls `play`. Only `S.lib.notes` calls `engine.noteRegion` and sends `regionStart` and `regionLength`, a voice sync's included (the record's `sync` calls `S.lib.notes.sync_own_region`; a test checks no other Lua file names them).
- **A missing slice plays nothing**: `play` returns false and sends nothing for a slice past the count, a slot with no markers, a slot whose frames are unknown, a voice that reads no buffer. A step naming one plays as a plain step; a MIDI note outside 36..36 + count - 1 on a voice that takes slice notes does nothing (no key held, nothing released).
- **Markers only from what the slot holds**: `count`, `region` and `play` read only the markers below the slot's frames (the peaks file's, `S.lib.waveforms.frames`, or the record's when its frames over its rate give the slot's seconds within 0.01 s), so a tier that shortens the slot drops the slices past its end. `set_markers` keeps them sorted, the 0 first, none past the frames or within `SLICES.min_seconds` (10 ms at the slot's rate) of the one before, at most `SLICES.max` (64).
- **The onsets file is read by a clock, never in the poll's callback**, and only for the newest analysis of a slot (`analyses[slot]`, counted from 1 as the engine counts them) sent while the slot held the audio it holds now (`generations[slot]`, raised at every bind and unbind). It is taken only if its first line is `SLICES.magic`, its `slot` is the slot's number, its `frames / rate` is within 0.01 s of the slot's seconds, and both lists hold the same count of numbers, the frames whole, ascending and inside the frames, the strengths 0-1; anything else prints one line and is dropped. Stale candidates never become markers.
- **Candidates are transient**: held per slot in memory, never a param, scene field, PSET value or part of `samples.data`; dropped at every bind and unbind of the slot. Turning the sensitivity with candidates held analyzes nothing; new candidates make the markers again only when the record is not `edited`.
- **One analysis a generation** (#872): `analyze` sends nothing (and returns true) while a pass sent for the audio the slot holds now is awaited (`awaited[slot]` set and `analysis_generations[slot] == generations[slot]`), so a mode or sensitivity detent of the editor during a pass starts no other: the engine runs every pass it is sent, each over the whole slot. The awaited pass's candidates make the markers at the settings they find. A report that never comes (a lost poll) blocks for `SLICES.analysis_timeout_seconds` (30 s: a 300 s slot's pass reports in 0.6-0.7 s on a desktop, `docs/notes/engine/onsets.md`, so about 45 times that) from the send (`analysis_sent_at[slot]`): after it a detent sends a new pass, and the lost pass's report, should it still come, is refused by its count (only `awaited[slot]` acts). `analyzing` stays true meanwhile. A bind or unbind (new audio) moves the generation and drops the wait, so the next `make` or a take's end sends a pass for the new audio. The engine's half (an older pass stops at its next chunk) is `docs/notes/engine/onsets.md`'s.
- **A load never analyzes.** The pass runs at `make` on a transients slot with no candidates, and at a take's or an overdub's end on a transients slot (a bind whose slot holds a take and whose `records_sent` moved since its last bind), from the next LFO step.
- **The bind listener sends nothing** (it runs through `S.lib.ui_frame.contained_call`): it drops the candidates and marks the slot; the next LFO step ends the regions of every voice that reads the slot (`S.lib.notes.end_regions`: its extra notes with a region released, its own region cleared) and analyzes. A slice note started on the new audio between the bind and that step (at most one 48 Hz step) is ended by it too: accepted, a window of one step.
- **Each slice hit restarts its scan** (the controller's ruling on 2b's review): `play`'s own note and a one-voice step's slice send the region as a hit (`set_own_region(..., true)`), the same slice again included, in wrap and bounce; on a one-shot voice the hit's retrigger restarts the scan, so the same slice again sends nothing (one restart a hit, `docs/notes/lua/notes.md`); a send that is no hit (the option's clear, a region ending) sends only a change.
- **A step's region gives way to another owner's own note**: on a one or off voice, while another owner holds slot 1 (a MIDI key down, `other_owns_slot_1`), the step sets no region, as its chord's first note takes no slot 1; the held slice keeps its region.
- **The own region rides every voice sync**: the record's `sync` sends `regionStart` then `regionLength` from `S.lib.notes.own_region(voice)` (0 and 0 for none) for every sync, a start's included (before `voiceOn`), so a step that starts a stopped voice plays its slice from the first grain.

## What it owns

`lib/slices.lua` (`S.lib.slices`, 3.7, design §3.1-§3.6, package 2b), included after `lib/expression.lua`. The tunables are `SLICES` (`M.SLICES`): `max = 64`, `min_seconds = 0.01`, `first_note = 36`, `bank_size = 16`, `options = {"off", "one", "overlap"}`, `magic = "strata-onsets 1"`, and the builder's `key` (`slices`), `section` (VOICE's `synth`), the indices `off`, `one`, `overlap` and the file modes `equal` (2) and `transients` (3) (`S.lib.samples.SAMPLES.modes`), `sensitivity_max` (100), `threshold_margin` (1e-9: a strength printed at the threshold is taken, whatever the rounding of `1 - sensitivity / 100`), `length_tolerance` (0.01 s), `poll` (`bufferOnsets`), `default_rate` (48000, while a slot's rate is unknown) and `analysis_timeout_seconds` (30, #872: how long an awaited pass blocks a new one).

**Where a slot's slices live**: in its file's settings (`S.lib.samples`, `docs/notes/lua/samples.md`): `mode` (off, equal, transients), `count`, `sensitivity`, `edited`, `markers` and the `frames` and `rate` the markers were made on. This module writes them only through `S.lib.samples.set`.

**Functions**:
- `count(slot)`, `markers(slot)` (a copy), `region(slot, n)` (`{start, length}` fractions of the valid region: marker n over the frames, to marker n + 1 or the end), `voice_region(voice, n)` (of the slot the voice reads).
- `set_markers(slot, markers, edited)`; `make(slot)`: equal, `count` markers at `floor((k - 1) × frames / count)`; transients, 0 and every candidate whose strength is at least `1 - sensitivity / 100`, or `analyze` when none are held (made when they arrive); off, none. It clears `edited`.
- `add_marker(slot, frame)` (false at 64 or if the new list would lose any cut, including a cut past a shorter load's end; a refusal preserves the file's markers, frames, rate and edited flag), `delete_marker(slot, index)` (never index 1), `nudge_marker(slot, index, frames)` (never index 1; it stops 10 ms short of its neighbors): each through `set_markers(..., true)`.
- `analyze(slot)`, `analyzing(slot)`, `candidates(slot)` (a copy of `{frames, strengths}`, or nil).
- `option(voice)` (`"off"`, `"one"`, `"overlap"`), `takes_notes(voice)` (a buffer voice whose option is not off and whose slot has slices), `play(voice, n, note)`, `note_off(voice, owner, key)` (`S.lib.notes.release`, which leaves a note held to its end), `playing(voice)` (the slice numbers sounding on the voice: its held extra notes' regions, and the own region while it plays from any owner: slot 1's note held by MIDI or a grid, never a parked or played-out one, or set by the step playing, `S.lib.sequencer.playing_region(voice)`, which in one shot stops naming it once its pass from the step has gone).

**`play(voice, n, note)`**: `note` is `S.lib.notes.play`'s owner, key and velocity, with park, quiet, extra_only, expression and steals passed on when given. The note's pitch is the voice's own (`S.lib.notes.own_pitch`), `set_own` does nothing (a slice note never re-pitches the voice), `region` is slice n's, and `to_end` is `S.lib.notes.pass_seconds(voice, length)` (`S.lib.scan.pass_seconds`, read at each use: nil outside one shot or with the scan at 0), with `hold_to_end` while it is a number; the own note ends at that time as an extra note does. With the option `one`, or off (a step's), the note is `own_only`. It returns the slot taken, or false.

**The option**: the voice-param record `slices` (`v<i>_slices`, an option, default off, a discrete scene field, `fader = false`, VOICE's `synth` section while the voice reads a buffer, two layout states). Its action clears the voice's own region (`set_own_region(voice, nil)`): a slice played under one option never outlives it.

**The transients pass**: `analyze` counts the slot's analyses and sends `engine.analyzeBuffer(slot - 1)`, unless a pass of the slot's present generation is awaited (Must not break); the `bufferOnsets` poll (start step `slices_poll`, 64; stopped at cleanup step `slices_poll`, 131, with the read clock canceled through `S.lib.clocks.cancel`) reports `((count × 2) + failed) × NUM_BUFFERS + slot index` (`docs/notes/engine/onsets.md`). The newest analysis's report ends the wait (`analyzing` false); a written file is queued for one clock (`S.lib.clocks.run`, its handle in the local `reader.clock`, sleeping once through the handle before it reads; #870, `docs/notes/lua/clocks.md`; only cleanup ends it), which reads every queued slot's `buf<n>.onsets` (each read contained, `strata: onsets read failed: `, so one that raises never leaves the clock recorded) from the settings folder (`S.lib.scenes.scene_data_dir`) and ends. The engine's file lists the candidates without frame 0; their strengths are log2 of the rise over the four hops before, less 1, scaled so the strongest is 1, so the sensitivity is a threshold on that scale.
