## Must not break

- **One door** (plan's Global Constraints): only `lib/samples.lua` reads or writes `samples.data` and a slot's settings. Other modules read through `settings`, `tempo`, `bars`, `gain`, `rate_root` and `scale_reference`, and change a record only through `set` and `set_bars`; `lib/buffers.lua` and `lib/record.lua` call the bind functions below and nothing else.
- **Which record**: a slot bound with its scratch path (`scratch_take_path(slot)`), or a take with no path, holds a take and reads the slot's own record (kept in `buffers.data`'s `settings[n]`); any other path reads its file's record, found in `samples.data` by path and start together (a start reload plays other audio from the same path). `settings(slot)` is nil while the slot is unbound or reports no audio (`seconds` 0).
- **Binds** happen only where a slot's state settles: `confirm_buffer_audio` (a load that took effect, a replace take's end, the restore, an adoption), `apply_buffer_state` for settled audio (a failed load's undo, a canceled take's put-back), and a playable recording's "ended" report. **Unbinds** happen where it empties: `clear_buffer`, a replace take's start (its send when it is not armed, else its "recording" report, once: `take_started` in `lib/record.lua`; through an arm's wait the old audio plays on with its settings), a settle with no audio, and `apply_buffer_state` for an empty state. A load that is sent binds nothing: the old audio and its record play on until the new one takes effect.
- **One file in two slots is one record**: both slots bind the same entry, so a `set` acts for every slot bound to it (`sharing`): a `key` resends `scaleNote` and `rootNote` to the running voices of each, `normalize` sends each slot's gain (from its own peaks). A take's record is its slot's alone.
- **Every bind and unbind tells the `on_bind` listeners**, each through `S.lib.ui_frame.contained_call` (a listener never sends and never yields), and resends `scaleNote` and `rootNote` to the running voices that read the slot (`S.lib.variation.send_scale_note`, `send_root_note`). A bind clears nothing in the record.
- **The own record goes** at a clear, at a replace take's end (`start_take` in `confirm_buffer_audio`, or `drop_own` at its settle with no audio) and when the slot is bound to a file; never at a replace take's start, so a canceled take that puts back the take before it keeps its settings. An overdub's or a loop recording's end (`take_from_file`) starts it as a copy of the file's record it recorded over (a take dubbed keeps its own); the file's record is never written by a take. A synced take's tempo is in the record before its bind (`start_take(slot, tempo)`, `take_from_file(slot, tempo)`), so the listeners see it.
- **The gain is sent only from the peaks listener and from `normalize`'s `set`**, never at a bind (the peaks Lua holds at a bind are the audio before's): `S.lib.waveforms.on_read` tells `lib/samples.lua` after every successful read, the same values included, since the engine sets a slot's gain to 1 itself at a load, a clear and every recording's start. Nothing is sent while the slot records (`S.lib.record.state(slot)`): a take is heard at its own level while it is made, and its end's peaks read sends the gain. A canceled playable recording (an armed dub stopped, any dub canceled) keeps the slot's audio but the engine set its gain to 1 at the command, so `lib/record.lua` marks the slot's peaks stale and the read sends it again. With `normalize` off the listener sends nothing, so a 3.6 session sends no `setSlotGain`.
- **An unset key is 3.6's pitch exactly**: `rate_root(voice)` is `S.lib.notes.NOTES.rate_root_note` (60) and `scale_reference(voice)` is nil (the caller keeps the scale's root + 60). Only a buffer voice whose slot's record has a `key` gets it; input and synth voices never do. The four uses of the root note (`own_setters` in `lib/midi_in.lua`, `note_values` and `own_pitch` in `lib/notes.lua`, `value_note` in `lib/sequencer.lua`) read `rate_root`; `NOTES.rate_root_note` and `MIDI.rate_root_note` stay equal to the engine's `noteRateRootNote` (a docs test).
- **A record at its defaults is never written** (no `samples.data` entry, `false` in `buffers.data`), and a value that fails its check changes nothing (`set` returns false).
- **samples.data** is written only through `S.lib.data_files.write`, `SAMPLES.save_delay` (0.1 s) after the first change of a burst when what it would hold differs from what was last written, and at the cleanup step. Unreadable, of another version or without a file list, it is set aside as `samples.data.bak` (`S.lib.data_files.set_aside`) with one console line and the slots load with no settings; if it cannot be moved, nothing is written over it. It has no SETUP row (ruling 27). An entry whose path, start, `used` or settings fail their checks, a repeated one and one past `max_files` are left out, with one console line naming how many (the next write drops them).

## What it owns

`S.lib.samples` (3.7, design §4, decision 10; package 2d). The tunables are `SAMPLES` (`M.SAMPLES`): `file = "samples.data"`, `version = 1`, `max_files = 256`, `max_gain = 16`, `tempo_min = 20`, `tempo_max = 300`, `modes = {"off", "equal", "transients"}`, `default_count = 8`, `default_sensitivity = 50`, and the builder's `count_min = 2`, `count_max = 64`, `sensitivity_max = 100`, `key_max = 127`, `beats_per_bar = 4`, `seconds_per_minute = 60`, `save_delay = 0.1`, `backup_suffix = ".bak"`.

**A settings record**: `{tempo, key, normalize, mode, count, sensitivity, edited, markers, frames, rate}`. `tempo` nil or 20-300; `key` nil or a whole 0-127; `normalize` and `edited` booleans; `mode` 1-3 (default 1, `off`); `count` 2-64 (default 8); `sensitivity` 0-100 (default 50); `markers` nil or a list of at most 64 whole frames, ascending, the first 0; `frames` (a whole number above 0) and `rate` (above 0) the audio's when the markers were made, or nil.

**Functions**:
- `settings(slot)`: the live record of what the slot holds (a default one made on first use), nil for a slot with no audio. `set(slot, field, value)`: checks, stores, schedules the write, then acts (`key`: resends `scaleNote` and `rootNote`; `normalize`: sends the gain) and requests a redraw; true when stored (or unchanged), false when refused.
- `on_bind(fn)`, `bind(slot)`, `unbind(slot)` (above). `bind` of a file raises its `used` to a counter's next value (the largest `used` read at start, plus one per bind), and the entries are pruned: an unbound entry at its defaults goes, and past `max_files` the unbound one with the lowest `used`.
- `drop_own(slot)`, `start_take(slot, tempo)`, `take_from_file(slot, tempo)` (`lib/buffers.lua`, `lib/record.lua`); `snapshot(slot)` (a whole save's copy at its send, nil at defaults) and `store_file(path, record)` (the save's success: the path's entry at start 0 takes it); `stored_own(slot)` and `restore_own(slot, value)` (`buffers.data`'s write and restore; a damaged record is dropped with one console line).
- `rate_root(voice)`, `scale_reference(voice)`.
- `tempo(slot)`; `bars(slot)` (`seconds × tempo / 240`, nil without a tempo); `set_bars(slot, bars)` (`tempo = bars × 240 / seconds`, false outside 20-300).
- `gain(slot)`: 1 unless `normalize`; else `min(16, 1 / peak)`, `peak` the largest of the slot's 64 peaks (`S.lib.waveforms.peaks`), and 1 while none are read or the peak is 0 or 1. `send_gain(slot)`: `engine.setSlotGain(slot - 1, gain)`, unless the slot records.

**samples.data**: `{version = 1, files = {{path, start, used, settings = {...}}, ...}}`, a list, the most recently used first, at most `max_files` entries, defaults left out. Read at `START_ORDER.samples_load` (61, before the buffers' restore at 110); written at `CLEANUP_ORDER.samples_save` (37).

**The tempo of a synced take**: `lib/record.lua` keeps the clock's tempo at a `record_sync` take's start (`60 / beat_seconds_now()`, `synced_tempo`), and the take's end puts it in the take's record as its `tempo` before the bind (a replace take through `start_take` in `confirm_buffer_audio`, a playable one through `take_from_file` at "ended"); a tempo outside 20-300 is none.

**A whole save**: `send_save` keeps `snapshot(slot)` with a whole save (`save_buffer`), never with a loop save; `buffer_save_result` gives it to `store_file` when the report says the file is in place. A load between the send and the report does not change what the saved file gets.
