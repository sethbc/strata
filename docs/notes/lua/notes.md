## Must not break

- **`S.lib.notes` is the only module that calls `noteOn`, `noteOff`, `noteExpression` and `setNotePool`** (design §2.2), and notes are played, released and expressed only through it. Every engine call passes `voice - 1` and `slot - 1` (Lua's slots are 1-8, the engine's 0-7, 0 the voice's own note).
- **A voice's own note (slot 1) is 3.4's, message for message**: `set_own(pitch)` first, then the start of a stopped voice (the global `toggle_voice`, as MIDI does today), then `retrigger(voice - 1, velocity)`. A parked own note is unparked with `noteOn` on slot 0 and then accented with the `retrigger`, never `noteOn` alone (ruling 15: the stage's step gate and accent open only on `retrigger`, so a gated voice's unparked note would stay silent); a frozen synth voice gets the same `noteOn` before the `retrigger` (it carries the rate). A note cap of 1 or a pool of 0 sends nothing else. The own note never creates a node.
- **An extra note's start is exactly `noteExpression` then `noteOn`** on its slot (its first expression goes at once, before the `noteOn`, outside the flush's cap); a steal across voices sends `noteOff(v2 - 1, slot2 - 1, 1)` first. An extra note is one synth in its slot, which only this module starts.
- **Never stolen:** slot 1; a locked voice's note (`S.frozen`), for another voice; a note younger than `NOTES.cut_seconds` (0.05 s, the engine's cut). The last bounds the cutting synths at the pool's number.
- **The flush never sends more than `NOTES.sends_per_step` (16) `noteExpression` a step**, the due notes in the order first marked (so none starves), each once with its newest values, none for a note that ended; nothing is sent when a value arrives. Every note command but `setNotePool` is counted through `S.lib.engine_sync.count_sends` and never deferred.
- **A stopped voice holds no extra note**, and its slot 1 at most a `quiet` note awaiting its caller's start: the voice-stop hook forgets them all and sends nothing (`voiceOff` ended them in the engine), a later release of a forgotten key sends nothing, and branches 3 to 5 need a running voice (a stopped one refuses an extra slot: nil, nothing sent).
- **The pool is device-level**: kept in data/strata/notes.data, read at the start step and written by `set_pool`; a PSET or a scene never changes it, and it is never a param. **`set_pool` writes first** (as `S.lib.grid_core.set_rotation` does): the pool changes only when the write succeeded; then the newest held extra notes past a smaller pool are released, then `setNotePool` goes.
- notes.data is read only through `S.lib.data_files.read` and written only through `S.lib.data_files.write` (`docs/notes/lua/data-files.md`).
- **`note_values` is the one place a pitch becomes what the engine plays**: every note goes through it, and 3.8's tunings change it alone.
- **Extra notes, the pool, the freeze reference and expression values are never stored** in a param, scene or PSET. The note cap is a PSET param, never a scene field.

## Owns

`lib/notes.lua` (`S.lib.notes`, 3.5, packages 0a and 1b, design §2, §6.4), included after `lib/matrix.lua`, with its table `NOTES` (`M.NOTES`): `file` (`"notes.data"`), `version` (1), `cut_seconds` (0.05), `sends_per_step` (16), `rate_root_note` (60: the note at rate 1), `rate_range` (`{0.25, 4}`), `note_range` (`{0, 127}`), `a4` (note 69 at 440 Hz: a frequency's note), `owners` (`midi`, `seq`), `expression_keys` (`bend`, `amp`, `position`, `size`, `main`: `noteExpression`'s order), `neutral` (`{1, 1, 0, 0, 0}`), `expression_ranges` (bend 0.0625-16, amp 0-2, the rest -1..1: the engine's clips) and `cap` (the note cap's key, name and VOICE section).

### The model

A voice has `NOTE_SLOTS` (8) slots. Slot 1 is its own note: its pitch is the voice's params, set by the player's `set_own`. Slots 2-8 hold extra notes, each with a pitch of its own, counted against the pool all voices share. Each used slot holds a record `{voice, slot, owner, key, pitch, velocity, park, quiet, held, parked, started, expression, due}`; an extra slot is used while its note is held, slot 1 also while its own note is parked. Per slot the module keeps when its last note ended (`ended`), and the expression the engine keeps for it (`sent`, neutral at load; slot 1's back to neutral at a voice stop, since `voiceOn` resets it).

### `play(voice, note) -> slot | nil`

`note = {owner, key, pitch, velocity, set_own, park, quiet, expression}`: `owner` `"midi"` or `"seq"`; `key` any non-nil value, unique per owner; `pitch` a finite MIDI note number (fractional allowed); `velocity` a finite number, clamped to 0-1; `set_own(pitch)` the player's way of pitching the own note; `park` (boolean, default false) whether the own note parks when released under others; `quiet` (boolean, default false); `expression` a table of any of the five keys (each finite value clipped to its range, the rest neutral). Anything else, or a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES`, is refused: nil, nothing sent. The branches, in order (`now` is `util.time()`; a frozen synth voice's reference is taken first, before `set_own` can move its freq):
1. **The same owner and key held:** slot 1 plays as in 2; an extra slot is re-pitched and accented in place: `noteExpression` only when the note's expression differs from what the engine keeps for the slot, then `noteOn`. No slot is taken.
2. **Slot 1 free** (nothing in it, or the own note parked): `set_own(pitch)`, `toggle_voice(voice)` when the voice is stopped, `noteExpression` on slot 0 when the note's expression differs from the engine's (never for a neutral note on a fresh or restarted voice), `noteOn` on slot 0 for a parked own note (the unpark) or on a frozen synth voice (the rate), then `retrigger`. With `quiet`, `set_own(pitch)` and, for a parked own note, its unpark (`noteOn` on slot 0), so Lua's record and the engine agree (not parked); no start and no `retrigger`: the caller starts and accents the voice (the sequencer keeps its own order), and an expression that differs waits for the flush. A held slot 1 of another note loses it (the newest note wins).
3. **A free extra slot under the cap and room in the pool:** the free slot among 2..cap whose last note ended longest ago (a never used one first; a tie, the lowest), so a tail has the longest time to fade: `noteExpression`, then `noteOn`.
4. **A free slot but the pool full:** the oldest held extra note on any voice that may be stolen (by `started`; a tie, the lowest voice, then slot) is cut, `noteOff(v2 - 1, slot2 - 1, 1)`, and forgotten; the new note starts as in 3. None that may be stolen: branch 6.
5. **No free slot under the cap:** the voice's oldest held extra note in slots 2..cap that may be stolen is replaced in place (`noteExpression`, `noteOn`, no `noteOff`). None: branch 6.
6. **Nothing can be stolen** (a cap of 1, a pool of 0, every candidate protected): slot 1 as in 2, whoever held it.

Branches 3 to 6 run only on a running voice: a stopped voice whose slot 1 holds a `quiet` note (its caller has not started it yet) refuses a note that would need another slot (nil, nothing sent). `quiet` changes nothing for an extra slot.

### Release

- `release(voice, owner, key) -> boolean`: an extra note gets `noteOff(voice - 1, slot - 1, 0)` and its slot is free (ended now). The own note parks (`noteOff(voice - 1, 0, 0)`, the record kept as `parked`) when it asked to (`park`) and an extra note is held; otherwise it is forgotten and nothing is sent: the player decides whether the voice stops (MIDI) or its override ends (the sequencer). false when no such note is held.
- `release_owner(voice, owner)`: each of the owner's held notes, the extra ones first in slot order, then the own note (which so parks only under another owner's notes).
- `release_all(voice)`: forgets every note, a parked own note included, and sends nothing: for a voice the engine has stopped. The voice-stop hook calls it.

### The rest of the interface

- `find(voice, owner, key) -> slot | nil` (held notes only); `held(voice)` (the voice's held notes, the own note included: the count MAIN will show; 0 for a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES`); `slots(voice)` (copies of `{slot, owner, key, pitch, held, parked}` for each used slot, in order); `pool_used()` (held extra notes on every voice).
- `pool()`: notes.data's value, else the running tier's default (`S.constants.NOTE_POOL[S.performance.tier]`: 4 on standard, 8 on high; standard's while the tier is unknown).
- `set_pool(n)`: a whole number, clamped to 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` (16), written to notes.data as `{version = 1, pool = n}` (the folder made first), then set; while more extra notes are held than `n`, the newest (by `started`) is released as `release` does, by its slot (so the loop always frees one); then `engine.setNotePool(n)`; true. false, with nothing set or sent, for anything but a whole number (a string, a fraction, NaN, ±inf) and for a write that fails (with one line, `strata: notes.data not saved: <why>`).
- `set_expression(voice, slot, values) -> boolean`: a held note's expression takes `values`' finite keys (clipped), keeps the rest, and the note is due for the flush; false (nothing marked) for an empty or parked slot or a bad index.
- `note_values(voice, pitch)`: `{note, freq, rate}`: `note` the pitch clamped to 0-127, `freq` `musicutil.note_num_to_freq(pitch)` clamped to the voice's `v<i>_freq` range (20-2000 Hz), `rate` `2 ^ ((pitch - 60) / 12)`, or on a frozen synth voice `2 ^ ((pitch - reference) / 12)`, clamped to 0.25-4. nil for a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES` or a pitch that is not a finite number.
- `freeze_reference(voice) -> note | nil`: a frozen synth voice's reference, the fractional MIDI note of `S.lib.engine_sync.engine_base_value("v<i>_freq")` when it froze (the freeze-change hook takes it); one that froze without the hook taking it (a recall or PSET that left `freeze` at 1 while the source changed) takes it when first asked, as it starts. nil for any other voice, whose kept reference is dropped then: a voice that left the frozen synth state by a path that runs no freeze-change hook (a recall's source switch) never reuses an old one, and takes a fresh one once found frozen again. `S.lib.variation.scale_note` returns it for a frozen synth voice, so no resend of `scaleNote` moves it (`docs/notes/lua/variation.md`).
- `stop_flush()`: the cleanup step's (below).

### The flush (design §6.4)

A hook of the LFO clock (`S.lib.lfos.on_step`), registered by the start step, so it runs after every hook registered at include time (2c's voice-wide step among them). `set_expression` marks a note due, appending it to the queue unless it already waits there (so a later mark keeps its place and its newest values). Each step sends, in queue order, one `noteExpression` per due note still held in its slot, at most `NOTES.sends_per_step`; the rest wait, ahead of anything marked later, so no note starves; a note that ended or was parked meanwhile is dropped. The step's count goes to `count_sends` (none when nothing went).

### The note cap

`v<i>_note_cap` (number, 1-`NOTE_SLOTS`, default 8, name `note cap`, shown as its number), a registered voice-params record `notes` with `voice_rows = {section = "notes", rows = {"note_cap"}, before = "variation"}`, no grain keys, no `scene_defaults` (PSET only), `fader = false`, and a layout state per voice (`v<i> note cap`, the cursor on its row) for VOICE's layout tests and renders. VOICE's ALT+K2 on its section does nothing (no draw). Lowering it under held notes keeps them (`lowered[voice]`, cleared once no used slot is past the cap): branches 3 and 5 only use slots under the new cap.

### Registrations

- The start step `notes_load` (`START_ORDER.notes_load`, 109, after the matrix's load): reads notes.data (parsed, never run). A version 1 file whose `pool` is a whole number in 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` sets the pool. No file leaves the tier's default with no line. Anything else (text that does not parse, another version, a bad or missing pool) leaves the default too, with one line: `strata: notes.data not used (<why>): the pool is the tier's default, <n>`. A file it cannot use is written over at the next `set_pool`, with no backup, as grids.data is. Then `engine.setNotePool(pool())` and the flush's hook (once).
- The cleanup step `notes` (`CLEANUP_ORDER.notes`, 119, before the LFO clock's 120): `stop_flush()`: nothing is due, and the hook and `set_expression` mark or send nothing after it.
- `S.register_voice_stop` (`docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`): `release_all(voice)`, and slot 1's kept expression back to neutral.
- `S.register_freeze_change` (`docs/notes/lua/freeze.md`), for a synth voice only: at a freeze the reference is taken, every extra note is cut (`noteOff(v - 1, slot - 1, 1)`, in slot order) and forgotten (their sound is in the frozen ring); at an unfreeze the reference goes and the records stand. Either way `scaleNote` is resent (`S.lib.variation.send_scale_note` with the freq's engine value: the reference while frozen, the freq's note after). A buffer or input voice's notes are streams already: nothing changes.
- The record `notes` (above).
- The invariant `"notes"`: the pool is a whole number in 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` and the held extra notes never exceed it; every slot record is whole (its voice and slot, an owner and key, a finite pitch and start, a velocity in 0-1, held or parked and only slot 1 parked, a `quiet` flag, an expression in range); a voice's used slots are under its cap unless it was lowered under them; a stopped voice holds no extra note, and its slot 1 at most a held `quiet` note awaiting its caller's start; and slot 1's holder is one owner, checked as each owner and key held at most once per voice (across all its slots).

Every change of a voice's held count (a play that takes or frees a slot, a release, a steal, a freeze's cuts, a stop) calls `update_voice_list()` and `request_redraw()`.

The constants it reads are `S.constants.NOTE_SLOTS` (8; the engine's `noteSlots`) and `S.constants.NOTE_POOL` (`max` 16, the engine's `notePoolMax`; `standard` 4 and `high` 8, each tier's default).
