## Must not break

- **`S.lib.notes` is the only module that calls `noteOn`, `noteOff`, `noteExpression` and `setNotePool`** (design §2.2), and notes are played, released and expressed only through it. Every engine call passes `voice - 1` and `slot - 1` (Lua's slots are 1-8, the engine's 0-7, 0 the voice's own note).
- **A voice's own note (slot 1) is 3.4's, message for message**: `set_own(pitch)` first, then the start of a stopped voice (the global `toggle_voice`, as MIDI does today, with `retrigger_follows`: 3.7), then `retrigger(voice - 1, velocity)`, sent through `S.lib.engine_sync.retrigger(voice, velocity, "note")`, which tells the `voice_hit` hooks once for the two (`docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`, 3.7's seams). An extra note's start (`start_extra`) tells them too (`extra_note_hit`), unless its owner is the sequencer's (`NOTES.step_owner`): a step's chord is one hit, its step's. A parked own note is unparked with `noteOn` on slot 0 and then accented with the `retrigger`, never `noteOn` alone (ruling 15: the stage's step gate and accent open only on `retrigger`, so a gated voice's unparked note would stay silent); a frozen synth voice gets the same `noteOn` before the `retrigger` (it alone carries the rate). One rule for both kinds of play: after `set_own`, the slot-0 `noteOn` when the own note was parked or the voice is a frozen synth voice, sent only to a running voice (a stopped frozen synth voice's goes from the grain-restart hook at its start, after `voiceOn`, since `voiceOn` builds a new own grain synth), then `retrigger` unless `quiet` (rulings 15 and 18). A note cap of 1 or a pool of 0 sends nothing else. The own note never creates a node.
- **An extra note's start is exactly `noteExpression` then `noteOn`** on its slot (3.7: with the slot's `noteRegion` between them when one is due, below) (its first expression goes at once, before the `noteOn`, outside the flush's cap); a steal across voices sends `noteOff(v2 - 1, slot2 - 1, 1)` first. An extra note is one synth in its slot, which only this module starts.
- **A running voice with no held note never keeps its own note parked** (ruling 39): when a release, a steal or a freeze's cuts end a voice's last held note under its parked own note, the own note returns at once, neutral: `noteExpression(v - 1, 0, 1, 1, 0, 0, 0)` first when the expression the engine keeps for slot 0 is not neutral (then stored as neutral; nothing when it is), so a lifted finger's bend or amp never carries into the drone, then `noteOn(v - 1, 0, note, freq, rate, 0)` from `note_values` of the voice's pitch now (its freq, buffer rate or grain pitch as the engine plays it), velocity 0 so it comes back with no accent, and its record goes: the voice sounds on as its drone, nobody's note. A stopped voice's parked note is forgotten with the stop, nothing sent. The invariant `"notes"` checks it.
- **An arpeggiating voice's further notes are the arpeggiator's, never a slot** (3.8, package 2b, `docs/notes/lua/arp.md`): in `play`, after branch 1 and before `extra_only`'s branch, a running voice whose arpeggiator is on and whose own note is held hands every further note (`extra_only` too) to `S.lib.arp.hold`, which returns `NOTES.arp_slot` (0: truthy, never 1, so MIDI keeps its expression record and `own_note_taken` and the sequencer's `== 1` stay false); with slot 1 free the note is the own note (branch 2) and the list's new base, the same key held in the list leaving it first. A note with a region (a slice) is not taken. Such a note holds no slot and no place in the pool, and `find` does not find it; `release`, `forget`, `release_owner`, `forget_owner`, `release_all` and the voice-stop hook reach the arpeggiator when `find` fails. So `others_held` stays false and the own note never parks under the list. Turned on, the arpeggiator takes the voice's held extra notes through `take_extras` (each cut, `noteOff(v - 1, slot - 1, 1)`, and forgotten with no `ended`: the note moves to the list, keeps its start, and its one `ended` comes when it leaves the list, so the targets hear nothing at the move; a note with a region kept).
- **Never stolen:** slot 1; a locked voice's note (`S.locked`), for another voice; a note younger than `NOTES.cut_seconds` (0.05 s, the engine's cut). The last bounds the cutting synths at the pool's number.
- **The flush never sends more than `NOTES.sends_per_step` (16) `noteExpression` a step**, the due notes in the order first marked (so none starves), each once with its newest values, none for a note that ended; nothing is sent when a value arrives. Every note command but `setNotePool` is counted through `S.lib.engine_sync.count_sends` and never deferred.
- **A voice that becomes a frozen synth voice holds no extra note from before** (design §2.4; rulings 58 and 59): its freeze (the freeze-change hook) and a scene recall that leaves a voice it switched a frozen synth voice (`M.recall_switched(voice)`, which `lib/scenes.lua`'s `apply_entries` calls for each running voice whose source changed, after its sync, once every entry is applied: an instant recall, a fade's landing, a morph's switches) both cut every extra note (`noteOff(v - 1, slot - 1, 1)`, in slot order, `cut_extras`) and forget it, the own note returning if it was parked under them (ruling 39). What the voice is at the recall's end decides, never its state at the switch (ruling 59: a recall that switches a frozen buffer or input voice to its synth with the scene's freeze off leaves it unfrozen, and it keeps its notes, which the engine restarted sounding). A later key-up of a cut note finds nothing and sends nothing. Outside a recall a switch never cuts: the freeze module's source-change hook unfreezes the voice.
- **A stopped voice holds no extra note**, and its slot 1 at most a `quiet` note awaiting its caller's start: the voice-stop hook forgets them all and sends nothing (`voiceOff` ended them in the engine), a later release of a forgotten key sends nothing, and a stopped voice's slot 1 is always free (ruling 19): every play on it takes slot 1, replacing whatever quiet note its caller never started, so branches 3 to 6 run only on a running voice and no play is refused because the voice is stopped.
- **The pool is device-level**: kept in data/strata/notes.data, read at the start step and written by `set_pool`; a PSET or a scene never changes it, and it is never a param. **`set_pool` writes first** (as `S.lib.grid_core.set_rotation` does): the pool changes only when the write succeeded; then the newest held extra notes past a smaller pool are released, then `setNotePool` goes.
- notes.data is read only through `S.lib.data_files.read` and written only through `S.lib.data_files.write` (`docs/notes/lua/data-files.md`); a file the start step cannot use is set aside (`NOTES.backup_suffix`, `.bak`) before any write, unless it cannot be moved, when the next `set_pool` writes over it.
- **`note_values` is where a note's pitch becomes what the engine plays** (its note, freq and rate): every note goes through it. A tuning (3.8, `docs/notes/lua/tuning.md`) never changes it: a note's pitch arrives already tuned, a MIDI key's through `S.lib.tuning.pitch_of_key` (and `snap` on a quantized voice, `S.lib.variation.midi_note`), a step's degrees through `S.lib.tuning.step`, so `note_values` turns a tuned fractional pitch into its freq and rate as it does any other (#780).
- **The note hooks (3.6) hear every note once from start to end**: each record of `S.registry.note_hooks` gets `started` once a record is in its slot (`play_own`, `start_extra`), `moved` for a held note re-pitched in place (branch 1, and `play_own` on the same owner and key, which then gives no `ended` and no `started`), `ended` from the local `forget` of a held note and when an own note parks (a parked note already ended, so its later forget gives none; its next play is a `started`) and `expressed` when a note becomes due (`mark_due`): exactly one `ended` per `started`, whatever ends the note (a release, a steal, a freeze's cuts, a voice stop, a smaller pool). The arpeggiator's held notes (3.8) are heard the same way, once each, with the slot `NOTES.arp_slot` (0), through `M.run_note_hooks(field, record)` (a note `take_extras` moves into the list keeps its start, and ends there, never at the move): no hook indexes by the slot (the targets key played notes by owner and key). **A hook never yields and never calls `S.lib.notes.play`**; each runs through `S.lib.ui_frame.contained_call` with `(voice, slot, note)`, `note` a fresh `{owner, key, pitch, velocity}`, so one that raises prints once (`strata: note hook <name> <field> failed: `) and the note and the other hooks go on.
- **A note's region (3.7, design §3.4) is sent only by this module.** An extra slot's goes as `noteRegion(v - 1, slot - 1, start, length)` before its `noteOn` (after its first `noteExpression`) when the note has one, or when the region last sent for the slot was not `0, 0` (then `0, 0`); a note with no region on a slot that never had one sends nothing new, and a note re-pitched in place with another region sends it again. `regions_sent` is what the engine keeps: `0, 0` at load (the engine's alloc), and each slot's last region across a voice's stop, release or cut (the engine clears a slot's region only at alloc and at the area's stop, `docs/notes/engine/notes.md`), so a plain note in a slot that last held a slice, after a restart, sends `noteRegion(…, 0, 0)` first. The own note's is `setGrainParam(v - 1, "regionStart", start)` then `"regionLength"`, only through `set_own_region(voice, region, hit)`, each send with `S.lib.playheads.reset(voice)` (the engine restarts the own scan at every `regionLength`): a hit (`hit` true: `play_own` with a region, a step's slice) sends even the same region in wrap and bounce, so a re-hit of the same slice restarts its scan (the controller's ruling on 2b's review); on a one-shot voice the retrigger every hit sends restarts the engine's scan (`positionsHit`) and the playhead (`lib/playheads.lua`'s `voice_hit` hook), so there a hit sends only a change and resets no playhead: one restart a hit in every mode (the Wave 2 landing); any other set (the option's clear, `end_regions`, a step's region ending) sends only a change. `play_own` sets it before the start or the hit, so the start's sync (the slices record's) carries it. It is nil again at a voice's stop, at a running voice's source switch (the grain-restart hook; the sync after it sends the clear) and at a bind of the voice's slot (`end_regions`, from `lib/slices.lua`'s step after its bind listener); a region is never a param, scene field or PSET value.
- **A note that plays to its end** (3.7): an extra note with `to_end` (its own, or on a buffer voice `pass_seconds(voice, length)` over its region or the voice's loop while that is a number; `M.pass_seconds` reads `S.lib.scan.pass_seconds` at each use, nil outside one shot or with the scan at 0, with no guard, and `lib/slices.lua` reads it through `M.pass_seconds`) is released by the flush's step once that many seconds have passed since it started (or was re-pitched in place), and an own note with `to_end` (a one-shot slice) has its record forgotten then, nothing sent (it is silent past its end) and its `ended` hooks told: every note started ends once, and a release-mode voice whose keys are up then stops (`docs/notes/lua/midi.md`); one with `hold_to_end` is left by `release` (false), and only its timer, `release_owner`, `forget_owner`, `forget`, a steal, a source switch, a bind of its slot, a voice's stop and `release_all` end it. `plays_to_end(voice)` tells `lib/midi_in.lua` not to stop a voice in release mode while one sounds.
- **A steal of the caller's kind** (3.7, `steals`): an `extra_only` note that finds no room under the cap or the pool cuts the voice's oldest extra note (at least `cut_seconds` old) for which `steals(owner, key)` is true (`noteOff(v - 1, slot - 1, 1)`) and takes its slot; it never takes another voice's slot or a note `steals` refuses.
- **What is stored where**: the note cap and the two envelope times are params, so PSETs keep them; the envelope times are also scene keys, the note cap is PSET-only and never a scene field. Extra notes, the pool (`notes.data`, device-level), the freeze reference and expression values are in neither a param, a scene nor a PSET.

## Owns

`lib/notes.lua` (`S.lib.notes`, 3.5, packages 0a and 1b, design §2, §6.4), included after `lib/matrix.lua`, with its table `NOTES` (`M.NOTES`): `file` (`"notes.data"`), `version` (1), `backup_suffix` (`".bak"`), `cut_seconds` (0.05), `sends_per_step` (16), `rate_root_note` (60: the note at rate 1; 3.7: `note_values` and `own_pitch` read `S.lib.samples.rate_root(voice)`, a buffer voice's file's key when set), `rate_range` (`{0.25, 4}`), `note_range` (`{0, 127}`), `a4` (note 69 at 440 Hz: a frequency's note), `owners` (`midi`, `seq` and 3.7's `grid`, a grid's slice keys with the key `port * 100 + slice`), `step_owner` (`seq`), 3.8's `arp_slot` (0: what `play` returns for a note the arpeggiator takes, and the slot its hooks hear), `expression_keys` (`bend`, `amp`, `position`, `size`, `main`: `noteExpression`'s order), `neutral` (`{1, 1, 0, 0, 0}`), `expression_ranges` (bend 0.0625-16, amp 0-2, the rest -1..1: the engine's clips) and `cap` (the note cap's key, name and VOICE section), `envelope` (each voice's default `{attack, release}`), `envelope_ranges`, `envelope_keys` and `envelope_engine` (the engine's control names).

### The model

A voice has `NOTE_SLOTS` (8) slots. Slot 1 is its own note: its pitch is the voice's params, set by the player's `set_own`. Slots 2-8 hold extra notes, each with a pitch of its own, counted against the pool all voices share. Each used slot holds a record `{voice, slot, owner, key, pitch, velocity, park, quiet, held, parked, started, expression, due}` (3.7: and `region`, `hold_to_end`, `ends_at`; `slots(voice)`'s copies carry `region` and `hold_to_end`); an extra slot is used while its note is held, slot 1 also while its own note is parked. Per slot the module keeps when its last note ended (`ended`), and the expression the engine keeps for it (`sent`, neutral at load; slot 1's back to neutral at a voice stop, since `voiceOn` resets it).

### `play(voice, note) -> slot | nil`

`note = {owner, key, pitch, velocity, set_own, park, quiet, expression}`: `owner` `"midi"` or `"seq"`; `key` any non-nil value, unique per owner; `pitch` a finite MIDI note number (fractional allowed); `velocity` a finite number, clamped to 0-1; `set_own(pitch)` the player's way of pitching the own note; `park` (boolean, default false) whether the own note parks when released under others; `quiet` (boolean, default false); `expression` a table of any of the five keys (each finite value clipped to its range, the rest neutral). Anything else, or a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES`, is refused: nil, nothing sent. The branches, in order (`now` is `util.time()`; a running frozen synth voice's reference is read first, before `set_own` can move its freq):
1. **The same owner and key held:** slot 1 plays as in 2; an extra slot is re-pitched and accented in place: `noteExpression` only when the note's expression differs from what the engine keeps for the slot, then `noteOn`. No slot is taken.
2. **Slot 1 free** (nothing in it, the own note parked, or the voice stopped): `set_own(pitch)`, `toggle_voice(voice)` when the voice is stopped, `noteExpression` on slot 0 when the note's expression differs from the engine's (never for a neutral note on a fresh or restarted voice), `noteOn` on slot 0 for a parked own note (the unpark) or on a frozen synth voice (the rate), then `retrigger`. With `quiet`, `set_own(pitch)` and, on a running voice, the same slot-0 `noteOn` when the own note was parked (its unpark, so Lua's record and the engine agree: not parked) or the voice is a frozen synth voice (its rate, ruling 18; a stopped voice gets it at the start, from the grain-restart hook); no start and no `retrigger`: the caller starts and accents the voice (the sequencer keeps its own order), and an expression that differs waits for the flush. A held slot 1 of another note loses it (the newest note wins); the old record goes before the start, so the grain-restart hook never plays it.
3. **A free extra slot under the cap and room in the pool:** the free slot among 2..cap whose last note ended longest ago (a never used one first; a tie, the lowest), so a tail has the longest time to fade: `noteExpression`, then `noteOn`.
4. **A free slot but the pool full:** the oldest held extra note on any voice that may be stolen (by `started`; a tie, the lowest voice, then slot) is cut, `noteOff(v2 - 1, slot2 - 1, 1)`, and forgotten; the new note starts as in 3. None that may be stolen: branch 6.
5. **No free slot under the cap:** the voice's oldest held extra note in slots 2..cap that may be stolen is replaced in place (`noteExpression`, `noteOn`, no `noteOff`). None: branch 6.
6. **Nothing can be stolen** (a cap of 1, a pool of 0, every candidate protected): slot 1 as in 2, whoever held it.

3.7's fields (design §3.5): `region = {start, length}` (fractions 0-1, a start below 1 and a length above 0), `to_end` (seconds above 0), `hold_to_end` and `own_only` (booleans) and `steals` (a function), each checked as the others are. `own_only` always takes slot 1 as in 2 (the same owner and key held in an extra slot is released first). The own note's region and the timed end: Must not break, above. `own_pitch(voice)` gives the voice's own pitch, a slice note's.

Branches 3 to 6 run only on a running voice: on a stopped voice every play is branch 1 or 2, slot 1. `quiet` changes nothing for an extra slot. A note with `extra_only = true` (a boolean, default false: a chord's further notes, ruling 32) takes only branch 1 or 3, on a running voice: never slot 1 by branch 2 or 6, never a steal; otherwise it is refused (nil, nothing sent or recorded).

### Release

- `release(voice, owner, key) -> boolean`: an extra note gets `noteOff(voice - 1, slot - 1, 0)` and its slot is free (ended now). The own note parks (`noteOff(voice - 1, 0, 0)`, the record kept as `parked`) when it asked to (`park`) and an extra note is held; otherwise it is forgotten and nothing is sent: the player decides whether the voice stops (MIDI) or its override ends (the sequencer). false when no such note is held.
- `release_owner(voice, owner)`: each of the owner's held notes, the extra ones first in slot order, then the own note (which so parks only under another owner's notes); a note held to its end (`hold_to_end`, a one-shot slice) is left to its time, as `release` leaves it, so latch's next chord and a step's chord ending never cut a slice still playing (the final review's I-1). `forget_owner` (a reset, a panic) still ends every one.
- `sync_own_region(voice)`: sends the own region as it is (a clear for none) as `regionStart` and `regionLength`: `set_own_region`'s send and every voice sync's (the slices record's `sync`), so this module is the own region's one sender (design §3.4, the final review's M-5).
- `forget_owner(voice, owner)` (ruling 40, a player's reset): the owner's held extra notes are released (`noteOff(…, 0)`), and its held own note is dropped from the records with nothing sent and nothing parked: the own note keeps sounding and is simply no longer the owner's. An own note the owner parked earlier stays parked under the other owners' notes and returns when the voice's last note ends (ruling 39). Another owner's notes and a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES` are left alone.
- `forget(voice, owner, key) -> boolean` (ruling 55): `forget_owner`'s way for one held note: an extra note is released (`noteOff(…, 0)`), the own note dropped from the records with nothing sent (it sounds on as nobody's); false when no such note is held. MIDI's `keeps_own` forgets a latched own note with it when a step comes.
- When any of these ends a voice's last held note under a parked own note, the own note returns (ruling 39, above).
- `release_all(voice)`: forgets every note, a parked own note included, and sends nothing: for a voice the engine has stopped. The voice-stop hook calls it.

### The rest of the interface

- `find(voice, owner, key) -> slot | nil` (held notes only); `held(voice)` (the voice's held notes, the own note included: the count MAIN will show; 0 for a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES`); `slots(voice)` (copies of `{slot, owner, key, pitch, held, parked}` for each used slot, in order); `pool_used()` (held extra notes on every voice).
- `pool()`: notes.data's value, else the running tier's default (`S.constants.NOTE_POOL[S.performance.tier]`: 4 on standard, 8 on high; standard's while the tier is unknown).
- `set_pool(n)`: a whole number, clamped to 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` (16), written to notes.data as `{version = 1, pool = n}` (the folder made first), then set; while more extra notes are held than `n`, the newest (by `started`) is released as `release` does, by its slot (so the loop always frees one); then `engine.setNotePool(n)`; true. false, with nothing set or sent, for anything but a whole number (a string, a fraction, NaN, ±inf) and for a write that fails (with one line, `strata: notes.data not saved: <why>`).
- `set_expression(voice, slot, values) -> boolean`: a held note's expression takes `values`' finite keys (clipped), keeps the rest, and the note is due for the flush; false (nothing marked) for an empty or parked slot or a bad index.
- `expression(voice, slot)` (ruling 36): a copy of the expression the slot's note holds, `{bend, amp, position, size, main}` (its first expression as clipped, then each `set_expression`'s, sent or still due), held or parked; nil for an empty slot or a bad index. `lib/expression.lua` seeds a note's record from it.
- `note_values(voice, pitch)`: `{note, freq, rate}`: `note` the pitch clamped to 0-127, `freq` `musicutil.note_num_to_freq(pitch)` clamped to the voice's `v<i>_freq` range (20-2000 Hz), `rate` `2 ^ ((pitch - 60) / 12)`, or on a frozen synth voice `2 ^ ((pitch - reference) / 12)`, clamped to 0.25-4. nil for a voice outside 1..`NUM_VOICES` or a pitch that is not a finite number.
- `freeze_reference(voice) -> note | nil`: a frozen synth voice's reference, the fractional MIDI note of `S.lib.engine_sync.engine_base_value("v<i>_freq")` when the running voice froze, or, since, when it last started or changed source. The hooks keep it (Registrations, below), never a read: the freeze-change hook takes it for a running voice and drops it at an unfreeze; the grain-restart hook, which runs after every `voiceOn` and every running voice's source change (a recall's included), drops it and takes a fresh one; the voice-stop hook drops it. No source-change hook is needed: outside a recall the freeze module's own source-change hook unfreezes a frozen voice first (the freeze-change hook drops the reference), a voice that is not frozen keeps none, a running voice's switch runs the grain-restart hook anyway. A stopped voice keeps none: asked (the start's sync asks before `voiceOn`, for `scaleNote`), it gives its freq's note now, which its start then takes, so the two agree. A running frozen synth voice found without one takes it then. nil for any other voice. `S.lib.variation.scale_note` returns it for a frozen synth voice, so no resend of `scaleNote` moves it (`docs/notes/lua/variation.md`).
- `stop_flush()`: the cleanup step's (below).
- 3.8 (package 2b): `take_extras(voice)` (the held extra notes without a region, each `{owner, key, pitch, velocity}` in slot order, cut and forgotten with no `ended` hook, the own note returning if it was parked under them) and `run_note_hooks(field, record)` (the note hooks, for `lib/arp.lua`'s held notes).

### The flush (design §6.4)

A hook of the LFO clock (`S.lib.lfos.on_step`), registered by the start step, so it runs after every hook registered at include time (2c's voice-wide step among them). `set_expression` marks a note due, appending it to the queue unless it already waits there (so a later mark keeps its place and its newest values). Each step sends, in queue order, one `noteExpression` per due note still held in its slot, at most `NOTES.sends_per_step`; the rest wait, ahead of anything marked later, so no note starves; a note that ended or was parked meanwhile is dropped. The step's count goes to `count_sends` (none when nothing went).

### The envelope times

`v<i>_attack` (0.005-30 s, exponential, `s`) and `v<i>_release` (0.01-30 s), the next two rows of the `notes` record after `note cap`, so VOICE's `notes` section lists `note cap`, `attack`, `release`. Each voice's default is its default voice type's `envelope` (3.8: the type records', `docs/notes/lua/types.md`, read at init; 3.7's `NOTES.envelope` is gone: `{4, 8} {5, 10} {3, 6} {8, 12} {2, 4} {0.01, 2} {3, 5}` for the seven, each its def's own time, compared with the def's `attackTime` and `releaseTime` defaults by name in any engine file by a test), so a voice sounds as in 3.4. A type change keeps the voice's attack and release; a running voice's sync sends them to the new type's synth. They are voice params, not grain params: the sender is `engine.setVoiceParam(v - 1, "attackTime" | "releaseTime", x)` through `add_voice_sender` (a running voice only). So they shape a synth voice's voice synths only (its own and its extra notes'): a buffer or input voice has none, and a frozen synth voice's notes are grain streams, whose defs keep the ASR's 0.1 s and `grainRelease` (2 s); a frozen voice's voice synths keep the times for its unfreeze. The user pages say so (the final review's minor 3). On a running synth voice the release is heard in full on an extra note's release and the own note's park, since the voice's grain synth runs on; at the voice's stop (`voiceOff`) its grain synth releases over `grainRelease`, 2 s, so the heard fade ends within about 2 s whatever `releaseTime` says (3.4's too: `docs/notes/engine/lifecycle.md`, Starting). `voiceOn` resets them, so a start sends both from the grain-restart hook (after `voiceOn`, with the synth params), and a running voice's sync sends them from the record's `sync`. They are scene fields (`scene_defaults` is a function of the voice giving that voice's own times, 3.7, checked at init since 3.8; `scene_ranges` the engine's). Which times a recall gets: a scene captured from running voices always holds each voice's values; a scene that lacks the `attack` or `release` key for a voice (a hand-edited or partial file) gets that voice's own default (`fill_scene`). No `randomize`: ALT+K2 on the section leaves them.

### The note cap

`v<i>_note_cap` (number, 1-`NOTE_SLOTS`, default 8, name `note cap`, shown as its number), a registered voice-params record `notes` with `voice_rows = {section = "notes", rows = {"note_cap"}, before = "variation"}`, no grain keys, no scene key of its own (PSET only), faders for `attack` and `release` (`fader = {{keys = {"attack", "release"}, rank = 4}}`, the last on a voice's fader pages, the groundwork before 3.8; the cap has none), and a layout state per voice (`v<i> note cap`, the cursor on its row) for VOICE's layout tests and renders. VOICE's ALT+K2 on its section does nothing (no draw). Lowering it under held notes keeps them (`lowered[voice]`, cleared once no used slot is past the cap): branches 3 and 5 only use slots under the new cap.

### Registrations

- The start step `notes_load` (`START_ORDER.notes_load`, 109, after the matrix's load): reads notes.data (parsed, never run). A version 1 file whose `pool` is a whole number in 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` sets the pool. No file leaves the tier's default with no line. Anything else (text that does not parse, another version, a bad or missing pool) leaves the default too, with one line: `strata: notes.data not used (<why>): the pool is the tier's default, <n>; kept as notes.data.bak` (or `; not moved: <why>`): the file is set aside (`set_aside(file, NOTES.backup_suffix)`, as `io.data` is; the groundwork's ruling 35), and one that can be neither used nor set aside is written over at the next `set_pool`. Then `engine.setNotePool(pool())` and the flush's hook (once).
- The cleanup step `notes` (`CLEANUP_ORDER.notes`, 119, before the LFO clock's 120): `stop_flush()`: nothing is due, and the hook and `set_expression` mark or send nothing after it.
- `S.register_voice_stop` (`docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`): `release_all(voice)`, slot 1's kept expression back to neutral, and the freeze reference dropped.
- `S.register_grain_restart` (after every `voiceOn`, the voice not yet marked running, and every running voice's source change, a recall's included): a fresh freeze reference. A recall's switch is settled at the recall's end instead (`recall_switched`, rulings 58 and 59, above). At a start only (the voice not yet marked running), on a frozen synth voice whose slot 1 holds a held note, `noteOn(v - 1, 0, note, freq, rate, velocity)` from it: its rate on the new own grain synth, for a quiet note whose caller starts the voice now. A running voice's source switch, a recall's or not, sends nothing: a source switch needs nothing from Lua (design §2.4), the switch is sent before the freeze module's hook unfreezes the voice, and slot 1's pitch was set for its old source. A stopped voice's switch runs no hook (and no source-change hook is registered, see `freeze_reference`), which is why a stopped voice keeps no reference.
- `S.register_freeze_change` (`docs/notes/lua/freeze.md`), for a synth voice only: at a freeze the reference is taken (for a running voice; a stopped one's start takes it), every extra note is cut (`noteOff(v - 1, slot - 1, 1)`, in slot order) and forgotten (their sound is in the frozen ring); at an unfreeze the reference goes and the records stand. Either way `scaleNote` is resent (`S.lib.variation.send_scale_note` with the freq's engine value: the reference while frozen, the freq's note after). A buffer or input voice's notes are streams already: nothing changes.
- The record `notes` (above).
- The note hooks (3.6, package 0a; `S.register_note_hook`, `docs/notes/lua/state-and-voices.md`): read at each call, run as "Must not break" says; the targets (3.6's MIDI out, ii and nb) mirror notes through them.
- The invariant `"notes"`: the pool is a whole number in 0..`NOTE_POOL.max` and the held extra notes never exceed it; every slot record is whole (its voice and slot, an owner and key, a finite pitch and start, a velocity in 0-1, held or parked and only slot 1 parked, a `quiet` flag, an expression in range); a voice's used slots are under its cap unless it was lowered under them; a stopped voice holds no extra note, and its slot 1 at most a held `quiet` note awaiting its caller's start; slot 1's holder is one owner, checked as each owner and key held at most once per voice (across all its slots); and a running voice with no held note keeps no parked own note (ruling 39).

Every change of a voice's held count (a play that takes or frees a slot, a release, a steal, a freeze's cuts, a stop) calls `update_voice_list()` and `request_redraw()`.

The constants it reads are `S.constants.NOTE_SLOTS` (8; the engine's `noteSlots`) and `S.constants.NOTE_POOL` (`max` 16, the engine's `notePoolMax`; `standard` 4 and `high` 8, each tier's default).
