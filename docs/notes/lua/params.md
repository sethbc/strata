## Must not break

- Param ids are unique and prefixed (`strata_fx`, ...). norns has built-ins such as `reverb`, and the stub's system params make a collision fail.
- A group's size `n` equals the number of params that follow it. Groups cannot nest; the test stub errors on a group opened inside another, or one left short.
- **A param that carries a note's pitch has step 0** (`freq`, `grain_pitch`, `buf_rate`): a step rounds the value a MIDI or sequenced note sets, so its own note would play out of tune with its chord's extra notes, which get the exact rate from `S.lib.notes.note_values` (#679). A turn's size is the spec's quantum (default 0.01 of the raw range), never its step, so a step of 0 changes no turn, fader or arc ring; the display is norns' two-decimal default.
- The PARAMS menu order is the order of the `S.registry.params` records, and `init` runs each record's `add` in that order.
- A control param that reaches the engine stores its value and calls `send_param_to_engine` from its action, with a sender in `S.engine_senders` (`docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`).
- The actions of every param a randomize may set call `note_param_edit(param_id)`, so an edit ends the randomize undo (`docs/notes/lua/mutation.md`): the voice params `randomize_voice` sets (the synthesis params, `buf_pos`, `buf_rate`, `buf_scan`, `grain_size`, `grain_pitch`), the other grain and crossmod params a page randomize draws (`grain_density`, `grain_spread`, `mod_speed`, `mod_amp_amt_<n>`, `mod_freq_amt_<n>`), `master_density`, `drift_amount` and the master filter's, delay's and reverb's (`filter_type`, `filter_freq`, `filter_res`, `delay_time`, `delay_feedback`, `delay_mix`, `reverb_mix`, `reverb_size`, `reverb_damp`; #557; 3.8's `delay_to_reverb`, `lib/voice_mix.lua`, and shimmer's three, `lib/shimmer.lua`), and every per-voice param (`add_voice_param`'s action, the built-in ones' included).
- `redraw_on_param_changes()` runs once, after the last param is added. A param added, or an action set with `params:set_action`, after it runs requests no redraws.
- The tempo handler is installed once (never chaining to itself) and given back only while the script's is still installed.
- **What is timed from the tempo right after a change reads `S.lib.params_setup.beat_seconds_now()`, and the tempo shown reads `clock_tempo_now()`**, never `clock.get_tempo()` or `clock.get_beat_sec()` alone (#567): `beat_seconds_now` times a synced take's length and delay (`lib/record.lua`) and so BUFFER's `len` field (through `take_length`); `clock_tempo_now` is the 256's tempo flash. They differ on Link: timing reads the session's own tempo there, the flash the param a key set. A clocked LFO's rate (`lib/lfos.lua`) reads `beat_seconds_now` too, so a tempo change reaches an engine-run LFO at once.
- The script never bangs its params, so Lua defaults are what the engine plays, and the filter and delay SynthDef defaults must equal the Lua param defaults (a test checks).
- The registries (`S.registry.voice_params`, `fx_params`, `param_filters`, `source_change`) are read at init, inside a params record's `add`, never at include time, so a module that registers can be included before or after this one.
- Eight registries are closed once init begins to read them (`S.close_registries()`, run by the first params record, `performance`): `voice_params`, `scene_fields`, `lfo_destinations`, `fx_params`, `start`, `menu_close` and 3.4's `scene_blocks` and `pset_hooks`. A register function raises after that, naming the registry (#384), so a late record is never silently ignored. Register at include time. A record with a field outside the documented set is refused too, naming the field (#385).
- **The PSET actions run every PSET hook, each in its own `pcall`** (3.4): `params.action_read`, `action_write` and `action_delete` are installed once, by the master record (`pset.install_actions`), and a read runs `M.migrate_read_pset` first, then the hooks. A hook that raises prints `pset hook <name>: <error>` and never stops the next hook or the PSET's own read, write or delete. No other code sets the three actions.
- **The seven 3.4 menu records each add their module's group** (`macros`, `morph`, `scatter`, `matrix`, `input`, `gestures`, `seq`, after `lfos`): each calls `S.lib.<module>.add_params()`, read at init (every module is loaded by then). `seq`'s is the sequencer's, which adds `strata_seq` and calls `S.lib.quantize.add_params()` for the quantizer's params.
- **3.6's five menu records add their group only for a loaded module** (`out midi`, `crow`, `out ii`, `out nb`, `tape`, between `gestures` and `seq`): each calls `S.lib.<module>.add_params()` (`io_midi_out`, `io_crow`, `io_ii`, `io_nb`, `io_tape`) only when `S.lib.<module>` exists, so a branch without the module adds nothing.
- **Every per-voice param is a record's** (the groundwork before 3.8): the built-in `synth`, `grain` and `crossmod` records (`lib/voices.lua`, registered first, so they lead the menu, the sends, the scene keys and the faders) and every module's. A record's `add` (with its `add_first`, if any) adds exactly its `count` for the voice (a number, or a function of the voice; an error names the record and the voice otherwise), and every key its `grain_keys`, `voice_keys`, `scene_defaults`, `scene_keys`, `discrete` and `voice_rows.rows` name is one of the params it added with `add_voice_param`. Every key a `fader` group gives for a voice is a param some record added for it, or init errors naming the record.
- **A record names what MAIN's randomize, the mutations and the fader pages reach** (`main_randomize`, `mutate`, `fader` groups); nothing else does. The built-in records hold 3.7's sets (MAIN's randomize: a synth source's synth keys but `amp`, a buffer source's buffer params, then grain size and pitch; the mutations: the synth keys but `amp` and `pan`, or the buffer params, then grain size, pitch and spread; never the source, envelope, mode or crossmod). Of the module records only the voice mix (rank 1), loops (rank 2, buffer voices), variation's jitters (rank 3) and the notes' attack and release (rank 4) have faders; none has `main_randomize` or `mutate`. A page randomize of their own VOICE section, through their record's `randomize` draws, is separate (#557). Every registered voice param (freeze, variation, loops, history, the voice mix, the sequencer's `seq`) defaults to 3.1's sound, so a voice that never sets one plays as it did in 3.1.
- **`grain_mode`, `mod_source_<n>` and 3.8's `grain_cloud` send through `send_param_to_engine`** (ruling 14), as every `add_voice_param` param does: counted by the send budget and deferrable; the messages are 3.7's.
- The fx group's size is its 11 params plus every registered fx record's `count`, and the registered fx params follow tape drift.
- Every number param a params record adds (Strata's own, never norns' system params) steps whole numbers through its own `delta` (#534): norns' PARAMS menu turns a param by `d / 20` while K3 is held, and norns' number param adds that unrounded, so without it `midi_device` raised and the mod sources, the sequence length and `record_beats` held fractions. `register_params` replaces each one's `delta` after its record's `add`, with one that rounds `d` away from 0 as norns' option param does; the same loop lists each one in `pset.number_params` for the PSET read hook (#554).
- A filtered synthesis param's own `delta` marks a turn (`S.lib.params_setup.turn_on_own_delta`, #318): norns' PARAMS menu turns E3 with the param object's `delta`, which never passes `delta_with_detent`. Only the synth record's `add` calls it (ruling 15): a registered filtered key (expression's press and slide targets, the loop's start and length) keeps norns' own `delta`, so a menu turn of `press_target` never steps over `position`.
- **A PSET loads every param it has no line for at its default** (#628): `M.migrate_read_pset` first sets each param a params record added (`pset.record_params`, collected by `register_params`: Strata's own, never norns' system params) that norns saves and the file names no line for (`pset.named_ids`, found as norns' `params:read` finds them, each line through `M.pset_line(line)`, the one reader of a PSET line (a line's trailing `\r` dropped, so a file with CRLF lines reads its ids and values as norns' own read does but for that `\r`), which `M.pset_value` and 3.8's factory sets' `parse_params` use too) to its default (`M.load_default(param)`, 3.8's name for it, exported for the factory sets' load, which defaults a set's missing lines with it: a control's controlspec default, a number's, option's, taper's or non-trigger binary's `default`; a param with save off, a trigger, separator, group or file param is left alone, and a text param but one given a text default with `S.lib.params_setup.set_text_default(id, text)`, 3.8: the tuning's `tuning_name` and `tuning_tones`, so a PSET from 3.7 comes back in twelve equal steps; `M.read_defaulted(id)` says whether the last read gave id its default and that changed it (each read starts it afresh: a test, #842), and `M.pset_value(text, id)` reads a file's line for an id, for the tuning's read hook), through `params:set` with the read's `silent`, and prints one line counting them. A PSET saved before a param existed has no line for it, so the param loads its default instead of keeping the session's value. norns writes a line for every saved param, so a PSET saved now is untouched. It runs before the PSET hooks, so a hook that drops pending work (the macros' and morph's applies) drops what the defaults started.
- **A PSET has no format** (3.7, design decision 21): no param records when a PSET was saved, and nothing in a read is keyed on it. 3.3 to 3.6 saved a hidden `strata_pset_format` line; norns' `params:read` skips a line whose id is no param, so such a PSET loads as written, its Pulse `res` included (3.7 deleted the mirror of a PSET saved before 3.3). The rounding goes through `params:set` with the read's `silent`. A test reads `\strataPulse`'s `RLPF` line from the engine source (comments stripped) and fails unless its mirror is `PULSE_RES.mirror` and its clip the param's range, so neither side can change alone. No code converts a PSET by when it was saved.
- **A PSET's number params load whole** (#554): after the read the hook rounds every number param a params record added (`pset.number_params`, collected by `register_params`: Strata's own and the registered voice params, never norns' system params) to its step, a whole number (`util.round`). A non-silent read runs each param's action with the file's value first (norns sets each line through the param's own `set`), so a number param whose action reaches the engine makes its value whole in the action, through `pset.whole(param_id, x)`, before storing or sending it, and writes a fraction back silently (the param shows what is stored, and the hook has nothing left to round or resend): the crossmod sources (`setModSource` carries an `"i"`, which matron casts: 0.05 - 1 was voice 1) and every number param `add_voice_param` builds (the crossmod sources among them, and the custom intervals, sent with `setGrainParam`). A new number param whose action reaches the engine does the same. The others (`tempo`, `scene_sequence_length`, `midi_device`, `record_beats`) reach no engine command with their value, and the hook rounds them.
- **Every param 3.7.0 has keeps its id, type, name, default, spec and place but for a named difference** (3.8's package 0g): `test/unit/params_setup_test.lua` compares a fresh start's params, in order, with `test/fixtures/3.7.0-params.lua` (recorded from the tag; it stays 3.7.0's), field by field (`PARAM_FIXTURE_FIELDS`). Each difference made on purpose is an entry of `PARAM_FIXTURE_ALLOWED`, added by the package that makes it in the same commit, as a registration line in a block of its own (`-- <id>: <what> (design §n)`, every `why` beginning `"<id>: "`). The kinds: `removed` (3.7.0's param is gone), `added` (a new param directly `after` an id, a 3.7.0 one or an earlier added one, several after one id in the list's order; its `record` gives `t`, `name` and any other fields, and only those, its id and its place are compared, so a package adding many params from one table may give `{t, name}` and pin the specs in its own tests), `default` (with the formatted `string` at it when the param has a formatter), `name`, `range` (`min`, `max` or both, of a control or number), `options`, and `group_size` (`add = <k>`, several entries for one group summing, the only way a group several packages grow is sized, as `strata_fx` is: 1a's 1, 1b's 2, 2a's 3; or `n = <count>`, kept for 2b's LFO groups and never beside `add`). An `id` or `after` holding `<v>` (a voice's number) or `<v0>` (its engine index) stands for one entry a voice. One id carries one entry of each kind but `add`; `removed` stands alone but beside an `added` of the same id (a move). An entry for an id 3.7.0 lacks (but `added`), an `added` 3.7.0 id with no `removed`, an `after` no record has, a kind that does not fit the param's type, an unknown kind or a missing `why` raises. The self-tests ("the fixture comparison honors ...") make each kind the only difference, leave each entry out and try each misuse.
- `S.performance` is never the constant `S.constants.STANDARD_PERFORMANCE` itself (a copy, or the table `lib/performance.lua` reads at init). A tier-dependent spec reads it when its param is added (`record_duration`'s maximum is `S.performance.buffer_seconds`).

## Parameter system

Every adjustable value is a norns param:
1. Defined in its `S.registry.params` record with `params:add{}`, run from `init()`.
2. With an `action` function that updates the engine.
3. Read with `params:get("id")`, set with `params:set("id", value)`.
4. Grouped with `params:add_separator()`, or `params:add_group(id, name, n)` for a collapsible section (`strata_fx`, `strata_lfo_<n>` per LFO, `strata_buffers`, `strata_midi`, `strata_zones`).

Menu order:
- `strata` (master density, tempo), `strata_mutations` (named `mutations`), `strata_scenes` (named `scenes`);
- the fx, LFO 1-6, input (`strata_input`, `lib/input_level.lua`) and buffers groups, then the `strata_record` and `strata_positions` groups, the `midi` group (6 params since 3.5: `lib/mpe.lua`'s `add_midi_params` adds the last three), 3.5's `zones` group (`strata_zones`, 21, `S.lib.mpe.add_params`) and the `scale` section;
- then each voice's records' params: under `voice <i>: <name>` every record's `add_first` params, then the `synth` record's (no separator of its own), the `grain` record's under `voice <i> granular`, the `crossmod` record's under `voice <i> cross-modulation`, and every module record's under one `voice <i> extras`.

## Registries

`lib/state.lua` holds the registries and their register functions; each checks its argument and refuses a duplicate name. This module reads four of them at init (scenes, the LFOs and VOICE read the rest: `docs/notes/lua/scenes.md`, `docs/notes/lua/lfos.md`, `docs/notes/lua/ui/field-list-and-cursors.md`).

**Voice params** (`S.register_voice_params(record)`, `S.registry.voice_params`). Every per-voice param belongs to one record (the fourteen `v<i>_out_*` and `v<i>_zone*` rig params are the exception, in their own groups). `lib/voices.lua` registers the three built-in records first: `synth` (3.8: `v<i>_type` from its `add_first`, then every voice type's synth key, each sent only while the voice's type has it, `reaches`; `docs/notes/lua/types.md`), `grain` (`source`, a plain option that is no value, and the eight grain and buffer params; 3.8's window shape and skew, `lib/grain.lua`'s keys, sit in its fader group) and `crossmod` (three slots of source, amp mod and freq mod, then `mod_speed`). A module's record:

```lua
{ name = "freeze", count = 2, add = function(voice_idx) ... end,  -- count: a number or fn(voice_idx)
  add_first = nil,             -- optional fn(voice_idx): params a PSET must read before the synth params
  grain_keys = { freeze = "freeze", freeze_mode = { name = "freezeMode", value = function(x) return x - 1 end } },
  voice_keys = nil,            -- optional {key = name | {name, value, carry = false} | {send = fn(voice_idx, x), carry = false}}, or fn(voice_idx)
  send_order = nil,            -- optional: every grain and voice key once, in send order (default sorted)
  separator = nil,             -- the text after "voice <i> " (nil: "extras"; false: none)
  sync = nil,                  -- optional fn(voice_idx): the voice sync's sends after grain_keys (setGrainParam only)
  scene_defaults = { freeze = 0, freeze_mode = 1 },   -- each the param's default; or fn(voice_idx) -> that table (checked at init)
  scene_keys = nil,            -- 3.8, in place of scene_defaults: true | {key, ...} | fn(voice_idx, type_key), defaults param_default
  reaches = nil,               -- 3.8: optional fn(voice_idx, key) -> boolean: whether the key's sender reaches the engine now
  synth_keys = nil,            -- 3.8: true on a scene_keys record whose keys are the voice type's synth keys (the synth record alone)
  discrete = { freeze = {0, 1}, freeze_mode = {1, 2} }, -- whole range; the other scene keys crossfade
  voice_rows = { section = "mix", rows = {"freeze", "freeze_mode"}, when = nil, before = nil },  -- rows may be fn(voice_idx);
                               -- swaps = {[key] = fn(voice_idx) -> key | nil} replaces a row of the section (3.8)
  layout_states = nil,         -- optional {{name, setup = fn(context)}}
  normalize_scene = nil,       -- optional fn(values): a loaded scene voice's values made consistent (fill_scene)
  fader = false,               -- or groups {keys = list | fn(voice_idx), when = fn(voice_idx), rank = number}
  main_randomize = nil,        -- groups {keys, when, draw = fn(voice_idx, key, param_id)}: MAIN's randomize
  mutate = nil }               -- groups {keys, when, chance = 0-1, scale, warped = true | false}: the mutations
```

- `add_voice_params(i)` checks every record whose `count`, `voice_keys` or `scene_defaults` is a function for the voice (3.8, ruling 19: `S.check_voice_params`), adds the `voice <i>: <name>` separator (the voice's own type's name; the type's action renames it), every record's `add_first` (the synth record's `v<i>_type` first) (ahead of the synth params, because norns reads a PSET line by line in param order and a filter on a built-in param may read them: variation's `quantize`, read by the `freq` filter, #125), then each record's `add(i)` in registration order. A record whose `separator` differs from the record's before it gets `params:add_separator("voice <i> <separator>")` first (nil means `extras`, false none), outside its `count`: the synth record has none, grain `granular`, crossmod `cross-modulation`, and the module records share one `extras`. `add` and `add_first` add with `add_voice_param`, and together they add exactly `count` params.
- `add` calls `S.lib.params_setup.add_voice_param(voice_idx, record, key, param)` for each param: `param` is a `params:add` table without an `id` (its own `action`, if any, runs last: the grain record's `buf_pos` and `grain_pitch` set the drift's centers there). The param's id is `v<i>_<key>`. Its action:
  1. makes a number param's value whole (`pset.whole`, #554);
  2. runs `S.registry.param_filters[key]` (a changed value is written back with `params:set(id, v, true)`);
  3. stores the value in `S.voices[i].values[key]` (the voice's one table, built fresh at each script load);
  4. calls `note_param_edit` and `send_param_to_engine`;
  5. runs the param's own action.
- A key in `grain_keys` gets an `S.engine_senders` entry: `setGrainParam` with the engine name (and `value(x)`, if given), only while the voice runs; a key of the voice's `voice_keys` likewise `setVoiceParam`, or the entry's own `send(voice_idx, x)`. `sync_voice_values_to_engine` sends, per record, its voice keys, then its grain keys and its `sync` (`voice_engine_parts`, `docs/notes/lua/engine-sync.md`). A start sends every record's grain keys and `sync` before `engine.voiceOn`, so a record's `sync` sends grain params only (`setGrainParam`): `voiceOn` resets the voice-level controls, and a test fails on anything else before it (#325). The variation record's (the intervals and `scaleNote`) and the bookmarks record's (`scrubRate` 0 and `scanHold` 0) do. The voice keys follow `voiceOn`. `carry = false` sends the stored value at a start, never the param's override and offsets: the grain mode, 3.8's grain cloud and the ten crossmod keys.
- `fader_param_ids(i)` (`lib/grid/faders.lua`) takes every record's fader groups whose `when` holds for the voice, sorted by `rank` (default 0) and, within a rank, in registration order: 3.7's faders (the built-ins, rank 0), then the voice filter and sends, a buffer voice's loop, the jitters, attack and release.

**Param filters** (`S.register_param_filter(key, fn)`, one per key). `fn(voice_idx, value, previous, turned) -> value` runs in the action of every `v<i>_<key>` voice param, the synthesis params (`param_order`) as well as registered ones, before the value is stored. `previous` is the value stored before; `turned` is true when the set is a turn of this param (`S.turning_param == id`: an encoder detent or arc tick through `delta_with_detent`, a mutation step, or the param's own `delta`, #318; `docs/notes/lua/ui/detents-and-fine.md`). A synthesis param whose key has a filter gets a `delta` that marks the turn (`turn_on_own_delta`), since norns' PARAMS menu turns E3 with the param object's `delta` and `params:delta` calls it too. 3.2's quantized `freq` uses it; the loop filters ignore `turned`.

**Source change hooks** (`S.register_source_change(fn)`). `fn(voice_idx, source)` runs in every `v<i>_source` action right after the source is stored and sent, before the voice's sync and the grid redraw (so the sync sends what the hooks set: a switch never sends the old freeze first), except while `S.scene_recall_in_progress`: a recall sets every value itself. 3.2's freeze clears itself through one.

**Fx params** (`S.register_fx_params({name, count, add})`). The fx group's size is 11 plus every record's `count`; init runs each `add()` after tape drift, in registration order, and checks the count.

**PSET hooks** (`S.register_pset_hook({name, write = fn(path_base, number), read = fn(path_base, number, pset_path, silent), delete = fn(path_base, number)})`, each function optional; 3.4, for the per-PSET side files of the macros, the matrix and the recorders; 3.6's `io_keyed`, whose read puts each keyed choice on the key the file holds, `docs/notes/lua/io/keyed.md`). The master record installs norns' three PSET actions (`lua/core/paramset.lua`), which run the hooks in registration order:
- `params.action_read = function(filename, silent, number)`: `M.migrate_read_pset(filename, silent)` first, then every hook's `read(path_base, number, filename, silent)` (a hook that sets params passes `silent` on);
- `params.action_write = function(filename, name, number)`: every hook's `write(path_base, number)`, after norns has written the PSET;
- `params.action_delete = function(filename, name, number)`: every hook's `delete(path_base, number)`, after norns has deleted it.
- 3.8's factory sets' load (`docs/notes/lua/factory.md`) also runs six of the hooks' `read`, by name (`scenes`, `macros`, `morph`, `gestures`, `sequences`, `matrix`, in registry order), with the set's `path_base` (`<script>/factory/<key>/set`), `number` nil, `pset_path` its `set.params` and `silent` false, inside `S.lib.side_files.read_only(fn)`; the PSET actions themselves are untouched, and the `tuning`, `scatter` and io hooks are not run there.
- `path_base` is `filename` without its `.pset` (`<data>/strata/strata-01` for PSET 1). `number` is what norns passes: the PSET's two-digit number (`"01"`) for a PSET the PARAMS menu writes, reads or deletes, nil for one a script reads or writes by path. Each hook runs in its own `pcall`: one that raises prints `pset hook <name>: <error>`, and the next still runs.
- **The read convention** (#898): norns' `params:read` sets the PSET's lines one by one in file order, each through its param's action unless the read is silent, and then calls `params.action_read`, all in one call with no wait between. So a clock that a param's action starts during a read begins with `clock.sleep(0)` and does nothing until the next scheduler tick, when the read and every hook have returned: the scene sequencer's (`scene_sequencer_enabled`, `lib/scenes.lua`), morph's apply clock (`morph`, `morph_y`, `lib/morph.lua`) and the macros' apply clock (`lib/macros.lua`). A hook can therefore retract what the read started before it runs: the macros' hook drops every pending apply before it reads its file (`cancel_pending`, which stops the apply clock), and morph's stops its clock and forgets what it applied, so neither applies over the PSET's values; the scene sequencer is not retracted, and recalls position 1 at that tick with the PSET's transition time, duration and length in place (`docs/notes/lua/scenes/sequencer.md`). The hooks run in registration order, which is the include order (`strata.lua`): `scenes`, `tuning`, `macros`, `scatter`, `morph`, `gestures`, `sequences`, `matrix`, `io_keyed`, `io_midi_out`, `io_nb`, `io_tape`, `voice_presets`. A hook that relies on another's having run registers after it (the scenes' before morph's, which forgets its plan once the slots change: `docs/notes/lua/scenes/pset.md`), and every hook runs after `M.migrate_read_pset`, so it sees the defaulted and rounded params.

**3.4's menu records** (package 0a, design §0.1): seven records after `lfos`, in menu order, `macros`, `morph`, `scatter`, `matrix`, `input`, `gestures` and `seq`, each calling its module's `add_params()` (`S.lib.macros`, `S.lib.morph`, `S.lib.scatter`, `S.lib.matrix`, `S.lib.input_level`, `S.lib.gestures`, `S.lib.sequencer`). `strata_seq` is shared (plan item 11): the sequencer's `add_params` adds the group, 6 + `S.lib.quantize.PARAM_COUNT` params, and calls `S.lib.quantize.add_params()` for its fifth (`docs/notes/lua/quantize.md`). `macros` adds `strata_macros` (16, `docs/notes/lua/macros.md`), `scatter` adds `strata_scatter` (8, `docs/notes/lua/scatter.md`) and `gestures` adds `strata_gestures` (8, `docs/notes/lua/gestures.md`).

**3.6's menu records** (package 0a, design §0.1, §13): five records after `gestures`, in menu order, `{"out midi", "io_midi_out"}`, `{"crow", "io_crow"}`, `{"out ii", "io_ii"}`, `{"out nb", "io_nb"}` and `{"tape", "io_tape"}`, each calling `S.lib[module].add_params()` only when `S.lib[module]` exists (each module's package adds its own include). The groups they add are `strata_out_midi`, `strata_crow`, `strata_out_ii`, `strata_out_nb` and `strata_tape`, all PSET-only.

**`S.performance`** (`{tier, buffer_seconds, max_grains, history_seconds}`). This module's constructor sets it to a copy of `S.constants.STANDARD_PERFORMANCE` (standard: 16 s buffers, 128 grains, 16 s of input history). lib/performance.lua fills it at init (`read_active`), from the `"performance"` params record, which runs before any param is added ([performance.md](performance.md)). Every tier-dependent spec reads it as its param is added: `record_duration` goes from 0.5 s to `S.performance.buffer_seconds`.

## Tempo

- The script's `tempo` param is initialized from `clock_tempo`, with its range, read with `param_range("clock_tempo")`: 1-300, so a scene captured at any clock tempo recalls it exactly.
- Its action sets `clock_tempo`. Grid tempo buttons adjust `tempo`. The pattern clock only syncs (`docs/notes/lua/sequencer.md`), so external clock sources still work.
- `install_tempo_change_handler()` (from `init`, in the master params record) installs the global `follow_clock_tempo` as `clock.tempo_change_handler`.
  - It keeps the previous handler in `previous_tempo_change_handler`, a local of the module (#394), and calls it after its own work.
  - Installing again is a no-op, so the handler never chains to itself.
- `restore_tempo_change_handler()` (from `cleanup()`, step 10) puts the previous handler back only while the script's is still installed, so a handler installed after it stays.
- A tempo set elsewhere (PARAMETERS > CLOCK, an external source) is thus mirrored into `tempo` silently, clamped to the param's range (an external source's tempo reaches the handler unclamped).
- So scenes and grid buttons always start from the playing tempo.
- `beat_seconds_now()`, for timing (take lengths, sync delays), is a beat's length in seconds from now on (#567): `60 / params:get("clock_tempo")` when `params:string("clock_source")` is `internal`, otherwise `60 / clock.get_tempo()`.
  - On the internal clock `clock_tempo`'s action sets the clock at once, while `clock.get_tempo()` lags: matron's clock thread publishes a tempo from its tick after next (one tick at the old tempo plus one at the new, 1/24 beat each: about 42 ms at 120 BPM, about 0.9 s going to 3 BPM).
  - On Link, MIDI and crow the param is a whole BPM (an `add_number`) that norns' clock refreshes from the clock's tempo only once a second (`lua/core/clock.lua`): a fractional Link session would be timed off on every pass (16 beats of a 123.45 BPM session timed at the param's 123 are 29 ms too long), and a peer's or an external clock's change would be stale for up to 1 s. So timing reads the clock there.
- `clock_tempo_now()`, for showing the tempo (the 256's flash), is `params:get("clock_tempo")` on `internal` and `link`, the tempo a key set, otherwise `clock.get_tempo()` (MIDI and crow ignore the param).

## Registering params

- **Registering a param group**: one `register_params(name, add)` call in `lib/params_setup.lua`, at its place in the PARAMS menu (the registration order is the menu order). Keep the group's body in the owning module where you can, and call it from `add` (as the "lfos" record calls `add_lfo_params`).
- **Registering per-voice params** (3.2's freeze, variation, loops, history, voice filter and sends; the built-in synth, grain and crossmod, `lib/voices.lua`): one `S.register_voice_params(record)` call in the owning module's constructor; nothing central lists the params. The record is `{name, count, add = fn(voice_idx), add_first = fn(voice_idx), grain_keys, voice_keys, send_order, separator, sync, scene_defaults, scene_keys, synth_keys, reaches, discrete, voice_rows = {section, rows, when, before}, layout_states, normalize_scene = fn(values), fader, main_randomize, mutate, randomize = {key = fn(voice_idx) -> value}}` (Voice params, above; `randomize`, optional: the keys a press of ALT+K2 on their VOICE section randomizes, each with its draw, called with the voice's index, #557). `add` adds exactly `count` params, each with `S.lib.params_setup.add_voice_param(voice_idx, record, key, param)` (id `v<i>_<key>`, value in `S.voices[i].values[key]`); a module's follow the voice's cross-modulation params under a `voice <i> extras` separator, except the optional `add_first`'s, which come ahead of the voice's synthesis params: norns reads a PSET in param order, so a param that a built-in param's filter reads must load first (variation's `quantize` before `freq`, #125). Its grain and voice keys reach the engine through their senders and the voice sync, its scene keys are captured, crossfaded (or, `discrete`, set at the end after the source), validated and migrated (`normalize_scene`, if any, makes a loaded scene's values consistent), its rows join the VOICE page (a binary param is a `"binary"` row) and its `layout_states` VOICE's. A param reaches MAIN's randomize, the mutations or the fader pages only through its record's `main_randomize`, `mutate` or `fader` groups (a fader is one line: `{keys = {...}, rank = n}`); a page randomize of its own section (`randomize`) is separate.
- **Registering the other shared spots**: `S.register_scene_field({id, default, discrete, set})` (a global scene field, read after `SCENE_GLOBAL_PARAMS`; `set = fn(value)`, optional, 3.4, is what a recall, a transition step and `apply_entries` call in place of `params:set`), `S.register_pset_hook({name, write, read, delete})` (above), `S.register_source_change(fn(voice_idx, source))` (runs in each `v<i>_source` action once the source is stored and sent, before the voice's sync, never during a scene recall), `S.register_param_filter(key, fn(voice_idx, value, previous, turned) -> value)` (one per key; every `v<i>_<key>` action applies it before storing), `S.register_lfo_destination(label, suffix)` (`v<i> <label>`, in its voice's group after the built-in ones, by registration; an LFO keeps its destination by key) and `S.register_fx_params({name, count, add})` (the fx group grows by `count`; its params follow the 11). Each is one line in the owning module.
