## Must not break

- **The matrix adds offsets and never sets a destination param**: like the LFOs it modulates around the stored value (or a step lock's base override, `engine_base_value`), and no routing ever calls `params:set` on its destination. The only params it sets are its own: a depth (the 25 % rule of `edit_routing`, `clear`) and an on/off (`clear`).
- **One sum and one clamp per destination**: a Lua destination's routings sum into one extra offset, `S.lib.engine_sync.set_extra_offset("matrix", id, sum)` (the sum of `S.lib.lfos.offset_for(id, m)`, the LFOs' home routings on it included), which `with_lfo_offsets` adds to the macros' LFO extras and clamps once to the param's range: +100 % and −100 % cancel instead of clamping each other.
- **Fast destinations only through `setFastOffset`** (design §10.2, which binds over §13's test lines: ruling 62): the five fast params (`S.lib.lfos.fast_destination`: a voice's `grain_pitch`, `buf_pos`, `grain_size`, `filter_freq` and `amp`) never get a `setGrainParam` or a Lua offset from the matrix: their routings' sum and the macros' fast extras (`set_fast_extra`) go to the engine as `engine.setFastOffset(voice - 1, 0, destination, sum)`, the sum clamped to −2..2 half ranges in Lua (the engine clips to the same, but the stub accepts any finite offset), so one clip applies. The engine adds it to the engine LFOs and scatter on the bus.
- **Every fast send counts against the send budget** (ruling 65): each fast channel (voice, destination) is one deferrable id, its key `matrix fast <voice> <destination>` (no param id holds a space, and none starts `v<i>_`, so a voice sync's `forget_sent_voice_values` never touches it), whose engine sender (`S.engine_senders[key]`, registered at include time for the 35 channels) sends `setFastOffset`. The round robin's send is `S.lib.engine_sync.send_engine_value(key, sum)`, inside `deferrable`: counted in `ENGINE_BUDGET`'s 150 per 100 ms, past it waiting as the key's one pending value (the newest sum winning) and going out first in the next window. The value the engine last got is `S.engine_param_values[key]`, and the one it has or will have `expected_value(key)`. Never `engine.setFastOffset` straight from the step.
- **An LFO's home routing it does not own is summed; one it owns is not** (the groundwork before 3.8, design §4): `read_home(n)` adds `S.lib.lfos.home_m(n)` (nil while the LFO is off or at depth 0) to the param its home resolves to while the home's `status` is `summed`, never while it is `owned` (`S.lib.lfos.place(n)` `engine` or `direct`): a fast home is the LFO's engine node, a macro the LFO's own hand-over. A home on `v<i>_main` is never owned, so on a buffer or input voice it reaches its fast param through `setFastOffset`, as an R routing to it does (ruling 16). A home's read is contained under `matrix home <n>`. So an LFO off, at depth 0 or aimed elsewhere has its Lua destination given back at the next step, through the round robin (ruling 17), and a voice start or sync carries a Lua home's offset at once, as any extra offset (ruling 18).
- **An envelope's routing to a fast destination is not summed** (3.7, design §7.3): `read_routing` sums only a routing whose `status(k)` is `summed`, so it skips one that is `owned` (`S.lib.envelopes.owns(k)` reads that same answer), whose engine node plays it (`docs/notes/lua/envelopes.md`), so it never also reaches `setFastOffset`. It stays a routing in every other way: its depth and on/off, its scene block entry, its files, `uses_source` and `affects`. An envelope to a Lua destination is summed from Lua's model as any source.
- **Destinations never include macros, LFO params or matrix depths** (nor the morph): `destination_ids()` is the LFO destination list without `none` and the global `macro <m>` entries, filtered again by `MATRIX.never`, so no source's value can depend on a routing. `edit_routing` and learn take only these ids; a macro may target a matrix depth (Question 38), and an LFO on a macro leaves that target alone (`lib/macros.lua`).
- **Every send goes through the LFOs' round robin** (`S.lib.lfos.mark_due`): at most `LFO.sends_per_step` (12) a step for the LFOs and the matrix together, each inside `deferrable`. A Lua destination's key is its param id, the LFOs' key for it too, so one send carries every layer. A fast destination's key is its channel's.
- **A destination is resent when its value moved past `LFO.send_threshold` from `S.lib.engine_sync.expected_value` of its key** (#647: the value the engine has or will have, the one waiting in the send budget when one waits, whoever handed it, else the one the engine last got), never from the matrix's own last sum or a record of its own: the budget's owner is the one answer to what was handed to it, as for the LFOs. A Lua destination compares along its warp, unrounded: a value back at the delivered one while another waits in the budget (the matrix's own, an LFO's or a deferrable writer's, a macro apply's, morph's, a gesture's playback) is sent, so it replaces the waiting one; what the engine has forgotten (a voice sync forgets the voice's values and drops their waiting ones) reads nil, and the next step sends again (a voice sync and a start resend an offset param themselves, `resend_modulated_voice_params`). A fast destination compares its clamped sum, in half ranges, with its channel key's (0 before any send); a voice start or sync resends no unmoved channel (the offset node is persistent and keeps its values, `docs/notes/engine/modulation.md`, and no key starts `v<i>_`). **A destination whose sum returns to 0 is sent once more**: a Lua one gives its extra offset up and sends its base with the other layers' offsets, a fast one sends 0, whatever the size of its last offset, and also while a nonzero sum still waits in the budget (its key's expected value), so a stale sum never lands after the routing stopped. **An exact 0 is sent once, not every step** (#760): a Lua destination whose routings still play but sum to exactly 0 (+100 % and −100 % from one source, or an LFO's home and an R routing that cancel) holds no offset; it is marked due once as its sum returns to 0, then only when its value moves past the threshold from `expected_value` (another layer moves it, or the engine forgot it: a voice sync), as any destination is. One that starts at 0 (never held) is sent only if the engine lacks its value. A fast channel stays tracked until its sum and its key's expected value are both 0.
- **Each routing and each destination is contained** (#517): every routing's read (its source's value, its destination's resolution) and every destination's update runs in `S.lib.ui_frame.contained_call`, keyed by routing (`matrix routing <k>`) or param id (`matrix destination <id>`): one that raises is skipped at that step, printing `strata: <label> failed:` once per message, and every other routing and destination still plays at every step. A raising engine sender is the round robin's own containment (`LFO send`).
- **An idle step does nothing**: with no routing holding both parts, no offset held and no fast extra, the step returns before touching a table; otherwise its sums and id lists are reused tables, emptied each step, and its send functions and labels are made once per id.
- **A routing plays only while it is on, both its parts are set and known, and its depth is not 0**: `status(k)` is the one answer (#926), and `playing`, `affects`, `uses_source`, the step, `S.lib.envelopes.owns` and MOD's dim rows and rings (`routing_plays`) all read it (Owns, below); a source whose value is not finite reads 0 (`S.lib.mod_sources.normalized`) and reaches nothing: no extra offset, no `setFastOffset`.
- **`set_routing` never applies the 25 % rule**: a recall, a transition's or morph's switch and a PSET load leave the depth as their own params set it. Only `edit_routing` (MOD's edits and learn) gives a routing it completes at depth 0 the depth `MATRIX.new_depth` (25 %).
- **A routing whose source or destination differs between two scenes ramps through 0**: the scene block's `through_zero` names its depth, which a transition fades A × (1 − 2f) to the midpoint and B × (2f − 1) after it, with the routing switched (`set_routing`) once at the midpoint; morph does the same at its crossing (0.52 from A, 0.48 from B). A routing that matches in both crossfades its depth; the on/off switches at a fade's end.
- **Unknown parts are kept verbatim**: a source key or destination id this build lacks (a later release's scene, PSET or live file) is kept, saved again as it was, reported with a console line at each file load and, for a scene, once a session per distinct key or id at the first block apply that holds it (a recall, a fade's end and morph's crossing apply it again and again, the scene sequencer's recalls too), and the routing does not play.
- **The home routings are slots 17 to 22** (the groundwork before 3.8, design §4): LFO n's is `S.matrix[16 + n]` (`home_slot(n)`), its source always `lfo<n>` (a load, an apply and `set_home` force it), its destination any `S.LFO_DESTINATIONS` key but `none`, the macros included (`MATRIX.never` does not apply to a home), or `""`. `MATRIX_SLOTS` stays 16: every R function (`routing`, `set_routing`, `edit_routing`, `clear`, `playing`, `learn`, `uses_source`) keeps slots 1 to 16, so MOD's routing view, learn and the `R` mark keep their answers; `status(k)` takes 1 to 22, and `affects` counts the homes too (#881), so MAIN's `~` shows for an LFO on a voice's Lua param as for an R routing to it. A home has no `matrix_<k>` params: its depth, offset and on/off are its LFO's own. `edit_home` never applies the 25 % rule (an LFO's depth defaults to 25).
- **Files** go through the module's `S.lib.side_files.new` files only (#648; `docs/notes/lua/data-files.md`, Side files: `name` `matrix`, `contents` `routings`, `key` `matrix`, `validate` the scene block's check, `load` the block's load and the unknown parts' lines), which reach the card through `S.lib.data_files` (`write`, `read`, `set_aside`): a missing side file loads no routings (a PSET from before 3.4), an unreadable or invalid one is set aside (`<file>.bak`, ...) with a console line and loads none, and the PSET still loads. The PSET hook's write always writes, an empty side file too, so a PSET saved over never keeps an older one.
- **A live file that could be neither read nor set aside is never written over** (scenes.data's rule): the start step marks the live file loaded only when it was read, missing or set aside; otherwise the routings are not saved in the session (no timed write, nothing at cleanup), with one console line (`... the routings will not be saved this session`).
- **Cleanup sends nothing** (#461; ruling 63, over §10.2's "sends every fast offset to 0"): its step cancels learn and forgets the matrix's offsets; norns has freed the engine by then.
- **Learn** waits exactly while its timeout clock runs (`S.matrix_learn_clock`, the `matrix learn` invariant), `MATRIX.learn_seconds` (10 s); `cancel_learn` ends both. The clock is the clock helper's (`S.lib.clocks`, #870, `docs/notes/lua/clocks.md`; `S.matrix_learn_clock` is its handle's id): its end stops it through the helper, which never cancels it once its resume may be posted (#626): within the helper's margin (`CLOCKS.sleep_margin`, 0.1 s) of the wake it is retired (its wait returns false at its wake and it ends, touching nothing a newer learn armed; the helper's cleanup step cancels a retired one still waiting), and only earlier is it canceled, so a burst of learns holds no clock slots.
- **A learn's rewire is held by the one undo** (#927): the touch hook makes `edit_routing` inside `S.lib.mutation.hold_routing(k, {MOD, "matrix"}, writes, depth_id(k), param_id, before)`, `before` being the touch hooks' third argument (the param's value from before the edit, `lib/ui/detent.lua`), so a quick ALT+K2 on MOD's matrix (its rows or the routing view) puts back the routing's source and destination, its depth (a learn may have set 25 %) and the touched param's value. `set_routing` and `set_home` call `S.lib.mutation.note_routing_edit(k)`, so any other write of the routing ends the hold; so does any edit of the depth or of the touched param but the turn that learned going on (`docs/notes/lua/mutation.md`). A touch that is no destination, or a learn that changes nothing, holds nothing.
- **Every flash fits the header's flash room** (`S.constants.HEADER.flash_width`, 74 px) whole: `not a destination` (74 px), the widest learned destination `R10 > v2 spread` (68 px).

### matrix holding
- **State:** the local `held`: `lua[id]` (the extra offset the matrix set on a Lua destination), `fast[id]` (a fast destination tracked), `fast_want[id]` (what its next send carries) and `fast_extras[id]` (the macros'); what the engine has or will have is the send budget's `expected_value` (of the param id, or the channel's key `matrix fast <voice> <destination>`), never a record of the matrix's (#647). `S.lib.matrix.offset(id)` reads the sum. Per Lua param: `not held`, `held` (an offset), `returning` (no offset, its one send as the sum reaches 0 due in the round robin, `docs/notes/lua/lfos.md`); per fast channel: `untracked` or `tracked`.
- **Invariant:** none: each step sums every routing again from scratch and compares with the budget's answer, so every combination converges at that step; a stored record of what was sent was the fault (#647).
- **Test:** `test/unit/matrix_test.lua`: "matrix: clearing a routing restores the destination", "matrix: routings that cancel to exactly 0 send once, then nothing (#760)", "matrix: a fast sum that returns to 0 while its send waits sends 0, never the stale sum", "matrix: a voice start or a sync resends no unmoved fast channel", "matrix: an idle step sends nothing and leaves no offset".

No `h.transition_table` holds this table (design section 5 makes it a smaller entry, a table and no test): a param's holding is summed again from every routing, home and extra at each step, so its events are every writer of those; the modulation walk (package 3c, design section 6) checks that after a step a model fed by the engine calls agrees with what the params imply, and that an idle step sends nothing. A Lua param that plays but sums to exactly 0 (routings that cancel, #760) is `not held`. A step's sum takes in every playing routing but those the envelopes own, every LFO home it does not own, and (fast) the macros' extras.

## The step

The matrix steps in the LFO clock, as a hook of `S.lib.lfos.on_step`, after the six LFOs, so a routing from an LFO reads that step's value, and after the macros' hook (it registers first: `lib/macros.lua` is included before `lib/matrix.lua`). Per playing routing but the ones the envelopes own (above), `m = depth / 100 × S.lib.mod_sources.normalized(source)` (−1..1 for a bipolar source, 0..1 for a unipolar one, design §10.2), summed by the param the destination resolves to:
- a Lua destination: the sum's `offset_for` becomes the extra offset `matrix` on the param, and a send of it is marked due when its value moved (above), but for `delay_time` while `S.lib.delay_sync.time_unheard()` (a synced delay plays its division, so the send would change nothing: #741, `docs/notes/lua/delay-sync.md`); a destination the matrix held whose sum is now 0 (no playing routing reaches it, or its routings cancel) gives the offset up and is marked due once, and while routings that cancel keep it at exactly 0 it is marked again only when its value moved from `expected_value` (#760);
- a fast destination: the sum plus every fast extra on it, clamped to −2..2, is what its next send carries (`offset(param_id)`), marked due under its channel's key when it moved from `expected_value(key)`; the round robin's send hands it to `send_engine_value(key, sum)` (counted, maybe waiting for the next window), whose sender calls `setFastOffset`; one whose sum returns to 0 is sent 0 once (or as long as the key's expected value is another).

Each routing's read and each destination's update is contained (above); a raising one prints once and the rest of the step goes on. A step with nothing to do returns at once.

`set_fast_extra(source_key, param_id, m)` records the macros' extras (half ranges, by source key `macro<m>`, nil or a value that is not finite clearing it; a param that is no fast destination is ignored). The macros call it at once, never through the round robin (#636: the hand-over reaches no engine), so an extra joins the sum at the matrix's next step: the same step when the LFO step or the macros' hook (both before the matrix's) hands it over.

`uses_source(key)` and `affects(voice)` read the playing routings (`status` `summed` or `owned`): a routing that is off, incomplete, at depth 0 or with an unknown part counts for neither; `affects` reads the six homes as well, each slot's read (its destination's `resolve` among it) contained under `<the step's label> (affects)`: one that raises counts as not playing and prints once per message, so MAIN's draw goes on. `offset(param_id)` is the matrix's current sum on a param: the extra offset on a Lua destination (in its units), the clamped sum on a fast one (half ranges); 0 when nothing reaches it.

## Learn

`learn(k)` (MOD's ALT+K3 on the routing view's `dest` row, package 2a) arms routing k's destination; the next param a hand touches (the `param_touch` registry: an encoder, an arc ring, a grid fader) that is a destination becomes it through `edit_routing` (the 25 % rule applies), and the header flashes `R<k> > <label>` (`R3 > v3 pitch`). A touched voice's main param that is no destination itself learns `v<i>_main`. Any other touched param (a macro, an LFO param, a matrix depth, an option) flashes `not a destination`, and learn waits on. `cancel_learn()` (MOD's K2 in its view, or its routing view showing another routing), a new `learn` or 10 s without a touch ends it; a page change does not (ruling 80: the touch is made on another page). No key cancels a learn from another page; a learn made by mistake costs one undo (#927, the design's call): the rewire is held, as above.

## Storage

- Scenes: the depths and on/offs are registered scene fields (depths continuous, range-checked by `validate_scene`; on/off discrete). The routings are the block `matrix` (`register_scene_block`, discrete: a fade applies it at its end; sparse, `[k] = {source, dest}` for routings with either part set, an empty table when none is; its `default` is `{}`, which a scene from before 3.4 gets, and the fields' defaults are depth 0 and on, so a format 5 scene recalls 16 empty routings; its `validate` refuses a key outside 1-22, a routing that is no table of two strings of at most 32 characters, or a home whose source is not its LFO's `lfo<n>`; a home is held only when its destination is set, `[16 + n] = {source = "lfo<n>", dest = <id>}`; its `through_zero`, above, a home's naming its LFO's depth `strata_lfo<n>_depth`). A load or an apply tells each LFO whose home changed (`S.lib.lfos.home_changed`), after every routing is in place. Its apply loads the routings, prints a console line for every unknown part not yet named in the session (`matrix: a scene: routing 3's source ... is unknown here`), saves the live file and asks for a redraw.
- PSETs: the depths and on/offs are params; the routings and the homes are `<path_base>.matrix.data` (`{version = 2, matrix = <sparse>}`, k 1 to 22; a version 1 file, 3.7's, loads through the side file's floor with every home `""`), written, read and deleted by the PSET hook `matrix`. A silent read needs nothing more: the step reads the params at the next step.
- The live file data/strata/matrix.data (the same layout): loaded at the start step `matrix_load` (a missing file loads no routings), and, once that step has read it, found it missing or set it aside, written `MATRIX.save_delay` (0.1 s) after the routings change, one write per burst carrying the newest routings (`S.matrix_save_clock`), and at once at the cleanup step `matrix_save`, which cancels that clock first. A file that could be neither read nor set aside is never written over (above).
- 16 scenes of 16 full macros, 16 routings, six home routings and every step's attributes (the sequencer's own `steps` block, every attribute set through `S.lib.sequencer.set_step_attr`, eight notes and a gate, saved through `S.lib.scenes.save`) keep scenes.data inside `lib/data_files.lua`'s `max_tables` (32768): 7026 tables (3.7's `sequences` block adds five a scene; a format 10 voice is two tables, where 3.7's was eight; the six homes add six a scene; 3.9's `lanes` block eight, every lane length set), about 1.25 MB of its 4 MB. The test writes and reads one.
