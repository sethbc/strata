## Must not break

- **3.1's sound by default**: reverse 0%, pitch, pan and size jitter 0, intervals off, interval probability 100%, quantize off, scale off, root C. These are the params' defaults and the scene migration's (`scene_defaults`).
- **A quantized `freq` turned slowly moves by scale tones.** One encoder detent (0.8 semitone of raw travel) or one arc tick moves less than a scale step of twelve equal steps or any built-in scale, so the `freq` filter snaps a per-voice unsnapped shadow note, never the incoming value (3.8 ruling 41: a turn moves the shadow by its raw travel times `min(1, S.lib.tuning.mean_step() / one detent's travel)`, the detent's from the freq spec's quantum, so a dense tuning, 24 or 31 equal or Partch's 43, turns about one tone a detent and every twelve-tone scale as before): a filter that snapped the new value to its nearest tone would stick forever on the encoder, and one that stepped a tone in the direction of motion would jump a tone per arc tick. The shadow moves by the change of the incoming value from the stored one only for a **turn** (#318): the filter's fourth argument, true when `S.turning_param` names the param, which `S.lib.ui_detent.as_turn` sets while a turn sets it. Every turn marks itself: `delta_with_detent` (every page's `enc_param_delta`, every ring's `arc_sweep_param`), the freq param's own `delta` (norns' PARAMS menu turns E3 with it, and `params:delta` calls it; `lib/params_setup.lua` replaces it) and a mutation step (`set_mutated_param`, `lib/mutation.lua`). Every other set (a scene fade step, a mapped CC, a grid fader, a MIDI note, a script's `params:set`) is a value in its own right, however near the stored one: the shadow starts again from it, so a fade or a CC sweep moves one way only and lands on the tone nearest its own value, and a set never keeps a turn's leftover offset. The shadow also starts again when the stored value is not what the filter last returned (something changed it behind the filter's back), and it is clamped to the freq range, so turning back from past an end moves at once. A new caller that nudges a filtered param from its stored value without `delta_with_detent` must use `as_turn`, or its small steps snap back. Tested on E3 (`enc_param_delta`), the arc (`arc_sweep_param`), the param's own delta, a fade, a CC sweep, a MIDI note after a turn and mutations in `test/unit/variation_test.lua` and `test/unit/mutation_test.lua`.
- **`scaleNote` is never stale.** It is sent by the voice sync and on every change that moves it: the `freq` filter (every freq change, with the value it settled), the `root` action (every running voice), the `v<i>_quantize` action and a source change hook. An LFO on `freq` moves the sent frequency, never `scaleNote`, which follows the stored value.
- **The engine gets the scale only through `engine.setScale(root - 1, mask)`**, from the `scale` and `root` actions and `scale_mask`'s (which sends only while the scale is custom, so a scale or root change sends exactly one); scale off sends the chromatic mask 4095, which is also the engine's default. The tuning reaches it only through `lib/tuning.lua`'s `setTuning` and `setTuningTone` (`docs/notes/lua/tuning.md`).
- **`M.scale_on()`** is whether the scale param is not off (custom included); `S.lib.tuning.chord()` reads it.
- **`custom` is tested for by name** (`M.scale_is_custom()`, `VARIATION.custom_scale`, ruling 39): it is the last scale option, after musicutil's, and `scale_mask()` reads `scale_mask` for it (`current_scale()` gives nil for it, which would read as off).
- `intervals`, `quantize` and the five custom intervals are discrete scene keys (they switch at a transition's end); `scale`, `root` and `scale_mask` are discrete scene globals. MAIN's randomize and the mutations never touch any variation param (no `main_randomize` or `mutate` group). The fader pages show the three jitters, `pitch_jitter`, `size_jitter` and `pan_jitter` (`fader = {{keys = {...}, rank = 3}}`, after the loop's, the groundwork before 3.8), and no other variation param. A quick ALT+K2 on VOICE's variation section is the one exception (#557): the record's `randomize` draws `reverse` (0-50 %), `pitch_jitter` (0 to half its 12 st top), `pan_jitter` and `size_jitter` (0-0.5) and `interval_prob` (0-100 %), each uniform; `intervals`, the custom intervals and `quantize` are kept (they set the pitch content, and the set adds or removes rows under the cursor).
- The filter is one per key (`S.register_param_filter("freq", ...)`): no other module may register a `freq` filter.

## lib/variation.lua (`S.lib.variation`)

One registered voice-param record (`S.register_voice_params`, `docs/notes/lua/params.md`), `variation`, with twelve params per voice, stored in `S.voices[i].values`:

| Param | Kind, range, default | Engine |
|-------|----------------------|--------|
| `v<i>_reverse` | control 0-100 %, 0 | grain key `reverseProb`, as a fraction |
| `v<i>_pitch_jitter` | control 0-12 st, 0 | `pitchJitter` |
| `v<i>_pan_jitter` | control 0-1, 0 | `panJitter` |
| `v<i>_size_jitter` | control 0-1, 0 | `sizeJitter` |
| `v<i>_intervals` | option off/octaves/fifths/chord/custom, off | `interval1..8`, `intervalCount` (its action, and the sync) |
| `v<i>_interval_prob` | control 0-100 %, 100 | `intervalProb`, as a fraction |
| `v<i>_custom_interval_1..5` | number -24..24 st, -12, 0, 7, 12, 19 | `interval1..5` while the set is custom |
| `v<i>_quantize` | binary off/on, off | `quantize` |

- **Interval sets** (`M.interval_set(i)`): octaves -12, 0, 12; fifths -12, -5, 0, 7, 12; chord `S.lib.tuning.chord()` (3.8 ruling 40: with a scale on in twelve tones to an octave its degrees 1, 3, 5 and 7, two periods of the allowed tones; otherwise the tuning's tones nearest 0, 4, 7 and 11 semitones: 0, 4, 7, 11 with the scale off in twelve equal steps, a pentatonic's 0, 4, 9, 14; fractional semitones in another tuning); custom the five custom intervals. `send_intervals` sends `interval1..8` (0 past the list) and `intervalCount` while the voice runs, the list being `S.lib.arp.list(i, set)` (3.8, `docs/notes/lua/arp.md`: the set as it is while the voice's arpeggiator is off, so 3.7's sends; on, the set or the held chord in the mode's order): from the `intervals` action, a custom interval's action (custom set only), the `scale` action and `scale_mask`'s while custom (chord voices), and a tuning change (`M.tuning_changed()`, chord voices). The record's `sync` sends them, `scaleNote` and (3.7) `rootNote` (`S.lib.samples.rate_root(i)`: the note at rate 1, the file's key or 60; `M.send_root_note(i)` sends it to a running voice), unconditionally (`S.lib.engine_sync.start_voice`, which `toggle_voice` and a pattern step call, marks the voice running after its sync); its `scaleNote` reads the freq the engine plays (`S.lib.engine_sync.engine_base_value`: a step's note, set before the start it makes, #632).
- **`scaleNote`** (`M.scale_note(i, freq)`): a synth voice's `freq` as a fractional MIDI note (`69 + 12 log2(f / 440)`), or `root + 60` for buffer and input voices; 3.7: a buffer voice whose slot's file has a key gets the key (`S.lib.samples.scale_reference`, `docs/notes/lua/samples.md`). A frozen synth voice's is its freeze reference (`S.lib.notes.freeze_reference(i)`, 3.5) whatever `freq` is passed, so no resend moves it while frozen (`docs/notes/lua/notes.md`). `M.send_scale_note(i, freq)` sends it while the voice runs. **Design amendment (fix round 1):** a buffer voice quantizes relative to the rate it plays its slot at, `root + 60 + 12 log2(buf_rate)`, so a MIDI note (rate 1 is note 60, not the root), randomize or a buffer mutation transposes the voice without moving the scale. Lua still sends `root + 60`; the engine's scale area adds the rate's semitones and recomputes whenever `scaleNote` or `bufRate` arrives (`docs/notes/engine/variation.md`), so `buf_rate`'s own sender needs no change.
- **Scale and root**: `M.add_scale_params()`, registered as `register_params("scale", ...)` before the voices (so a PSET sets the scale before any quantized freq), adds a `scale` separator, `scale` (off + every `musicutil.SCALES` name + `custom`, off), `root` (C-B, C), `scale_mask` (3.8: a number 1-4095, 4095, the custom scale's classes from the root) and the tuning's two text params (`S.lib.tuning.add_params()`). `M.scale_mask()` is the scale's pitch classes from the root as a 12-bit set (arithmetic, not Lua 5.3's bit operators: the tests run on LuaJIT too). Both are registered scene fields (`S.register_scene_field`, discrete).
- **Snapping** (`M.snap_note(n)`): `S.lib.tuning.snap(n)`, the allowed tone nearest a note, a tie going down (the engine's table holds the same rule, 3.8 ruling 37); `tone_freq` comes inside the freq range with `S.lib.tuning.step`. The `freq` filter (`freq_filter(i, value, previous, turned)`, above) keeps the result inside the param's range (the nearest tone inside it at either end). A scene recall's values stand as saved (the filter does not snap during `S.scene_recall_in_progress`); a fade's steps are sets, each snapped from its own value, and its end, a recall, sets the scene's values exactly. Turning quantize on snaps the stored freq (not during a recall), and so does a `scale`, `root` or (custom) `scale_mask` change and a tuning change (`M.tuning_changed()`, from `S.lib.tuning.apply_text`), for every voice that quantizes (`M.snap_quantized_freqs`; not while a scene sets values, `S.lib.scenes.setting()`: a recall's apply or a fade step's own sets, whose values stand as saved; a change by hand during a fade snaps, #867): a quantized voice's stored freq is a tone of the current scale, so a PSET or scene saved later holds a tone. A buffer or input voice's freq snaps too, though only the grains' quantize changes what it plays.
- **A PSET loads the voice's exact saved pitch** (#125). norns reads a PSET line by line in param order, and the `freq` filter reads the voice's quantize as each value arrives. So `v<i>_quantize` comes before the voice's `freq`: the record adds it in its `add_first`, which `add_voice_params` runs right after the `voice <i>: <name>` separator, ahead of the synthesis params (the other eleven variation params stay under `voice <i> extras`). The scale and root come before every voice. A PSET with quantize off therefore sets it off before its `freq` arrives (never snapped by the quantize in force before), and one with quantize on snaps the current freq first and then sets its own, a tone of its scale since a scale or root change re-snaps (above). A scale or root changed by hand while a scene fades re-snaps (3.9, #867), and the fade's end sets the scene's scale, root and freqs as saved, a matching set. Tested in `test/unit/variation_test.lua` ("a PSET whose quantize differs from the current value loads the voice's exact stored pitch", "a scale or root change re-snaps a quantized freq, and a PSET saved after it loads exactly"). Scenes never depended on the order (the filter stands down during a recall). The tuning (3.8) comes before every voice too, so a PSET with its tuning lines snaps each freq in its own tuning; a PSET without them (3.7's) gets the default tuning only from the read hook, after the voices' lines, so `lib/tuning.lua`'s `read` PSET hook sets every voice's `freq` the file names again from the file's value once that default changed the tuning (`S.lib.params_setup.read_defaulted`, `pset_value`), and the voice loads its exact saved pitch in that case too (`docs/notes/lua/tuning.md`).
- **MIDI** (`M.midi_note(i, key)`): the key's pitch in the tuning (`S.lib.tuning.pitch_of_key`, the key itself in twelve equal steps), snapped on a quantized voice; `midi_note_on` (`lib/midi_in.lua`) calls it before it sets `freq` or `buf_rate` (`docs/notes/lua/midi.md`).
- **VOICE**: a section `variation`, after `mix`, with the rows reverse, pitch jitter, pan jitter, size jitter, intervals, interval prob, custom 1-5 (only while the set is custom, the record's `when`) and quantize. Its `layout_states` draw every voice with the custom set on a synth, buffer or input source.

`M.VARIATION` holds the constants (the sets, the ranges, the chromatic mask, the reference octave). The engine side (the draws, `scaleBuffer`, `setScale`, grain quantize) is in `docs/notes/engine/variation.md`.

Tests: `test/unit/variation_test.lua` (defaults and sends, interval sets, chords, the quantized freq on E3, the arc and the PARAMS menu's own delta, a fade, a CC sweep and sets after a turn, the recall's stand-down, a MIDI note after a turn, a stored freq changed behind the filter's back, the clamp at the top of the range, quantize on every voice and never on the other main params, MIDI notes, the scale globals and `setScale`, `scaleNote`, an interval change on a stopped voice, the scene round trip, the VOICE rows and layout, randomize, mutation and the faders, the record's `randomize` draws), the mutation walk in `test/unit/mutation_test.lua` and the MIDI case in `test/unit/midi_in_test.lua`.
