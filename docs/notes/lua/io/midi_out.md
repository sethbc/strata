## Must not break

- **A port a remote holds is absent** (the final review's B I1): while `S.lib.io_remote.holds(port)` (a remote grid's or arc's claimed port, or a port midigrid's device is on), `send` sends nothing there and `present(port)` is false, for every message: notes, expression, CC slots, program change, the MPE setup and takedown, the owed offs. **An off goes only where its on went** (#769): a note started while a remote holds its port (`record.held`) is kept so its end finds it, but takes no member channel, count, channel use or place in the expression list, and nothing of it is ever sent, its end included (by its own off, a voice's stop, a route change, a mute or cleanup), even once the port is let go. A note sounding on a port a remote then claims is ended there before the remote holds it: the module's `S.lib.io_ports.on_claim` hook runs once the owner is recorded but before `holds` answers true, and for an owner the remote named through `M.held_by` it settles the port (`settle_moved`), sets aside each note the device there got (owed to none) and sends one `note_off` per channel and note (channels and notes ascending), so its later end and the expression step send nothing. A controller's claimed port is sent to as set (the plan's call for 2a), and its claim ends no note. The port params' formatter (`port_text`: the voices', the CC slots' and `pc out port`) shows any owner beside the number, `3 (remote grid)`, as SETUP's `midi` row does. MIDI out keeps no list of owner names of its own: `lib/io/remote.lua` gives it the remote grid's, the remote arc's and midigrid's (`held_by`, one line at its include).
- **A zone's setup goes once its port settles with its device not held** (#772): a device a remote takes as it arrives gets nothing, and a port a remote grid, a remote arc or midigrid lets go gets its zone's setup before its first MPE note. An MPE port's arrival (the change hook, which still pays the owed offs at once), the release of a claim by an owner named through `held_by` (the claim hook keeps that owner in `state.held_owner[port]`) and a settle that could not reach a held port (`apply`) only mark `state.setup_due[port]`; the module's `S.lib.io_ports.on_settled` hook, which runs once every change and claim hook has been told (a remote's claim of an arriving device, or the arc's claim taken straight back inside the grid's release, included), sends the port's one setup when its device is there and nothing holds it, keeps the mark while one does, and drops it with no device (the next arrival marks again). A zone that goes away drops its mark, and a controller's claim and release mark nothing. A synth plugged into a port the rig still names for a remote grid or arc is the remote's (its `connect` claims whatever arrives there, a device moved there in SYSTEM > DEVICES included, since the menu close settles the port only after every move is told): it gets the remote's ask alone and no MPE setup until the rig's setting lets the port go, when the release sends it. An arrival nobody claims still gets its offs, then its setup, in the same call. **A zone taken down while its port is held is taken down once the port settles free** (#788): `apply` marks `state.takedown_due[port]` instead of sending a takedown a remote grid, a remote arc or midigrid would drop; the settled hook sends that takedown before any setup, once, when its device is there and nothing holds the port, keeps the mark while one does, and drops it with no device. A zone that returns to the port before then clears the mark (its setup stands for both), and the device's departure clears it, so a device that takes the port later (a hot-plug, or a move in SYSTEM > DEVICES, whose close settles the port with the remote already holding the new device) never gets it. At cleanup (#789) the cleanup step takes the zones down before it stops the module: each port with a settled zone, or a takedown owed, gets one takedown (`takedown`, never a copy of its bytes) when its device is there and nothing holds it, after the targets' step (142) sent every note's off and before the remote's (147) and the ports' (152) let the ports go. A zone or an owed takedown on a port a remote holds at cleanup is dropped with the script, never an error: the remote's release (147) comes after the module stops, and a port a remote holds then is a grid or an arc, not the synth. A takedown, at cleanup or where a voice leaves MPE, is the zero-member configuration message alone: the bend ranges the setup sent (the members' and the master's) are never put back, since Strata never knew the synth's own. So a zone is taken down when its voice leaves MPE, when its port changes and at cleanup, and a takedown owed to a device that has left is forgotten (above). Tests: `cleanup takes down every zone it set up, after the last note off and before the ports' cleanup (#789)`, `a played MPE note held into cleanup gets its off before the zone's takedown (#789)`, `cleanup sends no takedown to a port with no zone (#789)`, `cleanup sends no takedown to a zone's port with no device (#789)`, `cleanup drops a takedown owed to a held port, and takes down a free port's zone (#788, #789)`, `cleanup sends nothing to a zone on a held port (#789)`, `an MPE zone's setup waits while a remote grid takes its arriving device (#772)`, `a port a remote grid lets go gets its zone setup before its first MPE note (#772)`, `a port midigrid lets go gets its zone setup (#772)`, `a release the remote arc takes straight back sends no setup (#772)`, `a device that arrives on a port the rig still names for a remote is the remote's: it gets the ask alone, and the zone's setup once the rig lets the port go (#772)`, `a synth moved in the menu onto the port the rig names for a remote gets the ask alone (#772)`, `a zone taken down while a remote grid holds its port is taken down when the port is let go (#788)`, `a zone that returns before the port is let go gets its setup alone (#788)`, `a takedown owed to a device that leaves is forgotten (#788)`, `a takedown owed to a device that SYSTEM > DEVICES moves off its port is forgotten (#788)`, `a voice moved to another port while its old port is held leaves the takedown owed there (#788)`, `a zone taken down while midigrid holds its port is taken down when midigrid lets it go (#788)`, `a zone taken down while a remote arc holds its port is taken down when the arc lets it go (#788)`.
- **Nothing by default**: with every param at its default no message goes to any port. A voice is routed only while `v<i>_out_port` is above 0, a CC slot sends only while its source is not `off`, and program change in and out are off.
- **One door**: every message goes through the vport `S.lib.io_ports.out(port)` gives (`note_on`, `note_off`, `cc`, `pitchbend`, `channel_pressure`, `program_change`); `S.lib.io_ports.device(port)` says whether a device is there. The module never reads the `midi` global and never sets anything on a vport.
- **An off for every on** (#709): each sounding id keeps the port, channel and note its on went to, and its off goes there whatever the params say by then. Per port, channel and note the module counts the ids sounding there (`state.counts`), and every on and off follows this table, in plain mode (the voice's channel) and in MPE mode (the member channel the id was given) alike:

  | The count before | An id starts there | An id ends there |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | `note_on`; the count is 1 | nothing (an id that never started) |
  | 1 | `note_off`, then `note_on` with the new id's velocity; the count is 2 | `note_off`; the count is 0 |
  | 2 or more | `note_off`, then `note_on`; the count rises by 1 | nothing; the count falls by 1 |

  So whenever nothing sounds on a port and channel it has had exactly as many note offs as note ons, and while a note sounds exactly one more on than offs for that note: a synth that starts a voice for every note on is never left holding one. The `note_off` of a retrigger goes out before anything else the new id sends on that channel (in MPE, before its bend, pressure and slide). Every way a note ends (its own off, a route change, a voice stop, a mute, the patterns' stop, cleanup) reaches `end_note` through `S.lib.io_targets`, so the table holds through all of them. A device that leaves a port takes its notes with it: each note it got is set aside (marked departed and taken out of the port's counts), at the departure or before the port's next note starts or ends, so the note's end sends nothing to whatever takes the port next (its owed offs, below, go wherever the device arrives). A settle (below) that changes a voice's route calls `S.lib.io_targets.route_changed(voice)` exactly once, before any zone message, so the old notes' offs go out first. The route is what a note's destination or the zone depends on: off, or the port with its channel (plain) or its members (MPE); a change that leaves it alone (a channel while the port is 0, members on a plain voice, a channel on an MPE voice) ends no note, on MIDI or any other target (review finding 2).
- **A played note is never sent back to the port it arrived on** (plan ruling 17): a note with `info.source == "midi"` is skipped when the voice's port is the `midi_device` param's.
- **Routes settle; they are never applied one param at a time** (build ruling 25). A route param's action (a voice's `port`, `channel`, `mode` or `members`, and `midi_out_bend_range`) only notes the change: it refuses nothing, flashes nothing, sends nothing and calls no `route_changed`. The settled routes (`state.config`, and the settled bend range) are what every note, `routed(voice)`, the rows and the zones read, and only `settle` changes them. `settle(from_read)` compares the params with the settled routes, judges the whole new set, ends each changed voice's notes once, and sends each port's takedown and setup once, for the final state only: a port whose zone (its voice and members, and the bend range) is the same before and after gets nothing; one whose zone goes away or passes to another voice gets one takedown, and one whose zone arrives, passes or changes gets one setup, every takedown before any setup. So a synth never receives zone messages for a state that passes. `settle` runs in three places only: once at the end of `add_params` (`from_read` true: the routes at start, #709), the module's PSET read hook (`from_read` true; a read is one synchronous call, so no step falls inside it and every route line of the file is judged together, whatever the order of the lines), and the LFO step hook when something was noted since the last settle (`from_read` false): a change made by hand or by anything outside a read takes effect at the next step, about 21 ms later, and two changes within one step are judged together. The settle at the end of `add_params` builds the settled routes from the params as they stand when the group is added, with a read's judgment, so `state.config` never holds values of its own. On norns no PSET is read at init (Strata never calls `params:default()`), so this settle sees the defaults: init adds every param before any start step and nothing sets one before the first, and with today's defaults (every route off) it changes nothing and sends nothing. A change after it (a mod, a PSET read) is noted or read and settles as above.
- **One MPE voice a port, in its lower zone** (decision 20): a set of routes that puts a second MPE voice on a port, or a plain voice's channel inside an MPE zone on its port (1 to members + 1), is illegal. At a settle from a step the route params noted since the last settle (port, channel, mode, members) go back to their settled values (silently, noting nothing), the refusal's header flash `port has an mpe voice` shows once and no route changes; a bend range noted in the same step cannot cause a conflict, so it stands and is applied in that settle (each MPE port in use gets its one setup with the new range), and otherwise nothing is sent. The invariant `midi out` holds the settled routes legal.
- **A PSET's routes are judged as a whole** (build rulings 19 and 25): after a read the params hold the file's routes (a param the file has no line for at its default, as the read's migration leaves it, #628), and the hook's settle judges them together. Legal: every voice ends exactly on the file's values, with no flash, each voice whose route differs from the settled one gets one `route_changed`, and each port's zone messages go once. Not legal (a file edited by hand, or two MPE voices on a port): the voices are placed in index order, a voice that conflicts with those placed before it gets port 0 (its param set silently) and keeps its other values, and the read shows the flash once. A silent read runs no action and so notes nothing, but the hook still runs after it (it is told `silent`) and settles as for any read, since the params have changed.
- **A played MPE note's first values go before its note on**, on its member channel and in this order: pitchbend, channel pressure (when it has one), CC 74 (when it has one), read from `S.lib.expression.raw(voice, owner, key)`. That read works at the note on because `lib/midi_in.lua` calls `S.lib.expression.first_touch` before `S.lib.notes.play` (build ruling 8, `docs/notes/lua/expression.md`). A step note gets a centered bend (8192) before its note on, only when its member channel holds no other sounding id, and no expression: on a channel it shares (every member taken) it sends no bend and takes the channel as it is, so a played note's bend there is not reset (#709). A played note's first values go whether or not it shares its channel.
- **A tuned pitch's cents ride the MPE bend** (3.8, design §6.5): a note is sent as its nearest key (`round(pitch)`, clamped to 0-127) and its record keeps `detune`, the pitch less that key (beyond half a semitone only at the clamped ends, which the bend range still carries). `encoded(field, raw, detune)` adds it, in semitones, to the bend's `12 × log2(bend)` before the range; all three callers pass the record's detune (a note's first values, the other played notes' sent values on a shared channel, the express step), so an express step resends a changed bend with the same cents. A step note on a shared channel sends no bend (above), so it loses its cents. Plain mode sends the nearest key alone.
- **Later values are read, not waited for** (build ruling 9): the LFO step hook reads `raw` for each sounding played MPE note and sends each of bend, pressure and slide whose outgoing value differs from what that note last sent, at most `MIDI_OUT.express_per_step` (24) messages a step, round robin from the note the last step stopped at, always the value current at the send. The note hook `expressed` fires only when a voice's own mapped values change (`lib/expression.lua`'s `hand_over`), so a pressure or slide the voice maps to nothing would never reach the target through `express`; a played note's bend, pressure and slide go out whatever the voice's mapping, which decides only what Strata's engine does with them. So the target has no `express` field. A step reads `raw` once for each sounding played MPE note and keeps none of its tables across steps. Those notes number at most 23: each MPE voice (one a port, so up to all seven) holds its own note in slot 1 and extra notes from the pool all voices share, at most `NOTE_POOL.max` (16) together (`S.constants.NOTE_SLOTS` caps a voice at 8 notes, 7 extra): 7 + 16, with every voice MPE on a port of its own and the pool at 16 (11 at the standard tier's default pool of 4, 15 at the high tier's 8). The bound of 24 messages a step holds whatever the count. A note that ended sends nothing more. **A shared member channel** (every member taken, #709 review): only played notes are expressing records, so a step note sends nothing there and has nothing put back. Per member channel:

  | What happens on the channel | What the other played notes on that channel do |
  |---|---|
  | A played note's first values go out there (before its note on) | each is taken as having sent its current values: its `sent` is set, field by field, to what `encoded` gives for its raw values now (a field it has no value for is left as it is), so a resend pending for it is canceled and the step sends nothing for it until a value of its changes after this start (one it changed just before, not yet sent, is taken as sent too and waits until a played note on the channel ends); the channel carries the new note's values |
  | A note sends a later value there from the expression step | nothing: they share the newest value, and two notes with different values never resend against each other |
  | A played note on the channel ends, and other played notes remain on it | each forgets what it last sent (`sent = {}`), so the step sends its current values once, within the 24 a step and in the round's order: the channel goes back to what the notes still on it hold |
  | The last note on the channel ends, or a step note ends | nothing |

  A played note re-pitched in place is its end, then its start (`lib/io/targets.lua`), so its own first values stand over the resend its end asked for.
- **A device's return gets a note off for each note it was still owed, wherever it returns** (Review Focus 2): each note keeps the name of the device the port held as its note on went (`record.device`, the one that got it; nil when the port was empty). A move in SYSTEM > DEVICES changes the vports at once but is told only as the menu closes (`S.lib.io_ports.reconcile`), and the patterns play meanwhile, so before a note on a port ends or starts (`end_note`, and `start_note` before it reads the retrigger count) and before the LFO step sends a played MPE note's values (`express_step` settles each expressing note's port first, in port order, #749) `settle_moved(port)` sets aside every note on that port whose `record.device` is not the device the port holds now, a note that names no device included once the port holds one (#748). A note set aside (`set_aside`) is marked departed, leaves the expression list, is taken out of the port's counts and channel use, and its channel and note are owed to its device's name (`left_with[name][channel][note] = true`), so no off or value of it reaches whatever holds the port now; a note that names no device reached none and is owed to none (`set_aside` with a nil key records nothing), and so is a note started while a remote or midigrid held its port (`record.held`, read at `start_note`): nothing of it was sent, so its device never had it, it was never counted (`set_aside` only marks it departed), and a held device's arrival (paid before `S.lib.io_remote` claims the port again) gets no off for it (#767). A departure (`on_change` with no name) sets aside the port's notes the device now there did not get (all of them during an unplug, when the port names none), each owed to its own device, or to none. An arrival pays what its name is owed (`pay_owed(port, name)`, which takes the entry through `arrived_notes(name)`): a `note_off` for each owed channel and note, channels 1 to 16 and notes ascending, at the port it arrives on; the MPE setup of a voice on that port goes once the port settles with its device not held: an arrival, or a release by a remote or midigrid (below). An owed note that sounds there again (the same channel and pitch, sent to the device at its new port mid-menu) still gets its off: the device had two note ons. No CC 123 is ever sent (#750), so nothing cuts a note sent to the device at its new port mid-menu. As the menu closes, after `S.lib.io_ports`' hook (included first) has told every move, the module's own menu-close hook (`reconcile_owed`) pays each port whose device is still owed (#747): a device moved away and back to its own port in one visit is told nothing, since the port holds what it held before. So a note that ends mid-menu, one held until the close, a same-pitch note sent to the old port mid-menu (its own count, its own off, no retrigger off for the moved note), a note sent to the old port while it was empty, a note sent to the moved device at its new port, a move away and back, and an unplug all keep #709's balance on every device. Two edges stay, by design. A note still held on a port that was settled while its device was away (a note on that port starting or ending, or the expression step, while the menu was open) is set aside, so it is cut at the close when the device is back on its port, though the voice still holds it, a plain note as well as a played MPE one (with latch the default note off, a held note is common); its own end then sends nothing. And owed offs are kept by the device's name, so a second device of the same name on another port gets, at its arrival or a close, the offs owed to an unplugged namesake: the module cannot tell two devices of one name apart. The module learns no name at a note on for keying: a name read from the port mid-menu is the new device's.
- **Program change in is not echoed** (Review Focus 3, build ruling 20): each input port has at most one guard, `asked[port] = scene`, standing only for a recall that was asked for: `program_change_in(port, value)` sets it before it asks `S.lib.io_actions.recall_scene(value + 1)` (with quantize off the recall lands inside that call) and, when the recall is refused (an empty scene, out of range), puts back the guard the port had before, so a quantized recall still waiting keeps its guard; a newer program change in on the port that is asked for replaces it. The first `scene_recalled(n)` for that same scene consumes it and sends nothing on that port; a recall of any other scene meanwhile sends its own program change and leaves the guard, so the quantized recall it waits for still does not echo when it lands. The cost: if the scheduled recall never lands (replaced or canceled), the next recall of that scene sends no program change on that port, once. Not echoing wins, because two devices that each echo would loop.
- **Params are PSET-only**: none is a scene field, a macro target, an LFO destination, a fader-page param or drawn by a randomize or mutation. The CC slots' sources are kept by key (`S.lib.io_keyed`, plan ruling 24).
- **Hooks and targets never yield** and never call `S.lib.notes.play`.

## Machines

### midi out
- **State:** the settled routes `state.config` (what `routed`, the rows and the zones read), the counts `state.counts[port][channel][note]` and the channel use `state.use[port][channel]`.
- **Invariant:** `midi out`: the settled routes are legal (one MPE voice a port, no plain channel inside a zone), and no count or channel use is below 0.
- **Test:** `test/unit/io_midi_out_test.lua`: "a second MPE voice on a port, and a plain channel inside a zone, are refused" and every test of the module, after which the invariant runs. The routes' settle has no table: it compares at use (build ruling 25), the pattern its notes above give.

### midi out note
- **State:** a voice's note records `state.notes[voice][id]`, each `live`, `held` (`record.held`: started while a remote held the port) or `departed` (`record.departed`), and how many ids hold one note; a debt owed to a device is `state.left_with[name][channel][note]`, which no record names.
- **Invariant:** none: every mix of a record's flags is legal, `held` and `departed` together too (`set_aside` marks a held record departed, and `end_note` returns on either); the counts are `midi out`'s.
- **Two facts:** `record.departed` means "send nothing more of this" for three causes: its device left (owed to it), a remote claimed the port (its off sent then, owed to none) and, beside `held`, a held note whose device left. `record.device == nil` means the port was empty at the note on: the note reached no device and is owed to none.
- **Test:** `test/unit/io_midi_out_test.lua`: "midi out note: every cell is decided" and its rows: voice 1 plain on port 3, channel 1, a synth there, step notes of pitch 60 by the ids `s1` and `s2`. `state_of` reads the module's records; a debt is in no state, so each row that leaves one or pays it has a check that plugs the synth in again (it gets the owed off) or ends the ids (they send nothing more).
- **Walk:** `test/unit/io_walk_test.lua` (3.9's package 3d), four seeds of 400 steps: three synths on ports 1 to 3, voices 1 and 2 plain and voice 3 in an MPE zone, played from a keyboard on port 6; step, played and MPE notes start and end while the synths are unplugged and plugged (a namesake of one among them), moved in SYSTEM > DEVICES with the menu's close, claimed and let go by the remote grid or arc, rerouted, and the patterns stop; it ends with every device on a port again and `cleanup()`. Its oracle is per device in the MIDI stub and is decided from the walk's own log (what each device got, at which port, from which module; the ports' holders at each menu's open and close; the claims, moves and keys it made), never from this module's state: never more note offs than note ons for a channel and note; nothing from this module to a port the walk's record of the claims holds, and `S.lib.io_remote.holds` agreeing with that record after every step; a remote's claim leaves its device sounding nothing; a menu's close sends a device that ends the visit on the port it began it on an off only for a note it got at a port the close tells gone (that port's holder changed over the visit), or under an allowance; and after cleanup as many offs as ons. Its allowances, each counted and required: `namesake` and `away_cut` are the two edges of Must not break ("Two edges stay": an off owed to an unplugged device reaching another of its name, credited to that namesake; and a note on a port settled while its device was away, by a key played while the menu was open, cut at the close), and `stranded` is #988, a fault the walk found: a note sent mid-menu to a device at a port it visits, the device moved on or back before the close, is told nothing at the close when that port's holder is the same at the close as at the open, so the note stays on a port its device has left; its end sets it aside, owed to the device, which gets the off only at its next arrival or the next menu close. The walk marks such a note from its own log and allows that one note open until the device has its off.

`the menu closes` is the norns menu opened and closed with the device on port 3, if one is there, moved to port 4 in SYSTEM > DEVICES meanwhile; `a remote claims` is the rig's remote grid port set to 3. Eleven pairs cannot happen and are no rows: `the device arrives` at a live, a held or no note (the device is on its port), `a remote claims` a held note's port (a port has one owner) or a departed note's (a remote claims only a port with a device on it), and `the device leaves` a departed note (it has left).

### midi out zones
- **State:** per port, `state.setup_due[port]`, `state.takedown_due[port]` and `state.held_owner[port]` against the settled zones (`zones_of(state.config)`) and `S.lib.io_ports.owner(port)`.
- **Invariant:** `midi out zones`: a port never owes both its zone's setup and its takedown; while the module runs, `held_owner[port]` is the port's owner exactly when that owner is a holder named through `held_by` (a remote grid, a remote arc, midigrid), else nil. After `stop` the holders are not kept (the remote releases at 147, after the module stops at 143).
- **Two facts:** `held_owner[port]` is a second record of the port's claim (the ports' `owner`), kept so the release of a holder's claim is known from a controller's; the invariant holds the two together.
- **Test:** `test/unit/io_midi_out_test.lua`: "the midi out zones invariant names a port with its setup and takedown both due" and "the midi out zones invariant names a held owner the ports do not agree with".

## How it works

- **The zone messages**: setup, sent at a settle where a port's zone arrives, passes to another voice or changes (members, the bend range), and once the port settles with its device not held: an arrival, or a release by a remote or midigrid: `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 6, 1)`, `cc(6, members, 1)`; `cc(101, 0, 2)`, `cc(100, 0, 2)`, `cc(6, bend_range, 2)`; `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 0, 1)`, `cc(6, 2, 1)`. Takedown, at a settle where a port's zone goes away or passes to another voice, or, for a port a remote or midigrid held then, once the port settles free with its device there (unless a zone returned first), and at cleanup for each zone or owed takedown on a port with its device there that nothing holds (#789): `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 6, 1)`, `cc(6, 0, 1)`.
- **Plain**: note `clamp(round(pitch), 0, 127)`, velocity `max(1, round(velocity * 127))`, on the voice's channel.
- **MPE allocation**: the free member channel released longest ago; with none free, the one with the fewest notes, and among those the one given a note longest ago. A bend is `clamp(round(8192 + 8192 * 12 * log2(bend) / bend_range), 0, 16383)`; pressure and slide `round(value * 127)`.
- **CC out**, from the step: each slot whose source is not `off` and whose port has a device sends `cc(number, round(fraction(key) * 127), channel)` when the value differs from its last, at most 8 a step. A slot's settings changing, or its port's device arriving, sends it again at the next step. A slot following an LFO the engine runs shows ` ~` on IO: it sends Lua's model of it (decision 20).
- **Program change out**: with `midi_pc_out` on, each recall sends `program_change(scene - 1, midi_pc_out_channel)` on `midi_pc_out_port` (a fade tells the hook at its first step), unless a guard of that port names the scene (above).
- **Cleanup** (143, after the targets' 142 sent every off, before the remote's 147 and the ports' 152): `stop()` sends each port's one takedown (a settled zone, or one owed, on a port with its device there that nothing holds, #789), then drops the express queue and the owed takedowns, and the module sends nothing more.

## IO

- The target's `rows(voice)`: one `detail` row `midi` in the `voice` section (text `off`, `port 2  ch 3` or `port 2  mpe 15`; fields port, channel, mode, members).
- The section `midi` (title `midi out`): a `param` row `bend range`; eight `detail` rows `cc 1` to `cc 8` (text `off` or `lfo1  p2 ch3 #20`, fields source, port, channel, number); five `param` rows `pc in`, `pc channel`, `pc out`, `pc out port`, `pc out channel`. Status `[--]` when a port some setting sends on has no device (`S.lib.io_ports.device`, so a port a remote holds, whose device is there, is not one). Layout states: `without its device`, `with its device` (each with an MPE voice, a plain voice, a slot and program change out set) and `a detail list open` (`cc 1`'s). The target record adds the voice section's `midi detail list open` (voice 1 on port 2's synth, its `midi` row reached through `S.lib.pages_io.go_to_row` and opened with K3), listed as `voice: midi detail list open`.
