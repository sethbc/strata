## Must not break

- **Nothing by default**: with every param at its default no message goes to any port. A voice is routed only while `v<i>_out_port` is above 0, a CC slot sends only while its source is not `off`, and program change in and out are off.
- **One door**: every message goes through the vport `S.lib.io_ports.out(port)` gives (`note_on`, `note_off`, `cc`, `pitchbend`, `channel_pressure`, `program_change`); `S.lib.io_ports.device(port)` says whether a device is there. The module never reads the `midi` global and never sets anything on a vport.
- **An off for every on** (#709): each sounding id keeps the port, channel and note its on went to, and its off goes there whatever the params say by then. Per port, channel and note the module counts the ids sounding there (`state.counts`), and every on and off follows this table, in plain mode (the voice's channel) and in MPE mode (the member channel the id was given) alike:

  | The count before | An id starts there | An id ends there |
  |---|---|---|
  | 0 | `note_on`; the count is 1 | nothing (an id that never started) |
  | 1 | `note_off`, then `note_on` with the new id's velocity; the count is 2 | `note_off`; the count is 0 |
  | 2 or more | `note_off`, then `note_on`; the count rises by 1 | nothing; the count falls by 1 |

  So whenever nothing sounds on a port and channel it has had exactly as many note offs as note ons, and while a note sounds exactly one more on than offs for that note: a synth that starts a voice for every note on is never left holding one. The `note_off` of a retrigger goes out before anything else the new id sends on that channel (in MPE, before its bend, pressure and slide). Every way a note ends (its own off, a route change, a voice stop, a mute, the patterns' stop, cleanup) reaches `end_note` through `S.lib.io_targets`, so the table holds through all of them; a device that leaves and returns keeps the counts (its CC 123 below is not counted as an off). A settle (below) that changes a voice's route calls `S.lib.io_targets.route_changed(voice)` exactly once, before any zone message, so the old notes' offs go out first. The route is what a note's destination or the zone depends on: off, or the port with its channel (plain) or its members (MPE); a change that leaves it alone (a channel while the port is 0, members on a plain voice, a channel on an MPE voice) ends no note, on MIDI or any other target (review finding 2).
- **A played note is never sent back to the port it arrived on** (ruling 17): a note with `info.source == "midi"` is skipped when the voice's port is the `midi_device` param's.
- **Routes settle; they are never applied one param at a time** (ruling E). A route param's action (a voice's `port`, `channel`, `mode` or `members`, and `midi_out_bend_range`) only notes the change: it refuses nothing, flashes nothing, sends nothing and calls no `route_changed`. The settled routes (`state.config`, and the settled bend range) are what every note, `routed(voice)`, the rows and the zones read, and only `settle` changes them. `settle(from_read)` compares the params with the settled routes, judges the whole new set, ends each changed voice's notes once, and sends each port's takedown and setup once, for the final state only: a port whose zone (its voice and members, and the bend range) is the same before and after gets nothing; one whose zone goes away or passes to another voice gets one takedown, and one whose zone arrives, passes or changes gets one setup, every takedown before any setup. So a synth never receives zone messages for a state that passes. `settle` runs in three places only: once at the end of `add_params` (`from_read` true: the routes at start, #709), the module's PSET read hook (`from_read` true; a read is one synchronous call, so no step falls inside it and every route line of the file is judged together, whatever the order of the lines), and the LFO step hook when something was noted since the last settle (`from_read` false): a change made by hand or by anything outside a read takes effect at the next step, about 21 ms later, and two changes within one step are judged together. The settle at the end of `add_params` builds the settled routes from the params as they stand when the group is added, with a read's judgment, so `state.config` never holds values of its own: init adds every param before any start step and nothing sets one before the first, and with today's defaults (every route off) it changes nothing and sends nothing. A change after it (a mod, a PSET read) is noted or read and settles as above.
- **One MPE voice a port, in its lower zone** (decision 20): a set of routes that puts a second MPE voice on a port, or a plain voice's channel inside an MPE zone on its port (1 to members + 1), is illegal. At a settle from a step the route params noted since the last settle (port, channel, mode, members) go back to their settled values (silently, noting nothing), the refusal's header flash `port has an mpe voice` shows once and no route changes; a bend range noted in the same step cannot cause a conflict, so it stands and is applied in that settle (each MPE port in use gets its one setup with the new range), and otherwise nothing is sent. The invariant `midi out` holds the settled routes legal.
- **A PSET's routes are judged as a whole** (rulings C and E): after a read the params hold the file's routes (a param the file has no line for at its default, as the read's migration leaves it, #628), and the hook's settle judges them together. Legal: every voice ends exactly on the file's values, with no flash, each voice whose route differs from the settled one gets one `route_changed`, and each port's zone messages go once. Not legal (a file edited by hand, or two MPE voices on a port): the voices are placed in index order, a voice that conflicts with those placed before it gets port 0 (its param set silently) and keeps its other values, and the read shows the flash once. A silent read runs no action and so notes nothing, but the hook still runs after it (it is told `silent`) and settles as for any read, since the params have changed.
- **A played MPE note's first values go before its note on**, on its member channel and in this order: pitchbend, channel pressure (when it has one), CC 74 (when it has one), read from `S.lib.expression.raw(voice, owner, key)`. That read works at the note on because `lib/midi_in.lua` calls `S.lib.expression.first_touch` before `S.lib.notes.play` (ruling A, `docs/notes/lua/expression.md`). A step note gets a centered bend (8192) before its note on, only when its member channel holds no other sounding id, and no expression: on a channel it shares (every member taken) it sends no bend and takes the channel as it is, so a played note's bend there is not reset (#709). A played note's first values go whether or not it shares its channel.
- **Later values are read, not waited for** (ruling B): the LFO step hook reads `raw` for each sounding played MPE note and sends each of bend, pressure and slide whose outgoing value differs from what that note last sent, at most `MIDI_OUT.express_per_step` (24) messages a step, round robin from the note the last step stopped at, always the value current at the send. The note hook `expressed` fires only when a voice's own mapped values change (`lib/expression.lua`'s `hand_over`), so a pressure or slide the voice maps to nothing would never reach the target through `express`; a played note's bend, pressure and slide go out whatever the voice's mapping, which decides only what Strata's engine does with them. So the target has no `express` field. A step reads `raw` once for each sounding played MPE note and keeps none of its tables across steps. Those notes number at most 23: each MPE voice (one a port, so up to all seven) holds its own note in slot 1 and extra notes from the pool all voices share, at most `NOTE_POOL.max` (16) together (`S.constants.NOTE_SLOTS` caps a voice at 8 notes, 7 extra): 7 + 16, with every voice MPE on a port of its own and the pool at 16 (11 at the standard tier's default pool of 4, 15 at the high tier's 8). The bound of 24 messages a step holds whatever the count. A note that ended sends nothing more.
- **A device's return gets all notes off** (Review Focus 2): CC 123 on each channel the module had notes on as the device left or has now, then the MPE setup of a voice on that port; a channel that never had a note gets none. Expression a note sent while its device was gone is not sent again: after that all notes off no note it played is held on the device, so nothing is owed.
- **Program change in is not echoed** (Review Focus 3, ruling D): each input port has at most one guard, `asked[port] = scene`, standing only for a recall that was asked for: `program_change_in(port, value)` sets it before it asks `S.lib.io_actions.recall_scene(value + 1)` (with quantize off the recall lands inside that call) and, when the recall is refused (an empty scene, out of range), puts back the guard the port had before, so a quantized recall still waiting keeps its guard; a newer program change in on the port that is asked for replaces it. The first `scene_recalled(n)` for that same scene consumes it and sends nothing on that port; a recall of any other scene meanwhile sends its own program change and leaves the guard, so the quantized recall it waits for still does not echo when it lands. The cost: if the scheduled recall never lands (replaced or canceled), the next recall of that scene sends no program change on that port, once. Not echoing wins, because two devices that each echo would loop.
- **Params are PSET-only**: none is a scene field, a macro target, an LFO destination, a fader-page param or drawn by a randomize or mutation. The CC slots' sources are kept by key (`S.lib.io_keyed`, ruling 24).
- **Hooks and targets never yield** and never call `S.lib.notes.play`.

## How it works

- **The zone messages**: setup, sent at a settle where a port's zone arrives, passes to another voice or changes (members, the bend range), and when the port's device arrives: `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 6, 1)`, `cc(6, members, 1)`; `cc(101, 0, 2)`, `cc(100, 0, 2)`, `cc(6, bend_range, 2)`; `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 0, 1)`, `cc(6, 2, 1)`. Takedown, at a settle where a port's zone goes away or passes to another voice: `cc(101, 0, 1)`, `cc(100, 6, 1)`, `cc(6, 0, 1)`.
- **Plain**: note `clamp(round(pitch), 0, 127)`, velocity `max(1, round(velocity * 127))`, on the voice's channel.
- **MPE allocation**: the free member channel released longest ago; with none free, the one with the fewest notes, and among those the one given a note longest ago. A bend is `clamp(round(8192 + 8192 * 12 * log2(bend) / bend_range), 0, 16383)`; pressure and slide `round(value * 127)`.
- **CC out**, from the step: each slot whose source is not `off` and whose port has a device sends `cc(number, round(fraction(key) * 127), channel)` when the value differs from its last, at most 8 a step. A slot's settings changing, or its port's device arriving, sends it again at the next step. A slot following an LFO the engine runs shows ` ~` on IO: it sends Lua's model of it (decision 20).
- **Program change out**: with `midi_pc_out` on, each recall sends `program_change(scene - 1, midi_pc_out_channel)` on `midi_pc_out_port` (a fade tells the hook at its first step), unless a guard of that port names the scene (above).
- **Cleanup** (143, after the targets' 142 sent every off): `stop()` drops the express queue and the module sends nothing more.

## IO

- The target's `rows(voice)`: one `detail` row `midi` in the `voice` section (text `off`, `port 2  ch 3` or `port 2  mpe 15`; fields port, channel, mode, members).
- The section `midi` (title `midi out`): a `param` row `bend range`; eight `detail` rows `cc 1` to `cc 8` (text `off` or `lfo1  p2 ch3 #20`, fields source, port, channel, number); five `param` rows `pc in`, `pc channel`, `pc out`, `pc out port`, `pc out channel`. Status `[--]` when a port some setting sends on has no device. Layout states: `without its device`, `with its device` (each with an MPE voice, a plain voice, a slot and program change out set) and `a detail list open` (`cc 1`'s).
