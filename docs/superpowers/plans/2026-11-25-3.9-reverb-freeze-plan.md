# 3.9: The High Tier's Reverb Freeze: Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development to implement this plan task-by-task, as `docs/workflow.md` runs it (one worktree and branch a package, a batch landing). Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** On the high tier, `reverb_freeze` holds the reverb's tail for as long as the player likes: within 1 dB of its level and of its band above 6 kHz after a minute, with a step under 1 dB at the freeze, while an unfrozen reverb stays 3.8.0's sample for sample and the real-time pool gains room.

**Architecture:** Two packages, the second built on the first, landed as one batch. The engine package moves the FDN's eight lines into sixteen buffers (a `BufDelayL` and a `BufDelayN` a line), adds `frozen` to the def and the command `setReverbFreeze`, and gives the engine check a `reverb` area. The Lua package adds the `reverb_freeze` param to `lib/fx_hold.lua` and makes `offered("reverb")` true on the high tier; the pages already read it.

**Tech Stack:** SuperCollider 3.14.1 locally and 3.13 in Docker for the engine check; Lua 5.3 and LuaJIT against `test/norns_stubs.lua`.

**Spec:** `docs/design/3.9-reverb-freeze-design.md` (approved 2026-11-25). Its facts are the two files under docs/design/3.9-reverb-freeze-facts/ (`engine.md`, `lua.md`), gathered on 2026-11-25 from the tree `main` has at 80b28332; a task names the fact section it argues from. Line numbers there are that commit's: find each site by its text. The design's measurement harness (offline renders read through `Poll`) is kept in the main checkout's `.superpowers/sdd/2026-11-25-reverb-freeze-design/spike/`; a worker may copy it into its own scratch folder to measure a variant quickly.

## Global Constraints

- Everything in `AGENTS.md` applies: one audio-rate `LocalIn` a SynthDef, no `LocalBuf`, no delay line sized from a control, `areaCall` for long-lived frames, plain sends to `effectKeys`, bypass explicit, named constants, `send_param_to_engine`, `set_binary_param`, `request_redraw()` and `grid_redraw()`.
- **The bar** (design, Decided 6): frozen, the level and the band above 6 kHz are within 1 dB after 60 s, and the level's step at the freeze is within 1 dB of an unfrozen twin's level in the same window, with shimmer at 0 and at 1.
- **A miss stops the build.** If the engine package's identity check or its measurement of the bar fails on the real def, the worker reports BLOCKED with the figures and builds nothing further; the controller takes it to the maintainer. Nothing ships failing and nothing is cut silently.
- **An unfrozen reverb is 3.8.0's, bit for bit**: at `frozen` 0 the largest difference from a check-only copy of the def before this work is 0, the size still and moving, at shimmer 0 and 0.5.
- **The def loads at scsynth's defaults**: 64 wire buffers, 1024 buffers, an 8192 KB pool, as norns' server and the check's have them. Nothing raises a server option.
- **A reverb node is never born frozen**, and a reverb is never bypassed and frozen.
- **`\strataReverbFDN`'s limit falls**: `fdnDefLimitKB` goes from 235 to the measured figure plus about 6 KB (about 85 KB is expected). No worst case's free figure falls below today's.
- **`AGENTS.md` stays within 8,000 words** (7,927 at the start). Only the engine package edits it, and shortens other lines by what it adds.
- **`lib/Engine_Strata.sc`, `init`, `cleanup()` and `redraw()` are never edited.**
- **Changelog:** each package adds `changes/rf-<n>-<slug>.md`; no package edits CHANGELOG.md. The engine's fragment says the engine changed (restart norns from SYSTEM > RESTART).
- **A fault found on the way gets its own issue.**

## Review Focus

The five conditions the design implies that are most likely to bite a player; each has its test in the task named.

1. **The reverb is frozen, then bypassed, then lifted.** It comes back empty and unfrozen, the param at 0 and a mapped controller told. Tests in 1 ("a freed reverb is never frozen") and 2 (the reverb's machine: a bypass ends the freeze first).
2. **A PSET is loaded while the reverb is frozen.** A load that leaves the reverb running keeps the freeze; one that bypasses it, silently or with actions, releases it. Test in 2.
3. **The freeze is set where it cannot act**: on the standard tier, while bypassed, from a MIDI map or PARAMS. The param goes back to 0, the controller hears the 0, and no `setReverbFreeze(1)` is sent. Test in 2.
4. **Size, damp and shimmer are moved while frozen**, by hand, an LFO or a scene. The size waits for the release and glides; damp shades the output only; shimmer adds nothing. The held level stays. Tests in 1 ("a size change waits", "damp shades the output", the hold at shimmer 1).
5. **The freeze is held far longer than a minute, or toggled fast.** The loop is whole-sample reads at a gain of exactly 1, so nothing accumulates; ten toggles in two seconds leave one node, no bad value and a level within the bar. Test in 1 ("toggled ten times in 2 s").
